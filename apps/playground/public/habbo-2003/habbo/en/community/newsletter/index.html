DISNEY CHANNEL KIDS AWARDS - THE WRITE-UP!

A few weeks ago we offered a cool prize in Funky Friday of an exclusive family ticket to the Disney Channel Kids Awards in London which took place on September 20, 2003. Unfortunately official winner, smileyciaran couldn't attend so we decided to pick (at random) another Habbo who could gop and see the show. Here's what Bubblebaby had to say about it -
Bubblebaby: "Habbo asked me and Warkle to write about the Disney Channel Kids Awards, so here you go :)
We got to see Craig David, Busted and Girls Aloud! Girls Aloud were the best dancers, Busted were much louder (and cuter!) than I thought they would be. The best part of the night was definitely Craig David 'coz when he won an award they called him Craig Davis! Hehe..
Everyone had such good time and we got all kinds of cool free stuff - hats, pens, balloons and magazines. The time went so fast, it felt like we had only just sat down when the show was over :( "

Warkle1993:
"I had a great time. It was cool to see Gareth Gates live, even though his hair is really really long! He sooooo needs a hair cut! My best bit was the monks at the start though! They jumped around in bright orange doing all kinds of somersaults, back flips and crazy ninja skills. One of the monks accidentally banged his head, but it only added to the fun! All in all, the four of us had a great time, but it was such a shame it was over so fast!"

GUEST REPORTER: Rules for safe Habbos by catz! *edited by Jibbi*

Hello Habbos..
Let me warn you - rules are there for a reason and are not to be ignored. If you follow the rules carefully, you'll remain a safe and happy Habbo. Here are the main rules to follow:

1.) NEVER ever give out your personal details e.g. your email address, your name, your mobile or home telephone number.
2.) NEVER tell anyone your password as it is your Habbo heart and if you give it away your heart will be broken and stolen from you (and probably your furni will be stolen too!)
3.) DON'T fall for the scams such as "I can get you free credits if you tell me your password" or "go to this website, it will get you rares and free HC membership". They are scams and always ask for your Habbo password. If they do - don't give it out. You'll lose everything and gain nothing.
4.) Always ask permission from your mum, dad or guardian (or whoever pays the phone bill) before you buy Habbo Credits on the phone. If you don't - they'll find out when they get the phone bill and then you'll be the one in trouble :(
5.) READ the Habbo Way CAREFULLY, look out for any new scams that have been happening from a report in "Habbo Happenings" or Habbohood Watch" so that you can be aware of them and make sure you don't fall for them.
6.) Think wisely, be smart, STAY SAFE!!!
