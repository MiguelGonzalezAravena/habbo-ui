Habbo Club

Habbo Club is Habbo Hotel's exclusive club and as a member of this club you get privileges that are not available to Habbos without Habbo Club. Membership to Habbo Club will cost you 25 Coins for one month, 60 Coins for 3 months or 105 Coins for 6 months, but it is well worth the money!

As A Member You Get:

  • Priority access to the Hotel and Public Rooms
  • A cool Habbo Club badge so everyone in the Hotel will know you are a member
  • A rare piece of Furni delivered to your hand each month you are a member
  • 400 extra spaces for friends on your Friend List
  • Three new funky dances
  • Exclusive hair and clothes
  • Access to exclusive Habbo Club-only Public Rooms
  • Special Guest Room layouts only for Habbo Club members
  • Special in-game commands
