Rare Habbo Club Furni

where's my HC Furni?

If you buy more than one months Habbo Club, don't expect to get a gift immediately. Your gifts will come once a month every month. The day you join Habbo Club you will get one gift, then after 31 days another, then after another 31 days another, and so on.

Is my gift late?

If you are not sure when you will get your next gift, go to Hotel View and click the Habbo Club icon to open your Habbo Club pop-up. It will tell you how many months you've been a Habbo Club member and how long till your next gift there.

My gift hasn't arrived

If your Habbo Club gift hasn't arrived in your hand by the end of the month then you should use the Habbo Help Tool to contact Player Support.
