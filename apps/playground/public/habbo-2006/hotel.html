Habbo Hotel is a virtual community where you can hang out with friends, design your own unique space and create your own games and competitions.

To get started:
1. Get Shockwave (it's free)
2. Enter Habbo Hotel
3. Create your Habbo and play!

Habbo Coins

Habbo Coins are the currency of Habbo Hotel, and can be used to do lots of stuff in the game including:

- Decorate your room with Habbo Furniture
- Adopt your very own Pet
- Join Habbo Club (our exclusive VIP club)
- Play games like Wobble Squabble and Battle Ball
- And much more!
