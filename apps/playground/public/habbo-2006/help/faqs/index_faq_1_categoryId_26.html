Habbo Coins

Habbo Coins - the currency you use to buy furni, join the Habbo Club, go diving, play Wobble Squabble and other things.

How can I buy Habbo Coins?

There are many different ways to purchase coins. Click here to access our main coins page.

What is my Habbo Purse?

Your Habbo Purse is where all your Habbo Coins are stored. You can access your purse by clicking on the purse Icon in the bottom of your screen once you have checked in.

Do I need Habbo Coins to Play in Habbo Hotel?

No! You only need Habbo Coins if you want to use extra features such as tickets for diving, furniture for your room etc.

How much do Habbo Coins cost?

Habbo Coins cost $.20 in US dollars. When you use some payment methods such as PayByCash there is a small fee added to the cost of the coins.

How do I buy Habbo Coins from the UK?

You can either pay by credit card, or purchase coins over the phone from the UK. CLICK HERE for more information. Please do not send cash under any circumstances. You can also check out the payment pages linked from your Purse for more information.

Why can't Habbo Coins be free?

Habbo Hotel is available to anyone over the Internet. Visiting the hotel is free - unlike other services that provide the kind of experience we do, you don't have to buy a CDROM or pay a monthly subscription fee. We only charge for 'extras', such as furniture and games, and we have priced these at an affordable level for everyone - anyone can have a digital TV in the Habbo Hotel! You are welcome in the hotel, whether you buy Coins or not.

What is SMS?

SMS is a method of purchasing Habbo Coins through text messaging on your cell phone. If you have a cellular phone with Sprint PCS, Cingular/AT&T, Verizon, Nextel, T-MobileÂ® or Boost you can buy Habbo Coins by texting. Just follow the simple 3-step process below to get 10 Habbo Coins. The whole process will cost $2.00 plus what your cellphone carrier normally charges you for sending Text Messages. CLICK HERE for additional information.

What are Vouchers?

Vouchers are currently just the new way to redeem and get your Coins in your purse when you buy them via SMS. You still get a special code but now you must log in to the hotel as the Habbo you've bought the Credits for to type it in and get your Credits. Once you've logged in, click on your purse and click the 'vouchers' button. Enter the code in the box, click 'get Credits' and they will be added to your purse.

What are Habbo Coins?

Habbo Coins are the hotel's currency - they are what you use to pay for extras in the hotel, like diving and furniture for your guest room. Click Here to purchase Habbo Coins.
