Habbo X

Information for and about the Habbo X program in the US Habbo hotel.

Can Habbo Xs enter full rooms?

Yes, Habbo Xs can enter full rooms.

Why has my Habbo X application been rejected?

When you don't meet the Habbo X criteria, the system will reject your application. If your application is rejected, you should try again when you meet the criteria.

To be a Habbo X, do I have to live in the US?

No, Habbo X is open to all Habbos regardless of nationality and creed.

How do I apply to be a Habbo X?

To become a HabboX you need to meet the following criteria:



  1. Have been visiting Habbo Hotel US for at least six months


  2. Have visited the Hotel more than 200 times.


  3. Know and live by the Habbo Way


  4. Like to help new Habbos


  5. Be patient and good at explaining things


  6. Have a clean Habbo record on Habbo Hotel US

Applications are closed at the time, please check later for any changes in this program

How can I recognize a Habbo X?

A Habbo X will have a special 'X' badge (see right) and they will always show it when asked. Don't fall for impersonators, always ask to see their badge. Do not fall for impersonators without a badge.

Does Habbo X have an age limit?

No, Habbos of all ages can apply to be Habbo X.

Where can I find Habbo Xs?

Habbo Xs will mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Rooms.

Who are the Habbo eXperts?

Habbo eXperts can be found on this page:


Habbo X Information Page

Will unfair bans be counted against me for Habbo X?

No, any unfair bans will not be counted against you or your application to become a Habbo X.

Who can become a Habbo X?

We are looking for Habbos with lots of experience, so we ask that Habbo X applicants have been visiting the site for at least six months. You’ll need to be the kind of person who enjoys meeting new people every day AND have the patience of a saint, as explaining how Habbo works can be tricky at times. You’ll have to breathe, eat and sleep the Habbo Way and know it by heart! Having a clean Habbo record will also help immensely!



The good news is, there is no age limit for Habbo Xs. We will only choose stellar, friendly, well-respected, exceptional Habbos from within the Habbo US community. The X team will only include a small amount Habbo Xs at a time, so it will be very tough to be accepted

What are Habbo X?

Habbo Xs (short for eXperts) make friends with new players, chat with them, and show them the ropes. Xs will mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Rooms. Habbo Xs will introduce the Habbo Way and help make a new Habbos first visit to the Hotel safe and fun.



Habbo Xs are completely different from Hobbas and Moderators. Habbo Xs meet and greet new users and introduce them to the Hotel, answer questions, show them how things work, etc... They don’t have tools, (other than the badge) and they don’t moderate the Hotel. Think of Habbo Xs as a gracious host and a good-will ambassador - Habbos who are there to take care of our newest Habbo visitors.
