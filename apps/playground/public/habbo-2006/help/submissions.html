Be a Safety Superstar

Habbo is all about making friends and having fun, but to do that you need to know how to keep yourself and your Habbo account super safe. After all, there's no fun in losing all your furni to a scammer or suffering bullying outside Habbo because you've given out your contact details. Of course it's hard to avoid these problems if you don't know some basic safety information.

That's why we're calling on the safety superstars among you to send in your hottest tips. From spotting common scams, to why it's important not to give out email or messenger details, we want to hear how you keep yourself safe on Habbo.
