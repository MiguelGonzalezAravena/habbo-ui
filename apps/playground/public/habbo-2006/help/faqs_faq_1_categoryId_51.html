The Habbo Way

If you are have a question or a problem regarding the Habbo Way then you should read through these Frequently Asked Questions.

What happens if I avoid the bobba filter?

If you avoid the bobba filter you will be banned from Habbo Hotel.

What will happen if I break the Habbo Way?

If you break the Habbo Way you will be alerted, kicked from the room or banned from Habbo Hotel.

What should I do if I see someone break the Habbo Way?

If you see someone break the Habbo Way and you are uncomfortable with it, you should send a 'call for help' and report them to an on duty Hobba and/or Moderator, then leave the room.

Why is there a Habbo Way?

Habbo Hotel is a place where people come to relax, hang out and make new friends in a safe, non-threatening environment. The Habbo Way helps to maintain this safe, non-threatening environment..

Why have I been banned?

 If you can't get into the hotel and you got a pop-up window saying that you were removed for breaking the Habbo Way, you have been banned from the hotel.





Most bans are for two hours only. If your account has been banned from Habbo for more than 7 days and you want to question the ban, then you should email

Player Support using the



Contact Us page.

Am I allowed to use a special program to alter things in the hotel?

No, because these programs change the code that is sent from your computer to the Habbo Hotel servers. This is expressly forbidden in our Terms and Conditions. Any Habbo using such programs will be banned permanently and his/her details may be passed on to the police.





This applies to any changes you make to your Habbo character, to guest rooms and to items of furniture.



Why has my son or daughter been banned from the hotel?

If your son or daughter has been banned from the hotel, it's most likely only for two hours.



However, if he or she cannot get in when this time has elapsed, he or she has most likely broken the hotel's rules in a more serious way and has been banned for longer, maybe even permanently.

Why do my words come up as 'bobba'?

All areas of Habbo Hotel have a word filter on them, which filters out offensive words. This covers swearing and racist or sexist language, plus email addresses and phone numbers.

Offensive words are replaced with 'bobba'. The Bobba Filters also work on Habbo names, missions, Stickies and all types of messages sent through the Console.



What is the Habbo Way?

The Habbo Way is like a code of conduct, a guide to how Habbos should act in the Hotel. Habbos who break the Habbo Way are not welcome in the Hotel and may be banned.
