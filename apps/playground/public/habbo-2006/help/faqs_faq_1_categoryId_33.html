Habbo Club

If you are have a question or problem regarding Habbo Club, then you should read through these Frequently Asked Questions.

If I buy more than one month of Habbo Club will I get more than one gift?

Your gifts will come once a month every month. The day you start you get one gift, then after 31 days another, then after another 31 days another, and so on. You will need to remember when you first joined and work it out from there. If you extend your membership 15 days before it runs out, you won't get your new gift straight away - you'll have to wait for those 15 days to be up first. Don't expect to get a gift immediately, just because you extend your HC membership.

I've seen someone with an HC badge - what does that mean?

It means that they're a member of Habbo Club. To find out more about it, open the Catalogue (go to a guest room to find one) and read the Habbo Club section.

How do I get the special Habbo Club clothes, furni and room layouts?

Look in your Hand - within 3 days after you join the club, you'll have a special green HC sofa in there that only Habbo Club members can get. Every month for the first eleven months, you'll get one item of special HC furni as a member of the club. To get your cool clothes, hair, and hats etc, go to 'Update my Habbo ID' and scroll through the options - you'll see the Habbo Club stuff there. To get the special room layouts, make a new guest room and you'll see the Habbo Club options in the Room-O-Matic.

How do I know how many days I have left in my Habbo Club membership?

Go to Hotel View and look next to the Habbo Club icon - the number of days you have left is shown there. But don't worry - we might send you a reminder console/email before it runs out. But just in case, keep an eye out!

How much does Habbo Club cost?

It costs 25 Coins a month to be a member of Habbo Club.

I want to leave Habbo Club, can I get my Coins back?

Membership is paid on a monthly basis, so we cannot give a refund on days you don't want.

If I extend my Habbo Club membership, will I get another green sofa?

No, you only get that exclusive green sofa once and that is when you join the Habbo Club for the first time within the first month. One green sofa per Habbo. However there are other exclusive items that Habbo Club members get so it is worth staying in the Club.

Can I buy my friend Habbo Club membership as a present?

Sorry, but not at this time.

If I decide not to extend my Habbo Club membership, do I get to keep my exclusive clothes and room layouts?

If you decide to leave Habbo Club, you won't be able to keep your cool clothes, hair styles or colors or your HC badge. You will keep whatever rooms you've already made with the exclusive room layouts and any furni you've got (including the green sofa!).

If I join Habbo Club in the middle of the month, will I just get half a month's membership?

No! You will always get a full month's membership from whatever date you join. There are 31 days in most months of the year so each month, your Habbo Club membership lasts for 31 days (the first day being the day you join). For example, if you join on the 28th December - you membership will last until 27th January. That way you always get value-for-money with Habbo Club.

Why have I not yet received my HC gift?

Have you recently extended your membership? Or bought a block all at once? Just because you have done these things does not mean you will get your gifts straight away. Your gifts will come once a month every month. The day you start you get one gift, then after 31 days another, then after another 31 days another, and so on. You will need to remember when you first joined and work it out from there. If you extend your membership 15 days before it runs out, you won't get your new gift straight away - you'll have to wait for those 15 days to be up first. Don't expect to get a gift immediately, just because you extend your HC membership.

How do I join Habbo Club?

Click on the HC icon next to the Hotel Navigator in the bottom right of your screen.
