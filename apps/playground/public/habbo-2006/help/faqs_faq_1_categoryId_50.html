Safety

If you are have a question or problem regarding safety, then you should read through these Frequently Asked Questions. You should also visit Account Security.

What makes a good password?

A good password contains a mix of numbers, upper and lower case letters. Update your password every few weeks to be safe! Do not use words or numbers like your name, birthdate, or Habbo name!

Is it ok for people to ask me ASL?

We do not ban Habbos for asking ASL as many of them use it as more of a greeting than a question, but it is still not wise to give out personal details. Why not try saying Hello, then ask them what sort of music they like.

What should I do if someone asks for my personal details?

If someone asks for your personal details you should send a 'call for help' and report them to an on duty Hobba, then leave the room.

Where can I find out more about safety?

You can find out more about safety by reading the Safety Tips Section section.

If I type my password will it come out as bobba?

No it won't it will come out as your password and everyone will be able to see it. There are to many different passwords for them all to be included in the bobba filter.



If anyone ever tells you that your password will come out as bobba or ***** you should send a 'call for help' and report them to an on duty Hobba or MOD then leave the room.

What should I do if I think someone knows my password?

You should change it immediately, you can do this by clicking on 'Update my Habbo ID' when you're in logged into Habbo and on Hotel View (NOT in any room) go to page two of your Habbo ID and choose 'change your password'. You have to know the date of birth on your Habbo account to change your password. This is the date of birth you entered when you registered and created your Habbo. If you do not know this, you can't change your password.

NOTE: NEVER change your password to something someone has told you to, they could steal your account, and all your Habbo furni. If someone asks for your password, or asks you to change it, then you should send a 'call for help' and report them to a Hobba.

What should I do if someone asks for my password?

If someone asks for your password you should send a 'call for help' and report them to an on duty MOD, then leave the room.

What should I do if I've been scammed?

You need to go to Contact Us and fill in a scam report. Click on it and fill it out with as much detail as possible so that we can help you.

You'll find examples of the most common scams here.

I've been 'hacked' - what should I do?

If you've been 'scammed' or 'hacked' and can't sign in as your Habbo, please email Contact Us and fill out a Scam Report.

Is it ok for me to give out my personal details?

You should never share your personal details with anyone at Habbo Hotel, Habbos may not be who they say they are.





This is why we have created the Habbo Console - so that you can email without knowing other Habbos details.

If you are caught asking for or giving out your personal details you will be banned from Habbo Hotel.
