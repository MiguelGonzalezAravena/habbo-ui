The HabboX's Speak! [5/23/06]

Jeffoo

Find out when and where you can find a HabboX...

Wondering what the eXperts have to say about our hotel? Well here’s your chance to find out! Once a month, your very own USA Habbo X’s will be asked a question about their eXperience in Habbo Hotel. They love to spread new ideas to as many new (and seasoned!) Habbos out there. You never know just what new and eXciting activities they have, that will be perfect for YOU to try!

Habbo Xs: Do tell! What is the best part of being a Habbo X, and when can new Habbos find YOU for Hotel assistance?

LuckyGuess: Meeting new people! There are a lot of nice habos out there I like Xing at about 7:00 am PST/10:00 am EST because a lot of the people are real calm 

Stream: Helping out brand new Habbos. They are unsure of what to do, and I show them the basic things in the hotel. After we're all done, I feel very pleased with myself. I made a newer Habbo's life somewhat easier. I'm most likely to be found in the Welcome Lounge, "The Library of Alexandria" or the "Habbosphere Pool Deck during the weeks from 1-2pm PST/4-5 PM EST, and around 4-5am PST/7-8 AM EST.

-.-: I like that people look up to you for help and it makes me happy to know I can help them. I come on Habbo from 7:00 to 8:00 am EST, then I am on after school about 4:30 to 11:00 pm EST

Asafar: My fave part of Xing, is helping people.

Fatharry: You get to meet so many more faces that you previously didn't know. It's also great to be recognised as an X and most Habbos are super nice! Also people never stop needing help :P! I am most likely to be helping between 10pm PST/1am EST – 11pm PST/2am EST Then the same times but in am times!

Mouser: when a Habbo finally understands something new they've never known and come even closer to an eXpert stage! I am available for help from 1pm PST/4pm EST -3pm PST/6PM EST most of the time. You can find me in the Welcome Lounge or in the Theatredome, so if you need help, look for me or any of the other awesome X's.

SatoGumu: Throwing Q&A parties in the Welcome Lounge! I’m on at various times but mostly between 4pm PST/7pm EST to 12:00 am PST/3am EST.

Nv: Chit chatting to all the different Habbos from other hotels who stop by, especially the French. I love trying to pick up on all the different languages that are out there. It's always nice to have a foreign Habbo pop by and start a conversation! I am in the welcome lounge from 2pm PST/5pm EST -4pm PST/7pm EST.

YoYoChan: I get to meet so many new Habbos! It's also nice to know that people can rely on me for information about the hotel. I've always loved to help people, especially if they are new at something! Getting to meet all the other experts has been great as well, since they're all so kind and caring! I'll usually be hangin' around the hotel at about 4pm PST/ 7:00pm EST on week nights, and about 1pm PST/4:00pm EST on weekends.

Straw-Kat: Being able to help others more than I could before. I also like the cool badge and having fun meetings. I can be found in the Welcome Lounge about 3pm EST but I go to a lot of public rooms to help out.

Madam: Meeting so many new people and getting to show them all the cool things they can do on the hotel. And also that excitement that they show when you show them something they didn't know before. And how quick they scurry off to check it out...LOL. You can find me Xing in the Welcome Lounge & Pool area or in a Falling Furni room!

Rysav: Watching new habbos learn the game more in 5 minutes than I did in 5 weeks. Im always assisting habbos, but I focus my efforts weekdays between 3pm PST/6pm EST and 9pm PST/12:00 am EST and weekends, between 1am PST/4am EST and 3AM PST/6am EST in the welcome lounge for most of this period.

JudgeJudi: I have the chance to meet dozens of new people every day. To me, making friends is a big part of Habbo and being an X lets me to do this easily. I'm usually on from 8am PST/11am – 2pm PST/5pm EST. I'll be helping in some of my favorite public rooms: Welcome Lounge, the Hallway, and the Main Lobby among others.

Rubbles: I get to help so many people in such a relatively short space of time. It is also great to hang out with so many nice habbos who do the USA Hotel proud. I’m normally in the Welcome Lounge from 10am PST/1pm EST

Sparkles: Being able to meet new habbos, and the feeling you get afterwards when you know you’ve helped someone tremendously. You can usually find me in the Welcome Lounge around 4pm EST

TackyPenguin: The comfort level most habbos feel that they can come to you with their questions, that we are a safe haven. I’m usually on from 5am PST/8am EST – 11:00 am PST/2pm EST. I hang out in the Picnic Room and the trading rooms too.

TriStar: Definitely Definitely has to be helping people who have questions that people can’t answer  If you wanna ask me a Q I’ll be in the Welcome Lounge around 5pm PST/8pm EST.

AzkabanGuy: To be able to help more Habbos than I did before because they know where to come if they have any Habbo related questions or need guidance. I can be found in my home(The Welcome Lounge) at any time.

Devlik: Meeting all kinds of new people whether they be new Habbos or old ones that drop by to say hello. I’m usually wandering around Welcome Lounge around 5 or 6pm EST

JJ-Rulez: Most habbos know to come to me when they are in need of help. Instead of going into the hello lounge and asking 'Does anyone need help?' I can just dance in the centre of the room and I get flooded with questions, the more questions I get asked the more help I can give. You can usually find me 'Xing' within the hotel weekdays around 8am PST/11am EST – 1pm PST/4pm EST. and weekends from 1am PST/4am EST – 10am PST/1pm EST

And there you have it…so much help on Habbo Hotel! So the next time you run in to one of those Badged eXperts, be sure to say hi and start up a convo about anything!
