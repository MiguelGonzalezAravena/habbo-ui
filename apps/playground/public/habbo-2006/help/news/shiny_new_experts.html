Shiny New Habbo eXperts Named [11/3/06]

Becs

Find out if you were accepted...

6,112 Habbo X applications were filled out by 6,112 fabulous Habbo Players. Unfortunately we can't have a team of 6,112 Habbo Xs so we had to choose a handful out of your applications. We're keeping the applications on file this time so no need to reapply in the next round. And take heart, we'll be recruiting Xs fairly regularly. If you didn't make it this time, it's ok - no worries. Just hang in there!

It wasn't easy choosing 30 (that's right, count em...THREE O) out of 6,112 Habbos. Here's what we did to choose:

We looked at the recommendations by current Habbo Xs and Staff members and chose 15 new Habbo eXperts from the recommendation lists. Then, we went through the 6,112 applications and made sure to look at applications from each day of the week that the apps were up. This method allowed everybody a fair chance. Then we looked at the last question (the essay part) and chose candidates based on their essays. Then we looked up each candidate's account history. Then we made our final decision for this round of Xs.

Try not to be disappointed. Get involved, be a good sport, and be patient and you'll likely have a chance to join the team.
