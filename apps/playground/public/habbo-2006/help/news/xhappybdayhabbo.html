Hear what the X's have to say about Habbo USA's 2nd Anniversary...

Wondering what the eXperts have to say about our hotel? Well here’s your chance to find out! Once a month, your very own USA Habbo X’s will be asked a question about their eXperience in Habbo Hotel. They love to spread new ideas to as many new (and seasoned!) Habbos as they can, and you never know what new and eXciting activities they have that will be perfect for YOU to try!
