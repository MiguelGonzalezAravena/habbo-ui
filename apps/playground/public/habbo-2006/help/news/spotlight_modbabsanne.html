Spotlight on… MOD-BabsAnne! [8/29/06]

Jeffoo

This is a feature that will bring some insight in to each Habbo as to just who ARE those MODs, and what do they do?

MOD-BabsAnne, do tell!

1) What is your favorite Habbo look?

I like habbos with different colors, so like, one color for hair, another color shirt, pants, shoes. What I really think though is that girls should have dresses and hairstyles with tiaras. I cannot believe I go through my habbo life without a tiara. :-)

2) What is the nastiest scam you have ever seen?

The worst scam I saw was a Habbo who bought a pet and made it starve on purpose to bring animal lovers into the bare room. The scam was, "Donate furni that I can trade for pet food." After someone donated, he logged off. My suggestion: if you can't afford to feed your pet, pick it up. That way no one sees them suffer, and when you can get them food again, put them down.

3) What's your favorite color and why?

My favorite color is a light, sun yellow. It makes me happy. :-)

4) What's your favorite band/song/genre?

My favorite music is classical music because I was brought up with it. I like Renaissance choral music, Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Brahms, Prokofiev, Tchaikovsky, and all music written for the ballet. My favorite ballet company is the New York City Ballet. I recommend that everyone get a DVD of Suzanne Farrell dancing with Peter Martins. Recommended ballet: Chaconne. And if you're in New York City over Christmas, you must see the Nutcracker!

5) If you could meet anyone who would it be and why?

I'd like to meet Nelson Mandela. He is a hero of such greatness and courage. He survived 27 years in prison and changed the world when he got out. Can you imagine the strength of such a man. I would have liked to have met Pope John Paul, too.

6) What did you do before Habbo?

I got a masters degree at the Interactive Telecommunications Program, and created the first radio - chat community on the internet as my thesis project in 1998. What an adventure that was! However, no experience anyone has can really prepare you for Habbo. Habbo is fast, and has to be experienced and understood, in itself. :-)

7) What's the hardest part of being a MOD?

Knowing that dangerous people are out there on the internet. Knowing that young people will continue to share personal information online that could lead to being located in real life.

I will say this: if you are on, late at night, troubled, and telling your secrets to someone you think is a friend, on any service -- especially IM, watch out, and call for help. It is our highest priority as Moderators to make sure that nobody can or tries to hurt you, make you do something you don’t want to do, or scare you.

8) If you had one thing to tell all Habbos, what would it be?

Self-esteem is your most important defense tool! To get an abuser banned, a Habbo has to call for help during the offensive conversation, so Moderators have a log. Think enough of yourself that you not only deserve help right away, but you deserve to see the hurtful conversation go away. Call for help immediately. Press IGNORE <insert article or song about ignore here>.

After being a Mod on Habbo for two years, I have great faith in this generation. Your creativity with multimedia is changing the way the world thinks about technology. Be proud of yourselves, and keep on keeping on!

All the best, MOD-BabsAnne
