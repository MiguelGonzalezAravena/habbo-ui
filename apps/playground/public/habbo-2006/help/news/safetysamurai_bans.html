Safety Samurai’s Standards to Staying Safe while Surfing -A Habbo Activity guide

Safety Samurai’s Standards to Staying Safe while Surfing -A Habbo Activity guide

Foul language - includes: swearing at a habbo, avoiding the filter, unacceptable rooms or behavior in rooms, unacceptable Habbo names, unacceptable discussion or unacceptable ascii art.

Cyber/Cyber Sex - includes: cybersex, advertising prostitution, soliciting or participating in cybersex or lapdances, strip teases, furni for cybersex, discussion about sexual acts, giving out porn urls, asking for nude pictures, sexual webcam requests, etc.

Harassment - includes: bullying, sexual harassment, multiple rooms created to harass another member, cloning a member name to harass, yelling out loud personal information about another player in a room, creating any Habbo name to harass another player, and the like.

Disruption - includes: attempting to disconnect another player, blocking public spaces, abusing the Call for Help tool, etc.

Hate Speech - includes: racial or religious slurs, gender slander, homophobia, etc.

Illegal Activity - includes: promoting the use or sale of illegal drugs, password phishing, keylogging, using third party software to disrupt the programming of the hotel, discussion about illegal activities, violent role playing, role playing cyber-rape, making death threats, making bomb threats, disclosing or sharing or posting confidential Sulake information on the web or via email or any electronic or printed form, as per your Terms and Conditions agreement, etc.

Personal Identifying Information - includes: asking for, receiving, or giving out ANY personally identifying information of a player, including yourself, or any staff member or family member of theirs or another player or information about any staff member. Examples include: web cam offers or requests; selling, trading or lending Habbo accounts, school names, paypal account names, Instant messenger details (AIM, MSN, SKYPE), email account information, streets, real world locations and/or meeting places, phone numbers, mobile numbers, photos, myspace pages, etc.

Scamming - includes: offering free coins in exchange for furni, or Habbo accounts, or information, or Instant Messenger details, or email addresses and the like. Promoting scamsites via their URL or links. Offering to sell voucher codes in exchange for furni, offering or advertising the purchase of furni, HC or habbo accounts for real money, and the like.

Fraud - includes: Credit Card Fraud, SMS text fraud, landline fraud, any coins fraud.

May this list help make your Habbo experience that much more enjoyable – Sensei Jeffoo
