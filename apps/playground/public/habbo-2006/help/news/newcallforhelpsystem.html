Call for Help Tool: For Emergencies ONLY! [5/12/06]

Becs

Find out all about the new Call for Help System...

Daisy is Hassling Me!!!

Ever wonder where that example phrase in the ‘Call for Help’ box originated? Read through the article and find the answer. You never know when it may end up a Habbo trivia question.

Speaking of trivia, some of our ‘Call for Help’ questions are candidates for the Habbo Trivial Pursuit game that will no doubt end up on game store shelves some day. The Habbo MODs see all kinds of questions in the “Emergency” category that are not emergencies at all. Here are a few examples:

I need a boyfriend. Can you help me find 1 pleez?
or
A Habbo stepped on my foot and it hurts. I need some ice please.
or
I bought coins and they aren’t showing up yet! Where are they! I need them NOW!!!!

Aside from the fact that some Habbos need a few lessons in netiquette and online courtesy (cough), none of the above examples are really and truly emergencies. And sending non-emergency calls keeps the Moderators from helping Habbos with real emergencies.

Starting May 1st, you may be banned for abusing the ‘Call for Help’ (CFH) Emergency system. You better read on to make sure you’re up to snuff with what’s coming! Read on to find out more.

We’re changing things around here, yes indeedy. Consider us the “enforcers” from here on out. No more Mister Nice Guy (or Nice Person, to be politically correct)! That’s right, Habbo players are going to have to read the FAQs for help and follow the rules when using the ‘Call for Help’ tool. :::hears the sounds of distant chanting “no fair, no fair, no fair….” off in the distance:::

Yes fair! And you know why we want Habbos to follow the rules after all this time? Because we said so. How’s that for fair or unfair??? Ha! Puh! And other annoying sounds exclaiming “so there.”

A long time ago, in a land thousands of miles across the ocean, the first Habbo hotel created the ‘Call for Help’ tool. This tool was created so Habbos could get help right away when they needed it. Daisy, the first hotel owner, used her name as an example of what kinds of things to type in the ‘Call for Help’ box. Over the years, her name lives on in posterity within the walls of Habbo hotels. But I digress…

There are ‘emergencies’ and then there are “emergencies.” In our FAQs, we’ve broken it down for you so there should be little confusion as to what’s what. (what IS what, anyway? What is what, that’s what what is.) Better read ‘em while they’re hot! Ouch! Ewwww yeowiee ouchiewouchie ssssizzlin’ yikes! CFH FAQs RTN (read them now!).

How do I call for help in the hotel?
Habbos may call for help in any room by clicking the BLUE QUESTION MARK (graphic of the blue question mark). A shiny, helpful Pop-Up will appear with instructions, showing you exactly how to get help for your question.

Why did I get banned for using the ‘Call for Help?’
We only ban Habbos who abuse the ‘Call for Help’ by choosing the category ‘Emergency’ when the question is not really an emergency at all. There are many ways to get help in Habbo hotel but only one way to get EMERGENCY help. You must have a problem that is a real emergency.

Here are some examples of what we consider REAL EMERGENCIES:

  • Someone saying something that scares you
  • Someone using extremely graphic or bad language
  • Someone using hate speech about you or another Habbo (racism is not tolerated in the hotel)
  • Someone threatening to hurt you offline or in real life
  • Someone threatening to steal your account
  • Someone acting out rape or extreme violence in the Hotel
  • Someone asking you very personal information such as email, pics, webcam, AIM, etc.
  • Witnessing dangerous behavior in the hotel (personal information sharing, arranging meetings offline, or the like)

What am I supposed to do about bothersome Habbos?
All Habbos have the power to block someone’s chat. You HAVE THE POWER! You are a Habbo Jedi! The IGNORE button is easy as pie to use. Just click on the Habbo avatar and then click on the IGNORE button.

You don’t need to report every single thing. Especially things that YOU can control. In fact, reporting every little thing keeps the Moderators from attending the real emergency calls.

Here is what should NOT be reported in the ‘Call for Help.’ You will be banned for 2 hours for choosing the EMERGENCY category for any of the following problems:

  • Flooding (use the IGNORE tool)
  • Casino/Trade Scamming (use the Help Tool to report scams, and don't give your furni away)
  • Coin problems, asking how to become a Habbo X, how to get Habbo Club or how to purchase coins can be answered by the options on the Call for Help Tool. IF they cannot, please use the Help Tool and wait for a Player Support Agent to contact you within 24 -48 hours
  • Technical problems (use the Tech Problems route in the Help Tool or read the FAQs)
  • Personal problems
  • Questions about the game such as “How do I dance?” (please read the Help and Safety section of the website)
  • Asking questions about Habbo staff, quests, or competitions (read the front page of the site please)
  • Asking for free furni

I don’t think it’s fair to be banned for asking for help!
We agree. We won’t ban you for asking for help as long as you follow the proper procedure when asking for help. We only ban Habbos who ignore our procedures about how to ask for help. Remember, it must be a real emergency. Be patient and wait for your answer from the Player Support Agent if you’re instructed to use the Online Help Tool.
