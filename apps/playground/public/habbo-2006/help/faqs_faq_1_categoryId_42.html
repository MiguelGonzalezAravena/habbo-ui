What should I do if my furniture is missing?

If your furniture is missing, it could either be in the walls of your room (placed there by some nasty Habbo) or someone could have gained access to your account and stolen it.

You should first send a 'call for help' and ask for a member of the Moderator Team to come and check your walls, remember to be patient as the MOD team is very busy and it may take some time for them to arrive. If the furniture is not in your walls then you should change the password to your Habbo account and scan your computer for Keyloggers. You can learn how to keep your account safe by reading the Account Security section.



Why don't my teleports work?

When you purchase teleporters, they come as a pair that link to each other. If your teleporters don't link to each other, then you were traded a non-linking set.

NOTE: There is nothing you or the Habbo Staff can do to make two teleporters link. We do not recommend to trading for teleporters due to the large number of non-linking teleports in the Hotel.



How do I interact with furniture?

To sit or lay down: Click on the floor tile underneath the seat or bed you want to sit or lie on.
To get food or drink drink: (from a Mini Bar, Ice Cream Machine, etc) just double click on the item you want to interact with. If you are close enough, then you will recieve the food or drink visibly on your Habbo.



How do I open a present?

To open a present you need to place it on the floor in one of your own rooms. Double click it, and a gift tag will appear. Click on 'open the present' and the contents of the present will be moved to your hand. You can then place the item in your room by dragging it from your open hand.



Why can't I see the new items in the catalog?

Are you using AOL to access Habbo Hotel? If you are, this is probably why you can't see the new stuff. AOL remotely caches website pages and so what you are seeing is not the latest version of Habbo Hotel. To avoid this, use AOL to get a connection to the internet and then use a different browser, such as Netscape or Explorer to load the Hotel.



How do "dice machines" work?

You need to be standing next to the dice machine to operate it - this is so that other players can't mess up dice-based games. To turn a holo-dice on, click on the bottom. To 'spin' the dice, double click on the dome (the top).



Where can I buy stickie notes from?

Stickie notes can be found in the furniture catalog in the 'Accessories' section, and cost three credits for 20.



Where is the furniture that I just purchased?

When you buy an item of furni from the Catalogue it will be placed in your hand. Click the hand shaped icon located on the bottom right side of your Habbo window, and a giant hand containing the furni you bought will appear. You can select the furniture from the giant hand and drag it to the room. Click on the hand to close it.

If you have bought furniture and cannot see it in your hand, it may be due to lag on the site. You should wait until the servers have been rebooted (this occurs at 9:30 pm or 10:30 pm EST depending on the time of year). If your item of furni is still missing then you should use the Contact Us page to contact Player Support who will investigate the missing item/items.



What is the giant hand for?

When you have bought items from the Catalog, we 'hand deliver' them to your room. If you are in a guest room you will be able to see a hand shaped icon, located on the bottom right side of your Habbo window. Click on the hand and a giant hand containing the furniture you bought will appear. You can select the furniture from the giant hand and drag it to the room. Click on the hand to close it.



How many Habbo Rollers can you put in a room?

You can place a maximum of 25 Rollers in your Habbo room. If you try to put more then 25 in a room, an alert will pop up telling you that you have reached the Roller limit.



How do I buy furniture?

To buy furni you need Habbo Coins, you can find out everything you need to know about Habbo Coins by clicking HERE

If you have Coins and want to buy an item from the Catalog, you should first open the Catalog. Click on the item you want and click 'BUY', and then confirm you wish to buy the item by clicking 'OK'. If you have enough coins in your Habbo Purse the item will then be sent to your hand.



What are Habbo Rollers?


Rollers are moving tiles that you can place in your room. You can stand your Habbo on them, or place items of Furniture on top of them, and they will move a Habbo or Furni in the direction the Roller is facing. If you place more than one roller in your room you can create a moving walkway or conveyor belt depending on how you arrange the Rollers.

You can stack most items on top of Rollers, and they will get moved around. Watch out, the movement of the Rollers makes this a little tricky!

To see the Rollers in action you can search Kedo and go to his "Roller Squabble" room.
