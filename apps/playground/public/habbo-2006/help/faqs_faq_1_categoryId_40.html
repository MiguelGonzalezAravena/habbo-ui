How do I get furniture that is not in the catalog?

Furni that isn't in the Catalogue is classed as rare furniture, you can find out more about rare furni by reading the Rares section of the FAQ pages.



How do I buy furniture from the catalog?

To buy furniture in Habbo, you need Habbo Coins. You can find out everything you need to know about credits by clicking HERE

If you already have coins and want to buy an item from the Catalog, you should open the Catalogue, click on the item you want and click 'BUY'. Then you must confirm the purchase by clicking 'OK'. If you have enough coins in your Habbo Purse the item will then appear in your hand.



How do I use the furniture catalog?

While you are logged onto Habbo, go to any guest room and click on the furni Catalog icon (see right), this can be found on the bottom right hand side of your Habbo window.



What is the furniture catalog?

The Catalog displays all the different items of furni available for you to buy in Habbo Hotel. It is divided up into different sections to make it easy to navigate.

You can find out more about furni by reading the Furniture Guide and Rares sections of the website.
