Navigator

If you are have a question or problem regarding the Navigator, then you should read through these Frequently Asked Questions.

Why can I not trade in some rooms?

To give Habbos who do and don't like trading more choice, you can now only trade in some of the categories. This mean if you put your room in the Sporty rooms category, you WILL be able to trade in there but if it's in the Discussion Rooms one, you won't.



If you want to be able to trade in your room, you just have to make sure it's listed under the Trading Floor.


How do I see how many Habbos are in a room?

To see how many Habbos are in a Public room, click on the name of the room and hold down the 'shift' key as you do it. A box should pop up on your screen telling you 'Who's in' that room.



To see how many Habbos are in a Guest room, open the Navigator and click on the name of the room. At the bottom of the Navigator an information window will open that tells you how many Habbos are in that room.


What does the green, yellow and red mean?

The red, yellow and green highlighting the 'Go' button are indications of how popular the room is.



If the room is full, the 'go' button is highlighted in red, if it's nearly full the 'go' button is highlighted in yellow, if there's lots of space but some Habbos inside, it's highlighted in green and if there's no-one in there, it's just grey. It's warning you how busy it's going to be before you get in there!

What does it mean when it says ?Your Room Has No Category, Select One From The List??

It just means you haven't selected a category for it.

You don't have to put your room in a category - but it will help it get popular if you do.



To select one, click 'modify' in the Navigator (in the ?Own rooms? view, click the room you want to modify and then "modify" button) and then pick the category from the drop down list. When you?ve chosen, everybody can find your room in that category as long as you're in the room.

What will happen if I don'?t put my room into one of the categories?

Every time you load the room you will get a notice, a box that pops up to remind you that your room is not in a category but it's not against the law. You don't have to put your room in a category, but it will help it get popular if you do.

How do I put my room into one of the categories?

To choose a category for your room, click 'modify' in the Navigator (in the ?Own rooms? view, click the room you want to modify and then "modify" button) and then pick the category from the drop down list.

When you?ve chosen, everybody can find your room in that category as long as you're in the room. In fact every category view, works in the same way as the good old popular guest rooms list. So the more Habbos in your room, the higher on the list your room will be!

What do the different categories mean?

You can add your room to any of the categories below.   The

other categories are for use by Habbo Staff, they are either recommended rooms

or rooms they are using for competitions.



There's no set rule that says what category you have to put

your room in, but here's a guide for you:





Party and Chill Floor ?Room to party and chill in.



Game/Race/Maze Floor - For the games and races you

create!



Trading Floor - Get your foot into the Habbo Market!



Models Floor Here's where you can get that big break as a star!



Help Centers Floor Want to help other Habbos? Create a Help Center.



Habbo Stars Rooms that are top-notch examples of extraordinary design by other Habbos.

The Popular room list has gone and it has been replaced with a list of room types, how do I see which rooms are popular?

There are now loads of popular lists - not just one! each category has it's own popular list so just click on the one you want to go to. For example, trading rooms - and it'll show you the most popular trading rooms in the hotel at that time! Clever, eh? That means there's more chance for habbos to have a popular room and it's easier to find the type of room you want - so you just have to look in the right category.

When I look at the list I can only see half of the Public Spaces, why is that?

Some of the Public Spaces are now in Categories. If you can't see them all, you either just need to scroll down or you need to click on a category to find them. For example, all the cafes are in the Cafes category. Just click on it and see them.

How do I use the new navigator?

The Navigator is still divided into two sections, Public Spaces and Guest Rooms. The difference is that now there are Categories as well, which separate rooms depending on what they are. For example, all the Lobbies are in the 'Lobbies' category. Just click on it to be taken to them. You can always click the arrows to go back.



With Guest Rooms, you can now add your room to a Category so if it's a maze (for example) it could go under 'sporty rooms' or 'gaming rooms'. Putting your room in a category is good as it is easier to get it on a popular list. There are now lots of different categories and therefore lots of different popular lists! You can change which category it's in at any time.



You can also see which rooms are popular with the new Navigator. If the room is full, the 'go' button is highlighted in red, if it's nearly full the 'go' button is highlighted in yellow, if there's lots of space but some Habbos inside, it's highlighted in green and if there's no-one in there, it's just grey.



The Navigator has lots of other features. Have a look for yourself... You can't break it!
