Scripted items

Anything that's not in the Habbo Catalog or on the rare list to the right is illegal. Such items have never been on sale and are all scripted (illegally produced). Do not trade for these items - they won't work properly, so you'll end up with something worthless. We will not refund the furni you traded.

Current examples are Pods and Stickies in colours that are not in the Catalog, and Chess Boards.

trophies

Certain trophies are worth more especially if they are from Habbo Staff or Moderators. Unfortunately when you trade for a trophy you can't see who it is from until you put it down in your room. Even if a Habbo shows you a staff trophy before you trade, it's easy enough for them to place a cheaper trophy in the trade window.

Safest option: don't trade for rare trophies.
