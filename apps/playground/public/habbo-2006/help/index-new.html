House Rules

Habbo Hotel is a place where people come to relax, hang out and make new friends in a safe, non-threatening environment. Our House Rules, known as the Habbo Way, are very simple. Stick to them and you'll have fun; break them and you'll get yourself banned

The Habbo Way

Here are the most important rules you must follow inside Habbo Hotel. Our Terms and Conditions contains the full list of rules - please read those if you are unsure about how to act. If you break the Habbo Way or the Terms and Conditions, you will be banned from Habbo Hotel.

You must not:

  • Ask for or give out passwords, email addresses or other personal information
  • Swear or use racist or offensive terms
  • Use any programs to hack, script or edit Habbo Hotel in any way
  • Trick other Habbos into giving you their passwords, Habbo Credits or furniture
  • Give away, trade or sell your Habbo account
  • Trade credit voucher codes with other players
  • Describe sexual acts to other Habbos
  • Pester people who don't want to talk to you
  • Type your password anywhere except in the "Check in" box on www.habbo.com
  • Break the law or talk others into breaking it

If you see anyone breaking the rules, please Call for Help so that a Moderator can take action.

You should:

  • Have fun!
  • Hang out with your friends
  • Make new friends
  • Respect other people's opinions and beliefs

Parents Guide

Habbo Hotel is a virtual Hotel, where teenagers can hang out and chat. Each person in the Hotel is represented by a personalized character called a Habbo. It's a bit like a computer game, but all the characters are real people in different places.

Is it safe?

Habbo Hotel is moderated 24 hours a day, every day by professional moderators. The moderators are fully trained and players can ask them for help at any time by clicking on the blue question mark.

Conversations in all parts of Habbo Hotel are logged and can be monitored at any time without warning. Any questionable behavior is reported to the police.

Every conversation in the hotel passes through the Bobba Filter before it appears on the screen. This filters out swearing, racist and sexist terms and other words unsuitable for children. It also filters out email addresses and phone numbers so that they can't be given out. The filter covers Habbo names and missions, as well as room names and descriptions. The filters are updated on a daily basis and contain many hundreds of words and terms.

In addition to these measures we also work hard to educate our members about being safe in the hotel and online in general. Along with our prominent safety information, we produce a weekly newsletter containing articles on safety and display safety messages while Habbos are moving from room to room.

To help us keep your child safe, please go through our Safety Tips with them before you let them use the hotel.

Are my child's personal details safe with Habbo?

We have a strict Privacy Policy and will not share your child's details with anyone, be they fellow Habbos or companies other than Sulake Inc.

Why do you ask for my child's email address?

We ask for email addresses so that we can send password reminders and feedback from our Player Support team should they have any problems on our site. Habbos can also choose whether they want to receive emails from us (such as the hotel newsletters).

Does it cost anything to go to Habbo Hotel?

It is free to register and check into the hotel - there are many Habbos who enjoy the service for free. We only charge for 'extras' like gaming elements (diving in the swimming pools), Habbo Club membership and room furnishings (referred to as 'furni' by most Habbos).

These items are paid for with Habbo Coins, the hotel's currency. Habbo Coins can be bought in a variety of secure ways, such as by mobile phone text message or from a home phone*. All of our payment systems have limits to prevent overspending and we make it very clear that players should ask permission before using them.

*We do NOT operate automatic 'dialers' - the only way a charge can appear on your telephone bill is if someone in your house has picked up your phone and dialed the Habbo Coins Line.

Can my child's Habbo Coins or 'Furni' be stolen?

Not if your child keeps their password secret. Someone else needs to know their password to check into the hotel as them and take their 'furni' or spend their Habbo Credits. Unfortunately, there are always some children who try to trick others into giving out their passwords, or who won't play fair. However, if your child keeps their password secret and uses the safe trading window as instructed, their account and items will remain safe.

If you do not have any anti virus software, your computer could be at risk from keyloggers, which are files sent to you via email or instant messengers. Anti virus software is relatively inexpensive and can be purchased from many high street shops.

Still need help? Contact us via the Habbo Help Tool.

Account Security

Keeping your stuff safe

In real life, would you trust someone you hardly know with your furniture or prized possessions? Probably not, so why do it on Habbo Hotel?

Stay Safe

Your furni cannot be stolen from your room without your help. You have to give someone your password or fall for a scam (trick). Unfortunately bad Habbos come up with new scams all the time - we're listed the ones we know about here. Check out the Account Security homepage for news of the latest scams. To protect your stuff, keep your password secret and only use the safe trading system (see below) to exchange items with other Habbos.

Good Passwords

A good password contains a mix of numbers, upper and lower case letters. All Habbo passwords must contain at least one number.

Safe Trading

The only way to safely trade items with other Habbos is to use the safe trading system. First, go to a room on the trading floor in the Navigator. There you can view a whole list of rooms that have trading enabled.

Next, click on another Habbo and choose 'trade'. Drag furniture from your hand into the trade window to show what you want to offer. Wait to see what they put in the window. When you're happy, click 'accept'.

You cannot trade Habbo accounts, Game Tickets, Pets or Habbo Credits.

Email Scams

Your Email Address

When you register with Habbo Hotel we ask you to include your email address in your Habbo ID. It's really important that you use a valid email address, that only you can access. If you put somebody else's email address, or if somebody else has access to your email accounts, they can find out your Habbo password and steal all your Habbo Credits and furniture.

And it's also important that you put in a real email address when you register or update your Habbo ID. If you make one up, we can't send you password reminders and if the worst happens and someone steals your account, we won't be able to get it back for you.

Change your email address to...

Don't do it! Never change your email address to something someone else tells you to. The person who owns the email address will get your Habbo password and then steal your stuff!

Phishing

Some bad Habbos send emails to people whose email addresses they know, which seem to be 'official' Habbo mail. These emails ask you to reply with your password, sometimes as a 'security check'. Habbo Hotel will never ever request your password in an email. Delete any such emails and let us know about them so we can take action.

Instant Messengers (AIM, MSN, ect.)

The best way to stay in touch with your Habbo friends is via the Habbo Console. That way you can send and receive messages without ever giving out your email address. If you chat with people you don't know outside the Hotel, using an instant messenger you open yourself up to all kinds of problems.

Firstly, it's very easy to find out personal information over IM without you even realizing you are giving it out. There are no Moderators keeping an eye on you either. You are also at risk from keyloggers. Keyloggers are nasty little programs that record every keystroke you make, including your password, and send it to the person who sent you the program.

Keyloggers are very hard to remove. We recommend that you only chat to people you know in real life over IM, and even then never accept file transfers, especially files ending in .exe. Always scan files you receive with antivirus software.

Phone Scams

Some Habbos claim to be able to get you free Habbo Credits via our SMS and Habbo Credit Line payment methods. Don't believe them.

When you buy Credits via SMS, a special code will be sent back to your phone. Then you enter this code on the 'vouchers' window in your Habbo Purse to get your Credits.

If someone offers you a code in return for some of your furni, say no. The code won't work and you will lose your stuff.

Habbo Credits Line

When you call the Habbo Credits Line you have to enter a code. This code is your Habbo ID in numerical form. If you enter a code that is not your own, that person gets the Habbo Credits, but you pay for them.

Furni Scams

Double Your Furni

No one can multiple or clone furni - it's impossible. Habbos trying to steal your furni in this way will say things like: "Put it in the trade window and click accept six times," or "Put it down in my room and I'll double it." Ignore them and then report them to our Moderators using the Call for Help.

Pet Presents

Pets cannot be traded, so some Habbos have been tricking others by saying that they will buy you a present with a pet in it if you give them furni in a trade. They won't - they'll take your stuff and run, or at best buy you a present worth 1 Habbo Credit.

Habbo Pets cannot be placed in other people's rooms either. Once you have purchased a pet, it is your faithful companion forever. Should your pet be old and weary, you can put it out of its misery by placing it in an empty room and deleting the whole room. All other items including wallpaper and flooring will also be deleted. Pets cannot be brought back to life, so only delete them this way if you are really sure.

Decorators

Habbo Hotel has some cowboy builders who offer to decorate your room in return for payment with Furni. But these dodgy decorators just run off with your stuff - it's impossible to place wallpaper, paint or flooring in another Habbo's room.

 Some of these cowboys might promise to buy you the decorating materials as a present. Don't be taken in - once they have your stuff you won't see them for dust.

Paying For Jobs

Would you like a job that pays in Habbo Hotel? Of course you would, then you'd have lots of Habbo Credits or furni. But would you pay to get a job in real life? Not unless you are stupid. So, ignore the Habbo who says:"If you give me some Furni and I'll give you a job that pays.

If you are looking for a job try looking on the popular list for rooms that say **JOBS**. Beware that not all jobs pay when they say they do. It's far better to spend your time doing what you want to do, rather than what someone else wants, especially when they might not even pay you for doing it!

Password Scams

Good Passwords

A good password contains a mix of numbers, upper and lower case letters. All Habbo passwords must contain at least one number.

For You Eyes Only

The only place you should type your password is in the 'check in' box when you come to Habbo Hotel. We will never ask for it anywhere else. If the URL (the address of the website) doesn't start with http://www.habbohotel.com it isn't Habbo Hotel USA.

If someone knows your password then they can log into your Habbo account and do anything they like. They can steal your Furni, delete your rooms and friends, walk round pretending to be you, or even break the Habbo Way and get your account banned.

Never tell anyone your password, not even your best friend. And don't fall for any scams - read this page very carefully indeed...

Tell Me

Rather blatant this one - a Habbo asks you straight out for your password. They might say it's so that they can make you into a Moderator, or perhaps load your account with Habbo Credits, but either way, it's clearly a scam. Just don't do it!

Filtered Passwords

Habbo Hotel has a Bobba Filter, but it is not able to recognize when you are typing your password. If someone tells you to type your password to see if it comes out as 'bobba' or 'xxxxx', they are trying to find out what your password is. Never say your password out loud in Habbo Hotel.

Change your password to...

If someone asks you to change your password to something they choose, they will then log into your account and steal your stuff. They might say things like: "Change your password to money and you will get lots of FREE Credits!" or "I can make you a Moderator, just change your password to badge."

Never change your password when asked by another Habbo.

Game Room Scams

Racing to get rich

There are lots of racing games on Habbo Hotel: races where you get food out of fridges; races where you rush to stand on a doormat; and even 'musical chairs' (without the music though).

These games and races are great fun, but not all the Habbos that run them are honest. Don't be surprised if they don't give you a prize when you win and never 'pay to stay'. We cannot help if race owners take your items or refuse to give you a prize, so it's best just to play for fun :)

Casino Catastrophes

Some casino type games are run by honest Habbos, but some are run by scammers. As in real life casinos you place a bet first and then play to win. In Habbo you trade a piece of furni to the casino owner and then spin the dice to play. The scammers take your item and then kick you out of the room if you win.

The management of Habbo Hotel does not support casino rooms. We are unable to return items, so we advise you not to play.

Scam Websites

Get your freebies here!

There are also sites that offer free Credits, rare's or Furni. These sites work in a more open way, they claim that the person running the site is a Moderator, is the relative of a staff member, or has a program that will give free Credits, Furni or rare's. Just give them your Habbo name and password and they will add them to your account.

When you log into these sites, they harvest your password and then log in as you to steal everything you own. Or at least the good stuff!

The Good and the Bad

Not all websites are bad: lots of Habbos make great sites and fill them with alt's, alterations and lots of other fun stuff. Check out the Official Fan sites to see some great examples.

But some Habbos are greedy and make sites that will try to trick you into giving them your password. If a site asks for your password, or asks you to download something don't. The only place you should ever type your Habbo password is to check into Habbo Hotel US.

Habbo 2.0

Scammers often make sites that look exactly like Habbo Hotel - they even have the 'check-in' screen. But if you look closely you can tell it is different from the real thing. These sites claim to be the new Habbo Hotel or the next version.

Log into these with your Habbo name and password and bingo - they've got your password and now they can steal all your furni and Credits.

Safety Tips

Safety Online

The Internet can be a great place to meet people and form new friendships. But some people you meet online might be pretending to be younger than they really are or they might want to be friends with you for the wrong reasons.

So, to stay safe in Habbo Hotel and on other websites, follow our Safety Tips.

Always...

Tell your parents or guardians what you are doing
By telling them where you are hanging out online - and perhaps even showing them - you will put their minds at rest.

Call for Help if you need to
All responsible websites have an emergency help button – don’t be afraid to use it. In Habbo Hotel, use the Call for Help button (click on the blue question mark) to tell our Moderators about anything that makes you feel uncomfortable.

Never...

Give out your personal details
Never tell anyone your home address, telephone number, email address or mobile phone number. Don’t even tell them your real name or which school you go to.

Meet in real life
It’s not safe, no matter how long you have been friends with them online.

Talk to people who make you feel uncomfortable
If someone says things to you that you don't like or makes you feel uncomfortable, just leave Habbo Hotel or the Websites you are on - one mouse click and you're out of there!

Engage in sexual activity online
You will be banned from Habbo Hotel and other responsible teenage sites for using adult language and pretending to have sex (often referred to as 'cyber sex'). Play safe and don't take the risk of being banned.

Habbo "X"

What is Habbo "X"?

Habbo Xs (short for eXperts) are friendly Habbos here to make your stay at the Hotel an enjoyable one. They make friends with new players, chat with them, and show them the ropes. Xs mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Rooms. Habbo Xs introduce the Habbo Way and help make new Habbos’ first visit to the Hotel safe and fun.

Habbo Xs are different from Hotel Staff and Moderators. They meet and greet new users and introduce them to the Hotel, answer questions, show them how things work, etc... Xs do not have tools, (other than the badge) and they don’t moderate the Hotel. Think of a Habbo X as a gracious host and a good-will ambassador - Habbos who are there to take care of our newest Habbo visitors.

Look For the "X" Badge
Habbo Xs wear a special 'X' badge. It will appear next to them on the right side of your screen. Look for the Habbo X badge when you click on a Habbo. Habbo Xs will always show their badge when asked. Do not fall for impersonators without an "X" badge!

Habbo "X" Criteria

When searching for the next Habbo X to join our team, we are looking for certain qualities. We search for Habbos with lots of experience playing on Habbo Hotel USA. You need to be the kind of person who enjoys meeting new people every day AND have the patience of a saint, as explaining how Habbo works can be tricky at times. Breathe, eat and sleep the Habbo Way and know it by heart! Having a clean Habbo record will also help immensely!


The good news is, there is no age limit for Habbo Xs. You don’t even have to live in the US. We choose stellar, friendly, well-respected, exceptional Habbos from within the Habbo US community. The X team only includes a small amount Habbos, so it is very tough to be accepted and quite the honor when it happens.

Habbo X Privileges
Habbo Xs can enter full rooms and get to wear the X badge. You also get to help people and feel the satisfaction that you can make a difference in the community!

Habbo "X" Applications

It's tough being a Habbo X, but someone's gotta do it! Think you've got what it takes? You'll need to fit this criteria in order to be considered:

  1. Have visited Habbo Hotel USA regularly for at least six months
  2. Have visited the Hotel more than 200 times
  3. Know and live by The Habbo Way
  4. Love to help new Habbos
  5. Be patient and excellent at explaining things
  6. Have a clean Habbo record on Habbo Hotel US
My Habbo X application was rejected! What's up with that?
When you don't meet the Habbo X criteria, the system will reject your application. Sorry about that. If you are rejected, please try again when you meet the criteria.

Unfair bans don't count against you
Yay! Any unfair bans will not be counted against you or your application to become a Habbo X.

Applications are closed at the time, please check back later.

Not Receiving Email?

Not Getting Answers

Are you having difficulty receiving help through the Habbo Help Tool, new passwords and the newsie? Maybe our email isn't getting to you.

It’s very important that email from Habbo gets through to you, so that you can receive help when you need it and get a new password sent to you should you forget your own.

Some email providers block our email or treat it like junk mail. We have solutions for some (see below). If you're not getting email from Habbo and your email provider isn't listed below, one solution we suggest is to sign up for a free email service and use this new email address for your Habbo. There are hundreds of free services to choose from and you can browse a handy list by clicking here.

Once you have your new email address, you can register your account to it by either clicking on the ‘Update my Habbo ID’ link when logged in to the Hotel, or by signing in at the top of the Habbo homepage and clicking on ‘Edit your settings’ and then ‘My email’. Once you’ve done that, you’ll be able to receive responses from the Habbo Help Tool again. It’s as simple as that!

If you use AOL or AIM, follow these directions...

Verify these settings.

From the main “Inbox” page, locate and select the “Settings” tab in the top right of the window. A list of categories will appear in the left column. Please choose the “Spam Controls”, and mark this option at either “Low” or “Off”.

Below that should be a button that says “Control from whom I get email”. Select this, and make sure it has the option “Allow mail from all senders” selected. You will also want to make sure that the option titled “Blocked mail” is set to “delivered to spam folder”. Once all of these are set, please make sure to click the “Save” button.

You may also want to add the following domains to the “Safe List” or “Allowed senders list” and are NOT on the “Blocked senders list”.

@habbo.com

@sulake.com

@habbogroup.com

@habbohotel.com

Once these settings are in place, you should be receiving our emails.

After it's working, remember to verify your email address to make sure you get the latest news and updates! Click here to verify your email address!

If you use Hotmail, follow these directions...

Verify these settings.

If you are using the Hotmail or Live Mail interface, you will need to locate the “Options” button. This can be found in the upper right hand corner of the window. (If you are using a different email client to retrieve your hotmail messages, please log out and access your hotmail account via the website)

Once you are in the options panel, please verify that your settings correspond with the ones listed here –

Junk email filter à Low or Standard

Delete Junk Email à “deliver to folder and delete later”

Also, add the following domains to the “Safe List” or “Allowed senders list”:

@habbo.com

@sulake.com

@habbogroup.com

@habbohotel.com

You may also want to ensure that the domains listed are not on the “Blocked senders list”. Once these settings are in place, you should be receiving our emails.

After it's working, remember to verify your email address to make sure you get the latest news and updates! Click here to verify your email address!
