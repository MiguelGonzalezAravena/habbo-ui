I heard that some Habbos are eXperts, what's up with that?!

Habbo X are Habbo players just like you, who have been around the hotel awhile and gained a special badge for their experience and activities within the hotel.





eXperts help out around the hotel and serve as mentors to new Habbos or Habbos who need a little extra help.





You must have an impeccable record to become a Habbo X. That means you don't break the Habbo Way and are a safe, helpful, caring Habbo.



How do I contact a Moderator?

Easy! Click on the blue question mark, located at the bottom right corner of your screen.





The 'Call for help' is Habbo Hotel's emergency service - please only use it in emergencies. Explain clearly what the problem is when you call for help, and include the Habbo's name that you're calling about if possible.





For general questions about the hotel, feel free to approach a Hobba whenever you see them in the hotel.



How do I become a Habbo X?

You must have an impeccable player record. Habbo X represent the most experienced, safe, honest, respectful and knowledgeable Habbo players, so we expect only the best Habbos to become Habbo X.





You must have been a US Habbo for at least 90 days . If you haven't been in the hotel long, you probably wouldn't be an experienced player. We require all Habbo X to be highly experienced players.





You must have bought some furni and you must own 5 guest rooms. If you have not done either of these, you most likely wouldn't know how all aspects of Habbo function.





You may not receive or keep a badge if you have more than 1 (one) ban.



We have a few more criteria as well but we'll keep those a secret :)





Annoying MODs or Hotel staff by repeated begging and emails will not help you become a Hobba :)





If you meet all of the above, you may apply to be a Hobba and see if you pass. We'll keep everyone updated over the next few months so please read the newsie and check this spot for updates.





The Habbo X application process is not open right now. Please do not use the Call for Help form or CONTACT to ask about becoming a Habbo X. Watch this spot instead ;)













What is a Habbo X?

Habbo X's, or Hotel Guides, are experienced Habbo players just like you who help new Habbos or Habbos in trouble.





Habbo X's (short for eXperts) make friends with new players, chat with them, and show them the ropes. Xs will mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Rooms. Habbo Xs will introduce the Habbo Way and help make a new Habbos first visit to the Hotel safe and fun.





Habbo Xs are completely different from Hobbas and Moderators. Habbo Xs meet and greet new users and introduce them to the Hotel, answer questions, show them how things work, etc... They don’t have tools, (other than the badge) and they don’t moderate the Hotel. Think of Habbo Xs as gracious hosts and good-will ambassadors - Habbos who are there to take care of our newest Habbo players.





Habbo X also make Habbos feel welcome and provide assistance to new Habbos who have questions about the hotel. You can recognize them by their special "X" badge next to their Habbo.





What is a Moderator?

A Moderator is a professional online community moderator. Moderators ("Mods") work hard to make sure that everyone has an enjoyable and safe experience in the hotel.





Moderators have the prefix MOD- (that's an uppercase "o", not a ZERO) , with the hyphen, in their names. Such as MOD-jeffoo.





Any other name is a scam name and not a MOD. You can always tell staff by clicking on them and checking their badges. All staff must ALWAYS wear their badges.
