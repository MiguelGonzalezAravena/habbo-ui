Public Rooms

If you are have a question or a problem regarding Public Rooms then you should read through these Frequently Asked Questions.

Why is the bot not responding to me?

Bots only respond when you speak to them with 'say' if you use 'shout' then they will ask you to repeat what you are saying.



For more information on controlling your Habbo click HERE

Can I get food and drink in a Public Room

Yes some of the bots serve food and drink, but remember they will only respond to 'say' if you 'shout' your order at them they either won't understand, or will be so shocked by your rude actions that they will refuse to serve you.

Does it cost to play games?

All the games in the Cunning Fox Gamehall are free, but if you want to dive or play Wobble Squabble then you will have to buy tickets.

You can use the same tickets for diving or Wobble Squabble, you can obtain tickets by clicking on the Red Boxes on Rooftop Rumble, or by clicking on the ticket machine beside the pool (see picture to the right). Tickets cost 2 Habbo Coins for 5 tickets, if you don't have any credits you can find out how to buy some HERE




Where can I go swimming on Habbo?

You can go swimming in the Habbosphere pool deck or either Rooftop Rumble or Rooftop Revenge, but don't forget to change into your swimming costume or you won't be able to get into the pool!

What are Public Rooms?

Public Rooms are rooms that anyone can use, they belong to the Hotel, not individual Habbos



Each one of the Public Rooms has a theme, for example: restaurant, swimming pool, disco, theatre etc...



You can find the Public Rooms by opening the Navigator and clicking 'Public Rooms'

Why were Piers and Maria rude to me?

The Staff in the Public Rooms: Piers, Maria etc... are all bots, they are not real people.



If you found what they said offensive then we apologize, put it down to our poor sense of humor.

What Public Room games are there?

There are lots of different games, and activities you can join in with in the Public Rooms, they can be found in 3 different rooms:





Cunning Fox Gamehall



* Chess


* Tick Tack Toe


* Battleships


* Poker



Habbo Lido





* Diving



Rooftop Rumble & Rooftop Revenge





* Wobble Squabble 1 & 2



You can find out more about Wobble Squabble by clicking HERE
















Can I work in one of the public spaces?

Sorry, but all of the Hotel's Public Spaces are fully staffed right now. Eric, Maya, Piers and all the other Hotel Staff are bots, not real people and work for free, without lunch breaks or pay!

What are Bots?

Bots are computer generated Habbos that work in the Public Rooms, they serve drinks, food and offer advice.

Bots are not real people, but they have been programmed with responses so they will interact with you when you speak to them.
