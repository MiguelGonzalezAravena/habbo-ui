Xpress your Xpertise!

Xpress your Xpertise! All About HabboX
by Becs

Do you remember what it was like the first time you logged into Habbo? You didn't know where to go or what to do AND you were a 2 inch, pixelated walking target for meanies! Wouldn't it have been nice if someone had been there to explain the basics? And how cool would it have been to have someone help you find some shiny new friends who would have shown you how fun Habbo could be? Dream no more…that's what Habbo Xs are for!

What Are Habbo Xs?
Habbo Xs (short for eXperts) make friends with new players, chat with them, and show them the ropes. Xs will mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Rooms. Habbo Xs will introduce the Habbo Way and help make a new Habbos first visit to the Hotel safe and fun.

Habbo Xs are different from Moderators. Habbo Xs meet and greet new users and introduce them to the Hotel, answer questions, show them how things work, etc. They don’t have tools, and they don’t moderate the Hotel. Think of Habbo Xs as gracious hosts and good-will ambassadors - Habbos who are there to take care of our newest Habbo players. Habbo Xs help with events, games, and safety. They’re eXtra special. Habbo Xs have funkeh badges too! A real Habbo X will always show their badge when asked.

Do You Have What it Takes?
We’re looking for a few good Habbo Xs. Just fill out the application below, if you qualify. Keep in mind that not just any Habbo can become a Habbo X!

We are looking for Habbos with bags of experience, so we ask that Habbo X applicants have been visiting the site for at least six months. You’ll need to be the kind of person who enjoys meeting new people every day AND have the patience of a saint 0:-), as explaining how Habbo works can be tricky at times. You’ll have to breathe, eat and sleep the Habbo Way and know it by heart! Having a good Habbo record will also help immensely - *winks* The good news is, there is no age limit for Habbo Xs. We will choose stellar, friendly, well-respected, eXceptional Habbos from within the Habbo US community. The X team will likely only include 50 Habbo Xs at a time, so it will be very tough to be accepted. It’s not a lifelong commitment. You can be an X for as long or as little a time as suits you. People come and go as Xs and that’s good because it gives others a chance to X as well.

You might think it's unfair that we have selection criteria but we want Habbos who know Habbo like the back of their hands to welcome the new players. We also want friendly Habbos - that's why we'll check to see if you behave. Habbo Xs are guided by the US Community Manager and the Community Team.

Can you commit? You'll need to commit to spending a certain amount of time in the Welcome Lounge and other Public Rooms, each week. You'll need to commit to attending orientation and regular meetings with your team mates and Community Team members. You'll need to be a peacemaker in all situations and not be bothered by bullies or impish behaviour. You'll be setting an example for new players who will look to you when determining what is and what is not acceptable. Are you up for the challenge? Read on!

We know HabboXs will improve the Habbo experience for new players, make them feel welcome and teach them to keep their accounts safe. If you agree and think you have what it takes then we want to hear from you - fill out the application below!
