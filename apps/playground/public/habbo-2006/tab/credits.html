Habbo Coins

Habbo Coins are the currency of Habbo Hotel, they are kept in your Habbo Purse and can used for a wide variety of things. Habbo Coins allow you to decorate your room with Habbo Furni, buy gifts for your friends, join Habbo Club our exclusive VIP club or play games like Wobble Squabble and Battle Ball. Habbo Coins only cost around 20 cents each - that's five for one dollar!

Habbo Coins can be bought in a variety of ways; read about the different purchase methods below. Then, when you've found a method that suits you, click the link.

Habbo Coins Help

If you are having problems buying Habbo Coins then please use the Habbo Help Tool to contact Player Support. Make sure you select the relevant Payments heading from the drop down list.

Habbo Coins are not free. Always ask permission from the bill payer before buying Habbo Coins. You will be permanently banned from the site if you don't have permission, or if the payment is declined/cancelled. Any Habbo Coins that are not paid for will be removed from your Habbo account.
