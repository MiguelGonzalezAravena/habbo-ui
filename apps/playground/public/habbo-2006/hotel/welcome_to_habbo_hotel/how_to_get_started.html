Getting Started

Before you can explore the Hotel, you'll need to 'check in'. The first time you check in to Habbo Hotel, you have to register an account and create your Habbo.

Creating a Habbo account is simple and it will only take a few minutes - just follow the instructions below:

Installing Shockwave
Before you can check in to Habbo Hotel for the first time, you need to download and install Shockwave- it should only take a couple of minutes. If a 'security warning' box appears when you are installing, just click 'yes'.



Checking in for the first time
Once Shockwave is installed you're ready to check in to Habbo Hotel. Click enter Habbo Hotel and a new window will open. When the Hotel loading bar reaches 100%, you'll see the Hotel View page.


Creating your Habbo
The first time you check in to Habbo Hotel, you have to register an account and create your Habbo. A log in window will appear as soon as the page has loaded. Click on 'You can create one here' to open the registration window.


The registration process is simple, just confirm that you're over 11 years old, pick a name for your Habbo, decide whether you want to be a girl or a boy and choose your clothes. When you've done all that... click next!

Now the fun begins! You'll need to choose a name for your Habbo, decide whether you want to be a girl or a boy and tell us what your mission is. Oh - and don't forget to choose your clothes! You can change everything except your name later, so don't take too long deciding.


When you've entered your details click 'Next' and you'll be asked to confirm the details you've entered are correct. Click 'next' is everything is correct, or 'back' if there is anything you want to change.




Congratulations, you are now a Habbo!

Please note: during the registration process we ask for your date of birth and email address, but you don't need to worry. We won't tell anyone your personal details: your email address is needed so that we can contact you should you forget your password, or win a competition.
