The Habbo Console

The Habbo Console lets you keep in touch with your Habbo Friends through instant messages. When you click the Habbo Console icon. your Habbo Console will open. If it's flashing, you have a message or a new Friend Request!

Adding a Friend
If you click the 'Friends' tab in your Habbo Console, you will see a list of people you've added to your console. When you first check in the list will be empty, but you'll soon meet lots of new people you want to add to that list.

To add someone to your Habbo Console, you need to send them a Friend Request. You can send a Friend Request in two ways. You can either click on the Habbo you want to be your friend with while you are in a Habbo room and then click the 'Ask to be friend' button that appears under the character. Or, you can open your Habbo Console and click 'Find', type in the name of the Habbo you want to add to your list and then click find. If the Habbo's found, you'll see an 'Ask to be friend' button, click it to send them a Friend Request.

If they accept your Friend Request their name will appear in your Friends List. You can add up to 100 Habbo friends to your Habbo Console (500 if you join Habbo Club).

Sending a message
When you've added someone to your Friend List you can send them a Console Message even when they are offline. To send a message; open your Habbo Console and click 'Friends', then click to select the name of the Habbo you want to send the message to. (Remember: you can send the message to more than one Habbo, just click and select everyone you want to receive it).

Click on 'Compose a message' enter your message and click send. If you change your mind and decide not to send the message, you can click 'Cancel'.
