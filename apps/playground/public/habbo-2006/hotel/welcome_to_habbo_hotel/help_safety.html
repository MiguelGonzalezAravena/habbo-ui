Calls For Help

If you are worried about something - maybe you've just seen someone break the Habbo Way - you can ‘Call For Help’, by clicking on the blue question mark in the bottom right corner of your screen when you're logged into Habbo.

A Moderator will then either send you a message or come and see you.

The 'Call for Help' is Habbo Hotel's '911' service - please only use it in emergencies.

Help and Safety

If you have a question about Hotel Hotel, or need some advice about staying safe, then you're in the right place.

Check out all the different places you can go for help:

The FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

If you have a question about Hotel Hotel or need some advice about staying safe, the best thing to do is check out the FAQs. The FAQs are jam packed full of helpful information and sorted into sections for easy navigation.

Habbo X

Habbo Xs (short for eXperts) meet and greet new users and introduce them to the Hotel, answer questions and show them how things work. Xs will mainly hang about in the Welcome Lounge, but occasionally you will see them in other Public Spaces. Think of Habbo Xs as gracious hosts and goodwill ambassadors - Habbos who are there to take care of our newest Habbo visitors.

Habbo X have a special 'X' badge and they will always show it when asked. Don't fall for impersonators, always ask to see their badge.

Account Security

We do everything we can to make sure that Habbo Hotel is a safe place to hang out, but you have to watch out for Habbos who are out to trick you. These bad Habbos will try to steal your Furni, account or Credits, either by tricking you into handing them or your password over.

The more time you spend on Habbo, the more knowledge you will gain about safety related issues. But, new tricks are thought of all the time, so make sure you check out the Account Security pages to keep one step ahead.

Contact Us Via Email

You can use the Habbo Help Tool to send an email to Habbo Hotel, but before you use the tool, please take a look at the FAQs to see if your question is answered there.
