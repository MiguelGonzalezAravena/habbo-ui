Your Own Room

Want your own room, but not sure how? Read on!

Creating your own Habbo Room
Guest rooms are free and you can get one by visiting one of the Room-O-Matic kiosks. You can find these in the Hotel Navigator or in the Lobbies and Cafés (they look like yellow arcade games). You get to choose the name, description and shape of your room, and you can decide whether to lock it or let anyone come in:

Open - anyone can enter.
Doorbell - a pop-up appears when you are in that room asking if the person trying to load the room is allowed in.
Password - only people that know the password to your room can enter.

When you click 'Done' in the Room-O-Matic window, you can click the 'Go to your room' link and head straight to your new room.

Decorating and furnishing your room

You can find everything you need to customize your new Habbo room in the Hotel Furni Catalogue. There's a huge selection of items, from humble doormats, plants and bathroom suites, to digital TVs, teleports and fully-stocked fridges. You can read more about Furni here.

Before you can buy Furni you'll need to buy some Habbo Credits.

To open the Catalogue click on the Catalogue icon (see right). The Catalogue is divided into lots of different section: Habbo Club, Spaces, Presents, Rollers, etc… and you should have a good look at all the sections before you make a purchase. Once you've bought something, you can't send it back!

If you have Habbo Credits and want to buy an item from the Catalogue you should click on the item you want and click 'buy'. A pop-up will appear asking you to confirm you wish to buy the item; click 'OK' to confirm. If you have enough Habbo Credits in your Habbo Purse the item will then appear in your hand.

Room rights
Room Rights allow another Habbo to move Furni in your room, open hatches, kick other Habbos and delete what it says on your stickies. You should only give rights to Habbos you trust. Nasty Habbos might move your Furni about, delete the writing on your Stickies and kick all your friends, when you don't want them to. So, be careful who you trust!

Giving room rights
Only the room owner can give out room rights. When your Habbo character is in one of your own rooms, you can click on another Habbo in the room and then on the 'give rights' button. If you click that button the Habbo will have rights in your room, they will then be able to move Furni, open hatches, kick other users and delete writing on Stickies.

Removing room rights
You can remove rights in two different ways:

1) If you are in one of your own rooms you can click on any Habbo that has rights and under their name on the bottom right hand side of the screen you will see the 'remove rights' button, if you click on this it will remove their room rights.
2) To remove rights from everyone in the room you need to go to Hotel Navigator and under 'Guest Rooms' select 'Own Rooms'. Click on the name of the room you want to remove everyone's rights from and then at the bottom of the Navigator. Click on modify. At the bottom of that page, select reset and follow the instructions from there.
