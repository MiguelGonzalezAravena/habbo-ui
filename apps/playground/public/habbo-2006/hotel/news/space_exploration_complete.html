Space Exploration Complete [11/27/06]

Luna

What a long, strange trip it’s been…

We were blinded by the stars and flickering of hundreds of television sets on OmniTV. We got in touch with our inner robots on Megamecha. We witnessed Universum’s meltdown (and the actual meltdown of the Candy Furni line) while visiting the Universum Federation. After many successful competitions and planetary trials, our space exploration is complete!

Donnie Santini has also left the building, but he didn’t leave empty-handed! He came seeking help with the mysterious Black Monolith’s message and thanks to YOUR clever detective skills, he is leaving with this knowledge. As it turns out, the Black Monolith left secret clues spelled out in Digital TVs on OmniTV, Morse Code on Megamecha and in photographs on the walls of Universum Federation. After you guys put the clues together and submitted your findings, Santini learned that this whole time, the poor Black Monolith was just trying to express his loneliness. His message? ME WANT FRIENDS.

Donnie Santini sends his thanks to the entire Habbo community for helping him in his space voyages. He also told me to personally thank the following three Habbos for submitting the Black Monolith’s message before anyone else: YodaofDarkness, Miah1991 and Sabotage.

The Black Monolith's message awoke a tiny bit of tenderness in Santini. He usually travels alone, but he’s bringing the Black Monolith with him on his next explorative journey. He doesn’t leave until tomorrow, so we’re selling Black Monolith TODAY! Be a good friend to yours…apparently this Furni is very sensitive.
