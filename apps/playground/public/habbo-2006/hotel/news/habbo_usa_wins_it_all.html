Habbo USA Wins it All! [7/7/06]

Kedo, Hotel Manager

We've scored another goal and the trophy is ours!

Habbo USA has scored our third goal... and it's the winning one! It was a tough competition but we prevailed and won it all!

Here is a quick run down of the final goals scored in the Habbo World Championships...

9. Forward "Best Soccer Game Designer" - Spain - ::Spain scores one goal for his namesake Habbo Country.
10. Forward "Habbo Soccer Hot or Not Competition" - Australia - Mattiiib0i scores a goal for being the hottest Habbo.
11. Winger "Best Creative Stacker" - USA - Seith scores the 3rd and final goal and wins it for Habbo US! Check out his room here.

Thanks to the late goal by Seith, we've won the Habbo World Championship! GO HABBO USA! The final tally is: USA 3; Spain, Australia 2; Norway, Germany, UK, Netherlands 1; the rest of the Habbo World 0! Way to go Habbos!

And now, since we won the Habbo World Championship, we can bask in the glory of winning, AND the prizes that come along with it! And for those of you who forgot... Here's a refresher on what Habbo USA gets for winning:
  • The friends list maximum for non-Habbo Club members is bumped up from 100 friends to 200 friends FOR A WHOLE YEAR!
  • The friends list maximum for Habbo Club members is bumped up from 500 to a MASSIVE 600 friends as well!
  • All Exclusive Habbo Club rooms are now open to all Habbos for ONE MONTH!
Enjoy it Habbos, you've earned it! It was a great Championship! It's a great time to celebrate since it's TGIF too! Get in the Hotel, it's time for a BIG CELEBRATION!


The Habbo World Championship Trophy is ours!
