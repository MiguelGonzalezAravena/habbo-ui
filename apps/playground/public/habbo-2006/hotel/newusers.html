Welcome to Habbo Hotel!

Habbo is a FREE virtual community for anyone aged 13 years or older where you can hang out with your friends, make new ones, create and design your own personal room, and take part in many activities, such as meeting and hanging out with famous celebs, or even make your own! There are a bunch of games to play and even more contests to take part in!

It's free to play, and once you've registered your Habbo, you can do almost whatever you can think of! So what are you waiting for? Follow the simple directions on the left and get playing, or click here to get started in Habbo!
