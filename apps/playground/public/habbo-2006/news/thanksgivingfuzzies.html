Warm Fuzzies for Turkey Day [11/23/06]

Luna

Find out what Habbos are thankful for this year...

Man, we’ve got A LOT of thankful Habbos in the US hotel! I read through all 1,200 entries and ended up selecting 25 instead of 20. Some are funny, some serious, but none of them are uber cheesy! I really wanted to publish ALL of the entries we received, but iomegadrive didn’t trust me with that many Turkeys…he thought I’d stash some away for post-Thanksgiving turkey cravings. Oh well, sorry guys!

Anyways, if you don’t see your entry published below, I want you to know that your words were read and appreciated. I hope the words below fill you with warmth (and laughter in some cases) and that everyone has a happy, safe and delicious Thanksgiving!
