DJ-Bling is Back [12/5/06]

Luna

And he’s decking the halls with pure decadence…

We were all kind of surprised when Rasta.Claus came back to the hotel claiming to be Winter Boss even though he’d lost last year’s election by a landslide. We thought maybe DJ-Bling had forfeited the title so he could spend this December club-hopping in Miami. Rasta.Claus came bearing 25 days of gifts, so we kinda just let his defeat at the polls slide…

But now DJ-Bling is back to reclaim his winter crown, and to put the “holla” back in holidays! He’s only here for one day and he’s already put the Winter Holiday Furniback in the catalog and opened a holiday exclusive floor in the hotel (Candy Cane Lane)…he’s even lined up an entire month-long schedule of events just to be sure that all Habbos know that the Holidays belong to DJ-Bling. Read more about his triumphant return here.

The Winter Holiday Calendar of Events kicks off today with Two For Tuesday on Bobba Music. DJ-Bling and Rasta.Claus not only have different opinions about what the holiday season means, they also have extremely different musical tastes. They’ve each picked out a song for Whimsy and I to play on bobba music and they want you to vote for the song you like better. Listen in at 4pm PST/7pm PST, and then head back to the homepage to vote for your favorite song out of the two. We’ll randomly select five Two For Tuesday voters to win some Winter Holiday Furni – compliments of DJ-Bling, of course.

Happy Holidays, Habbos!
