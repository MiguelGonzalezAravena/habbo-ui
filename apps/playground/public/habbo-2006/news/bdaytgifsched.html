Habbo USA Turns Two on Friday! [9/1/06]

Luna

We’re celebrating with a SUPER-SIZED TGIF…

This Friday, September 1, Habbo USA turns 2! To celebrate, we are throwing a massive, all-day TGIF PARTAY full of games, prizes, food and fun!!! Your favorite staff members will all be there, running amuck throughout the hotel from 10am-8pm PST. The full schedule of events is below. Print it, study it, memorize it and whatever you do, live it!

10am
Habbo Trivia with Kedo
Come to the Theaterdome to test your trivia skills with Kedo!

11am
Birthday Maze/Race
Reckon you’re good maze runner? Come test your skills at Brown-Pants maze!!

12pm
Restaurant Floor Party
Get your lunch and head to the Restaurant Floor and party at the same time with Habbo Staff. We’ll be in EVERY ROOM on that floor, so get there and have some fun!
MYSTERY GIVEAWAY with ScribeX
Pick a Pressie! Any pressie! It could be cabbage or an HC couch! Who knows? Come try your luck and celebrate Habbo.com's second year with us! Search ScribeX, the room is called Mystery Giveaway. See you there!

1pm
Habbosphere Pool Party
Hey, we’re flying in the face of convention and swimming RIGHT AFTER we eat. Since, you know. It’s a POOL PARTY! Head to the Habbosphere Pool Deck and splash around with Habbo Staff. There might even be a diving competition!

2pm
Bobba Music – Studio Takeover!
JackietheDJ is out … and we’re taking over his Studio! Listen in as we take control and spin the tunes for an hour!
Party in the Batcave with Jeffoo
Jeffoo is also holding her usual TGIF party in the Batcave but with a twist! It’s the end of summer and she’s holding trivia to give away all her summer furni!

3pm
Manic Fallin' Furni with iomegadrive; Open Battle Ball with Luna
Oh come on, you know what this is. Get to iomegadrive’s Manic Fallin’ Furni for some games or head over to the Battle Ball Open floor and bounce around with Luna.

4pm (aka the Hour of Ducks)
Staff Pick of the Week Party
Special Staff Pick party this week! Keep an eye out for the alerts to find out where it’s going down.
MOD-Cleo has gone Quackers!
Join her for the Second Annual Great Duck Giveaway 4pm PST! Search Bageone!

5pm
Wobble Squabble with Staff in Rooftop Rumble and Rooftop Revenge
Come kick staff off their high horses in Wobble Squabble! It’s your only chance to slap Habbo Staff and get away with it.

 

6pm
Habbo USA Birthday Surprise!
We’re opening our present right now, so stay tuned to see what it turns out to be!

7pm
TGIF Radio Show with Jeffoo
It’s a special Birthday sized TGIF Radio Show with Jeffoo! There are so many surprise guests - you never know who will show up! Listen in.

 

Don’t want the party to end? It doesn’t have to! Plan a birthday party for Habbo this weekend, submit the details here, and we’ll publish it on Friday in the Habbo Classified section! Yay Birthdays!!!
