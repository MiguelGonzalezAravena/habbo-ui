Lady Sovereign Music Video [10/16/06]

Luna

Prove you’re a star and win free Furni…

Thanks to everyone who came out and participated in the Lady Sovereign “Gatheration” music video shoot last week. We got a lot of great footage, and the following Habbos were selected to win a small prize for their hard work and obvious dedication to the video shoot:

!.:Superbonnie:.!, ?PartyStarter?, Nuckels?, Shitzulover12, Brradin, miranda01 and catlovermarie

Don’t be upset if you don’t see your name up there, because I’m giving you a chance to win an even BIGGER Furni prize pack! Even though you guys definitely came out and represented for Lady Sov, I don’t think we've found our video STARS just yet...

This Wednesday from 4pm PST/7pm EST to 5pm PST/8pm PST, I’ll be patrolling the Lady Sovereign Public Warning Guest Room floor looking for the best performers out there, with the coolest dance routines and matching, hip hop-inspired uniforms. To win, your routine must be well-choreographed, consist of three or more performers and last but certainly not least, it must last a full minute! Remember, I've pretty much seen it all when it comes to Habbo dance routines, so put lots of effort into making yours unique and incredible.

If you want to participate in the Lady Sovereign “Gatheration” challenge, don’t forget to password lock your room and put it on the Lady Sovereign Public Warning Guest Room floor. Please also include “Gatheration Stars” in your room description if you think your team has what it takes to win.

Better start planning those dance routines if you want to be a video STAR!
