Setting the Furni World on Fire! [2/23/07]

RocketSkates, burning up

Red Mode and Red Iced erupt into the Catalogue!

A bold new era is almost here, once we finally depose Commander Kedo and end his reign of terror. Fire will rain down from the heavens, the seas will boil over, and mountains shall burst forth will liquid hot magma in anticipation of Kedo’s judgment day. Fire and redness shall be the symbols of our strength on that day of reckoning, and Kedo shall know my vengeance when I lay my hands upon him and he burns at my touch. Habbo will cleansed with the burning of the red…

Which is why, now, in anticipation of Kedo’s final day as the undisputed master of the Hotel, I am releasing both the Red Mode and Red Iced Furni lines so that you can show your support and ready yourself for Kedo Armageddon. These new lines look smoking hot, like they would scorch your carpet, yet they’re cool to the touch. Much like my rage towards Kedo burns with a white-hot intensity, and yet my moves against him have been absolutely cold and calculated. The dual nature of the Red Mode and Iced lines inspired me to build both my Street Fighter room and my Dhalsim room (or you can click here or click here to view the full lines), so enter them if you dare and swear your allegiance to the destruction of Kedo and the ascendancy of RocketSkates to the volcanic heights I am destined to achieve!


Street Fighter
Dhalsim

But much like lava must cool and form igneous rock, these lines will only burn with such intensity for so long before they need to be removed once again from the Catalogue. And by the way, I’ve stripped both Candy and Blue Area out of the Catalogue as well, since those were always Kedo’s favorites. We must take from him all his power before the glorious immolation of Red Mode and Red Iced can occur…
