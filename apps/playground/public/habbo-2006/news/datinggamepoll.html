You Choose the Daters [10/10/06]

Luna

The polls are open and it's up to you to pick Luna and Kedo's dates...

The Habbo Dating Game has come to an end, but we still haven't picked the lucky Habbos who will escort Kedo and Luna on their dates! We know not everyone could come to the show, so we have provided the questions that were asked (question authors will be prized), as well as the contestants' answers so that you guys can help us determine who should win. Read the answers carefully and then vote below for the best match for Kedo and Luna! Winners will be announced in the hotel tomorrow at 5pm PST so be sure to have your vote in before then!
