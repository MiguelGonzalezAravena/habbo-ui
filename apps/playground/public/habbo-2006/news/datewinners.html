The Dating Results are In! [10/11/06]

Habbo Staff

Your votes have been counted and Luna and Kedo's dates have been selected...


The results are in and man, was it a close one:

39.5% of you thought Wretched.Witch was the perfect pick for a date with Kedo,
32.4% of you favored rubygloom821 as his date
and 28.1% of you wanted Porshas to win the rendezvous with Kedo.

For Luna, 39.6% of voting Habbos felt g00dni9ht fit the bill,
35.5% believed FunkyJunky006 had what it takes to date Luna
and 25% were rooting for -Kaige to win.

You all did an awesome job, but unfortunately there can only be two winners: Wretched.Witch and g00dni9ht! At 2:30pm PST/5:30pm EST on Friday, October 13, Wretched.Witch and g00dni9ht will be given VIP badges so they can meet with Luna and Kedo privately in their Habbo restaurants of choice: Kedo and Wretched.Witch will meet at Palazzo Pizza and Luna and g00dni9ht will meet at Polarity. Then, it's off to the TGIF festivities starting with Battleball for Luna and date and Manic Falling Furni for Kedo and date. They will all move on to the Staff Pick of the Week party and finish off the date at Rooftop Rumble. In addition to the date, here's what else they've won: a month of HC, a Heart badge, 10 ticket bundles,a camera with film, a trophy inscribed by their staff date and a Rare that Kedo/Luna will personally pick out for them.

As for the other four contestants, we've got something for you too! iomegadrive's Manic Falling Furni room will be locked all day on Friday unitl 3pm PST, and the four of you will be given the password 30 minutes before it opens to the public! So essentially, you've all won a date with io! Plus, for winning the Love Tests, you'll also receive 50 Habbo coins and a pressie from Kedo or Luna.

Thanks for playing the Habbo Dating Game! *Blows Big Dating Game kiss*
