Carnival X Survey - Your Answers! [8/24/06]

Jeffoo

Thanks to all Habbos who participated...

Well, we asked for it, and you DELIVERED! Below are the results from our Carnival X survey. Many of your comments have helped us understand what you like about the Carnival, and also, what we need to do to make it even better! Read on for the results:

Were you able to find out times and days for each Carnival game easily?
*No, it’s confusing: 26%
*It was alright, but I would like to see the schedule at more locations, such as the homepage: 42.6%
*I had no trouble finding the game schedule: 31.4%

Were the games easy to understand?
*Yes, all the game rules were explained very clearly: 43.9%
*Most games were easy to follow: 47.2%
*I didn't understand any of the games: 8.9%

Would you be interested in attending a tutorial for about 30 minutes before the game starts to learn how to play, get tips and tricks?
*Yes, I would very much learn from a tutorial: 45.3%
*No, I’m fine just watching the habbos before me and reading the rules on the game walls: 54.7%

Were the game hosts nice, friendly and helpful?
*The hosts were charming, and very friendly: 48.6%
*The hosts were alright, but they were too busy to really help me out: 33.9%
*The hosts need to pay more attention to the habbos: 17.5%

What do you like most about Carnival X?
*The free prizes: 21.6%
*Learning new games on Habbo: 13%
*Gives me something to do (entertainment): 20.1%
*Meeting/talking to Habbo eXperts: 15.3%
*Playing fun games for free: 16.3%
*Meeting new friends inside the carnival: 13.7%

If you do not visit Carnival X, which of these would encourage you to give it a try?
*New games: 35.4%
*Better prizes: 53.9%
*Better Explanation of each game: 7%
*I don’t like Habbo games: 3.6%

The X team USA and I want to thank each of you for participating in this survey. As you can see, we learned tons and plan to use your ideas as soon as the next Carnie (coming September 8, 9, and 10), so look out!

Jeffoo and the Habbo X Team USA
