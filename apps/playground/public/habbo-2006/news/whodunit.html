A Habbo Whodunit [9/25/06]

Luna

You’ll never believe who it was…

I asked and you guys answered. 14,801 of you to be exact. Turns out 34% of you thought Jeffoo took Kedo, 26% blamed iomegadrive and hiro-p and brown-pants were tied at only 20%. I appreciate such a huge amount of feedback, but boy, were you guys wrong…

I returned to the crime scene this morning before checking into the hotel for the day. I just couldn’t bring myself to accuse the “Safety Samurai” of such a huge crime without having any additional evidence…I mean, there are plenty of red-haired Habbos in the hotel! I searched and scoured, looking for anything I may have missed the first time around. I had practically given up, when I spotted a piece of red and blue fabric lodged underneath some bushes that had just been trimmed. I yanked and yanked until the material gave and I fell backwards on my pixilated bum. I looked down and saw a Union Jack in my hands and the wheels in my mind began spinning like that teacup ride at Disneyland…who's British and kinda shady? brown-pants of course!!!!! I had finally found my culprit.

I gathered up the flag and brown threads in my messenger bag and stormed into the hotel, heading straight for brown-pants Le Resistance room…LE RESISTANCE! It was all coming together now! When I finally got to his room he wasn’t there, but I knew he wasn’t far…I checked all his other rooms and finally found him in his TV Stack room. He was in his getaway plane, ready to make an escape, and poor Kedo was in the corner of the room, imprisoned behind a wall of Yellow Laser Gates. Brown-pants took one look at my face, turned off the jet engines and just sat there sulking…he knew he had been defeated.

He let Kedo out of his prison and promised never to pull a stunt like this again. Before I left, I told him he had to set the record straight and come clean to you Habbos. Here is the letter he wrote:

Since joining the Habbo Hotel staff in July all I wanted to do was make friends and be a popular Habbo staff but every time I go into the hotel all I hear is Kedo, Kedo, Kedo. What about Brown-Pants! So I kidnapped him. I thought if he was out of the picture I could become hotel manager and use Kedo's key to the rares to buy my popularity.

Kedo has been locked away in my lair keeping my two guard dogs "Offense" and "Defense" company. He became my new pet and I called him "Dimples".

I would have gotten away with it to if it weren't for you pesky Habbos. But all that is over now. Until next time... but for now I make my escape!

Viva La Resistance
- Brown-Pants

Well, there you have it. The crime has been solved and Kedo’s back safe and sound. As for brown-pants…well, I forgive him. Turns out I’m a sucker for a British accent...
