Valentine's Winners [2/15/07]

Luna

The results are in...

It's a wrap! Cupid's Garden, Habbo's first ever reality TV show is now over! We hope you all had a great time tuning in and voting for your favorite couples. The winning couple, according to your votes was...Tasha and Binxers! They each took home 250 Coins and the satisfaction of knowing that over 1,000 voting Habbos out there think that they are the sweetest couple in Habbo.

In case you didn't make it to the Valentine's Ball yesterday, here are all of the other Valentine's competition winners:
