Habbo World Championship News [7/7/06]

Kedo

Breaking News from the International Games!

Have you been keeping up with the local Dream Team? Well of course you have! But even as our last members are chosen, our first members are going directly into competition against the Habbo worlds' very best. Our team is definitely strong and experienced, but can we hold up against such countries such as Australia or Singapore?

For our quick breaking news update, we bring you the very first goals scored Habbo internationally.

  1. Safety Goalkeeper - Norway (.-=ALEX=-.) scores 1 goal.
  2. Wingback "Best Patriotic Room" - USA (TEENV0GUE) scores 1 goal for the home team with her USA Stadium!
  3. Wingback "Best Photographer" - Australia (Amaranth) scores 1 point from down under.
  4. Central Defense "Best Habbo Webmaster" - Germany (http://www.habbotimes.de/) edges out our own BlueBobble.com for top Habbo website.
  5. Central Defense "Best Stadium Builder" - UK (Sensualized) scores 1 for England
  6. Midfielder "Top Battle Baller" - The Netherlands (rudymaccudy) barely beating the US, scores 1 goal.
  7. Winger "Best Alt Artist" - USA (HankMcCoy) destroys all opposition with his rendition of the Justice League (check it out in the gallery here)
  8. Midfielder "Soccer Room Re-creation" - Spain scores a goal of their own, to bring themselves up to winning level.

That brings USA to the lead with 2 goals!. Countries that are at a very close 1 goal are Habbo Norway, Germany, The Netherlands, Australia, UK, and Spain.

That brings you up-to-date with the latest standing in the Habbo Soccer World Championships. Keep your eyes peeled for an upcoming in-depth review of the winners, and their winning entries.

This is Kedo, signing off. Great job everyone who has participated so far :)
