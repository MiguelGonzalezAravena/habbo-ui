"Crowded" Music Video - Take 2 [8/30/06]

Luna

Yesterday's shoot was awesome...let's make today's even better!

Wuz good, Habbos? Yesterday between 3-5pm PST, Jeannie's Hip-Pop Hangout floor was bumpin' with the "Crowded" spirit!! I got lots of good footage to include in the music video we're putting together for Jeannie Ortega's awesome single, BUT I think we can do better! We've got to blow the lid off this song and prove that us Habbos are music-video making machines!! Who's with me?

Today, I'm only going to record Habbos that have put together a choreographed dance routine, with matching outfits and awesome dance moves. Or, if you are more actor than dancer, you can get a couple of your friends together and act out what Jeannie is singing about in the song (make sure you have the lyrics handy too so you can sing along)!

If you have a dance routine, please put a [D] in front of your room name, and if you are acting out and singing the lyrics, please put an [A] in front of your room name. All rooms must be on Jeannie's Hip-Pop Hangout floor and must be LOCKED so that no one can interupt us while shooting...I learned yesterday that a closed set is the only way to go!

Ok, better get started on planning those dancing/acting routines Habbos!! I'll be filming from 3-5pm PST today, and I'm serious about getting some GREAT footage this time! Click here to read more about the music video and to download "Crowded" for free!

Let's really put some effort into this music video and make Jeannie proud!
Yours truly,
Luna
