It's an Ice Age in Habbo! [12/4/06]

Kedo

Play and you could win some cool Habbo rooms...

Come dive straight into the fun with your favorite Ice Age 2 characters, and win some Habbo furniture along the way! You will get to see each of these rooms as you try to finish the Ice Age 2 Quest, which will be given to Habbo's at random who answer all the questions correctly?

Are you ready to start? You will need to answer a total of 4 questions for this competition. After you collect all 4 clues and find out the answers, just come to this page and enter all of your information!
