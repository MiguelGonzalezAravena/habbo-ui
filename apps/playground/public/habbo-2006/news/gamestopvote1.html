Vote Before Polls Close! [11/7/06]

Luna

Choose your favorite game out of the ten finalists...

Looks like there are lots of games out there to keep you Habbos busy! After reading through 400 GameStop “Invent a Game” entries (!!!), the list of Game Inventors and their respective games have been narrowed down to ten.

Now, we need all of you to vote on your favorite game out of the bunch…the one you could see yourself playing for hours on end. Read the descriptions below, look at the game set-ups (by clicking the image preview or the game name) and then vote for your favorite at the bottom of the page. The first round of voting ends on November 8 at 11:59pm PST. Now, on with the games...

Habbo Bowling
By: Amymome

How to play:

You get ten rolls Use this list to figure out how many points you add to the scoreboard!
1 - GUTTER BALL (0 points)
2 - Two pins
3 - Four pins
4 - Six pins
5 - Eight pins
6 - STRIKE - 10 pts.
Whoever has the highest score at the end of ten rolls wins.

Habbo Troop Patrol
By: MorrowTwins

How To Play:

There are 2 teams one of the left side & one on the right side.The Object of the Game, is to protect Your side (make sure no one sits in your starting chairs), while getting to the other side & back.

The Director of the Game will say, "Ready, Set, Go!" NO FALSE STARTS. You must move together as a team, and protect your side, while trying to win the game by sitting in the other teams chairs and going back to your original seats. Remember: The other team is trying to do the same thing, so you must HURRY! First team to go to the other teams chairs and back win. BOTH team members MUST be seated in their seats in order to win! This Game is Fun, Exciting, and Competitive!

Habbo Memory
By: shirtripper

Instructions:

Each row is numbered using 4 Scoreboards labeled 1-4. Using the 16 lodge doors make 4 rows of four spaced out. Use your 12 plants to put in between the doors. Take 16 items (8 different Furni items in pairs) and place them behind the doors so people can't see them.

The player Starts off the game by saying, for example: Row 3, Door 4 and Row 1, Door 2 and the person hosting the game will open those two sets of doors. If this doesn't match this player gets 1 strike. Each player gets 6 strikes to match all the items behind the doors, which the score is kept by using a scoreboard right next to the player. If the player unsuccessfully matches the items behind the doors they will be closed again for thier next try. This game is just like matching cards. HAVE FUN!

Hell's Gate
By: -Koby

How To Play:

The object of the game in to be the first out the the four players to sit on the green pod. The four players sit on the mode sofas at the back of the room.When the host opens the area gate, the game has begun. The players race into the tele. Once they teleport, they will be in a different room. This room has 4 teles in it. Players choose a tele. The tele will probobly lead to another room that looks exactly like the one they were just in. So again, they keep finding their way through. There are 4 of these identical rooms. One of the teles in one of the identical rooms lead back into the main room right next to the green pod. Then they will sit on the green pod if nobody else is already there.

You might also go in the tele where you came from. This tele would lead back to the main room. If this happens, check to see if somone is on the green pod yet. If there is, you lost, and you should reload the room to play again. If nobody is in the green pod yet, go back and continue trying to get out of hell.

Pick Up Fish
By: Khanor

Instructions:

  • Have fish either lined up or scattered
  • Let participators into gated sections
  • Close hatches
  • Give participators rights
  • Count down from 3 (optional)
  • Start when room owner says, "go"
  • Participants use rights to move fish into their area for 1 minute
  • Blue fish are +1, skeleton is -1 players can take fish from other people's areas and put skeleton fish in
  • At the end of minute take away rites and count up fish player with the most fish wins 10. The winner gets a prize

Virtual Volleyball
By: Mhur

The Objective of the Game: To get the ball to land on the floor of the opposing team.

How to play:

There must be a referee present during the duration of the game.
Each team will acquire rites in which they will use to place the ball on one of the opposing team's roller. The opposing team must then run in front of the roller to try and block the ball. Your team scores a point each time the opposing team fails to block the ball from hitting the floor. If you score a point, your team regains possession of the ball. If the opposing team blocks the ball from falling, the gain possession of the ball. These steps are repeated until one team reaches 7 points.

LETS PLAY BALL!!!!

Hab-Man
By: -Blast

What you got to do to win is roll all the dice and get back to your pod, but the problem is you've got "ghosts" trying to trap you if you get trapped you lose. What you need to build the Hab-Man maze is bars, corners, doors corners. Bars can be replaced by stuff like trees or plants or stuff you can't sit on.

It's a real fun game even if you're a ghost.

Habbo Spoons
By: Jamaicachicken

Okay this game is really based on the card game. It’s like Habbo bingo in a whole new way. So here is how you play:

  • The host will roll the dice
  • That’s when the players will roll their dice.
  • Once anyone gets the number that the host rolled "everyone" I mean EVERYONE can go for a chair that you see in the pic. It doesn't matter if you got the number the host rolled or not, once anyone gets the number you can go.
  • Last person to not get a chair will be eliminated.
  • Take out a chair (so there will be one less chair each time).
  • Repeats steps 1-5 till 2 people are left
  • Only 1 chair and 2 people left first one to get a chair wins the game!
  • AND OF COURSE THEY ARE GIVEN A PRIZE :)

Habbo Twister
By: Mathew

Rules: The rules of the game are extremely simple.

  • Once all players have read the rules the host will roll the dice to randomly call out a color.
  • You will then run to the colored mat that coordinates with the number rolled.
    (EX: if 1 = red and I roll the number 1 everyone races to the red mats.)
  • The last person to stand on the selected colored mat will be eliminated.
  • The remaining player will then sit back in the provided sofas for the next round.
  • The game will continue with the host rolling a number and the players racing to the coordnating color until one player (The winner!) is left.

To win: In order to win you have to be the last player remaining. However, the game isn’t as easy as just running to a colored mat! Because the TWIST is that the mats will be continuously moving because of the rollers! That's the game of HABBO TWISTER!

Climb the Prezzie Mountain
By: FreddyLover24

How to play:

Three people sit and wait at the bottom chairs. When the host says “GOO!!!” they have to race up the mountain. They have to race up and down the mountain. (So, up down, up down, up down.) If you're the first person to make it down after your third lap, you win! It's simple, easy, and fun!

The competition is not currently available.
