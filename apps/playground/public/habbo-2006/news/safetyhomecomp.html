Competition Name: Dude, Where’s My Bar? The Habbo Home Page Comp
Competition Dates: February 12 - February 20

Dude, Where’s My Bar? Iced bar, that is. If your credits or furni is missing, it’s likely because you also “misplaced” your password in someone’s console message or scam site :x
