Join Habbo Club

Joining Habbo Club is a long, tiring and difficult affair best left to the professionals. Just kidding. Actually it is simple, really, really simple.

To join Habbo Club you have to have logged into the Hotel. Once you've logged in stay on Hotel View and click the Habbo Club logo, where it says 'Join Habbo Club'. A pop up window will appear and if you have 25 Habbo Coins in your purse you can click 'ok' and buy.

You can now buy Habbo Club for longer periods of time and get the additional months at a reduced price:

  • 1 month = 25 Habbo Coins
  • 3 months = 60 Habbo Coins (Save 15 Credits)
  • 6 Months = 105 Habbo Coins(Save 45 Credits)
