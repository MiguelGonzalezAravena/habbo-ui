Buy With CVS PrePaid Habbo Cards

Habbo Prepaid Cards are in CVS locations across the nation! The newest series features DJ Drop, Razzie, Habbo Staff, The Dudesons, and Habbo Pets. For just $10 you can purchase your favorite collector’s card at a CVS location near you and then redeem the value at Habbo.com for 50 Habbo Coins!

How does it work?

Getting Habbo Coins with a CVS PrePaid Habbo Card is EASY! Here's how:

  1. Purchase your Habbo Prepaid Card(s) from a CVS location - there are four versions to select from.
    Find a local CVS store here
  2. The voucher code (Pin #) will appear ON THE RECEIPT of purchase - make sure you hold on to this. If not, you'll be throwing 50 Habbo coins away!
  3. Check-in to Habbo.com.
  4. Once you are registered in the site, go to your Purse section. Click on the 'Voucher' button.
  5. A pop-up window will open, requesting that the voucher code be entered here. Enter the Pin # on your CVS receipt.
  6. Enter the code and click 'Redeem'.

You should get a message that 50 Habbo Coins were added to your purse!

help

If you are having problems with your CVS Habbo Prepaid Card, then please use the Habbo Help Tool to contact Player Support. Make sure that you select the relevant Payments heading from the drop down list. You can also email us directly at habbocards.us@sulake.com. Make sure to include the number from the back of the card (beginning with 009 or 014) and/or the pin # (voucher code) provided on the receipt from CVS.

CVS Habbo Prepaid cards ONLY work on www.habbo.com. They will not work on any of the other global Habbo websites.

Also, prepaid cards purchased at any store outside of the United States – are NOT valid on the US site. These include prepaid cards from: EB Games Canada, Petro-Canada, or a Chapters.Indigo store – These cards are for the Canadian Habbo site and cannot be redeemed for coins on Habbo.com.
