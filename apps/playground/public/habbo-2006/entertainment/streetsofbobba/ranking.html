Streets of Bobba Points Explained

 

With all these gangs fighting and raiding each other, it might be confusing to know who is winning. We have agreed on a ranking system so that we can figure out who is really the winner of this takeover.

Each gang will be running events to show they are the best, such as the "Emo Battle" currently being planned by iomegadrive and The Emo Oranges (Yes, we know all about your secret plans iomegadrive!). For each of these events, the winner will win "Gang Points" for both themselves AND the gang that they represent. But there's one catch! If you can beat a gang at their own tricks, then you earn extra points!

For example, let's say you manage to win at the "Emo Battle" that is coming up soon. Congratulations!

  • If you are an Emo Orange, then you have just earned your gang 10 points, and 5 personal points.
  • However, if you are a member of another gang, such as [SFK], you have just racked in 20 points for your gang! (along with the 5 points for yourself).
So try to win as many of these events as you can, but winning at the enemy gangs events will give you extra points.

 

At the end of the competition, the Top 10 ranking Habbos - and the winning Gang with the most points will be declared the winner and win special prizes.
