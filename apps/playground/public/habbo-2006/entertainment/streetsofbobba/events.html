Daily Event

Everyday, at a random time between 3 PM and 6 PM PST, the Gangs and Armies floor will be checked.

The gang with the most popular rooms at the top 20 of the floor will earn their gang 10 points. The owners of the top 5 rooms will recieve 5 personal points.

These rooms must be labelled with the gang in front of their room name: [TEO], [SFK], [FFF], or [JFF].
