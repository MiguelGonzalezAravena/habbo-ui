A Dream Come True!


You guys did it. You actually made some sense out of that crazy dream I had! The wobbling squabbling rats marked the beginning of a trail of animals from the Chinese Zodiac, which ultimately led us to Confucius’ second lesson.

Congrats to 98? for submitting the correct answer first, and to AZNRichBoy and john121-, our runner-ups! Thanks to you guys I was able to score the next item of feng shui furni, the beautiful Red Knots. These knots are believed to bring luck to their owners, so you better stock up before attempting to solve Confucius’ new quest! Speaking of which, another page in the mysterious book is now scribbled up with the Confucius’ latest message:

Well done Luna (and crew)! You’ve successfully learned my second lesson: “The superior man is modest in his speech, but exceeds his actions.”

I hope this lesson has inspired you to get off your lazy Habbo bum and do something! Yes, that’s right, that dream I sent was not only a clue about the Xi! I was also trying to tell you to get up and actually play Wobble Squabble for once instead of just watching. After all, actions speak louder than words, young Luna.

Speaking of which, it’s time for you to start acting on discovering my third lesson. The clue is out there. Search for it, find it, LEARN IT!

-Confucius

Well, I don’t really see myself playing Wobble Squabble anytime soon, but I definitely think we should take Confucius’ advice and get going on the next quest. If you discover and submit the third lesson first you’ll get the Calligraphy of the Masters poster, the Red Knots, the next feng shui furni Confucius gives me AND two months of HC!! I’ll also randomly select 3 of you who submit correct lessons to win the new piece as well.

Good luck Habbos! May the feng shui force be with you!
