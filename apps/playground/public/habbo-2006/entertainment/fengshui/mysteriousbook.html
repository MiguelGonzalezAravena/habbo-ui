The Mysterious Book


Wow, what a weekend I’ve had! It all started on Friday night, when I read a magazine article on the ancient Chinese practice of “feng shui.” Supposedly, if you rearrange your furniture a certain way, you’ll achieve personal peace and harmony. I was instantly sold, so I headed to my lair and started making adjustments. I worked all weekend making tweaks here and there, but I still wasn’t feeling any more peaceful or harmonious. On Sunday night, I gave up and fell asleep on my sofa, ready to give feng shui another shot in the morning…

This is where things get kinda crazy…when I woke up this morning everything looked the same, except for this big ol’ weird-looking book in the middle of my floor. When Shotzi was done sniffing it I picked it up and started flipping through it. All the pages were empty except the first one. This is what was written on it:

Luna,

What a silly and foolish girl you are! You think the path to righteousness and well-being can be achieved simply by arranging furniture a certain way??? Nonsense! You have a lot to learn young one, and I’m here to teach you….in spirit at least!

There are 4 lessons you must learn, but it is up to you to find out what they are. Sound complicated? Well I never said the road to peace and harmony was an easy one. I will guide you on these quests for knowledge but you must dedicate and apply yourself to reach the end.

While a lifetime of self-fulfillment should be enough of a prize for solving these quests I lay before you, I understand you probably need a little more incentive…not only are you foolish, you are greedy as well! Nonetheless, you will be rewarded for each lesson you learn with a piece of furniture beyond your wildest feng shui dreams.

Keep your eyes open Luna, for, “Ignorance is the night of the mind, but a night without moon and star.”

- Confucius

A little harsh, don’t ya think?? *Sighs* Well, if I’m going to get this feng shui furni he’s talking about, I guess I have some learning to do. Is there any way I can talk you guys into helping me? What about if I put the furni I’m rewarded into the catalogue? That ought to do the trick! Plus, to sweeten the deal, the first person to send me Confucius’ first lesson will receive this mystery furni and 2 months of HC for free!

I've already posted the first clue Confucius left me in my room. Head on over and get started! Thanks Habbos!
