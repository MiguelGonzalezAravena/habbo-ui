We're Almost Done, Habbos!


Nice job on the last quest you guys! I haven’t been able to remember my dreams lately, so I thought we were all doomed on this one. I owe a special thanks to Ih3aRtY00h (the winner) and Jfirex1, .x.Aqua.x. and mr.bachata2 (the runner-ups) who studied the ancient Book of Changes, the "I Ching", to teach me about the importance of modesty (even though I already knew all about it, since I am the smartest Habbo I know *hehe*). As a reward, Confucius gave me an awesome Red Lantern, which gives off the coolest scarlet glow. It’s in the catalogue now, and I personally think it’s a must-have for all those awesome Asian-themed rooms you guys have been designing.

I wish I could say I’m done learning, but like Confucius said from the beginning, I have four lessons to learn, not three. Here’s the vague clue that appeared in the mysterious book this morning:

Three lessons down, one to go. You have proved your sharp intellect young Luna. Now, a test of –:Patience:-. You will receive one clue per day for six days and with each answer you’ll have the tool to unlock your last gift of knowledge.

-Confucius

Oh man! Patience?! I hope you guys have some, cause I’m feeling reallllly impatient at the moment. If you are up to Confucius’ last challenge and you can stick with it till the end, please help me out and send me the lesson along with the answers to all six clues once you learn them. I’m sure there is a huge reward in store this time, which you know I’ll share with you! The first person to write in with the correct answer will receive all of Confucius’ gifts we’ve received to date (that would include the Calligraphy poster, the Chinese Knots poster and the Red Lantern) as well as the latest gift and 2 months of HC! I’ll also randomly select four additional Habbos that answer correctly to win one of whatever Confucius gives me next!

What are you standing around for?!? Get out there and learn! Thanks Habbos.
