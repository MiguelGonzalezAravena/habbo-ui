Luna's FABULOUS Feng Shui

Luna’s Feng Shui Challenge



You’ve learned Confucius’ lessons, but now comes the real challenge…can you properly feng shui your space? We have the complete line of feng shui friendly Asian Furni in the catalogue, so I think it’s about time we all get in touch with our inner chi’s and rearrange our Furni feng shui style!

Here are some common feng shui rules I will stick to while judging:

  • When sitting at a desk or lying in bed, the entrance door should be in a clear line of sight, and you should have a view of as much of the room as possible.
  • Straight lines and sharp corners should be avoided.
  • Rooms should be free of all clutter.

Also, some objects are believed to have the power of redirecting, reflecting, or shifting energy in a space, which is a good thing. These objects include mirrors, crystals, wind chimes, and flowing water (you’ll definitely need to be creative in re-creating these).

What are the prizes you ask? Simple. First place gets the entire line of Asian Furni, a Green Fountain Rare and 20 Habbo coins. Second place gets the entire line of Asian Furni and third place gets 20 Habbo coins and a pat on the back. Hehe.

To enter Luna’s Feng Shui Challenge, design your room, put [FS] in front of the name and put it on the Feng Shui Floor, of course. Then, take a screenshot of yourself in your best meditative pose in your room and submit it to competitions.us@sulake.com with Luna’s Feng Shui Challenge in the subject line. You’ll also need to include your Habbo Name and your Room Name in the email so that we can give you proper credit.

The competition will close at 11:59pm on Monday September 4th. Good luck and may the feng shui force be with you!
