Another (Strange) Clue!

I had the craziest dream last night, Habbos. I dreamt I was up at the Rooftop Rumble pool working on my tan, when all of a sudden, two giant rats were up on the Wobble Squabble platform duking it out. I didn’t know quite what to make of it so I clicked on them to see if they had Habbo names and missions, and both were marked with the following: *Zi*.

As soon as I woke up, I had to know if there were actually rat Habbos out there, and of course there weren’t, but I did come upon something very interesting…there is a locked room in the hotel called *Zi*!

After some googling, I found out that Zi is the first animal symbol in the Chinese zodiac, the Shengxiao! I don’t know how Confucius got into my dreams, but I definitely think this is another clue for his second lesson. Good luck deciphering Habbos…hope this weird rat clue helps.

-Luna
