Meet the Habbos Competing for the Job!

Name/Age: Tiffany, 17
Habbo Name: AWS-Tiffany
Favorite Public Room: Vertigo Dance Club
Favorite Habbo Activity: Dance competitions
Quote: “Look at me! Did you see that? Were you looking?”

Tiffany comes off a bit wacky, but she really is talented. And she knows she’s talented. She tends to blurt out whatever she’s thinking, (it’s usually about her) but her friends still like being around her. However, don’t underestimate Tiffany. Even though she seems daffy, she really wants to win. And she does have talent -- just ask her.

Name/Age: Gretchen, 16
Habbo Name: AWS-Gretchen
Favorite Public Room: Cunning Fox Gamehall
Favorite Habbo Activity: Playing Tic Tac Toe on the Cunning Fox Gamehall
Quote: "Who moved my chair?”

Gretchen is super quiet and a total neat freak. She loves to help her friends get organized, whether they’ve asked her to or not. She’s kind of obsessed with order. But just so you know, she secretly loves eating with her hands. And she might just silently work her way toward winning this Challenge and surprise everyone.

Name/Age: Piper, 17
Habbo Name: AWS-Piper
Favorite Public Room: Club Mammoth
Favorite Habbo Activity: Listening to Bobba Music
Quote: "What? What? I can’t hear you with my headphones on."

Piper lives for music. She doesn’t care what language, what style, what country it’s from: All music is awesome. Her ability to sing any lyric amazes her friends, even when they would prefer she just spoke to them. Maybe the only thing she loves more then music, is the idea of winning the Challenge.

Name/Age: Emma, 15
Habbo Name: AWS-Emma
Favorite Public Room: Picnic Garden
Favorite Habbo Activity: Trading furni in trade rooms
Quote: "Not now, I’m busy."

Emma is very goal oriented, and really, really wants to win the challenge. Even though she has lots of friends, sometimes her non-stop focus on how she would take the Spa and Resort to the next level drives them crazy. But she just can’t help herself. She was born to over-achieve.

Name/Age: Aubrey, 16
Habbo Name: AWS-Aubrey
Favorite Public Room: Rooftop Rumble
Favorite Habbo Activity: Wobble Squabble
Quote: "Did you hear me?"

Aubrey’s sort of loud, but very caring. Don’t ask her what she thinks unless you’re ready to hear the truth. Aubrey believes that it’s always good to get in the first word. And the last word. And all those other words in the middle. But even when she’s being blunt, her big heart is never in question.

Name/Age: Rachel, 14
Habbo Name: AWS-Rachel
Favorite Public Room: The Library of Alexandria
Favorite Habbo Activity: Helping friends find furni they need
Quote: "Go Team!"

Rachel is always looking out for the good of the whole group. Everyone loves her quick wit and infectious laugh, even when she says something slightly insulting followed by “just kidding.” She’s a natural leader who’s happy to stay in the background and let others shine. But maybe this time, she just might be the star.

Name/Age: Madison, 17
Habbo Name: AWS-Madison
Favorite Public Room: Always Spa & Resort Pool Deck
Favorite Habbo Activity: Catching rays on the Pool Deck
Quote: "“In a minute.”"

Madison is very sweet and laid-back. Some might even say a bit lazy. With as little effort as possible, she tries to make her friends feel better about themselves and that’s why they like her. But beneath that calm exterior, she plans to prove everybody wrong by winning.
