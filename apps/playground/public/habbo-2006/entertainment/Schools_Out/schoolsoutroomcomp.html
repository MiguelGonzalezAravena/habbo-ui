School’s Out 4 Summer! Classroom Design and DESTROY Competition

It’s that time of year again…time for summer camp, backyard barbeques, frequent slurpee runs and most importantly NO MORE SCHOOL!! We've come up with the perfect way to kick off summer celebrations: a Habbo competition that would make school teachers and principals everywhere cringe! What is this awesome competition you ask? It’s simple really, you guys create some classrooms, we choose the best ones and then we judge you on how well you destroy them!

Part One: Design a Classroom

I know the last thing you want to see at the beginning of summer is a classroom, but trust me, this is so worth it. We’re looking for the most organized and detail-oriented classroom...the kind of classroom that actually makes you WANT to study (if such a place were to exist). There aren't many rules because let's face it...summer isn't the time for rules and guidelines!

Here they are:

  • It must be a big room
  • It must contain at least 10 desks and chairs
  • It must have one speaker's corner at the front of the room

Other than that, just be creative and have fun…it is a virtual classroom after all, not a real one! We’ll be selecting TWO winning rooms. Both room designers will be awarded 25 coins and the chance to compete against each other for a huge summer prize pack including rare furni and 3 months of HC!

To enter the classroom competition, design your room, put [SO4S] in front of the name and put it on the School’s Out 4 Summer! Floor. Then, take a screenshot of yourself (the teacher) and your friends (the students) in the room and submit it to competitions.us@sulake.com with "School’s Out 4 Summer!" in the subject line. You’ll also need to include your Habbo Name as well as your Room Name so that we can give you proper credit.

All entries are due by Sunday, June 11.

Once we have our winners, we’ll describe how the trashing portion of the competition is gonna go down. I can tell you this much right now, it’s going to be an event to remember and YOU are going to choose the winner. So get crackin’ on those Habbo classrooms and stay tuned for more details on Demolition Derby: Classroom Edition!
