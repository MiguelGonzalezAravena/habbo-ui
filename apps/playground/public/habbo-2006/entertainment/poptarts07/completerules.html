HABBO HOTEL / KELLOGG’S™ POP-TARTS™ GOES TO HOLLYWOOD SWEEPSTAKES


OFFICIAL RULES

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. HOW TO ENTER: Beginning on or about 2/7/2007 at 12:00:00 AM (ET) through 4/30/2007 at 11:59:59 PM (ET) (“Promotion Period”) go to www.habbo.com complete the online entry registration, and then follow the instructions to play the Habbo Hotel / Kellogg’s™ Pop-Tarts™ Goes to Hollywood Sweepstakes (the “Sweepstakes”). Registration is free. The Sweepstakes consists of Quests 1 – 3 and Quests 5 – 8. Quest 4 consists of the Habbo Hotel / Kellogg’s™ Pop-Tarts™ Goes to Hollywood Contest and you can find the complete details by going to www.habbo.com. You may play each of the seven (7) quests in the Sweepstakes, but will only have automatic entry into the sweepstakes one (1) time only per quest. There will be no prizes awarded for Quest 8. Quests 1 – 3 and quests 5 – 6 will require registered entrants to attempt to correctly answer the Kellogg’s® Pop-Tarts® toaster pastries related questions associated with the respective quest. For each quest that an entrant completes by submitting correct answers to the questions, he/she will earn one (1) automatic entry into the Sweepstakes. To learn the correct answers to the Kellogg’s® Pop-Tarts® toaster pastries related questions, go to www.poptarts.com. For Quest 7, the first twenty (20) registered entrants who correctly answer the Quest 7 quiz questions will be determined to be the winners of the quest – subject to verification. Quest 7 begins on 4/18/2007 at 12:00:00 AM (ET) and ends on 4/22/2007. Answers to the Quest 7 quiz questions will be found in the webisode on www.poptarts.com. Entries for Quest 1 – 3 and Quest 5 - 6 must be received by 4/30/2007 at 11:59:59 PM (ET). Limit one (1) entry per person, per quest – maximum of seven (7) entries per person for the Promotion Period. In the event of a dispute regarding the identity of the person submitting an entry, an entry will be deemed to have been submitted by the Authorized Account Holder of the email address submitted at the time of entry, provided that person meets all eligibility requirements set forth in these Official Rules. “Authorized Account Holder” means the natural person who is assigned to an email address by an Internet access provider, online service provider, or other organization that is responsible for assigning e-mail addresses for the domain associated with the submitted e-mail address. Entrants may not participate with multiple e-mail addresses or registrations, nor may entrants use any device or artifice to enter multiple times or as multiple entrants. Any entrant who attempts to enter with multiple e-mail addresses or under multiple identities will be disqualified from participation in the sweepstakes and all entries submitted by that entrant will be void.

For Quest 7 – winners will be determined based upon the order and speed with which they enter and the correctness of their responses to the quiz questions. Winners will be notified by phone and/or mail and/or email at Sponsors’ discretion.
