The Habbo Dating Game


The Habbo event of the year is finally upon us...no, not Habboween: The Habbo Dating Game! This live, game-show style event hosted by iomegadrive, will start at 4pm PST/7pm EST today in Luna's "The Dating Game" room. Kedo and his three potential daters will go first, followed by Luna and the male contestants.

The audience seats are first come serve, so be sure to get to the room early, especially if you submitted a Dating Game question and are hoping to win that competition! When the room reaches full capacity, it will be locked so the fun can begin.

Win a Date with Kedo and Luna!


That’s right, your eyes are not deceiving you! For the first time ever, we’re giving two lucky Habbos the chance to win a date inside the hotel with staff, and not just any hotel staff…the lovely Luna and oh-so-handsome Kedo!

If you’ve ever arrived early to a TGIF event to reserve a dancing spot by Luna, or searched Kedo for two hours straight just waiting for him to leave Hotel View, this is definitely the competition for you! We’re looking for the Habbos who are clever, funny and devoted enough to charm the heck out of these tough to please staff members, and here’s how we’re gonna find them…

Over the next three days, we’ll be throwing three Love Tests, open for one day only. Love Test instructions will be posted on the day you have to enter them, so you’ll have to be on top of things to win. The six winners of the Love Tests (three Habbos for Luna and three Habbos for Kedo) will go on to be contestants in the ultimate dating showdown, the Habbo Dating Game, where the two lucky Habbo daters will be determined. All contestants will be announced at TGIF on October 6, and the the Habbo Dating Game, hosted by the always crazy iomegadrive, will take place on Monday, October 9.

The details of the date will be revealed at the Dating Game, but here’s a sneak preview of what the daters will be taking home as souvenirs from the greatest day of their Habbo lives: a month of HC, a Heart badge, 10 ticket bundles,a camera with film, a trophy inscribed by their staff date and a Rare that Kedo/Luna will personally pick out for them.

This is a once in a Habbo lifetime opportunity, so start spreadin’ the love!
