"Revenge"

"Revenge"
There is one night in Habbo when revenge isn't just a game...

By Xx....U.....xX
It was October 31st, Halloween, And all the Habbos of Habbo Hotel, were celebrating, with costume parties, games, and even trick or treating for Furni. However there was one Habbo named Fitted, he was hosting a game of Revenge (his favorite game), and as usual at the end of his games, he would kick the winner and lock the room, scamming the players of their Furni. Fitted had done this routine daily, and at the end of each day he go into one of his rooms, unload all the Furni he had scammed then count it while laughing like a maniac. The night was still young he thought, so he decided to host one last game of Revenge for the night.

He opened up his console and invited all of his friends (or victims). His room was full of potential victims and he began the game, as he asked questions he noticed one mysterious looking girl in the back of the room sitting ever so quiet. She gave shivers down his spine with her Pale face, black hoodie, and black skirt. By the end of the game she was still there, she was in the final two, and she for the first time, she had answered his question and won. Her name was "Spiritress". "May I have my prize now please" she said eerily", Fitted just laughed and said "Not in this life time", but before he could kick her she said something that would haunt him for the rest of the night, "I curse you Fitted, you shall be cursed forever!"

Fitted just laughed and said "It’s a Habbo noob" and kicked her. Fitted left his revenge room then went to one of his empty rooms to unload his Furni. After unloading all of his Furni he began to think about what Spiritress said. He thought to himself, "How can you get cursed on Habbo? Right...” Then someone appeared in his door, someone named "Spirit1". He gave a yelp, and then kicked the person and doorbell locked his room.

Then his doorbell rang, it was "Spirit2". Fitted became confused and a little scared. Another door bell rang, it was "Spirit3", then another, "Spirit4". Fitted didn't let any of them in but the doorbell kept ringing, so he changed the room to Pass lock, but that didn’t stop the doorbell, the spirits kept coming up until "Spirit666", Fitted now petrified, decided to turn off Habbo, but he couldn't.

And then she came, "Spiritress" appeared in his room, Fitted tried to kick her but he failed. "For scamming me, Fitted you have been eternally cursed, to stay in this locked room forever". After she spoke those words she left. Fitted opened up his navigator but there were no rooms, and whenever he tried to log out of Habbo, he couldn't. However, he kept getting doorbell rings, from "Spirit1" to "Spirit666".

No one has ever seen Fitted again, even though when you search his name he’s always online and in a guest room, and when you search his name in the navigator, there’s a room with 667/25 Habbos in it. So the next time you think about scamming, think about the curse of the "Revenge". Happy Habboween folks, muahaha
