Habboween Night of Fright

The ghostly whispers around the hotel are true! On the 31st of this October, we are going to have the biggest Habboween celebration we have ever had! Not only will they be in Habbo Hotel partying, they are also going to be live on bobba music talking live with us, your Habbo Staff.

Tune in live to bobba music, where the interview starts at 3:00 PM PST - and head into the Phantom of the Theaterdome. Spaces are limited, so get there early!
