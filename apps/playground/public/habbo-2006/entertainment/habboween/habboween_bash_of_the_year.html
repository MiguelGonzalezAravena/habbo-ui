Habboween Bash of the Year

 

Habboween celebrations are in full swing with our exclusive Habboween furniture in the catalog, and the grand opening of The Underworld floor. The only thing left is to throw the biggest and creepiest Habboween parties ever! We know that Habboween parties come in all shapes and sizes, from murder mysteries to costume parties, even Haunted Mansions. If you think you threw the very best Habboween Bash this year - send it in to us!

A Habboween bash should Halloween themed, include at least 4 pieces of Habboween furniture, and have people in it having fun!

 

This is what we need: (After you throw your party)

  • Habbo Name
  • Name of your Habboween Bash
  • Description (What did you do at the party, or what was the theme?)
  • Screenshots of your event! (Maximum of 3)

Send your entries in to competitions.us@sulake.com with “Habboween Bash of the Year” and you will be entered in a competition to see who is the best party organizer. The deadline for entry is every Thursday up until November 2nd.
Every Friday, we will run a vote with the screenshots and descriptions, and Habbos will get to vote on which bash they think is the coolest.

The best organizer each week (by vote) will win A Habboween Badge, 1 month of Habbo Club, and a secret rare!
