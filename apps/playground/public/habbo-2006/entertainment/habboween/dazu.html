~~Death Be Here~~

~~Death Be Here~~
by Dazu


This will scare your pants off! Once, a boy of the screen name, 'LittleCharlie' had logged onto Habbo Hotel for the first time that day. It was a normal lazy saturday afternoon. All of Charlie's Habbo friends seemed not to be on that day, so he thought to himself,"Perhaps, I shall go to the Welcome Lounge. I enjoy helping new Habbos." So he did. When he arrived, he saw many Habbos who were new. One Habbo was standing in the corner of the room. 'LittleCharlie' thought that the new Habbo looked rather scary. He ran shivers down Charlie's back. He was wearing the default Habbo cloths, except his skin was a deathly white. His Habbo name was 'DeathBeHere'. It was a rather peculiar name. Scary almost. Charlie sure didn't want to mess with this dude, so he went to the opposite side of the room.



Just then, Charlie froze. DeathBeHere had just friend requested him. "Oh no", thought Charlie. "Shall I accept or not?" Well it couldn't hurt to accept him, he was new to Habbo and probably needed a friend or two. Or so Charlie thought. After accepting, Charlie's mother called him for lunch. It was his favorite, grilled cheese. He decided to leave Habbo running. After lunch, his friend called him to hang out with some school friends, and Charlie didn't sign on Habbo until later that night. It was pretty dark when he got home. He unlocked his front door and stepped inside. "Mom, I'm home!", screamed charlie as he made his way to the living room. "They must already be asleep." He made his way over to the computer table. He had left Habbo running all day, so he had about 60 messages. He clicked "Delete all", they were all probably masses anyway. Then somthing caught his attention...

Someone was in his Habbo room. He immedietly clicked "Go" on his room button, and then he entered his room. "Oh my god!", he screamed out loud. There DeathBeHere was standing. His black default hair was gleaming in the corner of Charlie's eye. "Hi Charlie", typed DeathBeHere, "I've been expecting you." "lol", Charlie typed. His heart was racing. Who was this guy? Then, DeathBeHere smiled a most wicked smile. "Call me Nordy", typed DeathBeHere. "Ok lol." Charlie typed. What did this Nordy guy want with him? He didn't stick around to find out. "g2g", Charlie typed quickly. Boy, that guy was weird. He exited off Habbo and prepared himself for bed. "That guy gave me the creeps!", thought Charlie. He went to his room, and jumped on the bed. Well, another day, another adventure. Charlie shut off the lights and went to bed.



ZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzz ZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzzzz Then Charlie awoke. Had he heard some creaking downstairs? He pulled the covers closer to himself. It was probably nothing. "Stop scaring yourself all the time." , He thought. He went back to sleep.

ZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzz ZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzZzzzzz CREAKK Charlie joleted up and almost screamed. He did hear somthing! He pulled the covers even closer. Just then he heard a faint voice. "Charlie why did you leave your pal Nordy?" Oh my! Nordy? That strange kid on Habbo? It couldn't be! "Why did you leave your pal nordy?", the voice was getting louder, and closer. "LEAVE ME ALONE!" bellowed Charlie. "WHY DID YOU LEAVE YOUR PAL NORDY?" , the voice was outside his bedroom door. The door opened. Charlie's mind was racing. "WHY DID YOU LEAVE YOUR PAL NORDY?" screamed the voice. Nordy walked up to Charlie. Charlie was terrified. Pixels don't just get up and walk out of a computer screen! Nordy was his pixel in real life. He was holding a giant bloody knife. "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH"...

"Charlie wake up!" said Charlie's mother as she opened his room door. That was odd. He wasn't there. " He must already be downstairs", she thought. When she got downstairs, he wasn't anywhere to be seen. Then she spotted somthing in the corner of her eye. Charlie had left the computer running. When she got closer she noticed that the normal desktop of the computer wasn't there. The whole screen was taken up by somthing different. As she got closer she let out a terrible scream. " OH MY GOD!" Charlie's Habbo was laying face down on the computer screen, in an empty Habbo room. He had a giant pixel knife in his back, and pixel blood running down the floor. Then in the corner of the room sat Nordy, with his default clothes and hair. His face was deathly white with an oh so wicked smile on his face...~~ The end ~~
