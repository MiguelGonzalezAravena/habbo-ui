Gamma Habbo Kappa

Are you ready to turn Habbo into the most party-hearty Hotel on campus? Well, that crusty old dean has finally taken us off double secret probation and let us start recruiting new members, so Rush Week is on! The Kappa Habbo Gammas are recruiting now, and if you can tough out our rigorous hazing schedule, including Soda Can Chug-Offs, the Greek Alphabet Pledge Check-In, Rug Run, and Know Your Frat Trivia, maybe you can join our ultra-secret society which covertly runs all major corporations, which in turn run all the governments in the world. If you make it, you’ll get our exclusive pin that you can give to your best girl (it’ll go great with your old letterman jacket she’s wearing until you get home for good) – the Kappa Habbo Gamma badge! Wear it with pride and let everyone know that you’re a future CEO!

It’s not all abuse and badges, though – we’ve got plenty of those famous college ragers planned for everybody, too! After all, if you choose to rush with us, you’re gonna want to celebrate! That’s why we are opening the Greek Row floor for you to Build Your Own Frat House! Not only that, but we want to see pictures from some of those awesome Greek System Themed Parties that we’re always hearing about! And, of course, once you’ve been accepted into Kappa Habbo Gamma, we’ll be holding a You Made the Frat Formal in celebration of our newly-forged bonds as brothers and sisters for life!

To make your decision easier, since this is something that you’ll need to be committed to for life, here are a few facts about Kappa Habbo Gamma that will let you know what we’re all about. Besides partying, of course.

  • KHG’s Official Colors: Green & Pink

  • KHG’s Official Flower: Dandelion

  • KHG’s Official Song: “Kung Fu Fighting” by Carl Douglas

  • KHG’s Official Movie: Mannequin 2: On the Move

  • KHG’s Official Soft Drink: Crystal Pepsi

  • KHG’s Official Dance: The Electric Slide

  • KHG’s Official Mascot: Duckbill Platypus

  • KHG’s Official Snack: Tater Tots

  • KHG’s Official TV Show: Saved by the Bell

  • KHG’s Official Band: Jefferson Starship
