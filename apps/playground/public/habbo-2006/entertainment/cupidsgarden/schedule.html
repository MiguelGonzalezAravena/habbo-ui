The Love Ducky Lottery

To participate in The Love Ducky Lottery, search Cupids.Garden in your Hotel Navigator, go to the Love Ducky Lottery room and count the number of Love Duckies on the ground. Every day the number of Duckies will change, so be sure to write down the totals and make sure you keep them in the same order as they appear. When the Lottery is over, we will ask you to submit all of the totals you have written down in chronological order (from the first day of lottery to the last) . From all of the correct answers we receive, we will randomly draw 10 lucky Habbos who will win 50 Coins and a special badge.

 

Some days the Love Ducky Lottery room will be locked. To find the password to the room, all you have to do is look at the bottom of the left hand column on this page, and the password (or a clue about the password) will be there. Please note, there will never be the same number of Duckies two days in a row so if your total matches the day before, that means the Duckies haven't been changed and you'll need to come back. The room description will also change everyday, so to make sure you are getting the correct total for the correct day, just check the room description!
