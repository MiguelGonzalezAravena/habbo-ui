In case you haven't heard, Cupid’s Garden is Habbo USA’s first romance-tinged reality show, where ten couples go head to head to convince the Habbo community that they are destined for everlasting love (and by everlasting we mean 6 months tops, hehe). Each week couples will gather in Cupid’s Garden and Habbos will watch on as they interact and reveal different things about their relationship. Then the Habbo community will vote for their favorite couple and the couples with the least amount of votes will leave the show.

In between tapings, Cupid’s Garden will be open to all Habbo Club members and Cupid’s Garden cast mates. If you aren't HC, you can still visit the Valentine Theaterdome and chat about the show and other lovey dovey topics in there. The Cupids.Garden Habbo Home Page will also be updated with show info, Habbo postings and secret crush submissions. To submit YOUR secret crush, click here.

If reality television isn’t really your thing, you can still celebrate the season by participating in our complete line-up of Love Games and Competitions, sponsored by Cupid’s Garden (see schedule below). There are some awesome prizes at stake so let yourself get hit by Cupid’s arrow and join in on the fun!
