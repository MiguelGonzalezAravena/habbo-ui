American Idol Room Competition

I can’t believe it’s almost over! My Tuesday and Wednesday nights are about to go from fab to drab, and I’m totally bumming! What am I going to do when I can’t laugh at Paula Abdul or watch Taylor’s crazy dance moves??? I’m going to cry, that’s what!! Ok, so it’s not going to be THAT bad, but you get the point: I’m gonna miss American Idol and I know a lot of you will too!

Since I’ve been the one smothering all you Habbos with my American Idol love over the past couple months, I figure now is the perfect time for you to throw it back in my face! That’s why I’ve decided to close the doors of American Idol Ave. on finale night, May 24th. That means I’ll have to host my American Idol event in someone else’s room…maybe YOUR'S!

I’m holding the ultimate American Idol room design competition to decide whose room I’ll be holding the final American Idol TGIF event in on May 26, 2006. Here’s what I’m looking for:

  • Color scheme must be red, white and blue…it IS called AMERICAN Idol for a reason!
  • It must be a BIG room to hold all of my precious Idol fans who come out for TGIF.
  • There must be blocked off area with seating for three judges – you, me and a friend of your choice!
  • There has to be an overall musical vibe because as we all know, American Idol is all about the music!
  • Last but not least, because I'm covinced that American Idol is the "coolest" show on TV, you have to somehow incorporate Iced furni into your room design. I've re-released Iced into the catalogue for a limited time only, exclusively for this room competition!

I’ll be judging the rooms based on the CRITERIA ABOVE as well as creativity in design and loyalty to the Idol spirit. Basically, you gotta be a fan of the show to win this room design competition…or at least be really good at faking it.

One lucky winner will not only get to host the TGIF Idol finale party on Friday, May 26th, but he/she will also receive 25 coins and an amazing American Idol furni prize pack, which includes the rare at the top of everyone’s wish list: a throne chair!

To enter, you’ll have to design your room, put [AI] in front of the name and put it on the American Idol Floor. Then, take a screenshot of yourself and your friends in the room and submit it to competitions.us@sulake.com with "American Idol Room Competition" in the subject line. You’ll also need to include your Habbo Name as well as your Room Name so that we can give you proper credit. We'll be acccepting entries through May 24th, so start your decorating engines!

Come on Habbos, design an American Idol room that would make Randy, Paula and Simon proud!
