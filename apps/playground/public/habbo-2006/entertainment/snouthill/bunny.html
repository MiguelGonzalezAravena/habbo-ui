Bunny

Bunny dresses like a bunny. It’s unknown whether he actually *is* a bunny, or whether he just dresses as one to look funky. Bunny lives with Peeking Duck in a trailer, in the trailer park. He usually stays in the background of the action, and is considered the ‘odd one’ in the group- a lot like Lil’ Kim.

Hi Bunny!
Hello! How are you?

Good thanks, and you?
I am super dooper.

Good to know. What’s your favourite film?
Oh, I haven’t seen a movie in years. However, my favourite book is ‘Are You There God, It’s Me, Margaret.’

How did you meet the duck?
I rescued him! He was thrown away, and left for bread in a dark alley. He was not good enough to eat and so I took him in. And I fed him. Because he was not good enough to eat on his own.

What is the precise nature of your relationship with duck?
What do you mean? We’re friends. We live together in a trailer. We have a toaster. On Sunday’s we like to eat muffins.

Why do you live in a trailer?
It’s quiet. And small. My trailer is quite small, so there’s no room for sadness.

How did you meet the guys?
We just got to know each other gradually. I always used to go to Dove’s Carrot Café at lunch time, and they were always there. Always hanging out, talking and being happy.

Are you really a bunny?
Yes.

Do you have any super-bunny powers?
No. I am a bunny. Not a super bunny.

What would you say is your greatest accomplishment?
I once saved the world from rug burn.

Extraordinary.
Yes.

Well, have a good day Bunny.
I will!
