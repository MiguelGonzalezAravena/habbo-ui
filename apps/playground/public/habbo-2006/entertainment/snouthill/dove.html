Dove

Dove owns the Carrot Cafe, Snouthills most pleasant organic cafe. She is a dedicated environmentalist and an idealist who wants to make the world a better place for everyone. While her café is always full with tired students in need of caffeine, the café doesn’t provide her with a huge profit- not that Dove minds so much: For her it’s more about spreading the word about eco-activism than making bags of money.

Besides, she has rich parents and lives in a mansion.

Hi Dove! How are you today?
I’m okay, I guess.

What’s on your mind?
Those crazy Happy Hotel people. They come in here every lunchtime asking for Moccha Chocco lattes. They even call me Moccha Mistress… they’re just so rude!

I hate corporate people too.
Oh, they’re so infuriating! They don’t care at all that they’re ruining the environment for everyone.

So what have you done lately, to help the environment?
Well, yesterday I chained myself to a tree.

Did it help?
Well it wasn’t cut down.

Was it supposed to be?
…No.

What’s your favourite movie Dove?
Die Hard 2. It’s got explosions and guns and Bruce Willis! Wheee!

That’s not about the environment!
There’s far more to me than just being obsessed with the environment you know! But, my second fave film is Free Willy: The Directors Cut.

Like what?
I like pez. I really like pez. And my favourite colour is green.

How do you get along with your brother?
Oh… Rich. He is just so embarrassing, It’s a wonder I have any female friends at all with the way he acts. You know… he’s never actually had a girlfriend? His first kiss was with a beagle.

Okay Dove, one final question before you go: Do you think being wealthy has helped or harmed your cause?
Oh, a bit of both. People just say I only do it because I’m bored, but if that was the case I would have taken up camping. It’s far more fun.

Okay. Bye.
Bye!
