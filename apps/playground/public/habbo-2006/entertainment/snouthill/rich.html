Rich

Rich is Dove’s brother. He is very cool. He is like the Fonz (he is a champion shark jumper). He works as a life guard at the beach and also as a DJ in several of the local clubs. His stage name is DJ Gudlookin’, and during his set he will usually dedicate a song to ‘the ugly people’, he considers this a nice act. He leaks confidence.

Hi Rich! How are you today?
I’m great. Women want to be me, men want to be with me.

Shouldn’t that be the other way round?
I don’t think so, no.

Have you ever had a girlfriend?
Loads, too many to mention. I’ve had more girlfriends than I have sunglasses.

Have you ever been in love?
Yeah. This girl I met a few weeks ago at a fancy dress party, we kissed for a full two minutes. She was dressed up as Conan The Barbarian… or was it Conan The Destroyer?

What was her name?
She said her name was Mike. She was beautiful.

Mike isn’t a girls name.
That’s what she said, but she assured me it was.

Okay, so you work as a life guard and as a DJ?
Yeah. I spin the ones and twos, down at the club. It’s awesome, feeling the vibe of the crowd, their attention totally focused on my music. As for being a life guard, well I watched Baywatch as a kid… I loved watching… the bay.

Who doesn’t. The bay is clearly where it’s at.
You got it my man!

Okay Rich… If you could have one wish, what would it be?
For the world to be a totally beautiful place.

I think we can all find some… discomfort in that. Thank you.
Outta here man, I got waves to catch!
