Phil

Phil is the most studious of the group. He works as a security guard at the town dump, he enjoys the job as its quiet and allows him to be alone with his thoughts. Phil studies philosophy at the local college and can always be relied on to bring balance and clarity to any situation, no matter how hectic.

Hi Phil! How are you today?
I’m good thanks! Not objectively good however, perhaps not even ethically good- not as much as I would like to be, but certainly in a subjective, emotional sense, I feel good.

That’s good to hear. So what’s your favourite movie, Phil?
Well I really enjoyed the Truman Show. I feel like that you know, maybe it’s paranoia, but it feels like everyone is watching me on a television somewhere. I can identify with that movie.

Do you think the movie identifies with you?
Well, I doubt the movie has sentience, so I wouldn’t think it was capable of identifying with anything. Perhaps, not even itself.

So, you are a philosopher? Why did you study philosophy?
It was that or global marketing theory. Philosophy was marginally the lesser of two evils. I considered Scatology for a bit, but I don’t think it would have suited me. I guess I got into philosophy because of when I was a young boy…

Is this a long, boring anecdote?
Well… it’s boring… I realized, at a very young age, that the Easter Bunny and Santa Claus were in fact the same being… And I couldn’t understand why… I struggled, danced, fought with this question for a very long time!

Did you find the answer in the end?
I did! It exists in the space between Schopenhauer’s Fourfold Root of the Principle of Sufficient Reason and Deleuze’s concept of the Plane of Immanence.

Yes, it’s so simple when you think about it!
I know!

So Phil, what do you like to do in your spare time?
Read books, and try to think of ways to be cooler. They never work, because I guess I really should just be happy with who I am.

Are you though?
No.

On that note, the interview comes to a close. Thank you Phil.
Thank you.
