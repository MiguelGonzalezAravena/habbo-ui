Isabella

Isabella is easy going, fluffy, sweet and adorable. She’s the girl next door. If you lived next door to a beauty salon in Iowa. Despite being superficial and self centred on the surface, Isabella (or Bella, or Bell to her friends) really she cares a lot about other people and what they think of her. Isabella studies Political Science and also works in Dove’s Organic Café.

Hi Isabella
Hi!

How are you today?
Just great thanks! Just went shopping. Had to buy a new dress. But then I needed shoes to go with the dress, and then I needed lipstick to go with the shoes. But I saw a really cool blue lipstick, so I had to go back and exchange the dress for another one…. And oh! It was a hassle!

It sounds it. What’s your favourite movie?
Clueless! I am so like that character. My mom says we were separated at birth!

So how many different shades of lipstick do you have?
68! The same amount of shoes I own.

Do you enjoy working at Doves?
It’s okay. But she recycles everything! At one point she even wanted to start recycling toilet paper, but I told her it would smell terrible! But I love helping people, it’s so rewarding!

What do you like to do in your spare time?
Shopping! Being able to shop is a real luxury, the gift of freedom. You know in some countries shopping is actually illegal?

I don’t think that’s particularly true.
Well, I think so.

So how did you meet the others?
What others?

Your friends?
Oh! Well I had just gotten fired from Happy Hotel… They made me so mad with all their weird types of coffee! And I went to Dove’s Café, to cry my heart out in an embarrassing public display of emotion. And while I was there Dove offered me a job.

Do you like working there?
Oh it’s great. Although Dove can be quite strict. She’s always saying things like “How many penguins did it take to make that coat?” I was never very good at maths!

What do you study at the local college?
Advanced Political Science, it’s a great laugh!

And what is your life long dream?
To be a movie star!

Okay, thanks Isabella!
Oh! One more question please! This could be my big break!

What do you think could make the world a better place?
A mall in every garage.

How so?
Uh… Because malls are huge, and if you could find a way to get a huge mall into every small garage then just think of the amount of free space would be created!

Wow. That’s pretty coherent actually. Thank you Isabella.
Toodles!
