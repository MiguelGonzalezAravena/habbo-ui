Winger - Best Creative Stacker

Who loves room competitions? We do! We do! Where else do you get rewarded for being a show off? Well here comes the mother of all room competitions! Beat down the competition, show us what you got, and maybe YOU will be the next team member of the Habbo World Club USA Dream Team. Now is the time to fill the coveted spot of Winger.

In real-life soccer, the Winger is a wide midfielder. It is the winger's duty to beat opposing fullbacks and to deliver cut-backs and crosses from wide positions. Fast like lightning with mind-blowing dribbling skills, they are arguably the most technically gifted players in the team.

Just like in real-life, the Habbo Hotel Winger blows everyone out of the water. For our Winger, we’re searching high and wide for the room designer of all designers. We’re talking the King or Queen of creativity. In this competition we want you to put together and showcase the most spectacular room in the hotel.

This means bust out with the most creative and original use of furni, stacking upon stacking upon stacking, and tie it all together with a killer concept.
Put your room together and take a screenshot. Send that image to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject Best Creative Stacker. Remember to include your room and your Habbo name! Please have your entries sent by Friday, June 30th 2006.

One lucky winner will be chosen by the Staff and will receive a Habbo soccerball, 75 Habbo coins, 3 month HC, and a golden shoe badge.

Think you are the best stacker at the hotel? Yeah… well we’ll see about that.
