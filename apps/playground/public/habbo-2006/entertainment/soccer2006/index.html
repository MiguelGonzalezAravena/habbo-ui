Game ON at the Habbo Soccer Hangout!

Hey Habbos!

If you haven't been keeping up with sports, last Friday kicked off one the biggest sports events in the world - The World Soccer Championship in Germany!

All of the Habbo Staff have agreed to have a huge celebration of the event inside Habbo Hotel. Cheer on your favorite teams and join up for the competitions and viewing parties we will be arranging.

The plan is simple: between June 12th and July 9th, Habbo will be ALL about soccer, Habbo Style.

We will be having Soccer Quests, “Habbo Direct” live comments of our team’s matches, Kick Warz, crazy room design competitions, the NEW Football Furni, big prizes… you name it!

It’s the Habbo Soccer Hangout, the ultimate place where to share, talk, and breath football together with friends and opponents alike!

Check out the Roster page to see what’s going on right now. Keep on checking this page regularly, we’ll have new stuff going on every week!
