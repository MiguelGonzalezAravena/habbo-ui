Safety Goalkeeper Competition



Only the strong survive…
Now is the time for heroes…
Goooooaaaaaalllllll……..!


In order to win a game, your team must score the most goals. In addition to scoring goals it is essential to stop goals being scored against your own team – thus the goalkeeper is a crucial element. Soccer isn’t a one person effort – it’s a team effort. In addition, the goalkeeper needs everyone’s help.

A successful goalkeeper communicates well with fellow teammates. Long kicks and good defensive play help to prevent the goalkeeper from ending up alone against the opponents’ dreaded striker. Apart from keeping it clean in front of the net, good behavior should also be promoted. The USA Hotel is looking for a hero, a survivor, a leader! Are you strong enough to be our hero?

The USA goalkeeper will create a slogan that encompasses excellent goalkeeping. The slogan must be short, no more than 2 lines long and should support your entire team to:

  • Deflect negative behavior
  • Block hooligans from scoring against the USA
  • Boost the team’s morale!

This slogan will represent team USA’s goalkeeper throughout the competition and will be submitted to win the WORLD CUP of Goalkeepers! For example:

  • Do not swear on the field – and use the ignore button to keep your pitch clean!
  • A tactical player distracts the other team – let THEM wonder while you’re smiling in the face of rudeness
  • Stay sharp, don’t get emotional and angry; remember the team!

Submit your slogans to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject "Safety Goalkeeper Slogan" NO LATER THAN 6pm PST Thursday, June 15th.

Here are the rules:

  • Each Habbo can only submit one time (so think clearly about what you want to say and then submit)
  • If two Habbos have the same entry, the FIRST ENTRY SUBMITTED TIME-WISE WILL BE CONSIDERED FOR THE COMPETITION.
  • Submissions should be in NO LATER THAN 6pm PST Thursday, June 15th.
  • Jeffoo will select the TOP 10 slogans to be posted by Monday, June 19th. Then the entire Hotel team will vote on who you think best represents Team USA!
  • The winner will receive: 75 Habbo Coins, 1 Gold Trophy, 3 months of Habbo Club, and the Golden Shoe Badge!
  • There will be 2 runners up, or “relief goalkeepers” and will be awarded The Habbo Football and 1 month free HC!
  • Remember to include your Habbo Name!

The goalkeeper and their 2 relief goalkeepers (runners up) will be announced by 6pm PST Wednesday, June 21st. Good luck, and good game!

Jeffoo
