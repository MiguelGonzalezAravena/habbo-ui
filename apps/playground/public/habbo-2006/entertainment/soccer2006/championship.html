Become a Habbo World Champion!

Greetings. Please allow myself to introduce... myself.

They call me the Arbiter. Don't worry, you can trust me.

In this shiny bald head of mine I have honed my judgement skills to be totally fair and unbiased. With my unique qualifications, I have been asked to become the official Referee of the Habbo World Championship 2006.

The purpose of this competition is most unique, and is twofold:

1. Select a team of 11 Habbo World Champions: That is, finding the 11 most creative, helpful, talented Habbos in the entire world and forming a Soccer-like Team of Champions of the Habbo arts.
2. Select the Best Habbo Community in the World: that is, finding out which of the 16 Habbo Communities is the richest in Habbo Champions, and deserving of an unique prize that will make it even better.

It will be a strenuous competition. Sixteen Habbo Countries and thousands of Habbos will struggle to be the Habbo World Champions and help push their community to the top.

I will be making sure everyone has a fair chance at winning. I told you before, I am fully impartial and totally neutral, and I'll send off anyone who disagrees with that.

I'm the Arbiter.
You can trust me.

 
The Rules

First Round: June 12th-June 30th
In this first stage, which lasts all of June, your Habbo Staff will organize 11 competitions aimed at finding the 11 most talented community members in the USA. These competitions will span Room Design, Live Games, Habbo Graphics, and much more.

Every week, several team members will be selected out of the many who compete, namely:
June 12th-19th: 1 Goalie, 2 Wingbacks, 1 Central Defender
June 19th-26th: 1 Central Defender, 2 Midfielders, 1 Winger
June 26th-July 3rd: 1 Winger, 2 Forwards

These 11 Champions will be representing you as the Habbo USA Dream Team.

The first 4 Competitions this week will be announced here.

Final Phase: July 3rd-7th
This is the real deal. The 16 Habbo Dream Teams of 11 members each - as well as their Habbo creations or merits (room designs, games, helpfullness, pics, and so on) - will then be judged by the Ultimate Habbo Board, the official organization that decides on the most important Habbo matters. The Board will judge the merits of each Dream Team's Champion and choose the best in each of the 11 roles.

How does Habbo USA win the World Championship?
Every time a Habbo is chosen as the World's best in their category, a Goal is scored for that Habbo Country. Each day of the week the Board will announce a few winners. The country with the most people selected to be in the World Champions Team (and therefore has the most goals) will be the 2006 Habbo World Champion! In the case of a tie, all winning countries will gain prizes in full.
