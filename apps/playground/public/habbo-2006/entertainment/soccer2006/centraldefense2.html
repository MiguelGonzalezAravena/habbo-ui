The screenshots have all been reviewed and we’ve slaved over them with dripping sweat to make sure we picked the best possible stadium creation in Habbo. And now the search is over! The job of the center defender is to stop opposing players, particularly the strikers, from scoring, and to bring the ball out from their penalty area. And that’s just what we plan to do with our newest USA Dream Team member, perfectly named, soccergirl. Good job!!

Soccer Stadium Design Competition

 

Calling all soccer fans, habbo architects, and other prospective room builders! Habbo Hotel is looking for you to submit a fully designed and functional Soccer Stadium for review! If your stadium is chosen you will be recruited into the Dream Team USA 2006 as a Central Defender. Your design submission will go on against other country designs, so make it good!

Build your stadium to the following specifications:

  • Must be built from a Big Room (A real soccer stadium can't fit into a small private room!)
  • Your Stadium must have a spectator area, a playing field, and a refreshment area (food and/or drink).
  • Spectator areas must contain seating for at least 12 people!
  • The playing field must contain at least 2 goals, and 2 scoreboards (more if you want!)
  • Refreshment areas can range from a single mini-bar, to a fully-fledged concession stand!

Here are the submission rules

  • Submit a screenshot of your stadium to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject "Soccer Stadium Design Competition" by June 27th, 2006.
  • Remember to include your Habbo Name with your submission, or you may not get chosen!
  • The screenshot you submit must be of an empty stadium. (Cannot contain any other players besides you, the creator!)

Remember, creativity and a solid design will always stand out over the other stadiums. Try to get ideas from your friends, and make sure you follow the specifications!


Good Luck, I look forward to seeing some amazing stadiums!
