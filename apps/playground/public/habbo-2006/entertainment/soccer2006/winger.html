The Best Alt Artist Announced!

With so many talented Alt Artists in Habbo Hotel USA, hiro-p had his hands full looking over each and every one of your entries into our competition. After some careful judging we not only have 1 winning alt, but also 13 other Honorable Mentions for their entries!

The winner of the Best Alt Artist Competition is HankMcCoy for his pixel rendition of the Justice League. Whether you know who they are or not, you can still appreciate the pixel skill involved :) HankMcCoy has earned himself a spot on the Dream Team as the Best Alt Artist and a few other prizes. Each of the Honorable Mentions have each won 50 coins and a month of Habbo Club membership!

Winger - Best Alt Artist

Hey Habbos! For this competition we are scouring Habbo Hotel for the very best... of the Pixel Alt Artists. An "alt" is an alteration of something inside Habbo that you edit with an image editor such as Paint, Paintshop Pro, Photoshop. These changes can be as small as changing the face on a Habbo to look like Brad Pitt, or creating a Habbo-styled spaceship.

Let your imagination run wild, but you have to follow this one rule:.
We do NOT want any recolors. While we love our Habbo graphics and furniture we have, we want to see what you can create rather than what you can repaint! This leaves you totally free to make furniture alts, public room alts, private room alts, and Habbos of all shapes and sizes, or anything you can think of!

See that guy to the left? That's hiro-p, and he's the Pixel Artist for Habbo! hiro-p will be judging your entries and deciding who has the most talent from the alts you send us.

Contest Information:

  • Submit your alt entries to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject Best Alt Artist by Tuesday, June 27th.
  • The winner will be awarded some great prizes!

Remember that you are free to make or change pixels for your submission, so get creative!. I know there's a lot of you out there that are good at making alts, so let's see who's going to submit the best work out there!.
