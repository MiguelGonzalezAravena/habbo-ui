Forward - Best Soccer Game Designer

Hello Habbos,

We've had 10 crazy positions up for grabs to the best Habbos in the US Hotel. Personally I'm so impressed with the first 10 that I don't really think we need another, we can win a man down!

What do you think iomegadrive?
Ten should be good right?
You think we need one more?
You think we need one more.
All right, we'll get one more.

Our final position is a Forward, we need someone that can take the following task and make it a reality. You may be asking yourself questions like, "What do I have to do?", "Is this my last chance?", and "Who is Kedo?". Well sit right back, and take a look at last final player we need on our Dream Team.

The forward in Soccer is one of the most well-known positions on a team, they're the ones usually in the spotlight scoring the goals and setting up shots for other players! Those players are often the ones doing cool new moves, and changing the way the game is played.

In this case we need someone to come up with the best Soccer Game Design ever seen on Habbo Hotel. We need a new way to play Habbo Soccer on Habbo Hotel USA. Not only do you need to create the game, along with all the rules, you need to make a guide so that other players will be able to look at your instructions and figure out how to play!

Competition Rules:

  • Create your own Habbo Soccer Game, complete with rules and instructions
  • Write a guide for your game, along with Screenshots and description of your game
  • Submit your Soccer Game, rules, instructions, and tutorial to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject "Best Soccer Game Designer"
  • Send all of the above to us by Sunday, July 2nd, 2006

Now this Soccer Game can be as simple or as hard as you want it to be. Of course the most important things are that it is new, FUN, and that we can figure out how to play with your guide.

Good Luck!

Kedo, Hotel Manager
