The Best Habbo Photographer Winner!

The search is over for the best Photographer on Habbo USA. We got as many different submissions as you can think of, from shots of teams standing off, stadiums, and even some amazing montages! Remember that we wanted you to display your natural talent, without any kind of image editing done! Creative thinking played a big part in this competition, and the people who thought about what would come out great in a Soccer Action Photograph were the ones that got to the final round!

In the end, only 3 photographs remained, and we decided that Ry- came up with the most creative concept for his photograph. For his contribution he has earned a place onto the Dream Team USA as well as other great prizes. As for the other 2 winners, their soccer action shots have not been forgotten! Both LandenKid and PointeGirl have earned 25 coins, 1 month of Habbo Club, and 1 Soccer Ball Lamp! You can find all three entries below.

Wingback - Best Habbo Photographer

Photography plays a big part in Habbo Life. They help us take a picture of a cool moment and they hold memories we want to keep. So, the task for the second Wingback position is heavily based in using your eye to catch Habbo life in action. This is what we need from you:

  • Take a screenshot of some crazy Habbo Soccer action going on in Habbo (A game, team practice, a real soccer game).
  • Do NOT photoedit or try to enhance this screenshot with a program, we want to see your natural photgraphy talent in Habbo
  • Submit one screenshot to competitions.us@sulake.com with the subject "Best Habbo Photographer"
  • Submit this screenshot by Monday, June 19th
  • The Best Habbo Photographer will get a position on the Habbo Dream Team as a Wingback, the Golden Shoe Badge, and all the other prizes associated with being on the USA Dream Team.
