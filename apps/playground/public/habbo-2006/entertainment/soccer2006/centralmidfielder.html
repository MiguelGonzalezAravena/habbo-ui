Central Midfielder - Best Soccer Game Re-creation

Do you like directing your Habbo friends and arranging them into scenarios and formations only your mind can think up? Use these skills to form a realistic-looking soccer match, and you could end up on the Habbo USA Dream Team as Central Midfielder!

As you probably know from all the soccer you’ve been watching, midfielders have lots of control over how a match is played because of their central location on the field. Prove that you have what it takes to orchestrate an entire soccer game set-up, and we’ll appoint you to this powerful position!

The competition is simple. Using Habbo friends, foes, furni and anything else you can think of, design a soccer match in one of your rooms and take a screenshot. Your game should look believable, unique and beyond awesome with two teams, a cheering crowd, a referee and whoever else you think would be hangin’ out at a soccer game.

Once you’ve created and captured the perfect screenshot, submit it to competitions.us@sulake.com with “Soccer Re-creation” in the subject line. Be sure to include your Habbo name as well as the names of every single Habbo that appears in your screenshot along with the role they are playing.

Whoever is chosen for the Central Midfielder position on the Habbo USA Dream Team will win a Soccer Ball Lamp, 1 month of free HC and a Golden Shoe Badge. Since your friends will basically be busting their booties to help you out, we’ll also reward each of them with 1 month free HC as well. Talk about incentive!!!

No need to remain true to the red, white and blue for this competition! This competition is all about the game so wear whichever colors you wish. Now get on that console and start recruiting!

All screenshots are due by June 25.
