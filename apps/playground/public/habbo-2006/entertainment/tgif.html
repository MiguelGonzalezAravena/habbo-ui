TGIF - It's the biggest party every Friday!

Each Friday, Staff is going crazy and needs to let off some steam. We work hard and we like to play hard. And that’s where you all come in! We need people to hang out and party with and we figured, hey, there isn’t any one better than the Habbos! So, we’re making it official; each Friday we’re going to have a TGIF Celebration! It’s your chance to hang out with your favorite Staff member and play your favorite games. Check out the schedule below to find out when and where to be!

NEW! All-Level Battle Ball with Luna at 3pm PST/6pm EST
Manic Fallin' Furni with iomegadrive at 3pm PST/6pm EST
Staff Pick of the Week Party with iomegadrive at 4pm PST/7pm EST
Wobble Squabble in Rooftop Rumble with Staff at 5pm PST/8pm EST

Come join Luna in the Battle Ball Open Lounge. There you can show off your mad Battle Ball skills against Habbos of all levels and hang with Luna in between games. The Battle Ball frenzy kicks off at 3pm PST/6pm EST!

It's time for some MANIC FALLIN' FURNI! It starts at 3pm PST/6pm EST in iomegadrive's room, "Manic Fallin' Furni!" Be sure to get there early since it ONLY goes for one hour and not EVERYONE will be able to play! The winners of each round will get a prize! Get your speedy shoes on, you'll need it; the competition is FIERCE!

Next head over to the Staff Pick of the Week to hang out with iomegadrive and the featured Habbo! Not sure which room to get to? Check out the newsletter and find out! Iomegadrive will be at the Staff Pick at 4pm PST/7pm EST to hang out with the featured Habbo and anyone else who wants to explore the room!

Pull on your trunks and break out the suntan, since the next TGIF event is Wobble Squabble with Staff! At 5pm PST/8pm EST head over to Rooftop Rumble and test your skills against some of the best (and Staff too). Hey, you might even see iomegadrive win three in a row. But I wouldn’t count on it.

So there ya’ have it! The whole TGIF Shabang! Hope to see you at one of the parties!
