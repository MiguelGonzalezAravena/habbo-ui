Jeannie Ortega's Hip-Pop Hangout

Star in the Habbo Music Video for Jeannie Ortega's "Crowded"!




Hey Habbos, Luna here!

My new friend Jeannie Ortega had lots of fun visiting with us last week. In fact she loved Habbo so much that now she wants to invite all of you to make the first ever Habbo Music Video for her song “Crowded”. It’s up to YOU to create the perfect backdrop and dance moves for Jeannie Ortega’s awesome single. She was even nice enough to give me some really cool stuff to give away to Habbos who participate. I’ve got GirlNext CDs, autographed tank tops and even Habbo Club subscriptions and coins for you guys to swipe up!

First thing’s first…the music! Download Jeannie’s hit single

Jeannie’s Hip-Pop Hangout floor is up and running so set-up your video set and strut your moves! I’m not talking about any slow stuff here – I’m talking about feeling the groove and shakin’ that booty with all your friends!

You have until August 29th to find your partner(s) and practice your dance skills before I come around to find the top talent for the video. I’ll be popping in and out of rooms on Jeannie’s Hip-Pop Hangout floor between 3pm and 5pm PST August 29th-31st, handing out some prizes and recording the best dancing Habbos for the video, so be prepared!

I’ve posted a calendar below so you stay on schedule as the video shoot draws nearer…good luck and I’ll see you on the 29th!

Jeannie Ortega “Crowded” Video Shoot Calendar:

August 21st: Jeannie Ortega’s Hip-Pop floor opens - start picking your partners and get those rooms decked out!

August 29 – 31 (Tues-Wed-Thurs): From 3-5pm PST each day we’ll be roaming random rooms on Jeannie’s floor filming the video. Be sure to be in your rooms when we send out the alert. Get ready for stardom!!!

End-September: We’ll be throwing the first ever Habbo Fans Music Video Viewing Party for Jeannie Ortega’s “Crowded”. This is your chance to check out whether you made it into the video!!!
