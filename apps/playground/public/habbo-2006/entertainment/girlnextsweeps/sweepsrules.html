DESCRIPTION OF PROMOTION:

This Habbo’s Girl Next Groove Sweepstakes (“Sweepstakes”) is sponsored and produced by both Sulake, Inc. and Hollywood Records (“Sponsors”). The Sweepstakes provides entrants with the opportunity to win a Grand Prize as more specifically described below. The Sweepstakes is structured as four (4) separate individual Sweepstakes, whereby there are four (4) months September 2006, October 2006, November 2006 and December 2006 in which winners will be selected and participants who register as provided for below will have the opportunity to win one (1) iPod Nano Prize Pack for the applicable month.

Each Sweepstakes begins on or about August 8, 2006 and ends at 11:59 P.M. Pacific Standard Time (“PST”) on the following dates for each of the four (4) months: (1) September: September 30th, 2006, (2) October: October 31, 2006, (3) November: November 30, 2006, (4) Dember: December 31, 2006. No Entries will be received after such time, unless the Sweepstakes Periods are extended by the Sponsors, in Sponsors’ sole discretion.

WHO MAY ENTER:

Sweepstakes is open only to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and the District of Columbia, but specifically excluding residents of Connecticut, Florida, New York and Rhode Island who are thirteen (13) years of age or older, at the time of entry, are eligible to enter. Officers, directors and employees of the Sponsors and any vendor engaged by Sponsors to conduct the Sweepstakes, and their parents, subsidiaries and affiliates, and each of their immediate family members (including, without limitation, those persons living in the same household of each) are ineligible to enter the Sweepstakes or win a Prize. Sweepstakes is open to registered users of www.habbo.com or individuals that complete the alternative 3 x 5 card entry described below. Additional eligibility requirements are set forth elsewhere in these Official Sweepstakes Rules.

THERE IS NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN AND A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR ODDS OF WINNING. There are two separate ways to enter the Sweepstakes, but only one (1) form of entry and one (1) entry per person will be accepted:

1) On-line Entry. Visit www.habbo.com (“Sweepstakes Website”) Go to the registration area of the site, answer any survey questions and fill out the electronic registration form with your Habbo name, address, city, state, zip code, daytime phone number, e-mail address, age and Sweepstakes password (“On-line Entry”). Click “submit” (“Entry”). Use of any automated system to submit On-line Entries is prohibited and will result in disqualification.

2) 3 x 5 Card Entry. Fill out a 3x5 card (“Card Entry”) labeled “Habbo’s Girl Next Groove Sweepstakes Mail-in Entry” with your name, address, phone number, e-mail address, (if you have one) (all of which must be legible) and mail to the Sulake, Inc., 1460 4th St., Suite 302, Santa Monica, CA 90401 (Attention: Sweepstakes). Use of any automated system (e.g., a photocopy machine) to submit Card Entries is prohibited and will result in disqualification. The Sponsors will not use personal information obtained through your Card Entry, except in connection with this Sweepstakes.

The Sweepstakes is intended for viewing and participation in the United States and the District of Columbia only and will be construed and evaluated only according to United States law. Do not enter the Sweepstakes if you are not physically located in the United States or the District of Columbia at the time of entry. On-line or Card Entries made by individuals not physically located in the United States or the District of Columbia will be disqualified.

Entries become property of the Sponsor and will not be returned. All On-Line and Card Entries must be submitted to Sponsor by the deadline of 11:59 P.M. EST, December 31st, 2006 unless otherwise extended by Sponsor.

In the event of a dispute as to the identity of the entrant, any On-line Entry will be deemed submitted by the holder of the e-mail account or IP address, at Sponsors’ election, from which it was sent. In the event of a dispute as to any On-Line Entry, the authorized account holder of the email address or IP address used to enter will be deemed to be the entrant. The “account holder” is the person assigned an email address or service by the organization responsible for assigning addresses for the domain associated with the submitted address. Winners may be required to show proof of being the authorized account holder.

One day after the expiration of the applicable Sweepstakes Period (subject to extension by Sponsors), a random drawing for the Prize will be conducted from amongst all eligible Entries received in accordance with these Official Rules.
