Prepaid Scratch Cards at GameStop and EB Games


Brace yourself Habbos, the moment you’ve been waiting for has finally arrived…$10 Prepaid Habbo Scratch Cards are NOW available at GameStop and EB Games stores nationwide! That’s right, instead of calling, texting or clicking for coins, you can now walk up to the register at your local GameStop and EB Games, slap down a ten dollar bill and walk away with 50 Habbo coins and a collectable Habbo scratch card!

To find a GameStop or EB Games store near you, click here. We suggest that you scratch the back of your Prepaid Card in the store to make sure you didn't get a SAMPLE card....much better than getting all the way home and finding out your Prepaid Card doesn't have a voucher code! If you do end up with a SAMPLE card, simply tell a store employee and they'll swap it for another one. If you aren't near a store, you can also purchase $10 Habbo Prepaid Cards at Gamestop.com.

The $10 Prepaid Cards come in four unique designs, created to compliment all types of Habbo personalities:

There’s a card for sports fans...

One for music lovers...

Another for the Habbo who pulls all-nighters…

There’s even a card to suit your inner-pirate! Can’t choose one? Collect all four!

 

To celebrate the launch of Habbo Prepaid Cards at GameStop and EB Games stores, we are opening GameStop Galaxy, where you can grab a bite, chat about the latest video game releases with your Habbo friends and click on the billboard to purchase Prepaid Habbo Cards directly from GameStop’s website.
