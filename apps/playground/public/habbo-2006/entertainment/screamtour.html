Scream Tour 5 Takes Hold of US

It’s official. Sexy is back and will be bigger than ever when “SCREAM TOUR 5” rolls out on December 8th. Last year, the SCREAM TOUR sold-out arenas from coast to coast, hundreds of thousands of screaming fans witnessed performances from some of the biggest young stars on the planet. This time around--same scenario, different stars, still untouched mass appeal.

Two of R&B’s hottest, most sought after entertainers--the multi-talented triple threat SCREAM TOUR alum Omarion and platinum hit-maker Ne-Yo headline the tour, Accompanying the headliners are platinum selling artists Pretty Ricky and Mario, along with top-charting rapper Yung Joc. The tour also features the talents of Jibbs, Sammie and Usher’s new group, One Chance. Stay tuned for details as Scream Tour checks into Habbo for an exclusive live chat in December.
