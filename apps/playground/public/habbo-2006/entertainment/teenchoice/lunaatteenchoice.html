Luna's 2006 Teen Choice Backstage Journal!

What’s Up, Habbos! This past Sunday I was invited to the Backstage Creations "My Scene™ Fab Faces™ Dolls Celebrity Retreat," also featuring V.Flash™ Home Edutainment System by VTech® at the 2006 Teen Choice Awards. There was so much going on back there! I wanted to share it all with you guys, so I kept a journal of my experience to make sure I didn’t forget anything. Read on to find out who I met and what I saw:


5:00pm: After sitting in loads of LA traffic (those cars on Hotel View have it EASY) I finally made my way to the Backstage Creations "My Scene™ Fab Faces™ Dolls Celebrity Retreat," also featuring V.Flash™ Home Edutainment System by VTech®. The second I entered the tent, I couldn’t believe my eyes! There were lots of little tables set up featuring all the latest clothes, gadgets, accessories and CANDY. I instantly knew I was in for a very eventful evening!

5:05pm: After scanning the room, I was anxious to start checking out all of these goodies! Lucky for me, I didn’t have to wait long before my friendly Backstage Creations “escort” approached me to begin my tour around the tent.

5:10pm: First stop was the Neutrogena table, which was packed with all the lotion and sunscreen a girl could ask for! I’m a huge fan of the SPF (I have a fair complexion to maintain after all) so I was stoked to see their newest collection which includes the sweet-smelling MoistureShine® Lip Smoother with SPF 20! I wasn’t the only fan there…Jessica Alba was also at the table ogling Neutrogena’s Suncare line. Maybe sunscreen is the secret to her flawless skin…

5:25pm: Next up were the My Scene™ Fab Faces™ Dolls…you’ve got to see what the awesome My Scene™ team has come up with now!!! These fashion dolls are the first and only to make actual facial expressions!! At the push of a button they smirk, smile, pout and even look surprised. Vanessa Anne Hudgens LOVED the dolls and couldn’t wait to step into the photo booth they had on hand to pose with the Fab Faces™ Dolls and make some dramatic expressions of her own! Pssst…I saw my favorite American Idol judge, Paula Abdul, walk away with a whole set of them…I’ve gotta have these dolls!

5:40pm: Just when I thought I couldn’t get any more girly girl, I found myself at the Hello Kitty table checking out the new Hello Kitty iPod speakers. As I inched in to get a closer look at the cuteness, Ludacris (of all people) snuck in front of me to get his hands on the girly goods – don’t worry, turns out he was just picking one up for his daughter!


Hangin' out with my lovely pet dog, Shotzi.

6:02pm: Speaking of awesome electronics, have you guys ever seen Tiger Electronic’s I-DOG Interactive Music Companion? Shotzi, move over, there’s a new doggy in Luna’s life! This pup actually grooves to my favorite music…the only thing Shotzi does is play dead and eat his weight in cabbage and bones (just kidding Shotz, you’ll always be my #1 pet :-D)! As I was walking away, I saw funny man Marlon Waynes playing with one of the colorful pups and I knew this item was a hit!

6:20pm: The I-DOG wasn’t the only musical must-have in the tent…HoMedics was there with their iCush, the first portable cushion that syncs sound directly to massage vibes! When it was my turn in the hot seat, I selected “London Bridge” on the iPod they provided and sat back as Fergie’s beats massaged the knots out of my back. Too cool!! Plus, I found out later that Kevin Federline chilled out on that very same iCush before he hit the Teen Choice stage. To think, I got massaged by the same beats as K-Fed! Maybe that means I’m ready to start a rapping career of my own…or not.

6:35pm: It was hard to leave the iCush, but when I saw what was next I had no problem hopping off. What could make Luna jump so high with glee you ask? WONKA candy, that’s what!!! I am a huge Nerds addict (just like Travis Wall, my favorite contestant from “So You Think You Can Dance?”), so when I saw the giant Sour Nerds box sitting there I felt like busting out in song like a giddy Oompa Loompa! Snoop Dogg seemed to share my enthusiasm for the Sour Nerds as you can see to the right!
Photo provided by WireImage
Snoop Dogg autographs a WONKA-sized box of Willy’s latest creation, Sour Nerds, at the 2006 Teen Choice Awards.

7:00pm: I didn’t think anything could top my WONKA candy bliss until I saw what was coming up next…accessories! We started off at the GUESS Watches table where I tried on watches next to Christina Millian and found the perfect style to go with my favorite green tank top! Now that I had a watch, I needed to find a matching belt (I think there’s some kind of rule about that), so I followed Laguna Beach star Kristin Cavalleri over to the Melamed table where we checked out their amazing handcrafted leather belts…I fell in love with a black waist belt that hugged all the right pixels. We finished up our accessory hunt at the Chinese Laundry Handbags area where I got a sneak peak of the hottest purse for fall: the yellow Covertible Strap Must Have which was snatched up by Kristin, Rhianna and Jessica Simpson! If only we had handbags on Habbo…

7:15pm: Now that I knew which accessories to wear this fall, I needed some advice on what to wear with them. Lucky for me, JCPenney was also there with mannequins showing the hottest fall fashions for guys and girls. You guys have to check out the graphic tees and hooded canvas jacket from the Arizona Jean Co.® line and girls, you’re going to flip for the stylish pieces in the Miss Bizou® collection (coming soon!). All of these fashions are super affordable and great for back to school, and when your classmates ask you who your personal stylist is, you can tell them Luna dressed you. FYI, always stylish Paula Abdul also approved of these styles!

7:31pm: At this point in the evening my feet were killing me, so I told my escort I had to sit. She had a better idea…she led me over to the UGG Australia table where I got to check out the latest styles of their uber-comfy boots. Being a California girl myself, I especially liked their new sheepskin lined flip-flops. Just as I was leaving, Zac Efron came up to the table…I guess guys and girls are all about comfort!

7:40pm: After all the clothes shopping, my escort showed me to a table that required a little more brain activity (not saying that finding the perfect outfit isn’t mentally exhausting…). I was really impressed by the V.Flash™ Home Edutainment System by VTech® which actually makes learning fun with videogames featuring all my favorite cartoon characters like Sponge Bob and the Disney Princesses. I heard Britney Spears picked one up for Sean Preston and Baby No. 2! With the V.Flash™, maybe these babies will grow up to be the smartest kids in Malibu...maybe…

So, that wraps up my journey through Backstage Creations "My Scene™ Fab Faces™ Dolls Celebrity Retreat," also featuring V.Flash™ Home Edutainment System by VTech®. Hope you guys enjoyed this inside look into the latest and greatest star-approved products out there. AND, because you can always count on Luna to hook you Habbos up with the prizes, I’m going to give one lucky Habbo some of this awesome swag. Stay tuned for more details on Luna’s Surprise Swag Bag Sweeps and keep it real Habbos!
