Love Test #2: Warm Fuzzies

Now that you’ve expressed your innermost feelings through Furni, it’s time to put that pen to work! Unleash the poet inside and let Luna and Kedo know how you really feel!

Here’s how to send your sweet nothings to our judges:
  • Buy a prezzie and write the sweetest, sauciest, most awe-inspiring love poem or message you can think up and sign it with your Habbo name. The prezzies won’t actually be opened so the contents don’t really matter.
  • Send the prezzie to one of the following Habbos depending on who you are after: DateWithLuna or DateWithKedo. These are 100% legitimate Luna and Kedo clones who will drop the presents into a room for Luna and Kedo to browse through.

Make sure to send your prezzie to either DateWithLuna or DateWithKedo by midnight (PST) tonight to be eligible for Love Test #2. All Love Test winners will be announced on Friday, October 6 at the TGIF festivities. To read more about the Win a Date contest click here.
