Love Test #3: Puppy Love

The third and final Love Test doesn’t really have much to do with Luna or Kedo at all! It’s all about their beloved dogs, Socrates and Shotzi. See, Luna and Kedo both love their pups, which is why they can’t stand the thought of them staying home alone while they are out on their dates. So, Kedo and Luna got together and decided to make the last Love Test all about the dogs…after all, whoever they choose to date HAS to be a pet lover.

This Love Test is simple: Luna and Kedo are looking for the dog with the best name, best talents and best look to accompany their dogs during the big date. It might help to know a little bit about their dogs before determining if yours is a good match:

SHOTZI: Shotzi is a female dog who enjoys long walks in the park, playing with bouncy balls and cuddling on the couch with her owner Luna. She likes to smile, munch on marzipan men and hang out with dogs of dark fur.

SOCRATES: Socrates is a male dog who is a little more subdued than Shotzi. He likes taking naps, dreaming and playing dead. He is a big fan of chocolate mice and dogs with girly-colored fur.

If you think your dog would be a good match for your potential dater’s doggy, send an email to either DATE FOR SHOTZI or DATE FOR SOCRATES with all of the following information:

  • Your Habbo Name
  • Your Dog’s Name
  • Your Dog’s Talents
  • A Screenshot of you and your dog in one of your guest rooms. We will only look at entries with screenshots attached. Please make sure your name is displayed in your screenshot as “room owner” so we know it is actually YOUR dog.

If you find a playmate for Shotzi, you will be the final contestant competing for a date with Luna, and if you find Socrates’s soulmate, you will be contestant #3 for a date with Kedo. You only have until 11:59 PST tonight to submit your email and screenshot. All Love Test winners will be announced tomorrow, October 6, at TGIF.

Time to stop promoting yourselves and start hyping your dogs!
