Love Test #1: Room of Love Design Competition

Wanna impress Kedo and Luna? An innovative room design with your potential date's interests in mind is probably the easiest way to do it! That’s why Love Test #1 is all about design skills.

To get started on this competition, head over to Luna’s Lair or Kedo’s First Big Room and read the stickies on the wall to find out about their likes and dislikes. Once you have those committed to memory, start designing and don’t stop till you’ve created a room that will rock their sox off. We’ve also re-released the entire line of Love Furni back into the catalogue, so make sure to incorporate that mushy gushiness in there as well…this is a DATE you’re trying to win after all.

When you’re done, submit a screenshot of your room along with your Habbo name to us! To submit a room for Kedo click here or to submit a room for Luna click here. You can also include a SHORT description of what the room includes if you choose. All entries are due by tonight, 10/3 at 11:59pm PST. Any entries submitted after this time will not be ooked at.

In addition to winning a spot on the Dating Game where you will compete for the actual date with Kedo and Luna, the two winners of Love Test #1 will also win 50 Habbo coins and a mystery prezzie from their favorite staff member.

Now stop daydreaming about long walks on the beach and candlelit dinners and start designing!
