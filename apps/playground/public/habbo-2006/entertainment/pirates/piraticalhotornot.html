Pirate-Hot or Not!



Sure you know how to design a great room. Of course you are the master of disaster at navigating through mazes. But let’s talk about the really important stuff… are you Pirate-Hot or Not?! We’ve seen a lot of crews walking around the hotel, and some wickedly crafty ones at that. But who’s got the best-looking crew? We’ve hand-picked 15 crews to help us answer this mind-boggling question…

Yep, you got it. This competition is all about aesthetic flair. Got a hunky first mate? Think your cook is super sexy? Let’s see if the other Habbo pirates agree. Here’s how the competition’s gonna go down: as you see below, we’ve selected 15 crew applications and room screenshots that we think are totally H-O-T. Now, we want YOU to vote for the crew that makes you do a double take and ask yourself, “who are those fine-lookin’ pirates?”, “how do I sign up for THAT crew”, and so on. Simple as that.

One word of advice, me hearties…if your crew was lucky enough to make it to the final 15, you better not be a jerk around the hotel cause then no one’s gonna vote for you, no matter how cute you are. Even though this contest is about appearances, what’s on the inside can make or break you. ;)

The crew that recieves the most amount of votes will be given 1 month free Habbo Club as well as 10 Habbo coins each.
