To Me, My Habbo Pirate Crews!



I Kedo, the Manager of Habbo US, with this Letter of Marque declare open an official and sanctioned buccaneer-hunt.

The Habbo calling himself Captain Murphy and his crew of Pirates - Murphy’s Law - have declared war on Habbo. Therefore I ask YOU, brave Habbos, to walk the path of the Pirate and wage a war of wit and skill against this evil scum, whose only aim is to destroy Habbo and all it stands for.

Be Habbo Pirates, form a Crew of four Pirates and enlist in the hunt for Murphy. The prize is glory and gold, plenty of both actually!

But remember, only Pirate Crews of four members can participate in the Pirate Quests. No more, no less. No clones are allowed, of course.

Here’s how to create and register a Pirate Crew:

  1. Log in to Habbo Hotel and find 3 friends to form your Crew with.
  2. Decide who is the Captain, then fill up all the roles: Captain, First Mate, Fighting Crewman/woman and Cook.
  3. Decide on your Crew’s name, story, codeword and/or motto and so on (see below).
  4. Finally, whoever you choose as Captain must copy and paste the application form below in a new e-mail, fill it up and send the form to: habbopirates.us@sulake.com. Only the captain should submit this form!

RULES
The e-mail must be sent from the Captain’s legal Habbo e-mail address. The same Habbo or Captain cannot belong to more than one crew. If a Habbo appears as Captain or member in 2 different valid Crew applications, the latest one will be considered the valid one. The applications can be freely sent whenever a new crew is formed, but they will be registered only once per week.. A new Crew cannot participate to a competition which had already started on the day the application had been sent.

Also, in case you haven’t heard, we’re throwing a celebration of Habbo Piracy, so be sure to show your bravery and compete for more cool prizes!

- Kedo
