Living Jolly Roger



As the official symbol for bobba and all things bobba related, the skull is an important sign of self-expression here at Habbo. Because the skull says so much without saying anything at all, we’ve decided to honor its silence with a competition. We want you and your Habbo friends to use yourselves and your furni to physically express the shape of the skull! Plus, let’s throw in some crossbones -- it is Habbo Pirate season after all!

Introducing… the Living Jolly Roger competition! All you have to do to win is get your friends together and figure out how to form yourselves into a skull and crossbones formation, just like the one that appears on our beloved Jolly Roger! This is a chance for some serious teamwork, whether you’re on a pirate crew or not.

Once you’ve figured out how to re-create the Jolly Roger in your own way, take a screenshot and send it over to habbopirates.us@sulake.com, with “JOLLY ROGER” in the subject line. Don’t forget to include your Habbo Name along with the Habbo names of everyone else in the Screenshot so we know who to send the prizes to!

We’ll be judging the screenshots based on likeness to the flag, creativity and unique personal touches. The winner will be a very “jolly” Habbo when he/she receives 1 free month of HC, 25 coins and a pirate badge. Everyone else who appears in the winning screenshot will get 20 coins as well. A fine loot, me thinks!

All screenshots are due by Thursday, July 27 . Good luck!
