The Flying Tubman




Dead men tell no tales...or do they?

Who’s got some crazy mad furni skillz? The Flying Tubman, that’s who. Legend has it that he and Captain Murphy (yep, THE Captain Murphy) were once rival pirates both sharing an obsession for rare furni. The Flying Tubman loved furni so much he created a unique flying tub. He used to float around the hotel, showing off his completely personalized Rare. Unfortunately, that’s a big no-no, so he was banished from the hotel for unpermitted furni engineering, never to return again…

Until now. He’s back and he’s angry. He can’t stand Captain Murphy and he’ll do whatever it takes to foil his evil plot. The bummer is he has to live the life of a ghost ‘cause of what went down with the Habbo staff long ago. He now sails his tub unseen in search of revenge on all pirates and buccaneers.

That’s right, ALL pirates and buccaneers. The Flying Tubman is a ghost and is therefore jealous of all living Habbos. He knows many things about Captain Murphy and the stolen Rare, but he’s tricky. For fun he likes to play with the Habbos and may not always tell the truth when asked for help. So, we offer you these words of warning: Beware the Flying Tubman! He may prove helpful in your quests, but get too close or say the wrong thing and the Flying Tubman could lead you astray…

Mysteriously a new room has appeared at the hotel called The Black Bayou. We found a sticky with this strange limerick:

My spirit is the strongest on days that start the week.
Check the pacific pixel seas when the sun is at its peak.

I stay where I lay, where I drink, and where I take a dip
Speak with me at 4, or was it 3, to hear a special tip.

I hear whispers brought by the sea, listen if you dare.
You’ll soon find out the true location of the stolen Rare.

We think it might be a clue to when and where the Flying Tubman may appear. Prove you're no lilly-livered landlubber by deciphering the message, finding the Tubman and getting the right clues.
