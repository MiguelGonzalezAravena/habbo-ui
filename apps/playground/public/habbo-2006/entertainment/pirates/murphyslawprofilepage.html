Meet Murphy's Law



Wanna know more about the enemy? Check out Murphy's Law's personal pirate profiles here!

Captain-Murphy ~ Crooked.Cook ~ First Mate ~ Fighting Crewman



Captain-Murphy

Headline: The Sadist

About Me: I love the sound of screams. I love destruction! Me and my crew, the Law, lord over the seas. I’ll grind yer bones and pepper my stew with it. U wanna mess with me? Go ahead and try it… see what happens. Please? *evil grin*

I'd Like to Meet: Rich folks, Habbos with lots of rares who want to trade (yeah, “trade”, right…), pirate crewmen with elbow grease, and a hottie mermaid with seashells made of pearls. C’mon, pirates get lonely too.

Interests: The rack, a freshly oiled plank, treasure (mine, mine, all mine!!!!), sharks, cartography, skeleton keys, plundering, astronomy, and my spyglass collection.

Music: Anything metal or hardcore! System of a Down, Korn, Metallica, Atreyu, Rob Zombie

Movies: Friday the 13th, The Shining, Texas Chainsaw Massacre, Misery, In the Mouth of Madness, Silence of the Lambs.

Television: Too busy thieving and making people miserable to stop to watch much television. I am slightly addicted to Fear Factor and the Apprentice, tho.

Books: You mean those paper things? Good for one thing: firewood. Bah!

Heroes: Me. I’m the king! Everyone should worship Me.

 



Crooked.Cook

Headline: The Executioner

About Me: I can de-bone and fillet any creature in 2 minutes flat. Thanks be to the captain for giving me all the lovely dirty work to do. I take my work very seriously. There’s nothing better than slicing and dicing. Wear a red apron and you can barely see the stains.

I'd Like to Meet: The creator of Cutco, an Iron Chef.

Interests: Blood, dark water, sharks, thyme- (it’s such a versatile spice!), dirty socks, a rusty guillotine, black hoods, eyeballs, and my rubber duckie Burt.

Music: Wolfmother, She Wants Revenge, Interpol, Muse, and Portishead

Movies: Jaws, Leviathan, Akira, Edward Scissorhands, The Last Supper, Pit and the Pendulum, and the Dark Crystal

Television: Anything on the Food network, Martha Stewart Living, Space Ghost, Shark Week

Books: Ten Ways to Skin a Cat, Peter Pan, Decapitation and You, Cape Cod Cooking Secrets, Torture: the Brutal Truth, the Very Hungry Caterpillar, and the Old Man and the Sea.

Heroes: Henry VIII

 



First Mate

Headline: The Schemer

About Me: Some say it’s hard being a girl on a pirate ship, but *I* love it! I love the ocean, prolly ‘cause I’m a water sign. And yeah, uhm, my boss is like soooo cool, even if he is kinda psycho. Don’t EVER tell him I said that. He totally knows that I’m like the smartest person in the entire crew. That’s why *I* write up all the plans. Girls can do anything boys can do… only better! ;)

I'd Like to Meet: Kiera Knightly, George Clooney, Oprah, or maybe Madonna. I'm also looking for a special pirate to be bf.

Interests: Piracy! Sudoku, pedicures, spiders, eye patches, games like Battleship and Connect Four, cotton candy, and anything aquatic like star fish, sea horses, jellyfish, or dolphins! Gotta love dolphins.

Music: The Monkees, Paris Hilton (even tho she sucks, I still think she’s pretty), Ladytron, Cindy Lauper, Freezepop, Pink, Shiny Toy Guns

Movies: Princess Bride, The Hunt for Red October, Cocoon, The Little Mermaid, and omg I LOVE Titanic. *Sigh*… Leo.

Television: Powerpuff girls, Bill Nye the Science Guy, the Discovery Channel, and Kim Possible

Books: Profiles of Female Genius: Thirteen Creative Women Who Changed the World, Under the Black Flag, Complete Idiot’s Guide to Spies and Espionage.

Heroes: Hilary Clinton

 



Crewman

Headline: The Muscle

About Me: I eat a lot of food and work out all the time. Mmmm muscles good. Weight lifting good. I do whatever my boss tells me to do. Want me to push someone around? Need a punk banned from a room? No problem. You should see my delts. They’re spectacular.

I'd Like to Meet: the UFC champion, Jack Lalanne, and maybe a pretty lassie who can see through my rough exterior

Interests: Pumping you up! Bench pressing, beating up noobs, creatine, brass knuckles, kick wars, spitting gum in ur hair, getting crizzle with my crew

Music: Old Michael Jackson, Beyonce, Ice Cube, Eminem, Busta Rhymes

Movies: Raging Bull, White Men Can’t Jump, Boyz N the Hood, Jerry Maguire, Rocky, Friday

Television: ESPN, and I like to watch fights on Pay-per-view.

Books: Confessions of a Body Builder, Bullying 101

Heroes: Arnold Schwarzenegger, Mike Tyson
