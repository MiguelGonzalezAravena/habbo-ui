Build the Best Pirate Ship!


Everybody knows that pirates are just about the coolest guys out there. I mean, what’s more fun than hooks for hands, eye patches, and buried treasure? Designing pirate ships, that’s what!

Let’s see what mad pirate skills you guys have. We’re looking for best room-building buccaneer who can create the baddest and most believable pirate ship. The winner will receive treasures found in Captain Murphy’s latest plunder as well as lots of other cool pirate stuff.

The best part is, you don’t have to be part of a crew to play in this competition. Any Habbo can create a pirate ship and win the loot! We know you’re no scallywag, so brush up on your design skills and let’s see what’cha got.

Pirate Ship Blueprints:

Must be a BIG room. You’ll need enough room for a pirate crew, a poop deck, prisoners, the plank, and anything thing else you'll need for an awesome ship!

Must be in pirate theme. This means Lodge furni, Pirate Swag (available now for your ship building convenience) wooden floors and lots and lots of Jolly Rogers!

Oh, and don’t forget to create your room on the Seven Pixel Seas floor. That’s where all the pirate ships during this competition will be docked.

The winner will be judged on design of room AND creativity of ship name. After all, every pirate ship needs a name. Arrr, she’s going to be a beautiful lassie, and she’s gonna need a beauty of a name. The winning room will be featured on the homepage and the winning Habbo designer will receive 1 free month of HC, 25 coins to build up their treasure, and a special pirate badge.

Here’s how to enter: If you are not on a crew or are submitting your own ship, send a screenshot of you (and any of your crew) in your room to habbopirates.us@sulake.com with the subject "SHIP". Make sure you remember to send us your Habbo name and the name of your pirate ship.

If you are submitting one pirate ship for your whole crew, only the CAPTAIN should submit the screenshot (even if he/she does not own the ship room) with the entire crew on the ship, along with the pirate ship name, his/her Habbo name and the crew name and codeword/motto (please specify that the entire crew helped build the ship and if your ship gets selected, everyone on your crew will win the prize). Change the subject line to "CREW SHIP" as well.

Do you have what it takes to design the best pirate ship ever?! Aye, ye scurvy dog! We know ya do. So drop that anchor, screw on your peg leg and build your pirate ship from mast to end. Don’t forget to assemble your crew and man the ship as Captain. Do us proud or ya might find yerself feeding the fishes.

All entries are due by 11:59pm PST on Sunday, July 16. Time be wastin', get to it!
