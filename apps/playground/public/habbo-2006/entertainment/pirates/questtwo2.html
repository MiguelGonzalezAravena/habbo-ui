Save my Pet from the Scallywag!



Ahoy, my Habbo Pirates!

First of all, many thanks to all among you who fought hard and dirty to discover where the stolen Rares had been hidden by Captain Murphy! The fearless Black-Bobbles were the first to uncover the elegant Doric Classic Pillars and report their location. The Rares are now safe and in the Catalogue, and there they will stay for the rest of this week.

But enough with the celebrations. I have bad news.

That eye-patched scallywag did it again. Over the weekend, while I was enjoying my well-deserved rest, he kidnapped my Socrates. My little Socrates is gone!

What? Who is Socrates?

Ah, sorry, let me back up...Socrates is my pet dog. The FIRST Habbo Pet, to be precise. He holds in his code the original source code for all Habbo Pet Dogs. And yes, in case you haven’t noticed ALL the Pet Dogs have disappeared from the Catalogue. The reason is simple: the Dogs are on strike.

Until we recover their king and idol, no more pets will be generated by our mighty and almost-never-failing database. And no, no negotiations are possible. As you might have noticed during your Habbo experience, our system can be quite stubborn at times.

But this is not the worst. I also got this sticky note on my wall:

Ahoy again, you lazy and careless Manager!

I guess you’ve never heard the wise old saying “Guard your own goods”.

While you were so busy asking the Habbos to find your little Rares, we kidnapped your mangy little critter. Child’s play, really…almost makes me ashamed of having used my crew and my secret tricks for that.

But anyway, to the point.

I’ve got your “original” pet, and I know what it holds in its code. My Crooked.Cook is “studying” it right now, and he is almost imploring me to let me hand it over to him to hand over to the sharks, if you know what I’m saying.

I feel quite merciful today, but even mercy has a price. Me and my crew want Staff-level rights on Habbo…you have one week to convince the Habbo Big Bosses. After that, your dog will be fed to the sharks.

I wonder if dogs are good at walking the plank…

Arrr!

- Captain Murphy and his Law

This Habbo is insane! Dangerously insane.

Me hearties, the task at hand is clear: find where my dog is held captive and thwart this ruthless captain’s plan!

Get your Pirate Crews together and save Socrates and I’ll cover you with gold! In case you haven’t yet heard about the official “Call to Piracy” I have issued, read my Letter of Habbo Marque. There you can learn how to form a Pirate Crew as well.

The Prizes!
The FIRST Pirate Crew to submit the correct answers to this week’s Quest will win a 1 month free HC subscription, a permanent Pirate Badge and 50 Habbo Credits. Among all the other participants, I will randomly select 2 additional crews who have answered correctly to win a free one month HC subscription.

I also remind you we’re throwing a celebration of Habbo Piracy, so be sure to show your bravery and compete for more cool prizes!

Good luck, my Habbo Pirates. Bring my Socrates home safe and sound!

- Kedo
