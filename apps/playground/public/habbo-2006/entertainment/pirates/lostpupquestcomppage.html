The Mystery of the Stolen Dog


Stolen Dog Quest Competition Page

Ahoy, my Pirates!The problem is simple. Where did Captain Murphy hide my pet dog Socrates? And what’s the password to the room where he’s being held captive?

Use your expertise in everything Habbo and search in every hidden nook and cranny of the hotel: surely Murphy and his Law have left behind some traces!

It is also said that the mysterious Flying Tubman is hot on Murphy’s trail. If you see him around, be sure to ask his help… if you dare!

And don’t forget to stop by Davy Jones’ Locker for refreshments to refuel you and your crew as you search for Socrates. The server there welcomes all pirates, and is known to gossip…

Solve the mystery of Socrates’ tragic kidnapping by filling in the answers below!

NOTE! Only the Crew Captains are allowed to send the answers. The Habbo name entered at the bottom of the form MUST match that of the listed Crew's Captain or the answers will be discarded. To find out how to register a crew, click here.
