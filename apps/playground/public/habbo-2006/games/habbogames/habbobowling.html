Habbo Bowling with Amymone!

After two rounds of voting, Amymone won first place in GameStop’s “Invent a Game” challenge! I met up with her and her sister LexieBelle to get a tour of her winning Bowling Alley and chat about how Habbo Bowling came about. Read on for more and come to our GameStop "Invent a Game" victory party in Amymone's Bowling Alley TODAY, 11/17 at 3pm PST!


Amymone: Welcome to the Bowling Alley!
Luna: Hey you two!
Amymone: :D It's a honor to have you here!
Luna: It’s an honor to be here! Can you tell me more about the bowling alley?
Amymone: I created this bowling alley because it's a game anyone can play...at anytime. It's simple and I think it's fun. xD LexieBelle helped me make it. She encouraged me to enter it.
Luna: Cool! Do you bowl in real life?
Amymone: Sometimes I do. But I don't have much opportunity.
Luna: LexieBelle, do you bowl?
LexieBelle: Sometimes, in real life, all the time on Habbo. :D
Luna: lol, well of course! Do you know of any other bowling allies in Habbo? I've only seen this one.
Amymone: I saw one in the Classifieds the other day. I'm proud that this game's catching on.
Luna: Did you check it out at all?
Amymone: I did. :D
Luna: Was it like yours or different?
Amymone: It was similar.
Luna: Cool, you've definitely started a trend!
Amymone: :D
Luna: I have to admit, I'm not such a fan of bowling in real life, cause every time I bowl I break like all my nails. Sounds lame but its true :) Plus, I'm not very good.
Amymone: lol Have you tried Habbo Bowling? xD We have smaller balls on Habbo. xD
Luna: I've NEVER tried Habbo bowling!
LexieBelle: D:
Amymone: Would you like to play a game? :D
Luna: Sure, I'd love to!
Amymone: You stand in a lane, the scoring stickie is...the purple one by my head. xD
LexieBelle: I'll keep score for you. :D
Amymone: You go first. :D


Luna: *We bowl, and after rolling several 3’s, I lose* gg!
Amymone: Good game indeed! I'll put your name on the score stickie. :D
Luna: That was fun! And all my nails are in tact!
Amymone: Sin? and Styrophobical helped with the scoring system and the rules.
Luna: Nice! What is the highest score anyone's ever gotten?
Amymone: 96.
Luna: Whoa, lucky Habbo, who was it?
Amymone: :.:BMX:.: was the highest scorer.
Luna: nice, go BMX! *Heads over to the snack bar for some grub*
LexieBelle: How can I help you?
Amymone: Rent a snack, eat a shoe! xD
Luna: lol great motto!
Amymone: lol Thanks.
LexieBelle: Would you like a cheese burger and fries?
Luna: lol I want a corndog please…with lots of mustard.
LexieBelle: Sure thing.
Luna: SWEET
Amymone: lol
LexieBelle: *Serves corndog and lots mustard* xD Would you like a Habbo cola?
Luna: yes please! Diet please, I'm watching my figure, hence the fried hotdog!
LexieBelle: :D *Gives Diet Habbo cola*
Amymone: Our special in the snack bar is now Luna's CornDog.
Luna: Thanks!
LexieBelle: You're welcome!
Luna: The corndog is delish by the way…


Luna: So what other Habbo games do you 2 enjoy playing?
Amymone: Battleball! I love Battleball. I can't wait to play it with my new tickets. I totaled my last mouse playing Battleball. I played 6 hours straight.
Luna: LOL wow, a true gamer!
Amymone: :D I'm so grateful to Habbo, EB Games and GameStop for this chance to share this game.
Luna: Yay!!!! Have you gotten any Prepaid Cards from there?
Amymone: Not from there yet but I have from CVS. I'm excited about the new cards coming out.
Luna: Did you see the new styles from CVS? I'm on one of them! lol
Amymone: Awesome! I'm going to run out and get it tomorrow. I have a whole set of the first collection.
Luna: Sweet :)I'm also on one of the GameStop cards.
Amymone: We have a GameStop in town as well as a CVS. It's a great way to buy coins.
Luna: Totally
Amymone: I can't wait to get the gold and silver rollers and lazer gate for this room!
Luna: The gold and silver rollers are out of the catty!
Amymone: Oh well, I'll go trade. xD


Luna: So, I have to ask, how long have you guys been playing Habbo?
Amymone: I started when it first opened in the UK for a short while. We rejoined Habbo back in January of 2006. And I'm here to stay.
Luna: Great, its wonderful to have you 2 here.
Amymone: x3
Luna: Your game is really outstanding and I hope lots of Habbos come in here to play :D
Amymone: They do. :D And We have a lot of fun and memories here. We love playing Habbo because it's safe and fun.
Luna: :D Both wonderful reasons. Any outs before I go.
Amymone: Yeah! :D To LexieBelle, my real life sister and best friend to Steady and all my friends at `H`. And all my friends on Habbo. :D And Thanks to all the voters!
Luna: Yep, gotta give some love to the voters. hehe
Amymone: I want to thank all who voted. I couldn't have done it without them! This room will always be here for all to enjoy.
Luna: Oh, I meant to ask...do you have bowling leagues here?
Amymone: We've started Bowling Leagues. :D
Luna: How do you sign up?
Amymone: Put your name on one of these stickies back here above the love sofa. Pick a league and add your name. :D
Luna: That is too cool.
Amymone: :D
Luna: Well, thanks for the interview both of you!
Amymone: Thank YOU Miss Luna!
LexieBelle: Thanks Miss Luna!
Amymone: Thanks for stopping in! :D
LexieBelle: Bye!
Amymone: Bye!! :D
Luna: Bye!
