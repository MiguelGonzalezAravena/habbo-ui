Owner: Sixty2

BUT, there is one Holiday timetable that remains the same every year… Christmas tree shopping begins the week after Thanksgiving! That’s where our Staff Pick of the Week comes in…within the first 5 minutes of visiting Sixty2’s Christmas Trees For SALE! room, he asked me if I wanted to purchase a tree.
