Week of 08/19/07

Hey Habbos!

We are really excited to bring you the third week of HabboScopes! The positive response from you guys has been great so we're going to continue HabboScopes for a long time! This week we present Habbo Horoscopes and Habbo Pet Horoscopes but look out for a special jam-packed edition next week!


Habbo Horoscopes

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
Being optimistic is one of the major traits of Aries. Optimism can cause you to share your deepest secrets this week with people you truly trust. Remember, Aries have a very good judge of character so if you're comfortable with someone you have no problem sharing secrets with them!

Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
Your friends and family may be depending on you this week. The sign of Taurus can be very caring but your temperamental ways makes you only give so much without expecting something in return.

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
Fun is not what you need at this time. This upcoming week will be a stressful one but if you buckle down and take everything one step at a time you'll get by without any emotional or physical exhaustion.

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
Get ready to expect the unknown. Family matters this week can be on the rise but something will change the rifting ties.
Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
HAPPY BIRTHDAY, LEO! This week Leos, you will need at least one day to yourself. Work, family and friends are adding extra stress, so taking one day to yourself will make you Zen and happy.
Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Virgos have a problem with expressing their feelings toward someone. Even if you have the strongest feelings possible for someone they may not be able to see it because of your calm exterior. Try expressing more and more to that person you care for the most.
Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
You are the balance in the Zodiac. You're one that's not for confrontation but you do look at that both sides of a fight to find a clear and equal solution.
Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
This week the sun sets on your power planet Pluto, which means some mysteries of your life, will be revealed.
Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
This week is not such a great one for you. Your pessimistic trait is leaking out and you are expecting everyone is out to get you. Just relax and hang out with your friends at the pool deck and everything will work out fine!
Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
Relationships are in turmoil for you this week. Whether it's friendships or lovers, you are not very lucky in that area this week.
Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
Powerful desires are flooding over you this week. You either want a promotion or what a new love. Aquarians tend to know what they want and when they want it so if you know what you want, go get it!
Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
Pisces are hard to please but great when you please them! This week Pisces' desire for satisfaction is great and they won't stop at anything until they reach it!

 


Horoscopes
Habbo Cats:
Your cat may be feeling a little ill this week. Make sure you care for it and feed it a lot you don't want it to be sick forever!
Habbo Dogs:
"Pixels Best Friends" are not so friendly this week. Leave them to themselves and they'll be happy!
Habbo Crocodiles:
Crocs are very hungry this week! Make sure you buy some extra food to keep them happy!


Well that's it for the third edition of HabboScopes! We hope you guys enjoyed them! Remember, HabboScopes will be in the Entertainment section every week and published on Sundays!

Have a great week Habbos!
