Week of 08/12/07

WideRuledPaper & Loderse

Hey Habbos!

Welcome to HabboScopes week two! We are really excited to bring you our second edition! Thanks again for all of the feedback that you guys sent in last week; it really helped with making this edition! This week you'll find Habbo Horoscopes and Habbo Pet Horoscopes! Hope you enjoy!


Habbo Horoscopes

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
With the Leo New Moon appearing this week Aries are very creative. This isn't that unexpected with the spontaneous rams but your creativity will be kicked up a notch or two! Maybe you can use that creativity to create a new room or help a friend design their room!

 Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
Expression is a Libra's worst enemy. Libras can be secretive with their true feelings but if you express yourself to your friends and loved ones you'll be better! This week Libras are all about nature! So pick up some plants or volunteer at your local park to help pick up liter! The humanitarian in you will be satisfied!
Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
Similar to the Aries, the New Moon affects you also this week. The moon is falling into your 4th House of Roots, which means you must deepen your emotional connection with your home, family, and yourself. Remember to keep in touch with your friends over your Habbo console! If you do this you'll be a happier Taurus.		 Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
Scorpios can be very timid people but this coming week won't satisfy your like of personal privacy. You may be pushed into a social situation that you don't like or don't agree with. Use your common sense and you'll be okay!

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
Geminis are very helpful this week. Make sure you stop by the Welcome Lounge and help some of the newer Habbo players!

 Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
Sagittarians are great friends this week! You'll either help your friends out or solve some personal problems. Make sure you stay close to your friends because you may need them later!

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
Financial issues may be a problem this week. Watch your trading and spending this week or you could find yourself in a bit of trouble!

 Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
You're very goal oriented this week. Either you're planning for the new school year or desiring a hire position at your job. Work out plans to fulfill your goals properly!
Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
HAPPY BIRTHDAY, LEO!
This week will be a good week for Leos. Since your New Moon is rising things are looking up for you. There may be some issues with your job but everything else is looking up!		 Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
You'll be unR-A-V-Eling with some situations that are just confusing! Make sure you think about all of the possible ways that you can solve this odd situation.
Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Virgos are having an okay week. Last week their naivety was a problem but this week they're their own worst enemies. Virgos can sometimes be very critical of themselves but if you learn how to step back and appreciate what you have and who you are; you'll find yourself a happier Virgo!		 Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
You're astrological opposite is Virgo. Do not get into any friendships or relationships with Virgos because you are dooming yourself to a very unhappy time.

 


Horoscopes

Habbo Dogs:
Dogs want to have companionship this week. Maybe buy your dog a friend or pet it just a little more! If you stimulate your dog with friendship it'll be a happier dog!
Habbo Cats:
With having a week of leisure last week; Cats are very hyper and active this week! Make sure you feed them a little extra, all this activity will make their appetite rise!

Habbo Crocodiles:
The Croc's temper has calmed a bit and it's ready to play and have fun! Make sure you buy it an extra toy so that it can have even more fun!


That's it for the second edition of HabboScopes! Remember, HabboScopes has it's own section now inside the Entertainment section! Be sure to look for them every week!

Hope you liked them! Have a great week Habbos!

WideRuledPaper & Loderse
