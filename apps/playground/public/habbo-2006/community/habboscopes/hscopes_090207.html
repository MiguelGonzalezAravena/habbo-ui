Week of 09/02/07

Howdy Habbos!

Did you think with all this birthday excitement there wouldn't be HabboScopes? THINK AGAIN! We're back for our fifth week! We'd like to thank everyone who shows off his or her pride by joining the HabboScopes fan club! Remember, it's still open and has plenty of space for EVERYONE to join! With all this birthday excitement we've chosen to keep the Relationship meter for one more week but add a new section to it! This will be the LAST WEEK you'll see the relationship meter so be sure to check it out! Remember, if you have any suggestions/comments for HabboScopes be sure to sign the HabboScopes fan club guest book! We actually read them! Enjoy this edition, Habbos!



Habbo Horoscopes
& Relationship Meter

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
Aries are not having such a great week because their planet leader Mars is stressed by unpredictable Uranus. Aries tend to like structure, while the planet Uranus promotes no structure! Just lighten up a little Aries and you'll have a better week!

Most compatible for friendship: Gemini
Most compatible for love: Sagittarius
The worst sign for you: Libra

Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
A Taurus is happy when everything in their life appears perfect. When their relationships, work and school are all going well; that's when they're at their peak of happiness. Just remember Taurians, it takes a lot of work to get the “perfect” life, when it's not so perfect don't get mad, work more on your relationships with loved ones.

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Capricorn
The worst sign for you: Scorpio

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
Being a Gemini, you usually have a hard time expressing your feelings about someone. This week your lead planet Mercury is being shadowed by Pluto, which will cause you to reveal some inner secrets that are buried way down in your conscious. Be sure to not hold back your feelings this week!

Most compatible for friendship: Aries
Most compatible for love: Leo
Worst sign for you: Sagittarius

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
Long time goals are your obsessions your week. Whether it's what college you want to go to, or even becoming a Habbo X! Whatever it is, you're obsessed!

Most compatible for friendship: Virgo
Most compatible for love: Pisces
Worst sign for you: Capricorn
Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
You're on your way to success in either a career or school! Leos are extremely lucky this week whether it be a promotion or an A on your history paper! Something good regarding school or work is going to happen!

Most compatible for friendship: Libra
Most compatible for love: Gemini
Worst sign for you: Aquarius
Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Happy Birthday, Virgo!
Virgos are so restless that something will be changing this week that's major in your life. Virgos have trouble settling in one place so you will be faced with a decision the may just change your life.

Most compatible for friendship: Taurus
Most compatible for love: Scorpio
Worst sign for you: Pisces
Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
Libras are going to be dragged into other people's drama this week. You just have to step back and let your fair behavior take control and you'll find yourself a happier Libra.

Most compatible for friendship: Leo
Most compatible for love: Sagittarius
Worst sign for you: Aries
Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
Scorpios may be attacked this week. You tend to be vulnerable and care what others thing of you. Turn this off this week or you may be facing some emotional hurt.

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Capricorn
Worst sign for you: Taurus

Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
Saggitarians are very stubborn people but this week you open your mind to something new and it's very surprising. Learning that going out of your normal ways may be fun you'll be a better Saggitarian and person.

Most compatible for friendship: Scorpio
Most compatible for love: Virgo
Worst sign for you: Gemini
Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
Your main planet Saturn is entering liberal Virgo today. Meaning that you making decisions will be easier on you this week and you may actually follow through with some!

Most compatible for friendship: Taurus
Most compatible for love: Scorpio
Worst sign for you: Cancer
Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
You're about to enter or work on a relationship. Remember, as an Aquarian having a steady and healthy relationship is important to you. Remember this goes both ways, you may need to actually do some of the work this time!

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Aries
Worst sign for you: Leo
Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
You may need to defend your outspoken behavior against someone you would never expect to challenge your thoughts. Remember, facts are your forte and debating is one of your specialties! Don't hurt them to bad!

Most compatible for friendship: Scorpio
Most compatible for love: Cancer
Worst sign for you: Virgo

 


Horoscopes

Cats are taking more catnaps then usual! Just let have their time to themselves and they'll maintain happy!
Habbo Dogs:
