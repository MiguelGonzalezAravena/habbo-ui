Week of 08/26/07

Hey Habbos!

Loderse and I hope you’re enjoying your week and loving the new Japanese competitions! With the overwhelming success of HabboScopes these past weeks has made us want to do a very special edition! You may be used to just seeing Habbo Horoscopes and Habbo Pet Horoscopes but this week, along with the Habbo Horoscopes will be a special relationship meter! In this part we’ll tell you which other sign is your best bet as a friend and which one is your best bet as a lover. When you’re done reading this week’s HabboScopes be sure to join the new HabboScopes fan club! Join today!


Habbo Horoscopes & Relationship Meter

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
This week a serious problem at work or school can occur. If not attended to, this problem can stop you from reaching your goals in either work or school.

Most compatible for friendship: Gemini
Most compatible for love: Sagittarius

Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
As a Taurus you have a high level of intelligence but an even higher level of naivety. This week make sure you listen to yourself and your own advice instead of others. If you depend on others, you’ll find yourself in to much trouble then you can even bare!

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Capricorn

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
As a Gemini you tend to over extend yourself in work or school. A person can only take so much before you crack! This week try to relax and prioritize, remember relationships are just as important as work or school! Without your steady relationship between your family and friends you won’t be a happy Gemini!

Most compatible for friendship: Aries
Most compatible for love: Leo

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
The Capricorn Moon is affecting you more then Capricorns! Your more serious side is coming out this week and you may not be able to express your real feelings! Remember, if you have something to say, say it!

Most compatible for friendship: Virgo
Most compatible for love: Pisces
Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
With your birthday passing by you have a clean slate this year! You may need to improve on some past wounds that you’ve created but remember, by patching up a wounded relationship you get back one amazing friend!

Most compatible for friendship: Libra
Most compatible for love: Gemini
Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Happy Birthday, Virgo!
While Leos are patching up their wounded relationships, Virgos need to explore and get some more relationships! Virgos tend to stay inside the same group of friends and date the same type of people but the fact of the matter is, if you venture out you can only find greatness!

Most compatible for friendship: Taurus
Most compatible for love: Scorpio
Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
Normally, Libras are the most balanced and fair sign of the zodiac but this week that all can change. If you don’t pay attention to the agreements you are going into either verbal or written, something bad can happen to you or the person who you made the deal with! Put aside your normal fair behavior and really pay attention!

Most compatible for friendship: Leo
Most compatible for love: Sagittarius
Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
You’re highly productive this week! Remember your typical lazy behavior goes bye bye as you come into a week that you have so much energy you won’t know what to do with yourself!

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Capricorn
Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
Sagittarians are normally very optimistic and goal oriented but with Mars becoming your ruling planet this week, you’re tempted to set aside goals and have some fun! Remember, fun is important but goals get you places in life!

Most compatible for friendship: Scorpio
Most compatible for love: Virgo
Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
You’re about to face a lot of choices. Make sure you really think about which way you want to go because your decision may even change your life as you know it.

Most compatible for friendship: Taurus
Most compatible for love: Scorpio
Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
Your imagination is on a roll this week! Anything can spark that creative mind that an Aquarian possesses. This great quality that an Aquarian has will help you in the near future at either work or school. Try learning a new instrument or even a new language because nothing will stop you this week!

Most compatible for friendship: Pisces
Most compatible for love: Aries
Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
Pisces have a tendency to want everything their way. The live by the phrase “my way or the highway” but everything is changing this week. Circumstances will approach that you may not like and have zero control over. The best thing you can do is relax and not overreact!

Most compatible for friendship: Scorpio
Most compatible for love: Cancer

 


Horoscopes
Habbo Cats:
Cats are very loving this week! They want to be petted and played with so make sure you spend as much time with them as you can!
Habbo Dogs:
Your dog is still not in such a great mood. Don’t be surprised if they just lay around and sleep instead of being the normal happy selves.
Habbo Crocodiles:
After all that eating last week, Crocs just want to relax and have some fun! Make sure you buy them an extra chew toy!


Well that’s it for HabboScopes Week 4! Thanks everyone for viewing and we hope you enjoyed it! Remember to join the HabboScopes fan club and to catch HabboScopes posted inside the Entertainment Section on Sundays!

Have a great week, Habbos!
