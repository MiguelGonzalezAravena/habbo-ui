Week of 09/16/07

Howdy y'all!

HabboScopes is now on it's seventh edition! We're so excited. We hope you guys are too! We would like to thank TackyPenguin who came up with this week's add on into the HabboScopes! We're going to do “What Trax fits you best!” Enjoy this edition and remember, look out for something amazing for the tenth edition!

Habbo Horoscopes

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
Aries' are currently dealing with a situation that doesn't seem to be solving it self anytime soon. I suggest you really think about the problem because an immediate solution will not come anytime soon.

What trax suits you best: Mysto Magica

Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
As a Taurus, you like structure and routine but returning to your past routine doesn't seem to be happening which worries you. Try to work on getting things back to normal but it could take up to a couple of weeks to do successfully.

What trax suits you best: Ferry Nultado

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
As a Gemini you're about to make a decision that you've been thinking about for a while. You're decision will make a huge impact in your life so remember to really think it out before you act on it.

What trax suits you best: Rasta.Claus's Pack

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
Your deepest concerns about love, family, or work are going to be brought up this week. Don't worry you'll feel better after all of your secrets are going to be displayed.

What trax suits you best: Sunset Adventure

Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
Your heart is in trouble this week. You may not be able to deal with the disappointments of a break up or a rejection. Don't worry, you're worth a relationship and one may be coming sooner then you expect.

What trax suits you best: Little Tanga Beach

Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Happy Birthday, Virgo!
As your logics are focused on Mercury this week, that means you'll be coming to an agreement with yourself about your desires, wants, or a crush that you've had for a while.

What trax suits you best: A day in the Park

Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
Libras are taking up after Pisces this week in their test of what is reality. Don't worry about it, you need to spend a week in your dreams.

What trax suits you best: Café Muzzakh

Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
Scorpios are having a great week this week. Everything seems to be going well with all of your new responsibilities because of the new school year, enjoy it! Maybe you should go pick up some more Starbucks.

What trax suits you best: Compu FX

Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
You have a habit of working yourself into anxiety because you're trying to handle all of the situations life is throwing at you. Remember to relax this week and everything should get better.

What trax suits you best: Electronica

Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
Capricorns are actually surprisingly calm this week. You're dealing with several situations that are being thrown at you and you're managing to come up with a way to handle them all! Congratulations, enjoy this newfound freedom!

What trax suits you best: Boy Band Sensations

Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
Conflicts are rising this week. You're dealing with some apparent failures or success that you can't seem to understand. Taking personal responsibility will ease this problem and prevent conflicts.

What trax suits you best: Duck Funk

Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
Try not to act on impulses this week. If you refuse to act on impulses that are being given to you by your subconscious mind, then you'll avoid any problems with friends or family that may occur this week.

What trax suits you best: Snow Storm Theme
