Week of 09/09/07

Heya Habbos!

Wow HabboScopes is at it's SIXTH EDITION! We're so surprised on the amount of support we've gotten from you guys! HabboScopes has become a huge apart of the Habbo Community and we owe it all to you!

This edition we're whipping out a special thing on the HabboScopes! It's called the "What's your favorite ice cream flavor". This will only be around for a few editions so be sure to pay attention! Now, these Ice Cream Flavors aren't made up, we analyzed the taste buds of all the different signs and came up with something great! So, here's the Sixth Edition of HabboScopes! Enjoy everyone!

SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT: For the tenth edition there will be some supports of HabboScopes displayed inside the article! More to come on this in the near future!



Habbo Horoscopes

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
Arians interactions with others are not so great this week. You either sound cocky or condescending so Aries just watch what you say to people and remember, not everyone has as thick of skin as you!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Classic Vanilla all the way!

Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
As a Taurus you're always trying to find an easy way out! Either you're scrolling SparkNotes trying to avoid reading Romeo and Juliet or you're running stop signs to make up for your lateness. This week, taking the easy way out won't help you at all so remember to take your time and evaluate everything you do! Great things in life don't come easily!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Chocolate Chip with lots of toppings!

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
Gemini's are not normally artistic but this week that artistic side is shinning and you're loving every day of it! You love knowing that your thinking differently about things to each other and this will immensely help you in school and if you even have a little art project! Have a great time little artists!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Mint Chocolate Chip you like the weirdness of the flavors combined!

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
After stressing out about all of those goals last week, Cancers are taking this week as a "relaxing week". Just remember, even though you think this week is going to be chill doesn't mean you can get behind in school or work!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough!

Habbo Leo:
[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
Leos have overextended themselves for way to long! It's time for you to prioritize! Make sure that you get things done that you have to get done and not just things you're trying to do to make up for everyone else! Leos can be kind hearted when they want to be but don't take it to an extreme that it ends up you as other peoples' doormat.

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Leos are actually not typically big on sweets, you rather have your favorite food then a tub of Ice Cream!

Habbo Virgo:
[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Happy Birthday, Virgo!
Unlike your Cancer friends, Virgos are hyper and working hard this week! Virgos tend to slack off and all of that is catching up to you! It's going to be a week full of energy drinks and all nighters so be prepared!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Chocolate

Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
Desires are having Libras react in ways you normally wouldn't this week. Libras are usually very quiet about their desires but this week you're not! Maybe that cute boy that sits next to you in English or that super hot girl that sits in front of you in Homeroom, you'll find yourself wanting to know them more and even expressing your feelings to them!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Strawberry Shortcake

Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
The sympathetic side will come out this week. You're trapped in a river of your own emotions this week which usually isn't good for any Scorpio. As your protective lay or shrinks so does your confidence. Step back and remember you are a great person, if you don't tell yourself this you could find yourself going into depression.

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Triple Chocolate

Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
Sagittarians are famous for knowing you they are and were they're going, not this week! That supreme confidence that you hold so greatly will shrivel as if you're a slug and someone just poured salt all over you. Remember, you're not superior! If you remember this you'll have a better time of making and keeping friends.

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: You're not a huge fan of ice cream, you rather have some apple pie!

Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]
Distractions, distractions, distractions! That's all Capricorns get this week! You're trying to focus but you just can't seem to do it! Just lay back, and try everything you can do to focus on the task at hand.

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Strawberry

Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]
Aquarians are not having a great week at all. You're experiencing trouble in work and school and solutions just seem out of reach to you. Just remember, everything will eventually get better and you're not a God you can't fix everything!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Coffee

Habbo Pisces:
[Feb 20th - Mar 20th]
Pisces may be going in a new direction in your life. You have a new outlook on your attitude and even your clothing sense! Others may see it as irresponsibility but you see it as your typical dreamer self. Have a great week this week dreamers and remember, others can begin to become jealous of your material way of thinking!

Favorite Ice Cream Flavor: Cookies and Cream (or Oreo ice cream)

 


Horoscopes
Habbo Cats:
Your cats are actually feeling a bit ill this week. Just give them their space and make sure they stay well hydrated so they can beat this little Feline Cold!
Habbo Dogs:
Dogs are still hyper as ever and ready to love life! Make sure you play with your dog more then ever and keeps it's spirits up!
Habbo Crocodiles:
Crocs are done with being relaxed, they're ready to play! Maybe buy your Croc a companion or a new toy so they can enjoy this week even more!


Well that's it for HabboScopes SIXTH EDITION! We hope you guys are as excited as we are to make HabboScopes even better in the near future! Remember, if you have any questions/comments/suggestions be sure to sign the HabboScopes Fan Club guest book! WE DO READ THEM!

Have a fantastic week Habbos! Enjoy all that ice cream you'll be eating!
