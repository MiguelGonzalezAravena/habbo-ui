Habbo Aries:

Favorite Holiday: Thanksgiving

[Apr 21st - May 21st]

Favorite Holiday: Labor Day

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
You're receiving a lot of subconscious messages this week, trust them and follow those messages in your every day life.

Favorite Holiday: Earth Day

Habbo Cancer:

You're not one to hurt someone's feelings so you're holding back your comments about someone this week. Remember, honesty is the best policy especially in friendship.

Favorite Holiday: Memorial Day

[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
You're not one to be very in tune with your spirtual side but someone with a very strong spiritual side is entering your life and you're becoming more and more less judgmental.

Favorite Holiday: Your birthday!

[Aug 24th - Sep 23rd]
Virgos are more focused this week because your key planet Mercury is moving into Saturn's emotional period with it allowing you to focus more on your own and others emotions.

Favorite Holiday: The USA Independance Day (Fourth of July)!
