Howdy Habbos!

Besides supporters, we're going to announce the BIG SURPRISE! HabboScopes will be hosting a QUEST! The quest is called HabboScopes - Quest for the Future and it'll be up soon! Just keep watching the www.habbo.com homepage for more information! Haboot's idea of "What music genre suits you the best" is going to be used THIS EDITION! Thanks Haboot!
