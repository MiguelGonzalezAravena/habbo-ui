Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: “Itsa’ me, Mario!” Nintendo Trades
Owner: mikey-o

Ever wish you could dance the night away on top of a giant mushroom? Your dream is about to come true! This week I chose a room that pays tribute to the happy mushroom thingie in Super Mario Bros…mikey-o’s “Itsa’ me, Mario!” Nintendo Trades. The time and effort mikey-o put into this room is obvious the second you see it. Check out what went down when mikey-o and I met up in his Rare-less wonder of a room…

Luna: Well congrats, on winning staff pick of the week!
mikey-o: Why thank you! I'm honored.
Luna: This room is awesome.
mikey-o: Thanks x]
Luna: How long did it take you to make it?
mikey-o: It took quite a while...If I recall correctly, it took around 3 months.
Luna: Wow! That's dedication
mikey-o: I love some of the art that you can do with Furni.
Luna: How did you do it?
mikey-o: I first made a design in MS paint you know, made a layout, decided on something that would fit and drew something up. I wanted to match something like what Hiro-p had so I picked a game theme. The hard part was just getting all the right colored mats.
Luna: Nice...and what made you go with the mushroom?
mikey-o: Ah, Super Mario. You can't find much of a more classic game.
Luna: I personally like the Super Mario where you get a raccoon tail…
mikey-o: I like invincibility. Unstoppable. You get the big star and you go all shiny. The techno music plays really fast — and you run through the bad guys.
Luna: Oh yeah! You get all sparkly. I like that too.
mikey-o: x] Oh those were the days…
Luna: Did you play the old-skool Nintendo, like the original console?
mikey-o: Oh yes, back in the day. But now in college, everyone has the x-box and everything. I prefer the old school Atari and Nintendo personally.
Luna: Me too. Xbox and all that is too confusing for me.
mikey-o: Yeah, just push A to jump. That simple
Luna: lol exactly. And hold down and b to go really really fast. What is your favorite videogame EVER?
mikey-o: Favorite ever? Pong. The noise... "Bloop...Bleep...Bloop". So addicting.
Luna: io has a Pong room!!! I’ve never played it.
mikey-o: you should try it.
Luna: maybe I will....


Luna: So, how long have you been a Habbo?
mikey-o: I have played for many years. Four I think it is. I played UK, and when you were able to pre-register for .com, I immediately came over. I've been here since day one.
Luna: cool! Love the loyal ones! :)
mikey-o: Ah yes. I've been though it all. It has changed so much…But in good strides.
Luna: glad you think so
mikey-o: ;]
Luna: what has been your fave Rare released?
mikey-o: Personally, I have always been a fan of the Petal Patch. Old school Rare, and can make a good room look great.
Luna: and what about Norm? Doormat perhaps?
mikey-o: Norm has to be the Area Sofa. No doubt. I've been collection Area Sofas since day one. Too many to count -.-'
Luna: lol Cool! Do you listen to bobba music?
mikey-o: Yes, quite often.
Luna: Have you heard me on there?
mikey-o: Yes I have ;]
Luna: lol, now I’m embarrassed…
mikey-o: I doubt I was the only one who heard you. :P
Luna: I know...scary....
mikey-o: That would be weird knowing somany people are listening to you...and if you mess up, everyone knows. x]
Luna: :-O


Luna: So what is your fave music?
mikey-o: I like everything, I have to because I am a mobile DJ in RL.
Luna: Wow! Lots of DJs on Habbo.
mikey-o: Well, just started a few months ago.
Luna: What was the last album you bought
mikey-o: Last album was...Fort Minor's "The Rising Tide"
Luna: Cool, good choice! And how about movie? You have a favorite?
mikey-o: Movie, Radio. The message it portrays and the true storyline…
Luna: Radio?
mikey-o: Radio. The football movie
Luna: OH. I remember that one. Is football your fave sport?
mikey-o: No, actually I have never played organized football. I’ve run cross-country instead. For a good 10 years of my life. I’ve had a pretty successful career in track if I do say so myself. 7th in the nation in high school in the 100 m hurdles. Not to brag or anything :P
Luna: geez. That’s awesome!


Luna: So...are you participating in win a date?
mikey-o: Yes I am. But my room is secret.
Luna: If you submitted a room with my face in doormats I would be VERY impressed.
mikey-o: Oh, great idea... You've got me thinking now..
Luna: Too bad that Love Test is closed…but you can still make it!
mikey-o: With the limited colors of doormats, it could be quite difficult.
Luna: True. So, here's the question that I always ask…
mikey-o: Ask away
Luna: If you could invent a piece of Furni, what would it be?
mikey-o: The Habbo safe. For sure. So you don't have to make 20 rooms to store your furni. Only you could get in and see what you had. And the contents would be organized. It would say that you had 10 Plastos, not just show 10 and take up space. Simple. How I like it.
Luna: Cool concept
mikey-o: Thanks ;]
Luna: What is your fave Habbo game
mikey-o: Battleball.
Luna: me too!
mikey-o: I don't know how many tickets I've spent since it has come out. I don't even want to think about that -.-'
Luna: LOL So, do you have any shout outs?
mikey-o: Shout out to HankMcCoy and Hiro-p for helping me get into graphic design and everyone who helped contribute doormats for this project. They know who they are ;]
Luna: Nice! It'll be cool to see lots of Habbos dancing on the mushroom on Friday.
mikey-o: Yes it would. 7 pm EST on Friday October 6.
Luna: Si. Did you just pencil it in??
mikey-o: Sharpied. x]
Luna: LOL nice. Thanks for submitting to Staff Pick
mikey-o: No, thanks for taking the time to read it and to come and check it.
Luna: np :) I'll see you on Friday ok?
mikey-o: I'll see you then.

And we’ll see you guys, too! Iomegadrive, myself and jackiethedj will be broadcasting live on bobba music from mikey-o’s “Itsa’ me, Mario!” Nintendo Trades room on Friday, 10/6 at 4pm PST/7pm EST. So slap on your red suspenders and join us!
