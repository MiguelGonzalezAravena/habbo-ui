Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: HABBO BEACH [ORIGINAL]
Owner: DaPresentGuy

This week, Luna asked me (Caedma) to pick a room that I thought would make a great Staff Pick. This task was a lot harder than I thought it would be! I mean, there’s SO many great rooms, and only one gets chosen each week. So, I went from room to room, searching for the PERFECT room. I mean, there are SO many factors that all add up to a GREAT room. Does it look cool? Is there a theme? Is it original? And most importantly – is it big enough to hold a TGIF party? After hours of searching, I found the room that is worthy to be bestowed the honor of Staff Pick (well for this week anyway).

DaPresentGuy created a most excellent room that has me all pumped up for summer. Habbo Beach [Original] is a little slice of the ocean right here in Habbo. I was lucky enough to catch this busy Habbo online, and he was kind enough to let me interview him...

Caedma: Okay, so I guess the first question has to be... Where did you get the inspiration for this room? What made you think *Hey, Habbos need a beach*?
DaPresentGuy: Well, I love the beach, and I had gone on the French hotel when it first got V9 and saw the V9 rooms, and thought it would be perfect.
Caedma: Yes, this is quite the layout... I was impressed.
DaPresentGuy: Thank you :)
Caedma: Being that this is an indoor beach and all, what temperature is the thermostat in this room set on?
DaPresentGuy: 95 degrees F :P
Caedma: That’s what I thought. It is kinda warm in here.
DaPresentGuy: Hah!

Caedma: What’s the deal with the water in the center? I seem to remember the ocean being much larger…
DaPresentGuy: Kiddies pool :P But, of course, some adults like the kiddies pools too.
Caedma: Ahh... I see. I should have known. Ducks don’t swim in the ocean.
DaPresentGuy: Lol.
Caedma: So, you stated you love the beach... what’s your favorite thing to do when at the beach?
DaPresentGuy: Boogie boarding. Very fun, if you get a big enough wave.
Caedma: hehehe, Yes, most definitely. One of my favorite pastimes. Do you go to the beach often?
DaPresentGuy: Not often enough :(
Caedma: So, of all the places you ever been – Which beach is your favorite?
DaPresentGuy: Myrtle Beach is the best! I went there last summer for around 2 weeks.
Caedma: That’s in Florida right?
DaPresentGuy: South Carolina I do believe, I never took the time to check road signs :P
Caedma: So what do you do when you're not taunting the ocean with your mad boogie boarding skills?
DaPresentGuy: Haha. Usually spend time with friends or come on Habbo and chill.
Caedma: Most Excellent... and when you’re in Habbo... what do you like to do?
DaPresentGuy: Well, I love to help out, so I go into the Welcome Lounge often, or go into some trade rooms I also love doing mazes :)
Caedma: I like looking at mazes... but I always get lost when I try to get thru.
DaPresentGuy: Lol. Time and practice.

Caedma: Got any big plans this summer? I see something about Australia?
DaPresentGuy: Yes, going to Australia for 3 weeks in a student ambassadors program. So I'm very excited, I actually leave this Sunday.
Caedma: Crazyness! What are you going to do with all that time?
DaPresentGuy: A bunch of things! We basically go all over Australia. Cairns, Sydney, etc. Even get to hang out with the aborigines!
Caedma: Fun! Make sure to look out the window if you fly over the Great Barrier Reef
DaPresentGuy: That will be amazing to see! We get to go snorkeling there, too.
Caedma: So...now that I’m green with envy… back to the room...
DaPresentGuy: Alright.
Caedma: How long did it take you to make the room?
DaPresentGuy: Well, I had it pre-planned, so only around a day.
Caedma: Now when you create a room, do you usually preplan or do you kinda just build as you go?
DaPresentGuy: Not always. This was just something I REALLY wanted to make. I usually build as I go.
Caedma: What’s the track on the side for? Like a bike trail?
DaPresentGuy: Well, lots of beaches have piers, so I thought it could act like one.
Caedma: ahhhh... good thinking! What is your favorite Furni?
DaPresentGuy: Lol, I do like dragon lamps.
Caedma: Awesome, so do I!!

***a silence takes over the room***
Caedma: Okay... welllll… I don’t really have anymore questions. Any shout outs to anyone before I go?
DaPresentGuy: Hey to fL1rtyGaL, Iced-Mocha, Edible! And anyone else that I left out!
Caedma: Have a good time in Australia... Gratz on room of the week! Oh, and don’t forget about us while you’re gone...
DaPresentGuy: Thanks! Have a good summer!
Caedma: You too! Thanks for letting me invade your room... =)
DaPresentGuy: Ciao!

Thanks Caedma! The TGIF Party is at DaPresentGuy’s beach, I mean room, this Friday, so be sure to get there early and save yourself a spot! Be sure to check back next Wednesday to find out who the next Staff Pick is!
