Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Club Austin
Owner: Austin612

Whenever the New Year rolls around I reflect on everything that has brought me happiness in the past year, and friends are always at the tip top of that list! I thought the perfect way to ring in 2007 would be to select a room submitted by a friend of the room owner. I found the perfect fit in an email from awesomegirl789 asking me to check out her friend Austin612’s room. Club Austin is a cool and colorful place to party…and it’s also the first Staff Pick of 2007!


Austin612: :o LUNA!
Luna: hi!
Austin612: It's an honour to meet you! *bows*
Luna: lol nice to meet you too!
Austin612: This is SO! cool
Luna: :) Congrats!
Austin612: OMG I'm seriously screaming in Real Life!
Luna: This place is really rockin’ I’ve heard…
Austin612: lol Thanks... I've spent a buttload of money on this place and no one comes!
Luna: Did you have a New Years party?
Austin612: Yea, No one came :( except my bartender.
Luna: LOL, well that’s important! We'll get this place filled up on Friday, how does that sound?
Austin612: Umm.......umm. Can we have a couple parties?
Luna: well, usually its just one, but its awesome!


Luna: ok, tell me about Club Austin....
Austin612: Well...I live in Canada, so I started the whole thing on habbo.ca. Then I was a dumbutt and got scammed, then I went on .co.uk, but I couldnt buy credits :S
Luna: Thats no good…
Austin612: So I heard about .com so I went on, got rich, and was scammed!
Luna: oh man, this sounds like a soap opera :(
Austin612: Then my parents bought me 240 coins, got rich again, and stayed safe
Luna: awesome! glad to hear that
Austin612: And now I buy coins all the time
Luna: It shows, this place is seriously stocked
Austin612: Stocked? Meaning...
Luna: full of Furni lol
Austin612: oh Yuppers! I couldn't have been this successful without the help of chillaxin30
Luna: So tell me about your design inspiration?
Austin612: Well...I always used to be HC but always used the norm room, the one with the box in the middle, and I always liked to have my Rares in one spot. So I saw this HC Room and thought down here might be good for Rares.
Luna: Cool!


*Austin612’s cat, Zon, gets stuck on the rollers at the bottom and spins round and round*

Luna: The cat is hysterical!
Austin612: lol When my parents bought me 240 coins, I bought him first
Luna: What does his name mean?
Austin612: I used to play an online game called Second Life and my character’s name was Zon
Luna: cool
Austin612: And I got banned, so I named my cat after him. This is how it went, Habbo.ca > Second Life > Habbo .co.uk > Habbo.au > Habbo.com
Luna: the timeline of Austin/Zon! lol
Austin612: lol
Luna: so what is this spinny thing supposed to be anyways?
Austin612: I saw it in one of my friend’s rooms, I always have one in my clubs. Probably the thing I've had longest... would be the bar... up top...Just a place to order drinks, snacks. So how'd you get to be hotel manager?
Luna: hey, I thought this was YOUR interview!
Austin612: lol right...
Luna: Plus, Kedo is Hotel Manager, not me
Austin612: :o


Luna: What is your favorite public room on Habbo?
Austin612: The HabboSphere pool deck
Luna: You like to dive?
Austin612: Deffinately
Luna: cool, me too!
Austin612: I'm pro at it :)
Luna: LOL, I just type a bunch of keys and hope I look somewhat decent
Austin612: lol Same
Luna: lol, so i guess im pro too. So is diving your fave game?
Austin612: oh yes
Luna: favorite place to grab a bite in habbo?
Austin612: umm My bar :)
Luna: lol, good answer
Austin612: :0
Luna: how about your fave food in real life?
Austin612: umm umm ugh so many
Luna: hehe
Austin612: BBQ Ribs I guess from Boston pizza
Luna: mmmmmmmm delish
Austin612: =))
Luna: but way too messy for me
Austin612: rofl
Luna: I have to wear a bib when I eat ribs
Austin612: hahahahaha
Luna: It isn’t pretty lol Do you have a favorite band or musician?
Austin612: Umm AC/DC probably
Luna: That’s a favorite here too! io always blasts it!
Austin612: lol
Luna: So what’s your favorite Rare?
Austin612: DJ-Turntable... which I can never get a hold of :( I wanted to get one before you came but I couldn’t.
Luna: lol, I’m sure you can trade with someone for one! For the party on Friday you can count on bobba music to spin the tunes
Austin612: lol I wanted to get my own radio. There is one website to make one free but I cant set it up :(
Luna: Well, we'll play extra AC/DC just for you! If you could invent a rare what would you invent?
Austin612: :o Umm umm I don’t know. I have a good idea for Habbo rooms though. Like, every person that owns an HC Room should be able to send "room wide" messages...like you do...
Luna: That’s a cool idea


Luna: So do you have any New Years resolutions?
Austin612: To ban all the Habbo scammers :) if I could ban people :(
Luna: lol, any shout outs?
Austin612: um Yea. Shout out to chillaxin30, chanelr, codyscottmac, x.Andi.x, to !ring!, micro-jackson
Luna: what about Awesomegirl789...she’s the one who sent in the email about your room!
Austin612: oh and yea, shout out to awesomegirl. There are more shout outs but they cannot fit...
Luna: Cool :)
Austin612: oh and another idea…there should be a button on the console, so it sends an auto MASS
Luna: oh yes, that would be a NICE one
Austin612: make it easier :S


I haven’t found an easy way to mass quite yet, but you should still send a console to all of you friends and plan on being at the Staff Pick party this Friday, January 5 at 4pm PST / 7pm EST in Austin612’s Club Austin room. Let’s make it a belated New Years Eve party to remember…
