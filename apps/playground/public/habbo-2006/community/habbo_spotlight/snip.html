Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Turkey Trade
Owner: snip?

Gobble is the word this week, and snip?’s Turkey Trade room is the place to be! His giant turkey made completely out of multi-colored Plasto chairs definitely got me pumped up for some pumpkin pie and awesome Thanksgiving times. Read on for some good old-fashioned turkey talk:


Luna: Hi!!! How are you?
snip?: Good, How about you?
Luna: I'm great! Congrats on winning Staff Pick!
snip?: Thank You sooo much!!! Its always been a dream
Luna: Cool :) Well it just came true :)
snip?: Yep
Luna: Well, the inspiration behind the room is obvious…
snip?: Yeah Lol
Luna: How long did it take you to design?
snip?: Uhh It took me a few days to get the design complete. Well...While my r/l friend soci was sick and home from school. For a week he helped out a lot while I was at school.
Luna: lol, nice system ;-)
snip?: Yep We best friends
Luna: Did you draw the design first?
snip?: No, Actually it came to us both one night on the phone. We were thinking on a night off school and it just came to us. Then we worked 5 hours straight that night.
Luna: Did you have to get al lot of chairs or did you have most of them?
snip?: Oooo Yeah. Had to buy them All
Luna: Wow!


Luna: So, how long have you been a Habbo…this is the first time we've met I believe...
snip?: Well I started on co.uk in 2003 then joined USA in early 2005 but on another account. And I have seen you before in mikey-o's room but we haven't met.
Luna: lol, oh, well nice to meet you!
snip?: Same!
Luna: Was mikey-o's mushroom an inspiration to do floor art?
snip?: Not at all Lol It just came to me and my friend, and what made us think of it, I dont know.
Luna: lol, maybe you were both hungry
snip?: Yep, at the moment I was eating cereal and watching the Simpsons. I love The Simpsons
Luna: Sounds like my kind of party
snip?: Lol
Luna: What kind of cereal?
snip?: Kellog's Cinnamon Krunchers. I love all cinnamon cereal
Luna: Cinammon Toast Crunch! oooooooooooh yeahhhhhhhhhhhhhh
snip?: YEah!!!
Luna: Who's your favorite Simpsons character?
snip?: Probably Bart. Well...It depends on the episode
Luna: lol true. I heart Santa’s Little Helper

Luna: LOL
snip?: Lol It was so funny. He always scared teachers
Luna: ahhh, poor guy. So what kind of music do you like?
snip?: Counrty, Rap, and like soft rock, some Christian. O yeah and classic rock. Have you heard of the band Adam and Andrew?

Luna: oooooh a la mode?

Luna: Sweet, I'm a dessert girl myself
snip?: Lol
