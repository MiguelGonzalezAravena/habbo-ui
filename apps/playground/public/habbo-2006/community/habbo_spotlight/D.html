Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: My Lucky Turtle
Owner: :D

It’s been a while since I’ve selected a room with “floor art”, so I figured this was the week to do just that! My Lucky Turtle room called out to me the second I saw it…you know what it said?? “Pick me.” So I did! Check out what the lovely and loyal room owner, :D, had to say about her Turtleliscious masterpiece:


:D: Hi! :)
Luna: Hi!
:D: Nice hair. :P
Luna: Thanks! You too :P
:D: It's my fave style for girls. Haha
Luna: Congrats on winning Staff Pick!
:D: Wow. I am trying to keep cool. I can't help but smile in real life.
Luna: lol Well, I’m glad I could bring a smile to your face
:D: This is exciting for me. I've never had any exciting Habbo news like this before. >.<
Luna: Nice! Well, this turtle rox my sox
:D: Ahh thank you!
Luna: Tell me how he/she came to be...
:D: I was always inspired by those rooms with graphix made out of mats like mikey-o's room when he won staff pick last year. And I wanted to make one myself, but something personal to me. And since the hotel only has cats and dogs for pets on the hotel...I decided to make my own pet. Hehe, so I opened up MS Paint and print screened this room layout and figured out a way how to make a turtle out of the floor space. So nerdy of me. LOL
Luna: lol, nice. But why a turtle? Why not an elephant or a penguin?
:D: Lol I love turtles. They're just so cute! I can't help it! I guess I like reptiles more than furry animals.
Luna: They are really cute! And I like how slow they are. Sometimes I wish I could slow down and live life like a turtle
:D: Haha. Sea turtles can swim really fast!
Luna: oh true LOL
:D: I used to own 2 red ear slider turtles. I've had them since they were babies.
Luna: cute!
:D: My family and my bf raised them until they were big and stinky. And one day while I was at school my mom told my bf to donate them to the pet store so I lost them before I could say bye to them. :(
Luna: so sad!
:D: It's really hard to maintain aquatic turtles though.
Luna: why's that?
:D: They're big stinkers! So I understood why my mom wanted them gone
Luna: yuck! hamsters and rabbits are stinky pets as well…
:D: My bf has been taking me to the zoo twice a year now though. So he makes up for it. :D I once took a picture with the 3rd largest tortoise in the world. A Sulcata tortoise. It was awesome.
Luna: sweet, I love going to the zoo
:D: I love the zoo! It's one of my favorite places to go on vacation.
Luna: Wouldn’t it be sweet if there were actually turtle pets on Habbo? Their food is already in the catty!
:D: I would buy hundreds of them. LOL


Luna: I like the use of cabbage by the way
:D: Hehe. Thanks!
Luna: nice touch
:D: I was thinking of a way to fill up space.
Luna: So what is your turtle’s name?
:D: I named him Joleo. Like, Jo-Lay-O Hahahaa…I named him after my best friend. He helped me the most by getting almost all of the mats. He always knew I wanted a "big room" layout. So he gave me almost the whole room for my Christmas present.
Luna: lol, that’s really sweet of him. How long did it take you to get all the mats?
:D: It took about 30 min for me to make it on Paint. To see if it was actually possible to make one. But the mats... Hmm Around 4 hours I think?
Luna: wow, it usually takes a while to collect all of them!
:D: I dunno, I get really determined when I want to finish things on here.
Luna: cool, there are all different types of room builders on Habbo!
:D: Yeah! Basically I didn't sleep at all that night.
Luna: How long did it take to build once you had everything?
:D: For the turtle, since I already had it made on Paint, it took me about 15 minutes once I got everything in my hand. Then I noticed my room looked plain, so I added cabbage. And then I added the little feeding/sitting areas a day later.
Luna: That is some efficient room designing I must say :)


Luna: So how long have you been a Habbo?
:D: Ummm. I started on the UK one in about 2003. But I was never really into the UK cause I never felt "at home." So when the US Hotel opened in 2004, I moved here in the quickness. Hehe.
Luna: cool, so you’ve been here since Day One!
:D: Yup! People always ask how I made my name. Well, this is how I made it...I literally stayed up in front of my laptop refreshing and refreshing, waiting until the US hotel opened. And since I didn't want to use my pre-reg'd named from the UK I made this name as soon as I saw the US hotel opened. Pretty geeky, but it paid off. I love my name on here.
Luna: It's a sweet name! So where do you hang out most when you are on?
:D: My favorite public room is the Tranquility Garden. It's so beautiful and relaxing.
Luna: Favorite Furni?
:D: Okay, I have a few. Haha. My fave regular Furni would have to be bonsais. Seasonal, roses. And Rares, Aloe Veras. I love plants.
Luna: What girl doesn't like receive tons of roses, right?
:D: Roses are my favorite Furni overall, they're soo lovely! Too bad I'm allergic to them in real life. o.O
Luna: That’s no good! Does your BF give you daisies instead? (those don't smell)
:D: I'm allergic to all pretty flowers unfortunately. :( So he has to go the candy route which isn't that great, cause I don't really like chocolate. LOL
Luna: oh no! So...gummy bears?
:D: Anything fruity!
Luna: :) If you could invent a Rare what would it be?
:D: Oh wow. Good question. I never thought of that. Umm let me think. A dresser with a mirror on top? LMAO I know I can't live without mirrors in real life.
Luna: :) me neither
:D: That would be weird to look at your Habbo's reflection. I wish we had cars on here too.
Luna: Whats your favorite car in real life?
:D: :x I have a Scion xB You know those cars that look like toasters or shoe boxes?
Luna: Yes, I know :)
:D: The license plate holder on the rear says "Turtle Box"
Luna: seriously??
:D: Haha, yup!
Luna: that is so funny! The car kinda does look like a turtle...
:D: I would have got a green one too! But the only green one they had while we were at the dealer was a stick and I only know how to drive automatic. :-/
Luna: Sticks are no-go for me too. I tried to learn once and stalled like 15 times
:D: LOL I have a bad habit of putting on makeup while I drive. So I don't think my parents will ever trust me to try stick.
Luna: ooooh, my bad habit is text messaging while I drive lol Both not very smart at all
:D: LOL
Luna: We gotta be more careful ;)


Luna: Did you watch AI last night?
:D: You know what? I didn't actually.
Luna: oh boo!
:D: I came home from snowboarding yesterday and I was so pooped and tired, I went straight to bed. I slept around right when AI came on too. :-/
Luna: its ok, it wasn’t that great
:D: It gets pretty old, I think. The premieres. They always show the people who can't sing.
Luna: Yea, and the bad acts weren’t THAT funny, except for this one girl who sang like the lion from Wizard of Oz.
:D: I do enjoy watching the real competitions though.
Luna: Oh me too
:D: Like when they are down to their final 10 or 12 or whatever number.
Luna: That’s what I’m excited for
:D: Hehe.
Luna: So do you have any shout outs my dear?
:D: Hmm. M@rk EvilBrandon JOLE0 GreenBeanBoy FC/fogged. I think that's it. I hope no one gets mad at me for forgetting them! >.<
Luna: lol One more question before I get going
:D: :D
Luna: what is this certificate on the wall all about?
:D: Hehe. It's my turtle's birth certificate! The day he was made/born/created. My friend EvilBrandon dropped it off in my room so I thought it was a cute idea.
Luna: OMG, that is adorable. Well, we can all celebrate his birthday a bit late on Friday
:D: :D
Luna: It was so awesome meeting you!
:D: Noo, it was awesome meeting you! This is a first for me. LOL
Luna: :) well, I'll see you on Friday. Bye :D, bye bye JoJo (that’s my nickname for your turtle)
:D: I like that. I'll stick with it.
Luna: Yay!
:D: I'll see you on Friday!
Luna: see ya


Put on your best turtle suit (aka dress in green) and join staff in :D’s My Lucky Turtle room this Friday at 2pm PST/ 5pm EST for some good TGIF times. All that’s left to say is: “Heroes in a half-shell, TURTLE POWER!”
