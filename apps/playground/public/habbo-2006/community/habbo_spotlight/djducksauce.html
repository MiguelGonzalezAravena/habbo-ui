Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

DJ-DuckSauce's Dojo has lots of mojo, which is why it's this week's Staff Pick!

Room Name: DJ-DuckSauce's Dance Dojo™
Owner: DJ-Ducksauce

With the very recent arrival of the Calligraphy of the Masters poster and Red Knots to the hotel, I had to pick a room that had plenty of both this week! So, yesterday I went cruising down the Feng Shui Floor looking for the perfect Asian inspired room to party in when I stumbled upon DJ-DuckSauce’s Dance Dojo™. It was locked but I just had to see it so I kinda let myself in…I’m so glad I did! I instantly headed to the dance floor and starting gettin’ down while I waited for DJ-DuckSauce to come back (he was afk at the time).

When he finally returned he was nice enough to spend some time chatting with me, even though I totally crashed his pad:

Luna: Congrats DJ-DuckSauce, you're this week's STAFF PICK!
DJ-DuckSauce: Wow my rooms not even finished lol
Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
DJ-DuckSauce: 5 or 6 months
Luna: Cool!
DJ-DuckSauce: I used to be on way back in 2000 though and i was away for years and got back into it recently.
Luna: What brought you back?
DJ-DuckSauce: I went to college and I liked having a game I could play anywhere
Luna: Totally, that is a cool thing about habbo. No matter where you are, its always there.
DJ-DuckSauce: Yep, I love it
Luna: What are you studying in college?
DJ-DuckSauce: I'm a theatre major, I wanna be an actor
Luna: Awesome! Are you a comedic actor or more dramatic?
DJ-DuckSauce: I prefer comedy, but I like both.
Luna: Who is your acting inspiration?
DJ-DuckSauce: hmmmm, that's a tough one. I am inspired by guys like Denzel Washington who have a reputation for greatness.
Luna: Totally, he's a stud :)
DJ-DuckSauce: But there's up and coming actors that make me think wow, that could be me
Luna: Like?
DJ-DuckSauce: Jamie Foxx. Started as a funny guy and now he has an Oscar. Plus, he did TV and film which I wouldn't mind doing.
Luna: That's the way to do it!



Luna: Speaking of inspiration, what inspired your room?
DJ-DuckSauce: Well, when I saw the Asian Furni in other hotels I was like wow. I really liked the new designs and I couldn't wait for it to come here.
Luna: :-)
DJ-DuckSauce: I wanted it to look like an actual dojo in some so I made this dance floor to look like a fighting mat to have dance offs and such. lol
Luna: Great idea!
DJ-DuckSauce: I've been planning this for months actually but i needed the asian furni and Confucius is answering my prayers.
Luna: Have you been participating in the quests?
DJ-DuckSauce: I've attempted. I didn't get the last one though.
Luna: The last one was hard.
DJ-DuckSauce: Yeah, I got the zodiac thing but I didn't know where to go with it
Luna: Good luck with the latest one…
DJ-DuckSauce: It's crazy i was just looking at that one. I left and came back and here you are.
Luna: Did i scare you?
DJ-DuckSauce: No I was just like how did...Luna! lol
Luna: I don’t usually come through password protected rooms…
DJ-DuckSauce: It's cool, I'm glad you did.
Luna: Me too! I’ve been looking for a good Asian room to party in and voila!
The Dance Dojo! Do you like to dance?
DJ-DuckSauce: Love it
Luna: Fave dance song?
DJ-DuckSauce: hmmm I like the new song by Ciara “Get Up”. That's a hot one.
Luna: That is a good one. I LOVE the new Justin Timberlake song, it took a while to grow on me though.
DJ-DuckSauce: Yeah a lot of music has that affect on me too.



Luna: What is your fave Furni?
DJ-DuckSauce: Well I really like the imperial teles, but the lantern isn't out yet…I saw it on .ca and fell in love with it.
Luna: Patience my dear, we need to learn lessons first!!! hehe
DJ-DuckSauce: Yeah good for going back to school
Luna: Seriously, get you guys in the learning spirit!
DJ-DuckSauce: I love making rooms though, it's a lot of fun for me trying to design the best rooms that I possibly can.
Luna: Maybe you should be an interior designer if the acting thing doesn’t work out that is...
DJ-DuckSauce: Yeah I have this art background probably get that from my mother.
Luna: What kind of art?
DJ-DuckSauce: Fine art, I like a lot of modern art.
Luna: My favorite is Andy Warhol.
DJ-DuckSauce: I like his work.
Luna: Pop art is awesome, I'm just into everything pop I guess…
DJ-DuckSauce: I love pop culture too!
Luna: hehe! Well, it’s been great meeting you and chatting! Any shoutouts?
DJ-DuckSauce: hmmm kedo and all the habbos that don't beg and just appreciate the community.
Luna: awwwwww cheers to that!
DJ-DuckSauce: Alright it was really nice meeting you and thanks again.
Luna: Thank YOU! See you on Friday :)
DJ-DuckSauce: See ya



Join DJ-DuckSauce and I for an Asian-themed Staff Pick dance party on Friday at 4pm PST. And next time YOU lock your room and leave for a couple minutes, don’t be surprised if you come back and find me bustin’ a move on your dance floor. :P
