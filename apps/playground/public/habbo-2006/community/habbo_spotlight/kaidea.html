Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: The Habbo Fun House
Owner: Kaidea

Have the back-to-school-after-winter-break blues?? Lift your spirits by visiting Kaidea’s cheerful Staff Pick of the Week room: The Habbo Fun House. Here you can play your favorite Habbo games, meet Charlie (the friendly Fun House dog) and laugh hysterically with your awesome host Kaidea (she had me in tears!) Check out her interview here:


Luna: Hi!
Kaidea: Hey :D
Luna: Congrats on winning!
Kaidea: Thanks :D Took Lots of Work to make it!
Luna: How are you today?
Kaidea: Good. Stressed out because of School. Evil Teachers.
Luna: Which class is stressing you out?
Kaidea: French, Science and History. It's the Same Teacher. She's Hard. If you Say an English word...You're Dead. Literally!
Luna: lol, literally?? no!
Kaidea: Principles Office and Parent Call Pls.
Luna: omg! That is rough
Kaidea: Some of Us never Fully Recoverd. Especially Me. I never Say the Word "Heelies" anymore.
Luna: Heelies?
Kaidea: The Shoes with the Spiffy Wheels. I’m a Pro!
Luna: lol, those things scare me!
Kaidea: Not Me.
Luna: I always think people wearing those are going to fall or run into me…
Kaidea: Once I almost Knocked over an Old Granny. I died Laughing.
Luna: omg, I bet she wasn’t laughing! lol
Kaidea: I thought she was going to chase me with her purse. Thank god I lost her in the CD Section.
Luna: hahaha. Those wheels must come in handy when you are being chased by grannies
Kaidea: I Guess So. I like using them in Malls. You get around Fast.
Luna: Very efficient ;)
Kaidea: I used up my Old pair really fast. I shop to Much. Poor Credit Cards. Beware of Me. Seriously.
Luna: LOL I can totally relate to that
Kaidea: American Eagle Pls. Best Store Ever. Expensive but the Clothes Last.
Luna: I love that store!
Kaidea: Other then That it's like Old Grannie Stores at my mall.
Luna: Hence the old Grannie you almost ran over lol
Kaidea: Omg. Im scared Lol!


Luna: so tell me about this room!
Kaidea: Well. It was created by a weird Dream of Mine. A Clown was holding Balloons. Dramatic Isn't it?
Luna: LOL I’m scared of clowns. I guess I’m scared of a lot of things...
Kaidea: Amgs. Clowns Scare People. Kthnx. This is the 15th Attempt to the Room it took a while to see what Worked.There are Teacups in this room But I’m scared to turn them on. They usually Go crazy. One of the seats almost ran over Charlie (the dog). We Should Take a Trip in the Teles.
Luna: yes! Let’s do that
Kaidea: I have no clue where they lead Because I dropped them Randomly. Oh Yeah. This is the Ballpit ;]
Luna: oh sweet
Kaidea: Took me two Months to get all of these Rubber Balls
Luna: ball pits rock
Kaidea: Yes. My friend Andrea made them drop out As you can see. She's good at messes.
Luna: haha
Kaidea: This is the Viewing area :] You have to get by the Pods to use the Teleporters. And the middle is the Game room :o! It Changes everyday. It's pretty Neat.
Luna: Cool! What game do you usually play in there?
Kaidea: Anything and Everything ever Created by Habbo-Kind.
Luna: Awesome
Kaidea: Today it's Bumper Trivia but It's supposed to be Fridge Game.
Luna: Which game is your favorite to host?
Kaidea: Hm. I really don't have a Favorite But I grew up on Habbo Playing Falling Furniture So I guess that would be My Favorite Thing to Host. Brings back the Good Ol' Days! It's Fun, Because I have lots of Colored Plasto.
Luna: lol, that’s my favorite game to host too. I love watching Habbos run in flocks…
Kaidea: They remind me of Old Grandaddy's with Walkers running from Something ^_^


Check out Charlie playing with the rubber ball!

Luna: I LOVE how colorful this room is
Kaidea: Thanks. I almost tore it down when We started. But Holly Insisted that I kept it Up. She's like The Best Person to talk to for Opinions.
Luna: Well, i say it paid off, right?
Kaidea: Totally. I’m glad I didn’t listen to myself -Slaps my Cheek-
Luna: hehe
Kaidea: Deserved that Pls. Let's see what's behind Tele 1! -Builds up Temptation- Bam! Looks like we are in the Game room. Rawr. My Chair!

Luna: yeah, good idea
Kaidea: Race you to the Tele! :O! Muahahaha :D These two Parts are for Team Trivia Night. Happens once a Month. It's really awesome.
Luna: how does it work?
Kaidea: Well Me and Other Hosts Stand in the Game area and We ask questions Etc.. And they have to answer then Whichever team answers the most questions Get to play 1 vs One.
Luna: How do you keep the opposite team quiet?
