Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Trick or Treat Street
Owner: RareGirlB

When you think of Habboween, your mind probably conjures up visions of monsters, vampires, ghosts and werewolfs...well, I say, what about the CANDY?!?! That's one of the main reasons I chose RareGirlB's Trick or Treat Street as this week's Staff Pick. This room captures the true essence of Habboween, which I believe to be, free candy. It also helps that the room has an awsome design with freaky little details. Read on for this week's one-on-one:

RareGirlB: ;] hi
Luna: I LOVE this room!

Luna: Very nice
RareGirlB: ;]
RareGirlB: I tried to make it as realistic as possible. It’s supposed to be just like real life knocking at doors for candy
Luna: It looks so realistic. I like the use of streetlights.
RareGirlB: ;] ty
