Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Habbo Super Sports Complex
Owner: Nadda

Go! Fight! Win! Knowing nothing about sports (except for a couple cheers), I decided it was about time I learned something – after all, March Madness is coming up and I want sound like I actually know something when I discuss games with Kedo, iomegadrive and RocketSkates! I came across Nadda’s Habbo Super Sport Complex when looking through Staff Pick entries and decided this would be the perfect place to brush up on my sporty smarts.

Want to learn more about Habbo sports and hear some epic Luna blonde moments? Read on!


Nadda: Hello Luna
Luna: Hi!
Nadda: This is sick!!!!!!
Luna: Congrats on winning Staff Pick!
Nadda: thank u so much
Luna: So, you gotta tell me about Habbo sports
Nadda: ok what would u like to know?
Luna: What are the 2 games that are played here?
Nadda: This game here is called HHL, Habbo Hockey League
Luna: Oh! How does it work?
Nadda: It is played 3 on 3 with 2 forwards and 1 goalie. The forwards start on these mats and the goalie stays inside this little box trying to defend the sofa so the sofa is like the net of hockey.
Luna: Oh, and the forwards try to sit in the sofa
Nadda: yes
Luna: lol that is awesome! So the Habbos are the pucks??
Nadda: basically lol We ppl been trying different ways to play the game of hockey on here but none seem to work as good as this
Luna: Nice, I like it! It’s a great way to play hockey!!
Nadda: u want some basic rules of the game?
Luna: sure! For all the Habbos who have never seen a game...
Nadda: ok, ref sits in the little area were the throne sofa is. He will ask the players when they line up R?, R = ready. Then when the players say ready the ref says DP, DP = drop puck. Then the Habbos are off and try to get past their opponents and score b4 other team does and all games are played to 10 unless game is at 9-9 and it goes to an overtime
Luna: Sounds like its exciting and very entertaining to watch!
Nadda: yes it is we usually everynight get 25/25 habbos to attend at main hhl league
Luna: cool, who owns that?
Nadda: my good friend Vietnam
Luna: sweet, so when do teams play here, in this room?
Nadda: Teams don’t really play here it more of a pickup room for developing players
Luna: Ohhh, so they come here to train?
Nadda: basically
Luna: cool! Rookies, I like that.


Luna: So are there formal teams in the HHL?
Nadda: Yes we use real nhl team names
Luna: What team are you on?
Nadda: Montreal Canadians
Luna: Goooooooo Maple Leaves!
Nadda: :OOo
Luna: no?
Nadda: Canadian rival :)
Luna: OH lol I thought it was just your team's mascot like a dancing maple leaf or something…
Nadda: that is Toronto
Luna: whoops, my bad :P
Nadda: its alright were not all perfect :)
Luna: Most Habbos would call that a "blonde moment" lol
Nadda: LOL


Luna: So, tell me about the other game in here!
Nadda: This game is HBA
Luna: Say whats?
Nadda: HBA = Habbo Basketball Association
Luna: Ahhhh And are Habbos the basketballs?
Nadda: yes object of game is to get to your opponents side mats b4 your opponents do. There are lanes.
Luna: Lanes? Aren't lanes for bowling?
Nadda: LMAO I didn’t invent the game :)
Luna: sorry sorry, continue ;)
Nadda: lane 4 is the scoring lane and lane 3 is plugging lane to help lane 4 lane 2 basically goes 5 spaces and sits there as a blocker and lane 1 does some movement
Luna: you lost me at plugging...
Nadda: ok plugging is like if I get beat u would come and click my stop really fast so other team would not make it past us
Luna: hmmmm ok...I think I need to see this one played to really get it
Nadda: actually it is easy to learn watching, explaining is tough
Luna: Yeah, my head is spinning a bit with all the lanes and blocks and plugs…
Nadda: Like HHL we have a HBA league where all Habbos are allowed to join
Luna: sweet! How do Habbos sign up?
Nadda: Rookies would go to Skyflake and ask to join rookie draft and or team owners
Luna: Cool! Think I should join a team?
Nadda: Hae y not, we always looking for talent :)
Luna: lol I'm afraid I don't have much talent when it comes to sports on and off Habbo lol
Nadda: lol it’s the effort I always say that counts :P
Luna: I can run around and distract people…
Nadda: there u go
Luna: So what team are you on? Lakers??? Kobe???
Nadda: I play for HSBA All-stars. In HBA we make up our own team names
Luna: ahhhh gotcha

 


Nadda: So what would u like to see next
Luna: Whats this center area?
Nadda: Track
Luna: For? Another league?
Nadda: It’s not really for anything I just added it for the entertainment
Luna: cool, I actually really enjoy running in circles, for reals
Nadda: ya i know dizzy :P
Luna: lol So, while we run, I want to learn more about you!
Nadda: ok!!!!
Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
Nadda: hmmm I started Habbo in the summer of 04 I believe
Luna: Cool! What's you favorite thing about Habbo? Other then the sports...
Nadda: lol I like to go to casino's and test my luck
Luna: Nice! Are you lucky?
Nadda: Not really. That is y I like norms better. Less tempting to bet :P
Luna: Well, St. Patricks Day is coming up so be sure to look for lots of 4 leaf clovers! :)
Nadda: :O I will
Luna: What kind of music are you into?
Nadda: I like a mixture of music I into some rap and my favorite band is gym class heroes
Luna: It all comes back to sports huh?
Nadda: ya :) Sports is my life i love sports a day without sports is a bad day for me
Luna: Well, what's your fave movie? Love and Basketball?
Nadda: no
Luna: lol I love that movie
Nadda: hmmmmm...that is hard I like so many but I would have to say Fast and Furious 3 Tokyo Drift :)
Luna: Nice! Do you watch TV?
Nadda: yes I do
Luna: What's your favorite program?
Nadda: I like Shows on MTV and Fox and I been a Simpsons watcher since I could talk :)
Luna: Those are my 2 fave channels!!! I love Simpsons too!
Nadda: SICKNEST!!!!! I know I so happy after so long they coming to theaters
Luna: Which Simpsons character is most like you?
Nadda: I probably am a Bart. I can have a soft side at points but I cause havoc a lot too :)
Luna: good answer! I'm a Lisa for sure...Or maybe a Crazy Cat Lady...
Nadda: lol
Luna: either one

 


Luna: How about food? What's your fave?
Nadda: CHINESE!!!!!!!!!
Luna: CHOW MEIN FOREVER! lol
Nadda: HECK YES!!!!!
Luna: WITH CHICKEN!
Nadda: Sweet and sour chicken with fried rice!!!! My favorite dish
Luna: Oh man, that sounds delish right about now
Nadda: I know I might have to call my mom and have her bring me some :)
Luna: lol Now, I gotta know, what's your fave Furni?
Nadda: as u could probably guess idk y but mode sofas. I love my mode sofas
Luna: Do you like all of the re-colors that are coming out?
Nadda: yes I was so happy when they finally put red mode and iced in. I love the color red
Luna: me too, it’s good luck! where's the red Mode in here?
Nadda: I haven't got around to get any I am broke all I got is flies :(
Luna: All you got is "Nadda". Sorry I couldn’t resist!
Nadda: yep, Nadda Mucho!!!!!
Luna: If you could invent a Furni what would it be? A Hocket Puck? lol
Nadda: Nope, I would have to invent a juke box that would be sick so while playing sports u got music going on :)
Luna: Music always helps me when I work out so that would be awesome
Nadda: ya me 2
Luna: I could run around the track all day long
Nadda: I could play hockey all day long
Luna: :)

 


Luna: Well, do you have any shout outs?
Nadda: Yes I do. I’d like to thank my good friends kohl1992,:Juan, DLHC-Tech for being my friends for a long time and my first Habbo friends Defol and -=ERK=- and to 000 for helping me stack the mode sofas

Nadda: yes it was very nice finally meeting u in person. I am glad I could make Habbo know about Habbo sports a little better
