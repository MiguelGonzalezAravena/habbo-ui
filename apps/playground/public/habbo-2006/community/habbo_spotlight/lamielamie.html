Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Kitty Trade Room
Owner: lamie.lamie

The language of Hello Kitty is universal! Argentinean Habbo, lamie.lamie, built Kitty Trade Room as a haven for Sanrio-loving girls, but I think its impressive design will attract guy Habbos too. I had a great time chatting with lamie.lamie about food, music and of course the inspiration behind the room, Hello Kitty! Read on for more:


lamie.lamie: Hi Luna :)
Luna: how are you?
lamie.lamie: I'm fine thanks and you??!
Luna: I'm great thanks! I love this room!
lamie.lamie: Thanks!! :P
Luna: Do you love Hello Kitty?
lamie.lamie: yeah. Who didn't have a Hello Kitty thing when as a little girl? I love Sanrio :P
Luna: Oh I loved Hello Kitty as a kid! I had purses and lollipops
lamie.lamie: Hello Kitty and Bazmaru! I also had a blanket long time ago! lol
Luna: Who's Bazmaru?
lamie.lamie: The little penguin a black one!! Hello Kitty's friend
Luna: OH! I love penguins!!!! They are my favorite :) So, how long did it take you to make this room?
lamie.lamie: like 2 days because I couldn't find the red doormats at the same time. lol I changed the room twice.
Luna: How did you get the idea to put Hello Kitty on the floor?
lamie.lamie: I just transformed into my own idea. Hello Kitty is square so it was easy, not too much of a shape to make. Are u understand me? :S
Luna: yes :) you are doing great
lamie.lamie: wOOt :P
Luna: w00t is the same in all languages :)
lamie.lamie: hehe i learned from here. They don’t teach me slang in English classes. :P
Luna: :) So what country are you from?
lamie.lamie: Argentina!! in South America
Luna: WOW!!!!! That's awesome
lamie.lamie: :DDD Don't tell me you love soccer!
Luna: lol What brought you to the United States hotel?
lamie.lamie: I live in Usa. I came to study English! One day I found this game and I can't stay away now -.-
Luna: :) I'm happy you’re having fun.
lamie.lamie: lol thanks = gracias!!!
Luna: de nada :)
lamie.lamie: :o lol. Luna = Moon
Luna: Si, that's why I picked it. Because the moon controls the oceans and I love the sea.
lamie.lamie: Wow, that is awesome!


Luna: So, what is your favorite Habbo game?
lamie.lamie: I have a modeling agency SMS in Fashion Room Floor. I'm always there we are a family. You can visit us one day. We will be happy see you there :P
Luna: That’s great, I would love to! What does SMS mean?
lamie.lamie: Super Models Scouts…models with discipline :P
Luna: How many models do you have there?
lamie.lamie: wow, I think like more that 30 and each day more and more
Luna: WOW!
lamie.lamie: I told u we are a family and we are growing…
Luna: Very cool, maybe there will be more models after this is published
lamie.lamie: lol yeah i bet.. :P We had a party when I finished the room.
Luna: What music do you listen to?
lamie.lamie: music??
Luna: yes :)
lamie.lamie: :o well not the music that a hello kitty girl will listen :P
Luna: LOL
lamie.lamie: I'm into North European Goth bands!!
Luna: Like H.I.M?
lamie.lamie: That's correct
Luna: yay! One point for Luna!
lamie.lamie: Finland Bands ROCKS!!! wOOt :P
Luna: H.I.M is the only one I've heard of
lamie.lamie: No no…you need to listen Nightwish They are super cool also Apocalytica ( they are 80's instrumental rock band)
Luna: ok, I'll look them up! Do you have a favorite movie?
lamie.lamie: I like foreign Movies!! More than the Hollywood ones :S Especially from Spain Country (Almodovar)
Luna: Me too! My favorite is Amelie :)
lamie.lamie: AMELIEEEEE
Luna: What is your favorite food here in America? (hard question I know)
lamie.lamie: OMG, fatty food in America. I like ICE Cream allll ice cream possibles! Hehe. Have you try Argentinean food?
Luna: I love steak from Argentina, yummy!
lamie.lamie: GAUCHO!!
Luna: And there is this green sauce....
lamie.lamie: chimichurri lol
Luna: YESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS
lamie.lamie: I will send you some by email!!
Luna: lol Can you cook?
lamie.lamie: I try :s but.. is not a good guaranty so I just cook for me
Luna: Me too...I'm not very good at all :)


Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
lamie.lamie: I have been playing since March 2006
Luna: :o me too!
lamie.lamie: reallly?
Luna: yes!
lamie.lamie: We are from the same generation of Habbos! :D
Luna: Cool! What’s your favorite Furni?
lamie.lamie: Favorite furni? Can I choose 2? :P
Luna: sure!
lamie.lamie: I like Pods they are cute!! And I like HC sofas :D
Luna: :) What color pod is your favorite?
lamie.lamie: The purple one!!!
Luna: I LOVE purple!!! If you could make a Furni what would you make?
lamie.lamie: :o I think I will create Kitchen stuff, like a range because we have fridge and mini bar but how do we cook??? So we need a range with oven and stuff.
Luna: yes! and maybe even a barbeque?
lamie.lamie: Yeah to grill steak hehe
Luna: LOL so, do you want to say hello to any of your Habbo friends?
lamie.lamie: :DDD My SMS Family, !darkami!, my Best friend berrymelon (check her rooms are awesome), randas88, .EMIR., zibochundam soad, aiko47 :P, Gothnebel my sister-in-law
Luna: Is that all of them?
lamie.lamie: I have a lot of friends but I don't remember their habbo names :o
Luna: Well, it was very nice to meet you!
lamie.lamie: same here Luna!!! Remember to visit SMS one day!
Luna: I will!
lamie.lamie: :D Thanks!!
Luna: Have a great night
lamie.lamie: Thanks Luna and Take care :D
