Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: ~H~ Welcome to Hogwarts!
Owner: Muttle

The staff crews have quieted down in the hotel, but in this week’s Staff Pick room, four divided groups continue to thrive: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Slytherin. That’s right, I paid a visit to Muttle’s ~H~ Welcome to Hogwarts! room and I was very impressed with the way he brought the Harry Potter vibe to Habbo Hotel. Muttle filled me on the classes, rooms and proffessors that are crucial in keeping ~H~ Hogwarts open for business. I even got sorted into a House! Read on for more:

Luna: So, congrats on winning Staff Pick
Muttle: thanks lol.
Luna: Tell me about Hogwarts
Muttle: Ok well this is another one of Habbo Hotel's newly established Hogwarts. We have classrooms, floors, common rooms etc. It's really quite big.
Luna: wow, so its not just this room?
Muttle: Oh good heavens no x]
Luna: lol…Fill me in!
Muttle: Well through the Teleporter on the right it leads to the Groundfloor. Where students are able to find their classrooms & common rooms.We have lemme see…I think 7 floors.
Luna: oh my word...
Muttle: Yup. We made it as realistic to the movie as possible.
Luna: Nice! Who are the students? Do they have to sign up?
Muttle: Yes they first must come here and be sorted into a House. A Sorter will be sitting on the pillow & roll the dice. Then we tell them to change their missions & uniform.
Luna: Where are the dice?
Muttle: Right in front of you x] watch. We're going to sort you, Luna, into a House. :D *rolls dice* 2!
Luna: ! What house am I in?
Muttle: Hufflepuff!
Luna: YES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! I love Hufflepuff! So what would my uniform be?
Muttle: Yellow Top, Black Bottoms, Black Shoes. Mission: ~H~ Hufflepuff 1st Year
Luna: Hmmmmm…wow
Muttle: And your Head of House is my good friend -Daylen. My pals Rysav & Disorder are also Headmaster/Mistress
Luna: Which house are you the head of?
Muttle: Oh I'm the Headmaser :]


Luna: So is this where you hold meetings and meals cause I’m starving!
Muttle: Yup. Let's see what we have.
Muttle: You can tell which table to sit at by the colored rugs below them.
Luna: Oh Hufflepuff is yellow. Yay! I feel like Hermione!
Muttle: lol she's in Gryffindor though.
Luna: oh :(
Muttle: Ah yes I also have 2 pets here :] Mrs.Norris & Fluffy.
Luna: oh like in the movie!
Muttle: yeah x] Fluffy is the 3-headed dog & Mrs.Norris is Filch's cat.
Luna: So clever…so is Harry Potter your Favorite movie?
Muttle: Actually I'm not a totally way big super duper fan of HarryPotter. But I like the books & movies :D
Luna: It appears that way...
Muttle: x]
Luna: if you were one of the characters in the movie who would you be and why?
Muttle: Hmmmm that's a toughy. Hmmmm...
Luna: you can do it...
Muttle: Well I always thought Nevil Longbottom was a funny guy x]
Luna: well there you go :) What is wrong with Fluffy???
Muttle: That's Mrs.Norris's food!
Luna: He's eating the cats food!
Muttle: Strange pets -.-"
Fluffy: hrum, hrum, crunch!
Mrs.Norris: snuh..snuh
Luna: Hogwarts is a strange place..
Muttle: A suprise behind every corner :D


Luna: how long have you been a Habbo?
Muttle: Well I've been a Habbo for about eeeh 4 & a half years now I believe. Started on UK then came here :D
Luna: cool!!!!!!!!! What is your favorite Furni?
Muttle: Red Pillow
Luna: And if you could invent a piece of Furni, what would it be?
Muttle: It wouldn’t be in invention so much but it would be a leash that you can use to walk your pets in public rooms.
Luna: Awesome. What is your favorite band?
Muttle: Van Halen!
Luna: what about favorite tv show?
Muttle: Hmmm I don't watch a whole lot of tv but I looove CSI.
Luna: K-fed's gonna be on CSI! :-/
Muttle: Who?
Luna: Kevin Federline!
Muttle: OH!
Luna: hehe crazy huh
Muttle: I think there's a new season soon so I'll be tivoing that :]
Luna: nice. So, what is your favorite food you serve here at Hogwarts
Muttle: I'm quite fond of the ham. Moses likes the ham too.
