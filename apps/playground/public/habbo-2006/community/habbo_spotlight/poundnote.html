Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Chinese Wonders..
Owner: PoundNote

This week's interviewee comes to us from Jolly Ole England, although his room looks like something straight out of Shanghai! My new friend PoundNote was nice enough to invite me into his Asain inspired digs, and here's what I found out about him, his room and his mum:


PoundNote: Im shakin’, I’m so excited to hear something good… :)
Luna: Are you excited to win Staff Pick??
PoundNote: OMG! THANKS! I’m I’m.. I don’t know what to say omg
Luna: hehe
PoundNote: Well, the room was my mum’s idea ;)
Luna: really?
PoundNote: She loves Chinese
Luna: Is she a Habbo?
PoundNote: Nope
Luna: She's just your personal design assistant?
PoundNote: yup :)
Luna: hehe, that's pretty sweet :) I love the three dragons in the center of the room…
PoundNote: :o thanx. So what made u pick this room?
Luna: Well, the dragons for one and also the wonderful use of Asian Furni. You and your mom make a talented team!
PoundNote: :o thanks
Luna: And I also loved how you have your pets Woof and Meow in here to watch over the dragons.
PoundNote: yeah :) My real cat says meow :)
Luna: lol. Tell him/her meow from me!
PoundNote: She’s called Ying and my other one is called Yang, my mum named them :) Chinese again…ying = girl in Chinese and yang = boy in Chinese.
Luna: And together they bring balance to the household, yeah?
PoundNote: Balance?
Luna: Yeah, “ying yang” symbolizes balance
PoundNote: Not really, they are noisy :P
Luna: LOL
PoundNote: OMG…Eww. Ying just Farted
Luna: Nasty!
PoundNote: I know :l


Luna: Have you been participating in Confucius' quests?
PoundNote: I’ve tried :( I don’t understand them.
Luna: They're hard, huh?!
PoundNote: Yeah they r annoying :D Asian sofa next :P
Luna: Only if you Habbos learn Confucius' lesson! You should give this week’s quest a shot!
PoundNote: Cool. I might :)
Luna: So let's learn more about YOU!
PoundNote: hmm well what else you wanna know :P
Luna: What's your fave band/singer?
PoundNote: Probably 50-cent. You?
Luna: Led Zepplin!!
PoundNote: O.o I don’t know them Lol
Luna: lol, i hope you're joking!
PoundNote: nope :D
Luna: They are classic rock
PoundNote: I’m not into classic :D
Luna: yea, 50 isn't quite classic.
PoundNote: Nope :D
Luna: What's your fave TV program?
PoundNote: Eastenders - its a soap in the UK
Luna: And movie?
PoundNote: IRobot. I could watch it 10,000 times in a row :) Or not :/
Luna: What's your favorite food?
PoundNote: Fish &...Chips!
Luna: With malt vinegar or without?
PoundNote: LOADS AND MILLIONS OF SALT AND VINEGAR! :D
Luna: I love the vinegar!
PoundNote: ME TOO xD
PoundNote: mmm, shh, your making me hungry >:(
Luna: lol


Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
PoundNote: I dunno since 2002-2003
Luna: what is your fave Furni?
PoundNote: Guess its got something to do with dragons :)
Luna: lol yea that’s what I would’ve guessed :) And if you could invent a piece of Furni, what would it be?
PoundNote: The Habbo bunk bed
Luna: awesome!
PoundNote: Or a swimming pool :D
Luna: Like a kiddy pool for guest rooms?
PoundNote: Naw one in the floor. It would be like a carpet
Luna: oooh like a petal patch!
PoundNote: Yeah :D
Luna: NICE great idea. Ok, any shout outs before I go?
PoundNote: hmm :) Hello Cress! And hello Adam and Faith :)
Luna: What about your mum?
PoundNote: Hi Mum! :P
Luna: Well, it has been nice meeting you and talking to you.
PoundNote: Yep! And u :)
Luna: I'll see you here on Friday at 4pm PST for the Staff Pick party
PoundNote: oki


If you like dragons and dancing, then you won't want to miss this week's Staff Pick TGIF celebration in PoundNote's Chinese Wonders.. room. See you there!
