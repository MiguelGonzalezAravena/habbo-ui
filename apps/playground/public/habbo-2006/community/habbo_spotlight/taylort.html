Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: TAYLOR’S BASEBALL STADIUM
Owner: Taylor.T

Welcome to Taylor.T’s room! It’s a marvel of Habbo Designing, replicating almost exactly the feel and look of a classic baseball stadium. Check out the interview of Taylor.T with Luna!

Taylor.T: I am a bit nervous about this
Luna: No need to be nervous!
Taylor.T: :)
Luna: it will be fun and easy, I promise
Taylor.T: That's good!
Luna: So, are you a baseball fan?
Taylor.T: I love to play baseball
Taylor.T: I play on the school league
Luna: oh, you play! How awesome
Taylor.T: I would rather play it than watch it.
Luna: do you ever go to games?
Taylor.T: I have been to a couple Indians games
Luna: is that your favorite team?
Taylor.T: The Cleveland Indians, of course
Luna: What position do you play?
Taylor.T: I play pitcher and first base
Luna: Nice! I love playing softball, but I am not too good at it...
Luna: I like swinging the bat though...
Taylor.T: I have played with my cousins since I was small.
Taylor.T: and my Dad always played.
Luna: wow, nice. A family tradition
Luna: Do you ever host baseball games in this room?
Taylor.T: Not yet.
Luna: You should definitely host one, although I’m not sure how it would work.
Taylor.T: This is originally a sports arena for the sports competition
Taylor.T: and just turned it into a baseball stadium
Taylor.T: If there's any habbo teams that want to play here
Taylor.T: they are very welcome to use it.
Luna: well, tis the season for baseball so it's perfect!
Taylor.T: Yes, that's why I picked baseball
Luna: cool! Well, you will probably gonna get some f/r's after this is in the newsie
Taylor.T: That's fine!
Taylor.T: I love sharing my rooms
Taylor.T: I love making rooms!
Luna: your rooms are great! I loved the one you created for Easter Mystery

Luna: What is your fave piece of furni to decorate with?
Taylor.T: I guess I am sort of addicted to habbo.
Luna: lol, nothing wrong with that!
Taylor.T: Oh, gosh!
Luna: as long as you're having fun!
Taylor.T: the mochamaster is my very favorite
Taylor.T: if I had to pick one.
Taylor.T: I like the pink candy also.
Luna: Nice choices!
Taylor.T: I love it when a new item is added to the catalog.
Luna: if you could choose any rare to reappear in the catalogue
Luna: other than the mochamaster
Luna: what would it be :)
Taylor.T: Hmm, that’s a tough one!
Taylor.T: Let me think about that one!
Luna: ok, we'll come back to that
Luna: let's talk about YOU

Luna: who is your favorite band?
Taylor.T: Well, I like to listen to Green Day,
Taylor.T: but I really like country music
Luna: cool, so do I!
Luna: who is your fave?
Luna: I love Sugarland and Keith Urban
Taylor.T: I love Carrie Underwood's
Luna: nice, an AI alum!
Taylor.T: Jesus, Take the Wheel.
Luna: Who are you rooting for on Idol this season?
Taylor.T: Well, I like Ace Young
Taylor.T: but, since he's out, I might have to go with Katherine McPhee
Luna: Yea, she's pretty good
Taylor.T: Taylor is a little too crazy for me.
Luna: I was guessing you'd pick him cause of the name...
Taylor.T: He's seems a little old too for Idol
Luna: he does...a bit too odd
Taylor.T: Not quite the right image!
Luna: he makes me laugh though
Taylor.T: I agree, he's funny
Taylor.T: Chris is good too!
Luna: speaking of TV, what's your fave show?
Luna: I HEART Chris :)
Taylor.T: Right now, it's American Idol.
Luna: YES! good answer ;)
Taylor.T: And I love Seventh Heaven
Taylor.T: Maybe that's strange
Luna: ooooh I haven't seen that show in ages
Luna: I didn’t even know it was still on the air
Taylor.T: but I think it has a lot of good advice and morals for kids.
Luna: that is always good
Taylor.T: There's only one more episode and then it's off the air.
Luna: wow! I had no idea
Taylor.T: I like mysteries and medical shows
Luna: Do you like House?
Taylor.T: It's ok. I only watched part of it once.
Taylor.T: I don't watch a lot of TV
Luna: that’s my favorite Medical show...it’s a little mature though

Luna: what about school? What’s your fave subject?
Taylor.T: I love math. I am actually homed schooled
Taylor.T: My mom was a school teacher.
Taylor.T: So my hours are pretty flexible
Luna: cool! Any special plans for Mother's Day?
Luna: my mom’s a school teacher too
Taylor.T: We usually get Mom hanging baskets for the front porch
Taylor.T: and take her out to eat.
Luna: what is your favorite food???
Taylor.T: Spaghetti
Taylor.T: for sure!
Luna: with meatballs? ;)
Taylor.T: Of course!
Taylor.T: and lots of sauce
Luna: Delish! and cheese!!
Taylor.T: And Lemon Pie for dessert
Luna: mmmmmmmmmmmmm with meringue!
Taylor.T: Lots of meringue!

Luna: how long did it take you to build this room?
Taylor.T: Oh, I don't know
Taylor.T: Off and on, probably a couple hours
Taylor.T: I keep changing things in it.
Luna: I hope you leave the room up all summer long
Luna: we can host the Habbo world series in here!
Taylor.T: I probably will. I hope it gets lots of use.
Taylor.T: May I say one more thing?
Taylor.T: I want to congratulate SomebodysMom for winning the Habbowood
Luna: Cool, that's sweet
Taylor.T: Movie Contest.
Taylor.T: And also the other 9 finalists.
Luna: I’m sure they'll appreciate that!
Luna: do you have any other shout outs?
Taylor.T: Oh, I have so many Good Friends on Habbo
Taylor.T: One of my oldest Habbo friends is NickyZang
Taylor.T: Hello! Nicky!
Luna: Hi Nicky!
Luna: lol
Luna: cool
Luna: thank you so much for chatting with me!

And that's that for this week's Staff Pick of the Week! Be sure to check out the TGIF Party at the Staff Pick Room!
