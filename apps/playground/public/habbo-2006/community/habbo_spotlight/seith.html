Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: Solitary Falls
Owner: Seith

This week, in honor of the esteemed Petal Patch being in the catalog (only until Friday!), we have a great room showcasing the use of the awesome Petal Patch, by Seith, aptly named Solitary Falls. With a great waterfall made from Green Fountains, it’s truly an example of great stacking skills colliding with great designing skills. Read more about Seith and his great room…
