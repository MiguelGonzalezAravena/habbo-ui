Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Mount Habbo
Owner: T-K-O

With Thanksgiving right around the corner, it was refreshing to chat with a Habbo who is truly thankful for all the right things. I chose T-K-O’s Mount Habbo because the design was breathtaking, but after interviewing him I was happy to learn that the Habbo behind the design was really nice as well! T-K-O totally reminded me what the upcoming holiday is all about, and shockingly enough, it isn’t the food. Read on to find out what this family-loving Habbo had to say:


Luna: Hi!!!
T-K-O: Hello Luna. =)
Luna: How are you today?
T-K-O: Fine. How are you?
Luna: I'm great thanks...congrats on being selected!
T-K-O: Thank you for choosing me. xD I really appreciate this.
Luna: This room is simply incredible…the mountain is HUGE!
T-K-O: Thank you very much. I thought you hated it. xD
Luna: You thought I hated it??? Why??
T-K-O: Well, I just thought that this room wasn't good enough that's all.
Luna: Oh it is definitely good enough...it’s amazing!
T-K-O: Thank you. Thank goodness I didn't tear down this room today. xD Phew!
Luna: Yea, that would have been a mucho mistake
T-K-O: Yep. Mucho would be the correct word lol.
Luna: You should keep it up all winter so that other Habbos can enjoy it especially Habbos that don’t live in places with snow.
T-K-O: Don't worry. Now that I know I won, this room will be up for the rest of this year, if not, forever.
Luna: Cool! Glad to hear it.


Luna: How many snow patches are there?
T-K-O: Believe it or not, it only took a total of 90 Snow Patches. xD
Luna: Only 90...GEEZ lol
T-K-O: Yes, I know. Funny thing about this room is that I hate snow in real life. I started collecting Snow Patches ever since they were released as a Rare. One day, my friends told me that I should make a mountain out of them.
Luna: Cool. What inspired the room?
T-K-O: My siblings and good friends inspired me to create Mount Habbo.
Luna: Are you a pro stacker or did you learn for the mountain?
T-K-O: My brother (Elite-n00b) actually stacked the mountain for me. It is very easy to stack too. Don't let the size of the mountain fool you.
Luna: lol, well I bet he will be getting a HUGE shout out at the end of the interview…
T-K-O: Of course. xD
Luna: So, why do you hate snow?
T-K-O: Well, snow is VERY cold. I also slipped on black ice before while I was in Utah. I fell on my back and dropped all of my books. xD
Luna: lol I mean OUCH
T-K-O: Sounds like it would hurt a lot, but it didn't. Lucky me. xD
Luna: Lucky you is right!


Luna: So what are the other parts of the room all about =)
T-K-O: Habbos that visit Mount Habbo can stay at one of the four rooms that are inside this big room. The room you are in now is where Habbos can hang out to play with toys and watch cartoons. It's a room designed for little kids basically.
Luna: Nice, but I’m not a little kid and I dig it!
T-K-O: Haha. xD That's awesome. It was designed for kids, but adults can hang out here as well. You can help take care of the kids here or something. xD
Luna: hahahaha...I'd rather play, thanks. ;)
T-K-O: That's cool too. =) I also have a restaurant inside this room. Did you know that the food here is free for everyone?
Luna: NICE!
T-K-O: You can click on the stickies to see what I serve here at this restaurant.
Luna: mmmmmmmmmmmm, I’ll have one pad thai please and the Lobster
T-K-O: *cooks some pad thai* There you go. It's kind of spicy though. xD
Luna: I like spicy…keeps me warm in all this snow.
T-K-O: *throws a live lobster at you*
Luna: oh snap
T-K-O: Haha. xD Sorry about that. I didn't think it was going to pinch your nose.
Luna: Ouch :( What’s this room (see image below)?


T-K-O: This is where all the cool Habbos hang out.
Luna: It’s my fave, I must admit.
T-K-O: In this room, you can dance and listen to music. This is my favorite room too. It's a great room to meet Habbos.
Luna: Awesome. How long have you been a Habbo?
T-K-O: I have been playing Habbo for around 11 months now.
Luna: What’s your favorite thing about it?
T-K-O: I think my favorite thing about Habbo is the fact that you get to decorate your own rooms. I love how the Furniture looks.
Luna: What’s your favorite one?
T-K-O: My favorite Furni would have to be a Throne. The Throne seat, not the Throne Sofa.
Luna: That’s my fave too!
T-K-O: Yay! xD
Luna: Yay! And if you could invent a Furni, what would it be?
T-K-O: Umm...Probably a Habbo Popcorn Machine. I think that would be cool if Habbos could hang out in a room and eat popcorn and drink cola and watch movies all at the same time.
Luna: YES definitely...speaking of movies, what's your favorite?
T-K-O: Umm....Probably Lord of the Rings 1, 2, and 3. The whole set. xD
Luna: Nice, I only managed to stay awake during the third one…too long for me. Gotta love Frodo Baggins though.
T-K-O: Haha. Frodo? He became corrupt at the end. Sam is my favorite character.
Luna: Sam is a sweetie
T-K-O: Yes. I like people that are honest, sincere, and kind.
Luna: ahhhhh me too me too. So, what about music, what is your favorite band?
T-K-O: I listen to all types of music. I don't really have a favorite band. I mostly listen to country and pop though. xD
Luna: How about favorite food...I'm a total foodie!
T-K-O: Yeah, me too. xD My favorite food is probably pizza.
Luna: Fave toppings? Gotta ask…hehe
T-K-O: My favorite topping is spicy Italian sausage.
Luna: Yum, good choice!


Luna: So, are you excited for Thanksgiving?
T-K-O: Yes. I love Thanksgiving. I'm not talking about the food either. In my opinion, people should be celebrating Thanksgiving everyday. It's always good to reflect on the things that we take for granted and learn to appreciate them.
Luna: yea, very true. Who are you most thankful to this year?
T-K-O: I'm thankful for my family. I love them so much.
Luna: So nice! And how about the food....any favorites there?
T-K-O: You mean Thanksgiving food? If so, mashed potatoes are probably my favorite. xD
Luna: uh oh....we have a disagreement...lol
T-K-O: Haha. xD Oh no. =( What's your favorite? Pumpkin pie?
Luna: How did you know?!?!?!?!?!?!?!
T-K-O: Just a lucky guess. xD
Luna: nice! So, do you have any shout outs?
T-K-O: Yes, I do!
Luna: Go for it!
T-K-O: I would like thank my little sister Miss.1337, my little brother Elite-n00b. Plinith, Retoxicated, thisbeme2, GroovyGreen, and sk8terk.
Luna: cool!
T-K-O: A special thanks to all of my other friends as well. They know who they are.
Luna: Well, it was SO nice meeting you.
T-K-O: Thank you so much Luna. I appreciate this. My pleasure. I finally got the chance to meet you lol.
Luna: Stay "cool" lol Sorry, couldn’t resist
T-K-O: Haha. xD Nice.
Luna: You'll be around for the party on Friday right?
T-K-O: Of course. :)
Luna: Perfect!

There’s something for everyone at Mount Habbo, so put on some snowshoes and meet us there on Friday, November 17 at 4pm PST.
