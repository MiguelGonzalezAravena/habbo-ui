Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Room Name: Garden of Love
Room Owner: Susie

Kedo: What kind of music were you listening to when you built this room?
Susie: I was listening to Ryan Seacrest's top 40. Woot! I wonder what the #1 will be next week!
iomegadrive: What was the most challenging part of designing this room?
Susie: I would have to say the stacking around the Asian screens. It was very time consuming, but well worth it when the room was finished. :D
Luna: About how long did it take you to build your room from start to finish?
Susie: 3 or 4 hours...collecting the furni was the hardest part. That took a couple of weeks.
RocketSkates: If you had to imagine a story that could take place in your room, what would happen in it?
Susie: I would imagine a fairy tale where a prince would meet his princess.They would both drink love potion from the Candy Cauldron.Then the cupids arrow would hit them. They would fall in love and live happily ever after. :D
Whimsy: If you could add a feature to Habbo, for either HC or norms, for public or private rooms, what would it be?
Susie: I think we should be able to ban people from our own rooms. People who flood and harrass.
Kedo: If you could meet one person (dead or alive), who would you choose and why?
Susie: My grandmother because she died when my dad was very young.
iomegadrive: How many oranges do you eat in a typical week?
Susie: Maybe one or two, I prefer apples myself. :)
Luna: If you could have any animal as a pet, which animal would you choose?
Susie: In real life I would say a puppy because I already have 2 cats. On habbo, I wish there was budgie birds. :D Wouldn't that be cool, a budgie bird flying around this room? LOL
RocketSkates: If you could be any TV Show character, who would it be and why?
Susie: Thats a hard question. I don't watch much tv besides reality shows. So I am going to say Paul Abdul...so I could judge and watch American Idol up close. :) Then I can say a few words to Simon Cowell. >:)
Whimsy: If you had a super power what would it be and why?
Susie: Regeneration :) Imagine healing ourselves in seconds. :o That would be awesome.
