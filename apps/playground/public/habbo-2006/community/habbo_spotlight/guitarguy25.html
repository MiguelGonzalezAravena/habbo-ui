Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Downtown Underground
Owner: guitarguy25

After hitting the slopes last week, I was ready for a major change of scene this week…so, instead of taking the ski lift up, I took the escalator down to guitarguy25’s Downtown Underground room. His subterranean cross-section just screams "fun" and offers lots of nooks for everyone to chill in. He’s even built an above ground garden for those who prefer to stay out of gritty underground subway stations! I caught up with guitarguy25 after his Habboween candy binge, and here’s what went down…


Luna: Hi!
guitarguy25: Happy candy hangover day :P
Luna: lol, you too! What candy did you O.D. on last night?
guitarguy25: hmm.. just about every candy ever invented. Mostly Milky Way because I love those. Hmm, I basically got scared half to death by a six year old.
Luna: NICE, how?
guitarguy25: lol, I was walking around the corner of my house and he jumped out and he scared me half to death!
Luna: That would have scared me too, that’s why I stayed home!
guitarguy25: lol
Luna: I watched The Shining!
guitarguy25: :O me too! except a few days ago. REDRUM! REDRUMMM
Luna: REDRUM
guitarguy25: :O You’re scaring me!
Luna: lol So, tell me a little about this room.
guitarguy25: Well, I started out putting down the big X of track and I just built things out from there. I just did what felt right and it turned out to look great.
Luna: An organic room builder...
guitarguy25: yup, hippie-ish. Habbo hippie.
Luna: lol So what does each corner represent?
guitarguy25: Well, I kind of made it after Underground Atlanta cause I've been there before. The carousel thing is kinda like a subway. This room behind me is sort of like a security room. The bathroom is nothing more than...A bathroom. That room over there is just some room, somewhere to put the pets. OH and the petals are outside above ground.
Luna: SO COOL! What’s that area at the top of the stairs?
guitarguy25: That area is just the entrance, kind of where security guards would stand and make sure nobody too wierd is coming in.
Luna: Like me?
guitarguy25: :O noooo never! More like iomega
Luna: And brown-pants? lol
guitarguy25: and him xD No offense guys! Personally, I like the subway the best. I’ll ride it around and around till I get sick.
Luna: Awesome, do you ever have any parties in here?
guitarguy25: I have a few, but usually it levels off at about 15 people and dies down, unfortunately :P
Luna: Well, I'd bump up the room limit to 50 for Friday!
guitarguy25: I think it already is so I’m one step ahead!
Luna: Perfect!
guitarguy25: Theres only one bad thing about this room… there's a furni limit and it’s full.


guitarguy25: My cat is one day old today! Oh wait, 365 days in a year…He’s 356. Close though! Luna: lol very close
guitarguy25: I should have a little birthday party for him that would be funny.
Luna: you should…that’s what Friday can be!
guitarguy25: lol yeah!
Luna: BUT we don’t want a cat stealing your thunder…
guitarguy25: psh, duh! And I’m tired of hearing candy names anyway cause it makes me sick to my stomach after Halloween
Luna: lol poor Trix
guitarguy25: Twix, the bunnies are named Trix.
Twix: snuh..snuh
guitarguy25: AHHH! You made the train crash! Way to go!
Luna: omg I think I might be sick…
guitarguy25: Time for some ham! *squeezes mustard*
Luna: lol
guitarguy25: I got some rolaids if you need some! yum, my favorite dessert.
Luna: lol, nice! *Heads over to bathtub*
guitarguy25: :O *Sits on toilet*
Luna: :o
guitarguy25: :O turn around
Luna: lol NASTY
guitarguy25: okay... awkward, moving along...where to now?
Luna: Let’s go up to the above ground part.


guitarguy25: This is the above ground, kinda flowery… I guess we can say this is the park
Luna: very flowery. Smells yummy up here, unlike down there…
guitarguy25: My bad, let’s shut the bathroom door and turn on the fan.
Luna: LOL. There, much better. So, how long have you been a Habbo?
guitarguy25: well, I started Habbo back in May of 2005
Luna: wow, long-timer
guitarguy25: yup, Habbo Addict...
Luna: What’s your favorite thing to do on Habbo?
guitarguy25: I like all the great people I meet and all the great friends you make and the awesome parties and activities.
Luna: Cool! What about favorite Furni?
guitarguy25: Hmm...That’s a tough one, but I'd have to say Petal. They're just so....Green! Like money…
Luna: Do you know what im gonna ask next?
guitarguy25: Yep
Luna: hehe, what?
guitarguy25: What Furni would I want there to be on Habbo? Or something along the lines of that, I came prepared ;D
Luna: lol youve studied up.
guitarguy25: I would either want a retro jukebox...or a fireworks shooter that kinda looked like the smoke machine but instead of smoke there’d be fireworks.
Luna: omg, that would be so cool, I love fireworks!
guitarguy25: :O pyro! Jk me too. *Drops a fire breathing Dragon right in front of Luna*
Luna: lol
guitarguy25: you know you want to play with the fire, Luna. You know you want to!
Luna: LOL Nooooooooooooooooooooooooooo
guitarguy25: aww you hurt his feelings.
Luna: ahhhhh Dragon, I’m sorry.
guitarguy25: Dragons have feelings too.
Luna: I always get into situations with the dragons of Staff Pick…
guitarguy25: lol yeah you dated one last time or something?
Luna: NO, but he was checking me out…
guitarguy25: lol ohh You should go out with him
Luna: lol not my type
guitarguy25: Or we could hook our Dragons up!
Luna: YES, it was Emilburp’s dragon...talk to him ;)
guitarguy25: ha ha ha. I will


Luna: what is your fave band?
guitarguy25: I’m into classic rock. And I really like Boston.
Luna: nice! Favorite movie?
guitarguy25: Forrest Gump!My console mish says like a box of chocolates...OMG. I tried out for the school play and Im Tweedle Dee! ha ha ha! And Tweedle Dumb is like my best friend.
Luna: NICE!!!!!!! I love Alice in Wonderland. Are you an actor?
guitarguy25: sorta. I was in the last play
Luna: Cool, do you sing?
guitarguy25: Not so much! Twix would run and hide if I started singing.
Luna: lol, me too… I don’t know if you heard me on bobba music yesterday...
guitarguy25: Unfortunately no
Luna: We were all singing the time warp, it was bad… so, have any shout outs?
guitarguy25: Yes To all my friends especially .:lotsoflove:. See Geena? I told you I'd say it!
Luna: nice
guitarguy25: Well, I guess we both have to be getting on our ways cause my moms screaming at me! I’ll last about 2 more minutes…
Luna: Well, thanks so much for chatting.
guitarguy25: Thank you! I’m still shocked that I got picked xD
Luna: This room is awesome!
guitarguy25: Thanks, Ill see you Friday!
Luna: For sure
guitarguy25: For shizzle!
Luna: Now go before you get in trouble!
guitarguy25: Bye my nizzle! Lunizzle shizzle bizzle
Luna: have a good night!
guitarguy25: you too-izzle!


Come party it up in guitarguy25’s Downtown Underground on Friday, November 3 at 4pm PST/ 7pm EST.
