Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Room Name: STARTALK SHOW
Owner:GrimSP

This week I was looking for something completely different for Staff Pick…I chose GrimSP’s STARTALK SHOW ROOM because a. I LOVE late-night talk shows and b. I felt like it. I had a great time turning the tables on Grim SP and interviewing HIM for once. We even had a crazy, unexpected drop-in guest. You’ll have to read on to see who dropped by:

GrimSP: Hi Luna
Luna: Howdy! Congrats on winning staff pick!
GrimSP: Thanks, glad to win it especially on the first week of school:D
Luna: How's that going?
GrimSP: Going good
Luna: Good! So, tell me about this talk show of yours…I'm intrigued...
GrimSP: Well Startalk Show is like any other late-nite show like The Tonight Show, where me as the host has celebrities over as guests. Unlike most tv shows he celebrities on this show shows the satire of actually them for example you have, Larry the Cable Guy. People think he's a bad actor but people bring him in cause he was in CARS. But in this show Larry the Cable Guy would be like what he really is. He'd be saying annoying quotes and that’s where most of the laughs are from.
Luna: Interesting…
GrimSP: Thanks
Luna: Of all the late night hosts, who influences your style the most?
GrimSP: Conan O' Brien
Luna: He's my favorite too!
GrimSP: lol cool.
Luna: I love Triumph
GrimSP: Never heard of Triumph
Luna: The insult comic dog! He's awesome!
GrimSP: Maybe I'll check that out sometime

Luna: So, who is the funniest guest you've had on Startalk?
GrimSP: Well most are who everyone expects. We had Tom Cruise promoting his film Mission Impossible 3. In fact, he did his own stunt…
Luna: Did he jump on your sofa?
GrimSP: Yep and jumped on it and kept saying Katie Holmes’s name
Luna: niiiiiice
Luna: Who else?
GrimSP: Jackie Chan who does a skit called CRAZY STUNTS and this guest, she's not a celebrity but in fact a celebrity in Habbo, her name is ScribeX.
Luna: yes, yes ScribeX...very cool! Do a lot of people show up for the show?
GrimSP: Yep. Like 25-35, sometimes 40 if we're doing a MEGA GIVE
Luna: Wow, that's a lot
GrimSP: yea lol
Luna: What's a Mega Give?
GrimSP: Well we giveaway prizes, mainly RARES, that are popular. We gave away a Marquee last year!
Luna: Wow. You just randomly choose a Habbo to give it to?
GrimSP: yea, mainly non HCs
Luna: that's nice of you. So, how come I’ve never been invited to be a guest on your show...
GrimSP: :o that’s a cool idea. Never thought up that
Luna: :o! If I was a guest on your show, what would you ask me?
GrimSP: The first question would be when did you start playing Habbo?
Luna: Well that’s not very juicy info!!! lol
GrimSP: lol

Luna: What was the inspiration for the design of this room?
GrimSP: Well at first Startalk Show was just one of many rooms that were like sketch shows. Then when version 9 came out all I could think up was...MODE. And plus I love z-shelves
Luna: Cool - the wall decorations are a bit random...
GrimSP: yea well like the show, it’s also random :D
Luna: lol makes sense then! So, let's learn a little about you...
GrimSP: ok cool
Luna: Who is your favorite band/musician?
GrimSP: My favorite band is Queen and my favorite musician is Weird Al.
Luna: Fave tv show?? For some reason I’m guessing family guy…
GrimSP: lol yea Family Guy is one of my favorite shows. Most of my favorite shows are comedy and animated and 24 and House M.D. are also part of my favorite shows
Luna: Ok, how about your favorite food in the whole world... GrimSP: Pizza and French fries, which a lot of people say are a bad combination…
Luna: mmmmmmm, what do you dip your French fries in?
GrimSP: To tell you the truth, never dipped a French fry in any sauce.
Luna: Whoa, that's nuts!
GrimSP: lol
Luna: What’s your fave pizza topping?
GrimSP: Cheese
Luna: So you’re a very simple eater, no sauce no toppings…
GrimSP: yep very simple
Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
GrimSP: I've been in Habbo since June 2005
Luna: Cool, and what's your fave Furni?
GrimSP: Z-shelves, mode sofas, and lodge bars
Luna: if you could invent a piece of Furni, what would it be?
GrimSP: If I could invent a piece of Furni it would be blue ICED Furni or maybe a Furni where you can change clothes without going to HV.
Luna: Like a changing room?
GrimSP: yep

GrimSP: Oh wait getting a messege from Startalk Staff *cell phone* please don't let him in here
STS.VIP.1: GITT-R-DONE
Luna: oh no!!! Larry the Cable Guy!!!
GrimSP: Larry the Cable Guy the show is not on tonight...
STS.VIP.1: GITT-R-DONE
GrimSP: Stop saying that, at least please say something else...
STS.VIP.1: Watch my movie, THE HEALTH INSPECTOR!
STS.VIP.1: GITT-R-DONE!
Luna: lol
GrimSP: Sorry about that. Celebrities find ways to get in here
Luna: Any shout outs before I go?
GrimSP: I’d like to give a out to most of my friends and my Family and ..Hoopz..
Luna: Sounds good! GITT ER DONE!
GrimSP: lol
Luna: ok, well thanks for your time
GrimSP: Nice to have you here Luna
Luna: Hope we get some awesome celebs in here for the TGIF party!
GrimSP: ok

Larry the Cable Guy said it all…GITT ER DONE HABBOS! Come to GrimSP’s STARTALK SHOW room for what I’m sure will be a TGIF party we’ll all remember!
