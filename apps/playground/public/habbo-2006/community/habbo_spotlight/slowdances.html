Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: #1 Habbo Boot Camp -- Training Facility
Owner: Slowdances

After lazing around my Lair all weekend long and going through an entire Pura Fridge worth of food, I decided it was about time I got my pixelated booty in tip-top shape! After all, I am representing the greatest gang in Habbo (FFF Forever). The #1 Habbo Boot Camp -- Training Facility was just the place I was looking for and my personal trainer, Slowdances had me running and sweating up a storm in no time at all! Slowdances's Boot Camp is the perfect place to work out, hang with friends and grab a nutritious bite to eat. BUT, she offers all who come to work and play a couple words of warning - if you plan on visiting make sure to read her safety stickies before you enter!

I had a great time working out and chatting with Slowdances. Here's how the conversation went:

Slowdances: Hey Luna, are you ready to work out?
Luna: Hey! I am sooooo ready to work out!
Slowdances: Good :) Because you're gonna sweat :P
Luna: lol. This place looks AWESOME.
Slowdances: THANKS! :P
Luna: Tell me about the room!
Slowdances: Welllllll..This is a training camp for all of the gangs! FFF, TEO, SFK (My favorite, sorry) train here :D
Luna: nice!
Slowdances: This is an obstacle course and the snow is a cold jogging path. BRRRR.
Luna: BRRRR is right!
Slowdances: ;) Also we have certain rules here. We're looking out for EVERYONE's safety, like if an Emo Orange comes when they’re not supposed to we're not responsible if they get squeezed and possibly juiced.
Luna: lol you should make them sign a waiver!
Slowdances: We're also not responsible if an FFF gets their new outfit squirted with orange juice.
Luna: :O
Slowdances: :O is right. And we're definitely not responsible for JFFs hair buns getting messy. We want all of our trainees to be safe :)
Luna: What about the SFKs?
Slowdances: The SFKS work out under cover so no gangs can stop them :P They have an invisible sticky. Only S.F.K glasses can see. Sorry about that ;)
Luna: That's pretty awesome, but SFK glasses aren’t too fashionable…
Slowdances: Haha. I’m okay with that -- What the gangs learn here helps them take over the hotel.. THEIR OWN WAY! :O


Luna: Well, let's start sweatin’
Slowdances: okay
Luna: whoa *has some trouble completing the obstacle course*
Slowdances: *completes obstacle course* haha! FFFs need to get sneakers on instead of those high heels ;) This is after all.. a boot camp!
Luna: lol *finally finishes*
Slowdances: *Claps* Well done
Luna: *sweats*
Slowdances: Up for a cold jog? ;)
Luna: ok
Slowdances: Woooo *Sweats*
Luna: sorry, broke a heel on the way
Slowdances: haha

 


Slowdances: Over here is the dining hall :) Nutritional foods for all. No oranges. ;)
Luna: well, eating well IS fashionable. *holding nonfat frozen yogurt*
Slowdances: :o Don’t get food on your sweater
Luna: lol, I’ll try not too :)
Slowdances: :)
Luna: So, I want to hear more about you!
Slowdances: Wellllll, I run a lot of games here on Habbo. Like my Christmas game, that’s basically what I do :)
Luna: Christmas!!!! Thats right, you're the Grinch!
Slowdances: :D YES! I like music too. So bobba radio helps.
Luna: Who's your fave band?
Slowdances: Hmmm, that’s a toughie…Dashboard Confessional
Luna: nice
Slowdances: and yours?
Luna: ummmm Led Zepplin
Slowdances: Good choice
Luna: i also like this new band called Under the Influence of Giants
Slowdances: Hmmm. Never heard of them but I’m sure they’re good if you like them. FFFs tend to have good taste.
Luna: ahhhhh thanks :)
Slowdances: :) Wanna put your fashionable knowledge to the test? :o
Luna: sure!
Slowdances: Critique my outfit :)
Luna: I give it an A+! Your pink hoodie goes flawlessly with your fashionable bob haircut
Slowdances: THANKS :D I’m not in my S.F.K gear right now
Luna: I’m ok with that.


Slowdances: So whats your fav. activity here at boot camp?
Luna: I like ice jogging the snow patches are the best
Slowdances: Me too. It’s extremely "COOL"
Luna: hehe…what is your fave rare?
Slowdances: The Mocha Master. That was the 1st rare I traded for :)
Luna: Are you a coffee drinker?
Slowdances: I dont like coffee o.o
Luna: how about Frappucinos?
Slowdances: Mmmm Delicious
Luna: Totally! What is your favorite food?
Slowdances: *thinks hard* I love all food really. I eat basically anything :P I love sweets though so that narrows it down
Luna: Fave sweet?
Slowdances: Ice cream!!!
Luna: :) Fave flavor?
Slowdances: Nuts Over Caramel
Luna: omg my mouth just started watering
Slowdances: haha
Luna: *yawns* lets go take a nap..i ate too much fro yo
Slowdances: ZZZZZZZZzzzzzzz
Luna: totally zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz


Luna: To the park! How long have you been a Habbo?
Slowdances: I started playing in 6th grade but then I stopped and came back this year in 10th grade. I used to think Habbo looked so weird. The people looked kinda freaky a few years ago but now they’re fine.
Luna: Stylish in fact... ;-)
Slowdances: Very ;)
Luna: Do you have any shout outs?
Slowdances: OF COURSE! *Rolls out list*: Delicately, Bird33, Patcakes, SeeYeah, .::Sarah::., & Jamaciachicken!! ALL loyal players at my game :)
Luna: Yay!
Slowdances: shout out to S.F.K members too!
Luna: Thank you so much for taking the time to talk with me!
Slowdances: No problem :D
Luna: Friday is gonna be a blast!
Slowdances: I HOPE SO :)
Luna: You can get all the gangs in shape so the butt kicking can REALLY begin!
Slowdances: OF COURSE >:D


Ok, you lazy Habbos - DROP AND GIVE ME 50!!! Haha jk...but definitely do come and join me and Slowdances in her #1 Habbo Boot Camp -- Training Facility this Friday, 9/15 at 4pm PST for some heart-pumping party action!
