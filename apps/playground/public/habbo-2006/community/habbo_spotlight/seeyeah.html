Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Battlefield-JFFvsFFFvsTEOvsSFK
Owner: SeeYeah

Now that all us crews have buffed up (thanks to last week's boot camp Staff Pick), it's time to duke it out Habbo-style! That's why I went with SeeYeah's Battlefield-JFFvs FFFvsTEOvsSFK for this week's selection. Hope you all are ready for some non-violent combat!!

I met up with SeeYeah (who has been a Habbo in the USA hotel since day one!) and after a while, I couldn't figure out who was interviewing who! Check it out:

SeeYeah: Hey
Luna: Hi!
Luna: Congrats on winning staff pick!

Luna: This is a pretty crazy room you got here :)
SeeYeah: Thanks

SeeYeah: Well, I thought that a battle field would be a good idea... because JFF will own all the other teams here :D
Luna: And why's that...
SeeYeah: Because JFF is the best. No offense or anything :D
Luna: No offense taken. What are the cameras supposed to be?
SeeYeah: Weapons. ;o lol
Luna: No violent weapons right?
SeeYeah: Nope, all Love Guns :D
Luna: lol they fire bubbles like the Superlove guns, right?

Luna: nice! Precise bubble guns. FFF would so win!
