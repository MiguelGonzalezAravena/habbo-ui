Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: .:Mountain Climbing Walls:.
Owner: VampireBoy11

If you build it (and submit it) they will come...thanks to VampireBoy11's email, I knew to stop by and check out his AWESOME Mountain Climbing Walls room. After attempting to climb the two walls in there (and failing miserably) I decided this room was perfect because it had a little something extra for TGIF festivities...the climbing walls of course!

I hung out with VampireBoy11 (who is super funny btw) and found out all about him and his love for climbing and The Simpsons:

Luna: So, how long have you been working on this room?
VampireBoy11: Well, Since Last Week Actually. I've Been Adding Things To Make It Better...It Took Like One Whole Night And One Whole Morning To Do The Walls.
Luna: Wow! That’s dedication! What inspired you to build climbing walls in the first place.
VampireBoy11: Well, I Really Like To Do That In Real Life. Its Like A Hobby. But I Rarely See Any Walls =l
Luna: yea, these are the first I’ve seen in Habbo
VampireBoy11: :)
Luna: We should have a harness in the costume options cause I’ve fallen off like 10 times!
VampireBoy11: Yea, Actually That Just Gave Me An Idea. I Should Make A Changing Room Or I Should Put up the cost of renting harnesses :oo
Luna: That’s a great idea as long as you don’t really charge ;)
VampireBoy11: Yea. I Just Like Jumping Off And Breaking Bones.
Luna: Lol - Habbo bones are pretty tough
VampireBoy11: Yep Haven't Broke One Yet
Luna: Me neither...even after all the planks I fell off last month.
VampireBoy11: Lol - In BirdCage's Room, Correct?
Luna: yep

Luna: So how long have you been a Habbo?
VampireBoy11: Well I've Been Switching Accounts, But Since June 2005 :)
Luna: Wow, nice! Any favorite musicians?
VampireBoy11: Well, I Like Green Day And Black Eyed Peas And...Duran Duran =P
Luna: nice!
VampireBoy11: AND OMG U Guys Showed Me That Rhianna Is Awesome :) Kinda..
Luna: She is pretty good.
VampireBoy11: I Made My Own Version of S.O.S.
Luna: Oh yea?
VampireBoy11: But It's Called L.O.L
Luna: I LOVE IT
VampireBoy11: I Made It Up When I Was Bored And It Was On The Radio…
Luna: You should try out for American Idol with it.
VampireBoy11: Lol
Luna: I’d vote for you! So what TV shows do you like?
VampireBoy11: The Simpsons, Family Guy, Futurama And Also Smallville Kinda
Luna: Favorite Simpsons character?
VampireBoy11: Bart Totally Bart
Luna: Favorite Simpsons character outside of the family?
VampireBoy11: Uhh Milhouse Becuz He Looks Funny Oh And Apu
