Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Room Name: SUMMER LOVING
Room Owner: iTS,HANNAH

Kedo: What kind of music were you listening to when you built this room?
iTS,HANNAH: Nothing in particular. Just listening to my itunes library which mostly consists of rock, pop and alternative music.
RocketSkates: What was the most challenging part of designing this room?
iTS,HANNAH: The most challenging part of designing this room would probably have to be getting enough coins to buy all the furni! =D
Whimsy: About how long did it take you to build your room from start to finish?
iTS,HANNAH:Well, on and off it probably took a couple hours. Not sure exactly though, because I'm always adding new things to it.
RocketSkates: If you had to imagine a story that could take place in your room, what would happen in it?
iTS,HANNAH: I don't know if I could imagine a "story" but I could picture a huge blowout party going on in my room. A really big start of the summer kinda thing. =]
Whimsy: If you could add a feature to Habbo, for either HC or norms, for public or private rooms, what would it be?
iTS,HANNAH:Wow, there's so many things I would add. But what I would really like is the ability to mass my entire console with 1 click. haha. simple, but I would love it.
Kedo: If you could meet one person (dead or alive), who would you choose and why?
iTS,HANNAH: Any of my great-great-great grandparents. It would be awesome to meet them!)
Whimsy: If you had a super power what would it be and why?
iTS,HANNAH: I would want to fly. Simply put.
RocketSkates: If you could be any TV Show character, who would it be and why?
iTS,HANNAH:Well, not exactly a character, but I've always wanted to be on American Idol. Just to make a fool of my tone-deaf self. haha.
Kedo: If you could have any star play you in a movie about your life, who would it be?
iTS,HANNAH: If there was going to be a movie about my life, I would want to be the star. No actress could pull off being me. Im just that special. lol
RocketSkates: If you could have any animal as a pet, what would it be?
iTS,HANNAH: a panda. they're just really cute. =]

Kedo: Any shout-outs or thank-yous?
iTS,HANNAH: Hi Callum! I love you!! Oh and thank you everyone who was supportive of me when I was struggling to make this room. =] I love you all!

Escape to iTS,HANNAH's room, SUMMER LOVING!
