Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Tee-Ski Resort
Owner: Tasha

What's scarier than vampires, ghosts and Habboween goblins??? Seeing brown-pants' name "written" on a Snow Patch, that's what! Don't get me wrong, there were PLENTY of amazing sights in Tasha's Tee-Ski Resort, like her amazing stacked ski slopes and cozy ski lodge, but that b-r-o-w-n melted in the snow will be burned in my mind forever! On a happier note, Tasha is one "cool" chica and her room is a true winter wonderland. Read on for more:

Luna: Congrats on being chosen for staff pick!
Tasha: Thanks for choosing me :D
Luna: This is a pretty cool room...please tell me about it
Tasha: It's Habbo's own Ski Resort! :D It's an indoor ski resort with all the fake snow included
Luna: nice! Are you a skier?
Tasha: No but I love snowboarding, even though Im not the best yet.
Luna: It’s a super hard sport.
Tasha: For sure!
Luna: That’s why I choose to stay in the ski lodge and drink hot cocoa.
Tasha: haha! Stay warm and toasty XD
Luna: *brown-pants walks in the door* brown-pants!
Tasha: heya BP XD
Luna: We are doing Staff Pick!
brown-pants: I needed to p
Tasha: LOL
Luna: oy..
brown-pants: b...
brown-pants: r...
brown-pants: o..
Tasha: LOL!
brown-pants: w...
brown-pants: n...
Luna: OMG…are you “writing” your name in the snow?
Tasha: rofl! not again..
brown-pants: I need to drink more water…
Luna: Is he re-fueling?
Tasha: rofl I think so :P!
Luna: ok, see you later mate.


Luna: so, back to the ski lodge
Luna: What inspired you to create a winter wonderland in the middle of Habboween…I don't see one bloody duck any where!
Tasha: To be honest I LOVE snow patches, so I decided to collect a lot of them and started up a resort.
Luna: Nice, so when people have had enough spider webs and gore, they can come here and get their minds off of it
Tasha: exactly! :D
Luna: So, even though this room isn't Habboween-themed, I want to ask you some Habboween questions…Are you planning on dressing up this year?
Tasha: for sure! Not really sure what to dress up as yet though...
Luna: Well, you better figure it out missy, Habboween is less then a week away lol
Tasha: haha :P Maybe a witch? but thats so unoriginal lol
Luna: witches are always good, I was going to be a banana, but i gave up on that costume… too tricky
Tasha: ROFL! Now that’s an interesting costume..
Luna: Yea, too interesting…maybe I’ll be a witch too! lol
Tasha: Lol I have a costume contest coming up at my school soon. I really want to win this year lol
Luna: You better be someeeee witch
Tasha: rofl yeah I know right
Luna: hehe…So, what's your fave scary movie?
Tasha: I’m not into movies much :( lol I cant watch scary movies. Nightmares!! :( lol
Luna: me too!
Tasha: haha, glad to know I’m not the only one!
Luna: yea, you are totally not alone on that…so forget scary movies
Tasha: rofl! next question! haha
Luna: Let’s get to the real important Habboween question...
Tasha: uh oh lol
Luna: what's your favorite CANDY??? You aren’t scared of candy right? lol
Tasha: Rofl! nopes.. Chrunch! :D Love my chrunch bars!!
Luna: mmmmmm anything with chocolate and rice krispies is ok in my book ;)


Luna: So, I am dying to learn more about you! How long have you been on Habbo?
Tasha: I've been playing habbo since 2002 long long time :P
Luna: very long time :) and you have a fan site correct?
Tasha: yeps I do, BlueBobble.com! Voted best USA fansite! woot hehe
Luna: nice, that's quite a title. And you DJ yeah?
Tasha: yeps love to online DJ
Luna: What kind of music do you play?
Tasha: Hmmm a mix usually between rap/hip hop, and rock, and some pop. I like all kinds of music pretty much
Luna: Who's your all-time favorite singer or band?
Tasha: Linkin Park!! :D
Luna: How about Furni...is the Snowpatch your favorite?
Tasha: yep, snow patches and... pressies I like collecting them. I’ve been collecting them since first day USA hotel opened lol
Luna: Wow! How many do you have?
Tasha: No idea. I lost count a year or so ago Lol
Luna: If you could invent a Furni what would it be?
Tasha: oooooo hmm probablyyyy...a snowboard?? :D To go with this room, that would be nice.
Luna: That would be AWESOME! I always thought snowboards were so cool looking…never been on one though.
Tasha: Its fun! :D I saved up for one a while ago.
Luna: What color is it?
Tasha: It’s black with orange fire on it :P I’m a complete tom-boy lol
Luna: AWESOME


Luna: Hey, did you see yourself in the Jeannie Ortega video? Congrats on that!
Tasha: yeah! :D thankies! The video was really good
Luna: Speaking of the Jeannie Ortega video, let’s clear up a little rumor, or shall i say HUGE rumor
Tasha: uh oh lol
Luna: You and brown-pants...friends or MORE?
Tasha: just FRIENDS lol! Really good friends 'tis all
Luna: ok Habbos, you heard it here, Tasha and Brown-Pants are ONLY friends.
Tasha: haha XD
Luna: lol So, what is your favorite thing to do here on Habbo
Tasha: I’m all about throwing the big parties while I’m DJing on BlueBobble
Luna: Nice, when do you DJ?
Tasha: I DJ every Friday night at 9 pm EST on HabboHut.com and on BlueBobble.com anytime I can XD
Luna: Sweet! Well I'll be sure to tune in when I can…
Tasha: :D
Luna: Any shout outs Miss Tasha?
Tasha: Let’s see... *takes out a big piece of paper*
Luna: lol
Tasha: haha jk umm .:simpsonfreak:. for helping me with the room, Binxers, Stream, BandDiva, Brown-Pants and anyone else on my list.
Luna: Cool, thanks so much for chatting
Tasha: :D Thanks Lunaaaa you’re the best…this place will look really awesome filled up on Friday.
Tasha: :D Can't wait, every single one of you reading this make sure to come! lol
Luna: lol, yeah come! And dress up as snow bunnies in honor of Habboween! Guys can be yetis.
Tasha: rofl nice :P!
Luna: OK, well, I'll see ya soon!
Tasha: :P adios Luna
Luna: Adios!


You heard right! Suit up in your best snow bunny or yeti costume and chill with Tasha and co. this Friday the 27th at 4pm PST. Happy Habboween!
