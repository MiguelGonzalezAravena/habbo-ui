Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: X Marks the Spot
Owner: BirdCage

Pirates have taken over Habbo Hotel and the 7 Pixel Seas Floor is the place to be! As I was navigating through the floor, popping in on secret crew meetings and swimming through mini oceans, I came across BirdCage’s beauty of a ship. At first I was hesitant to hop on board (I’m known to get a little seasick), but once I teleported on to the poop deck I was sold!

I caught up with BirdCage and two of his crew mates, kornblinkrock and Envious? (Triscuit, the fourth member, happened to be offline) in X Marks the Spot and chatted it up pirate style:

Luna: How long have ya'll been Habbos?
Envious?: Almost a year
BirdCage: About a year for me, despite switching accounts
kornnblinkrock: Well, I have been a Habbo since October of 2004
Luna: whoa korn, since the beginning of time practically…
kornnblinkrock: Yea
kornnblinkrock: 1 Month late
Luna: What is your crew name?
BirdCage: Pixel Crusaders
BirdCage: ;)
Luna: Who came up with that, i like it!
BirdCage: Ha, I did.. it was kinda random
Luna: How did you gather your crew?
BirdCage: Wer’e all friends and we were playing defend your pod when the Rare was stolen.
BirdCage: :P
Luna: lol - how appropriate
Luna: Have you guys tried to solve the quest?
BirdCage: Of course
kornnblinkrock: Yes, we have been trying very hard
Luna: Any luck?
BirdCage: So far, we’re stumped :P
Luna: lol
Envious?: It’s hard, with all the fake people
Luna: Totally, I'm suprised by all the imposters!
BirdCage: Yeah it’s hard to know who’s fake and who’s real
Envious?: It keeps us busy though

Luna: So, how did you guys build this ship?
kornnblinkrock: It was teamwork. We all had a part
Luna: nice! Do tell…
BirdCage: Well I only started of with about 10 Lodge corners
kornnblinkrock: See, I built the treasure over there
Luna: The trophies?
kornnblinkrock: Yes
Luna: A fine pirate loot, hehe
BirdCage: Ha, of course ;)
kornnblinkrock: Arr
kornnblinkrock: And the island was my idea
BirdCage: And Envious with her amazing stacking skills :P
Luna: Envious?, did you stack the Lodge corners?
Envious?: The corners and the bars yea
Luna: Very nice
Envious?: :) Thank you
Luna: And who's idea was the plank?
BirdCage: Mine :P
kornnblinkrock: And did you find the X in the room?
Luna: No, where is it?
kornnblinkrock: Look at the room shape!
BirdCage: Its kinda hard to miss :P
Luna: ohhhhhhhhhh lol
Luna: nice! very clever
kornnblinkrock: We are right on top of it!
Luna: So this is the spot to be pretty much...
BirdCage: Definitley! :P

Luna: So, what are you guys hoping the new Rare will be?
BirdCage: Hmm that’s a toughy
kornnblinkrock: Well, I am hoping it will be.... A Blue Throne :P
Envious?: :o
Luna: hmmmm interesting
kornnblinkrock: Or maybe, a Sea Dragon?
kornnblinkrock: That would be cool
BirdCage: Or maybe a blue duck ;o
Luna: A blue duck that would be cute
BirdCage: Definitely :)
Envious?: I dunno what I want it to be, I just wanna know what it is :P
kornnblinkrock: :P
Luna: haha, im sure LOTS of other Habbos can relate

Luna: So, how did you all assign crew roles?
Envious?: I told them they should cook for me.
Envious?: But they disagreed
Luna: lol
kornnblinkrock: Well, since Bird had the idea, he was the Captain
kornnblinkrock: And since I am first at everything, I am the First Mate
Envious?: lol first at everything
BirdCage: and Triscuit is the Fighting Crewman
Luna: is Triscuit the feisty one?
BirdCage: of course ;)
kornnblinkrock: As you can see, he is not here
Luna: lol
BirdCage: lol
Luna: well, I think we should give a shout out to Triscuit!
kornnblinkrock: Yea!
BirdCage: Hey Triscuit!!
Envious?: Haha Triscuit, you’re not here.
kornnblinkrock: Arrrrr Triscuit, why are you not here matey?
Luna: He'll be here on Friday hopefully!!
kornnblinkrock: Yea
BirdCage: I'll make him :)


The Pixel Crusaders make me walk the plank (after I finally figure out how to get on it, that is ;-D)

Luna: Any shout outs to other crews or pirate friends?
Envious?: Ignoring
BirdCage: Well, I wanna shout out to ALL my friends on Habbo!!!!
kornnblinkrock: Ill give a out to OkHappy?
kornnblinkrock: And iomegadrive
Luna: Finally, any words for Captain-Murphy??
kornnblinkrock: And to you Captain-Murphy, we want your Rare
BirdCage: o.o
kornnblinkrock: We’re coming for you
BirdCage: >:D
Luna: ok, thanks so so much for all your time! See you guys here on Friday at 4pm PST for the Staff Pick Party!
BirdCage: Aww thank you!
kornnblinkrock: bye
Envious?: bye
Luna: Fair Winds!
