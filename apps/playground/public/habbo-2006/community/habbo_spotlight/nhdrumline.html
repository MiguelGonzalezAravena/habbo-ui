Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?


Room Name: Christmas Balooza
Owner: NHDrumline

The Holidays are in full effect here at Habbo Hotel, and your rooms are looking more beautiful and festive than ever! This week I chose a room that filled me with all those warm holiday fuzzies the second I laid eyes on it: NHDrumline’s Christmas Balooza room. There was just something special about the giant tree on the island surrounded by the beautifully constructed balcony. I caught up with NHDrumline and we had so much fun discussing the holidays and hotel happenings…you’ve gotta see what he used to think of iomegadrive!


NHDrumline: Hey :D
Luna: Hi there!
NHDrumline: lol I finally get to meet you...
Luna: hehe, well, it’s nice to meet you!
NHDrumline: the pleasure is all mine :D
Lun: haha, awesome room!
NHDrumline: Thxs :D
Luna: How long did it take to design and build?
NHDrumline: it took me 2 hours in total. 1 hour to find the stuff one hour to make it
Luna: Seriously? I really like how symmetrical it is.
NHDrumline: lol yeah...I tried to do that....I thought it would make it look better. I guess it worked....
Luna: And the xmas tree is so nice...its like you bought it at Sixty2's lot!
NHDrumline: lol I tried to get it there...he was all sold out....
Luna: LOL. How many vases are in there?
NHDrumline: ummm.....I lost count....
Luna: hehe, it’s huge.
NHDrumline: lol
Luna: So, what inspired this room other than the holiday season?
NHDrumline: My girlfriend in real life :D She’s the best I love her soooo much :D
Luna: awwwww, so sweet! Did she ask for an enormous Xmas tree?
NHDrumline: lol no... she just asked for a room :D
Luna: Thats so nice of you! So how about you, what do you want for the holidays?
NHDrumline: hmmmm....I asked for a lot of games and a new paintball gun....
Luna: Ouch, paintballs scare me
NHDrumline: lol It don’t hurt that much while you are playing. So what did you ask for?
Luna: I asked for clothes :) And iPod speakers
NHDrumline: lol nice :D I need an iPod....
Luna: My iPod is my best friend :)
NHDrumline: lol
Luna: What’s your favorite kind of music?
NHDrumline: Alternative rock… I don’t really have a favorite band...
Luna: Have you heard the new Incubus album?
NHDrumline: YES!
Luna: So good huh?
NHDrumline: they are pretty sweet :D I gotta get my hands on their cd...maybe santa can get me one XD
Luna: Maybe...


Luna: How long have you been a Habbo?
NHDrumline: Almost 2 years...
Luna: Sweet! So do you have any plans in Habbo this holiday season? Any big parties or anything?
NHDrumline: Parties....maybe a big Plasto giveaway like last year....
Luna: oooooooh nice! ‘Tis the season for giving!
NHDrumline: lol yup :D All these new Habbos lately XD they need something to start off with....
Luna: So sweet of you - its nice to know that there are generous Habbos out there who want to make them feel at home :)
NHDrumline: :D BTW
Luna: yes?
NHDrumline: I’ve got plenty of Dragons in here for you to choose from XD New dragon boyfriend or are you still taken?
Luna: LOL Well, the Black Dragons are really something...
NHDrumline: Yeah they are my favorite color besides red but those are hard to get.
Luna: So what do dragons have to do with Xmas anyways...
NHDrumline: idk.... they just crawled into the room
Luna: LOL better keep them away from that tree
NHDrumline: lol XD that wouldn’t be too good....
Luna: Nope, not good at all ;-) So, are dragons your fave Furni?
NHDrumline: They’re my second fave....my first fave is...LODGE CORNERS!!!! I love em....
Luna: LOL, I would never have guessed that...
NHDrumline: lol XD
Luna: How about if you could invent a Furni?
NHDrumline: I’ve been thinking about this one...it would have to be a type of furniture you can put all your extra furniture in so you don’t have to put it all in one room or your hand...
Luna: That would be awesome -- like a Garage or something
NHDrumline: lol yes!!! That would be perfect


Luna: So, are you going anywhere over Xmas break?
NHDrumline: Nope....I’ll probably be playing Habbo...(I hope I get those pre paid cards from GameStop for Xmas)
Luna: Sah-weet -- there's a lot going on so I don’t think you'll be bored…have you been participating in the activities so far?
NHDrumline: ummm…I went to TGIF Friday. I couldn’t get into any other rooms
Luna: Were you listening to bobba music?
NHDrumline: hecks yeah I was :D It made me laugh
Luna: LOL GOOD!!!! It was so stuffy in the studio…imagine being in a teeny room with io
NHDrumline: LOL I always thought io was a girl until 2 weeks ago... XD
Luna: SERIOUSLY?
NHDrumline: Don’t tell him I told u that XD
Luna: ROFL
NHDrumline: Whenever I heard (the name) iomegadrive I thought of a girl
Luna: But haven’t you seen his avatar pic?
NHDrumline: I have....but I never looked closely at the face...
Luna: lol That is the funniest thing I’ve heard all day
NHDrumline: lol yay :D


Luna: So, whose side are you on, DJ-Bling or Rasta.Claus?
NHDrumline: I’m on Rasta.Clause’s. He’s the man…he can dance too…
Luna: He sure can dance, but Bling can...spend money??
NHDrumline: lol that’s about it....
Luna: hmmm…I have to make a better case for him...
NHDrumline: Rasta makes some good brownies too
Luna: That's what I hear, I’ve never tried them, so personally I wouldn’t know
NHDrumline: I snuck into his kitchen XD
Luna: Sweet! LOL What’s your favorite public room?
NHDrumline: hmmmm...I would have to say...the Pool Deck?
Luna: You a Wobbler?
NHDrumline: Yup...but not that good....
Luna: oh really, I'm a pro
NHDrumline: lol I’ve seen XD
Luna: Uh oh...Busted
NHDrumline: io owns :D
Luna: he's SO good...I asked him to teach me and he said NEVER, so oh well!
NHDrumline: lol
Luna: But if I ever get a hand on those brownies, I wont give him any, so ha!
NHDrumline: LOL
Luna: Hey I have a better argument for bling...
NHDrumline: Whats ur argument?
Luna: Look, in the catalog...
NHDrumline: xmas furni :D
Luna: No no not that…well, that too BUT Green Iced!!!!
NHDrumline: SWEET!!!!
Luna: Yay!
NHDrumline: But Rasta's red pillow is sweet too...
Luna: It is really sweet too…So do you have any shout outs my friend?
NHDrumline: ohhh yes I do...MY AWESOME GIRLFRIEND!!!! ANDDDDD my best friend -typoe, all my friends on my list and allll the hotel staff
Luna: aww thanks!
NHDrumline: :D
Luna: Oh, I also wanted to ask you about your name what's the story behind that?
NHDrumline: lol i get that a lot…I’m in marching band at my school. I’m in the drum line :D
Luna: SWEET! I've always wanted to learn how to play the drums
NHDrumline: lol it’s easy to learn
Luna: Wouldn’t it be awesome to have instruments in Habbo?
NHDrumline: yess!!!! Halftime shows for the football games
Luna: What team do you root for during tailgating?
NHDrumline: Broncos :D
Luna: Cool! Man, I would rock the electric guitar if there were instruments
NHDrumline: You play guitar?
Luna: Nope I don’t, but I think I would look good playing one :)
NHDrumline: lol yeh :D
Luna: Well, it was great chatting :)
NHDrumline: yes it was indeed


This week’s Staff Pick party is going start a bit early…join hotel staff, NHDrumline and all of those gorgeous dragons in the Christmas Balooza room at 1pm PST/4pm EST. There will be wintry trivia, fun and lots of friendly faces. See you there!
