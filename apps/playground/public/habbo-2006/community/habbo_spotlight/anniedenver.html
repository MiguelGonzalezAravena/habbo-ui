Staff Pick of the Week - Could it be you?

Each week one Habbo and their room is featured on the site as the Staff Pick of the Week. This week we have...

Room Name: Grade A Trade
Owner: AnnieDenver

We have the first Staff Pick of the Week chosen by the new submissions system! If you have a room that you think should be featured here, let us know! For now, here is the interview with the awesome room from AnnieDenver. An avid collector, AnnieDenver has sought out to own every piece of furni in Habbo and is nearly there! For now, read on to find out more about AnnieDenver.

iomegadrive: So let's start shall we?
iomegadrive: When did you come to Habbo USA?
AnnieDenver: In January
iomegadrive: Of this year?
AnnieDenver: yes:)
iomegadrive: OH WOW!
AnnieDenver: :P
AnnieDenver: I’m addicted lol
iomegadrive: Haha so I see!
AnnieDenver: *blushes*
iomegadrive: What are you so addicted to?
AnnieDenver: well I became addicted when I decided I wanted 1 of everything
AnnieDenver: I’m getting close lol
AnnieDenver: I need a lert & a blue Hyacinth & I’m done lol
iomegadrive: Nice...and it looks like you're almost there
iomegadrive: How long did it take for you to get all of this?
AnnieDenver: ummm well ive been here 4.5 months
AnnieDenver: & started collecting 2 months ago
AnnieDenver: It’s been a challenge
iomegadrive: What was the hardest piece of furni to trade for?
AnnieDenver: the two I have left to get
AnnieDenver: Lert & Blue Hyacinth are very hard to find
iomegadrive: what about what you DO have?
AnnieDenver: Nelly & Petal were hardest
iomegadrive: Yeah? why were they so hard?
AnnieDenver: people like them so much that when they get 1 they don’t want trade
iomegadrive: you've got that emotional attachment thing.
AnnieDenver: exactly
iomegadrive: Why did you make the decision to get one of everything?
AnnieDenver: well I wanted to do something different with my room
AnnieDenver: & this was something I had never seen

iomegadrive: what is your favorite rare?
AnnieDenver: ooo good question
iomegadrive: :-p
AnnieDenver: ii think it would be 1 I don’t have yet
AnnieDenver: the laser gate
iomegadrive: Ahh the infamous laser gate
AnnieDenver: very hard to find lol
iomegadrive: yeah those are definitely COOL.
AnnieDenver: yes lol
iomegadrive: which color?
AnnieDenver: black
AnnieDenver: though I’ve never seen 1
AnnieDenver: just seen pictures

iomegadrive: So do you do a lot of trading in here?
AnnieDenver: I don’t trade things out of my collection but the room is open 2 all
AnnieDenver: and a lot of people come to trade or just hang out
iomegadrive: do you go looking for the items you need?
iomegadrive: or do people come to you?
AnnieDenver: a little of both but I really enjoy the other trade rooms
iomegadrive: what about them do you like?
AnnieDenver: the atmosphere is really fun
AnnieDenver: I like the fact that everyone is on a mission
iomegadrive: What the most you've traded for anything?
AnnieDenver: hmmm that would be the Petal
AnnieDenver: but I got a good deal
AnnieDenver: 3HC for it
AnnieDenver: I was happy that day :)
iomegadrive: Nice!
iomegadrive: What is your favorite norm?
AnnieDenver: hmm another good question
AnnieDenver: Can I consider my pets norms?
iomegadrive: Haha no!
AnnieDenver: lol ok
AnnieDenver: I guess it would be the mode mini-bar
iomegadrive: and now, your favorite pet?
AnnieDenver: lol :P
AnnieDenver: I have 8 pets & love them all but my 2 favorites are right down there
AnnieDenver: Flurry & Glacier

iomegadrive: I am noticing a lot of posters...
iomegadrive: which poster is your fave?
AnnieDenver: I LOVE the posters !
AnnieDenver: my favorite is the DJ Throne :)
AnnieDenver: because it goes over the top edge :D
iomegadrive: he is rad.
iomegadrive: punching you right in the kisser
iomegadrive: lol.
AnnieDenver: I like that :P
AnnieDenver: LOL
iomegadrive: which is your favorite seasonal line of furni?
AnnieDenver: hmmm you are full of good questions lol
AnnieDenver: I guess I like the Christmas trees the best
AnnieDenver: I wasn’t here to see the Christmas stuff in the Catalogue.
AnnieDenver: But am really looking forward to it :)

iomegadrive: so what do you like to do most in Habbo?
AnnieDenver: I like collecting & trading & making friends with the people that like
AnnieDenver: the same things I like
iomegadrive: oh nice.
iomegadrive: have you been to TGIF?
AnnieDenver: yes I have & its a blast !
iomegadrive: nice!
iomegadrive: which parties have you been to?
iomegadrive: on TGIF?
AnnieDenver: oh my lol I don’t keep track of what they are called *blushes*
iomegadrive: haha no worries.

iomegadrive: Alrighty, thanks for the interview
iomegadrive: you're definitely addicted!
AnnieDenver: TY for picking my room I’m glad you liked it :)
iomegadrive: Do you have any shoutouts?
AnnieDenver: oooh i see yes I do
AnnieDenver: Miah1991 gearhead429 LisaHLadybirD Megapixels Seith...
iomegadrive: Remember, party here on Friday! TGIF forever!

And that’s that! The TGIF Party is at AnnieDenver’s room this Friday, so be sure to get there early and save yourself a spot! Be sure to check back next Wednesday to find out who the next Staff Pick is!
