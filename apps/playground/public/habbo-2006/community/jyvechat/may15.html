Chat it up with jyve

Well, well Habbo US…….as you may have noticed, there have been some changes going on here. But one thing remains the same, Chat it up with Jyve. And as a special treat, in honor of Mother’s Day, we’re chatting it up with SomebodysMom. Some of you may already know her as the winner of Habbowood (congrats again!), but now we all get a chance to know a little bit more about her. SomebodysMom has been playing in Habbo Hotel US for roughly 13 months, 3 days, and 7 hours. She first came to Habbo because her kids wanted to play in this wonderful Hotel of ours, and she wanted to spend more time with them, so she joined too. Some of the many reasons that SomebodysMom loves Habbo so much are once again the people, the fun, and the trading!

SomebodysMom also sees another aspect Habbo that sometimes we may lose sight of. SomebodysMom sees Habbo as a great learning experience for kids. She thinks it helps Habbos learn about real life by teaching them the value of currency, and how to interact with people from different cultures and backgrounds. SomebodysMom likes how Habbo can allow kids to see what it’s like living somewhere else in the world by talking directly to people who live there. SomebodysMom’s favorite piece of furni is the Gold Dragon Lamp. If she could add any furni to the catalog, it would be a Pepsi Machine. If threw was one thing about she could change or would change, it would be NOTHING! That’s right. NOTHING! As she so succinctly put it, “Why fix something that’s not broken?”

Now with that out of the way, let’s have some fun with SomebodysMom. Her favorite type of ice cream is Chocolate Chip Mint, and favorite food is prime rib. Favorite flower is the gardenia. SomebodysMom loves the smell of freshly cut grass. If she were an animal she’d be a dolphin. When asked to choose a condiment to describe herself, she went with sugar cuz she’s super sweet. And if she were a crayon she would be a purple Crayola. Her favorite thing about being a mom, are actually her kids. SomebodysMom says her kids make her feel special all the time and she loves being able to see the world through their eyes.

And since this is in honor of Mother’s Day, I had to ask for a few suggestions from SomebodysMom on how to deal with mothers (and fathers too for that matter). I figured since she has the inside scoop, I’d get her to share it with us. Her advice? To just have patience, because eventually you too will be a parent also. Parents aren’t always right, but they do try their best. As for regular advice on just navigating the peaks and valleys of Habbo, SomebodysMom has this to say. ‘Stay safe online as that’s the most important thing. There are a lot of people out there that can not and should not be trusted. Don’t give out personal info like name, town, and or passwords. So stay safe always and obey the Habbo Way.’ Spoken like a true mom. So if you happen to see SomebodysMom or one of her kids (Jame. or Treva11), getting their Habbo Hotel US on, be sure to give them a big hello. You won’t be disappointed. And even though the actual day has passed, I’m sure SomebodysMom would still love a Happy Mothers Day from all you Habbos. Habbo Hotel US, meet SomebodysMom.
