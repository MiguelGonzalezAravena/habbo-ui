Never Ending Story

I awoke with a start as a hand touched my shoulder! My eyes flicked open and I found myself staring into the face of the wildest human I had ever seen. Massive earrings dangled from his ears, making a “chingling” sound. His combed yet frizzy black hair hung over his huge gray eyes, and his yellow teeth and colorfully painted face spread into a huge smile as he licked his lips, and stared straight at me...

“Hello,” I staggered, smiling in an inculpable way. The man raised his eyebrows and sat down to me. Then opening his ghastly smelling mouth he said, “Welcome. You look dreadfully exhausted, follow me.” He waved his hand into the direction of what looked like a moss covered door. He pushed his way through, with me dragging myself behind him. I managed to get in quite well but when I came in, I just couldn’t believe my eyes.

There was a huge castle-shaped building right in front of me! Its walls were covered with shining green moss, its windows were decorated with stunning blue ropes of berries and the roof was top to bottom tiled with fresh autumn leaves of light pink and sweet scarlet. The man stood right in front of it and bravely rose his rather hairy right arm proudly saying, “This, is my home. My palace!”

My jaw dropped. As the castle door opened the air filled with deliciously smelling dinner and I hurled in like a hungry hound. The master joined his family at the doorway, greeting me with a glass of fresh red wine. I gently shifted it away. “No thanks. I don’t drink,” I said, and immediately was served a cup of fresh water.

“Come on in,” the man’s wife said, showing me to the doorway and then to probably the most beautiful room of the whole building. I must be watching my manners, I thought. I must be dealing with a rather royal family…
