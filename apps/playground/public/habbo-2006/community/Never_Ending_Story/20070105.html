Never Ending Story

“Ahoy! I’m Captain Iomegadrive,” he said. “But you can call me Captain Io.

“Have you ever heard of Captain Kedo?” Io asked as he shoved a mop at me. “No,” I replied, looking at the disgusting mop. “He’s got something big under his sleeves, and we’re going to get it,” Io replied.

I wanted to ask him what Kedo had. Was it treasure? Was it money? But before I had a chance to speak, Io went below deck and back to his cabin. What had I gotten into?

I never had the chance to ask him. BOOOOOOM. A deafening sound came from the bow of the ship. I fell over the noise was so loud. So did a few other crewmates. For a second there was silence. Then in a split second every crewman on the ship ran to the bow of the ship. “ARRR,” screamed Captain Io. “We have been hit.” I ran over to the deck where Captain Iomegadrive was standing. “Was it Kedo’s ship.?” I screamed over the top of my lungs. Before I could even get an answer, I saw it. The ship that attacked us. The ship’s beauty was amazing. It had large beautiful flags, the shade of purple. I raced over to the edge of the Emo Orange ship, to get a look at the captain. She had only one eyebrow. I instantly knew who it was: Luna.
