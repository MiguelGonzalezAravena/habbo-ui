Never Ending Story

“Ahoy, matey! What were you doing out in the middle of the ocean? Sorry ‘bout bounding ya up, but I have to make sure you aren’t a enemy pirate or Captain Kedo will probably throw you over the side of this magnificent ship, the Kedo King. We wouldn’t want that, would we?” I shook my head no, then I remembered what Captain Io said ‘We’re out to steal all Captain Kedo’s treasure.’ I tried to shake off the feeling, but then I remembered the voice when I got here, ‘Swim as far as you can, and when you can’t swim anymore, you will find the portal.’ Both voices kept echoing in my head...

“...Swim, that’s what I have to do. I have to swim as far as I can, and maybe I will be returned to the hotel!”

The idea seemed good enough to me. The red-haired woman untied me.

“My name is Rennal,” she introduced. “I will untie you, as long as you promise to swear your life to Captain Kedo.”

I had no choice. “Ok, if need be, I will sacrifice myself for the Kedo King.”

As promised, Rennal untied me. This was my chance! I hopped out of the barrel and started to swim. The woman was too fast for me, and grabbed me around my throat. I saw red at the edges of my vision, and then everything went black.

When I awoke, I was tied to a chair in some room, surrounded by the three captains: Kedo, Luna, and Whimsy. I also saw Rennal, scowling at me from the corner of the room.

“Well, well, well,” Captain Kedo sneered. “What do we have here? You look like a spy to me. What do you two think?”

Luna and Whimsy nodded at the same time. “You know what we do to spies, right ladies?” Kedo asked again.

Again, the two female captains both leaned towards me, and whispered simultaneously, “Death Cage?”

Rennal was about to grab me, when we heard an explosion from above, and a chorus of shouts and yells...
