Never Ending Story


I could not believe it! He went red and the greed in his eyes was completely visible. He quickly ran his fingers through his hair and composed himself as if nothing had happened. "Anyway, for the last phase of my plan I have a proposition..." he looked at me with piercing eyes. I felt beads of sweat run down my forehead. "I need you to join me! What do you say!?"

A shiver ran down my spine and I was physically shaking, maybe he was hypnotizing me!? I broke his gaze and saw Saiph, doing something that made me even more petrified...

“I...I....I will join you in your quest for power.” she said, her gaze fixed on the mysterious Habbo.
I couldn’t believe what I heard. Was I going mad? Saiph walked over to him and kneeled. “I will be your faithful servant.”
“Good,” he replied. He looked right at my dumbstruck face. “And what do you choose?”

I couldn’t speak. The betrayal of Saiph, the secret Habbo, this organization...it was all just so confusing. My survival instinct kicked in, fearing for the worst. “I will...join you also,” I said. I gulped.
“Excellent. You both have made me very proud. Please follow me. I will give you a grand tour of my secret, underground facility,” he replied, the smile returning to his face. We were lead into a large room filled with robots assembling what appeared to be other robots. “This is our android assembly room. Here is where we are building the army I will command when we plan to attack this Hotel. All will fall before their power,” he said with an evil look in his eyes.
“He’s gone mad with power,” I thought. We moved through the assembly room into a hallway. I saw a door that was so tightly locked, even the best robber couldn’t have gotten in. “What’s in there?” I dared to ask.
“Ahh...that my boy, is where we are making our secret weapon should all else fail. Unfortunately, you cannot gain access unless you have gained my most sincere trust,” he replied.

Saiph and I were then lead into a large room with nothing but chairs that were bolted to the ground and a very, very long table, like a business room. “This is our briefing room. Please take a seat...Ohh, and by the way, my name is Pitholis, master of this great army…”
