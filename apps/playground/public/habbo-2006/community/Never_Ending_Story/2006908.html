Never Ending Story

We ran back to Saiph’s house. But when we got inside, it was ransacked. “What the heck...,” I said, fright in my voice...

“Woah!” I said as I looked around the house! I looked over at Saiph who looked as if he had just been in the final round of Millionare and lost.
“What happened to my house....?” said Saiph as he looked around the house for anything that might of not been destroyed. At that moment we heard something coming from the kitchen.
Saiph whispered to me, “Let’s go see who it is.”
I replied, “Are you crazy!?” I sort of said that too loud because the noises stopped. We saw a shadow coming on the wall. It’s getting closer....
“Ahhhhhhhhh!” I screamed at the top of my voice. RUN!!! We ran up his stairs and then to his room. When we got to his room the door was shut. I quickly opened the door but as quickly as I opened it I closed it because there was another guy in there! We ran to the guest room. In the guest room we ran until we ran into a wall.
“Ouch!” Saiph said and then I turned around. The two guys were coming into view. One was a big guy with a ski mask on and the other was in his 20s with a black hoodie on.
One of the robbers said, “Take that stupid mask off!”
“Ok”, said the other one. The two robbers crept closer and closer until we were trapped against the wall. Nowhere to run or hide... I said to Saiph, “This is it......”
