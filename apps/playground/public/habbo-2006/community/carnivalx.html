Carnival X: Festival of Fright!

eXpert Festival of Fright

Pumpkins are Orange
Cats are Black,
Get out your costumes,
eXpert Festival of Fright is BACK!

Need a good scare this Habbo Halloween? Looking to try new games in the hotel but just not sure where to go? Look no further! The eXpert Festival of Fright has BOTH! Starting FRIDAY THE 13TH of October, and running through Sunday, October 15th, the Habbo eXpert team will be on board to put you in a scary Habboween mood! We have new games as well as a few returning favorites:

  • Are you brave enough to handle our Devil’s Bingo?
  • Trick or treat down Spook Street – but only if you come in costume!
  • Monster yer Mash at the Fall Ball on Sunday with special eXpert DJ’s!

The Festival of Fright is THE place to be this weekend! Simply open your Hotel Navigator (that’s the blue and white square at the bottom, right-hand corner of your game screen), search the Guest room floors, and click on the Festival of Fright floor for OFFICIAL Festival games. There is NO CHARGE to play; this is for YOU and the love of this special scary holiday.

Remember, everyone that wins at the games will receive a prize the following week! The entire schedule can be found below. Happy Habboween!


-Jeff-BOO and the Habbo eXpert team
