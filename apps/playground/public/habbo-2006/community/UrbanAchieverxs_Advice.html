Dear Urban...

Hey Habbos,

It looks like you've found your way to my advice column. Each week, I ask Habbos to write in with their questions, concerns, thoughts, problems, issues and nagging doubts. I pick a few of the most interesting and do my best to answer them. My responses might not solve your problems, but they will make you think about them differently. So ask away!
