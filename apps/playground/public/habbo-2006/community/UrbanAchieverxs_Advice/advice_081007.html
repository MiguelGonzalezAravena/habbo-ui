Urban's Advice for 8/10/07

I received a ton of great questions for my first advice column. Instead of boring you with how thankful I am that the Habbo community came out en masse to make this a success, howsabout I just answer the ones I liked the most? Sound good? Good. Here we go...

Dear UrbanAchiever,

I have a problem with swearing. I can't stop swearing! It is bad to swear?

- DaLoneWolf

 It's just like saying "I love you" or serving your community. Do it too often and it loses meaning. Ain't nothing wrong with dropping the occasional Bobba-bomb, just do so sparingly (and creatively).
- UA

Dear UrbanAchiever,

Well there's a girl I like, the problem is, she has a boyfriend 3 states away. Now I'm not one to oppose long distance relationships, but I don't believe in them that much. I want to tell her how I feel but I'm nervous about doing it, and how she'll react already having a boyfriend. What should I do?

- HiMyNameIsSora

Go ahead and tell her how you feel. What's the worse that can happen? She doesn't feel the same way? According to the last global census there are like 3 BILLION women in the world. There are too many fish in the sea to be getting all hung up on one.

And Long distance relationships? Puh-lease. It will never, never, never work. The only reason you're not completely opposed to them is because you haven't had one yet. Trust me. They don't work.

- UA


How can I convince my mother to stop repeating things without making her turn into a homicidal lunatic? In other words, how can i tell her "I know, mom" without spending money or having her become all angry and crazy? And if you're wondering, she doesn't drink or have a mental disorder...so don't suggest rehab or medication.

- sasunarufan

So you want your mother to stop repeating herself when you've "got it"? All I can say is:

BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA.

Good bobbaing luck!

I'm almost 80 and my hundred-year-old mother repeats herself constantly. In MommaAchiever's case, it could very well be the dementia, but it's a problem I've dealt with since I was a LittleAchiever. What I'm trying to say is that it's never going to stop. Consider it practice for marriage.

- UA

Dear UrbanAchiever,

How can I make my hair as luxurious as yours? Should I lather, rinse, and repeat as needed, or is there a better technique?"

- Quill

Dear Quill,

I'm glad you recognize the importance of proper follicle care. While you're hair will never be as full of body and as shiny as mine, following these simple steps will get you pretty close:

1. Determine what type of hair you have. Is it thick, thin, straight, wavy, colored? I cannot emphasize the importance of this first step enough.

2. Find a shampoo and conditioner combination specifically tailored for your hair type. Use both daily. I've got issues with dryness, so I use Herbal Essences' "Mind Your Frizzness".

3. Avoid blow-drying, always towel dry.

4. Style and go about your day, confident that your hair is the envy of all those that pass you on the street.

- UA

That's it for this week's advice column. Got a question? Ask away. I'll be posting my responses next Friday.

Oh...I didn't mention this when I announced that I'd be starting this column, but I've started a Group for the Habbos whose questions I've answered. If you're one of them, you can join UrbanAchiever Advice Receivers.

(LawyerAchiever's Disclaimer: Urban's Advice column is not meant to be taken seriously. His opinions are meant for entertainment purposes only. His advice is kind of stupid, anyway.)
