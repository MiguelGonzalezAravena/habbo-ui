Urban's Advice for 8/24/07

Another week, another bunch of questions to answer. This week, I read through HUNDREDS of submissions and found my favorites.

Remember Habbos...if you've had your question answered, you're invited to join my group:

UrbanAchiever Advice Receivers

Enough with the talking! Time for advice!

Dear Urban,

My brother is incredibly annoying! He always goes through all my stuff when I'm not home! How do I get him to stop?

- samsterrr


Dear Samsterrr,

As a brother myself, I always relished the opportunity to go through my sister's stuff whenever she wasn't there. I think my snooping only stopped when I moved out. Sorry to say this, but you're in for years of yelling, "Stop touching mah stuff! Mooooooom!"

- UrbanAchiever


Dear Urban,

Do you like George W. Bush?

- Apada


Dear Apada,

Management informs me that I'm not allowed to answer this using the language I want to use. I will say that I'm thankful that American presidents are only allowed to serve two terms. That should probably clue you as to my feelings in right there.

- UrbanAchiever


Dear Urban,

I have a problem.
It's so critical.
It's upseting.
I CANT DECIDE. between a pink or a green shirt :[ Help!.

- x-.BumbleBee


Dear X-.BumbleBee,

You know as well as I do that this completely depends on your outfit. However, if you're going to base the rest of your ensemble on your choice of shirt, I'd say go with pink. I'm big fan of pink. It makes me feel like a man.

- UrbanAchiever


Dear Urban,

How do you cancel your account?

- kalvir-kav


Dear kalvir-kav,

I just started! Why would I want to cancel my account? That's the last thing on my mind right now. I'm having too much fun.

- UrbanAchiever


(LawyerAchiever's Disclaimer: Urban's Advice column is not meant to be taken seriously. His opinions are meant for entertainment purposes only. His advice is kind of stupid, anyway.)
