Urban's Advice for 8/17/07

It's time for my weekly dose of down-home wisdom. I've gone through the hundreds of submissions and I've picked my favorites. So without wasting anymore of your time...here we go!

Dear Urban,

My brother is a little strange, and he gets really annoying. I'll be like sitting on the toilet, or playing piano, or even talking on the phone, and he'll walk up and start talking about a new video game system. He doesn't leave me alone and he always talks about the same things. When I tell him I'm not interested in it politely, he says "okay", waits ten minutes, then starts bugging me again.

He's older than me and should know better, but I can't seem to get it into his head that I'm not in the mood for talking. How do I let him know to just leave me alone?

- OddMunk

Dear OddMunk,

It's interesting that you bring up the fact he's older and you think this means he should know better. It's a well-documented fact that girls mature much faster than boys. Being slow to understand is hard-coded into our DNA. Unfortunately, you're going to have to get used to it. If SisterAchiever was around, she'd tell you the same thing. Well, she'd probably start by telling me to "Get the bobba away!" from her, but eventually, she'd get around to agreeing with everything I just said.

Learn to appreciate him. You never know when you'll need some brotherly advice or a ride home after a bad night out or someone to bail you out of jail.

- UA


Dear Urban,

My signature look all over the Internet is purple hair, and it's not light purple, its dark purple. I've been thinking about dying my real hair that color, but it seems kinda weird, should I?

- gdark1.


Dear gdark1.,

Bobba yes! When I was HighSchoolOverAchiever, I dyed my hair this awesome shade of turquoise. My parents weren't too thrilled, but they realized that hair grows out, growing up is all about trying new things and their son was a little off.

The only thing I might consider if I were you would be get an idea of how your parents might react before you take the plunge into a vat of purple dye. Try something like, "Hey mom, Sarah got grounded because she dyed her hair bright orange. Do you think her parents overreacted?" If your moms is all like, "IF YOU EVER COME HOME WITH YOUR HAIR LOOKING LIKE THAT, YOU'LL NEVER SEE THE LIGHT OF DAY" you might want to think twice.

- UA
Dear UrbanAchiever,

I need fashion advice! I want to be just like you. Where do you buy your clothes??? Who does your hair??? I need the answers I want to be like a Mini-Achiever. HELP ME!!!

- Jonny9702


Dear Johnny9702,

You, my friend, have asked the right Habbo. Before I clue ya in though, I want to say this: you should be looking for your own style. You don't want to be just like me because creating your own look is part of the fun.

As far as where I buy my clothes, that's easy. I get them from everywhere. I'm not a label snob whatsoever. I tend to buy what I think looks good. If it's from Banana Republic or it's from a thrift store, it doesn't matter. All that matter is that I like it. I definitely don't follow the trends.

However, when your hair is concerned, you might want to consider going to a "nicer" place. You can always return clothes, but you can't undo a bad haircut. Also, once you've found a salon or stylist that works for you, stay with 'em.

- UA


Dear Urban,

My friend is a jerk. What do I do?

- Chad-Milestone


Dear Mr. Milestone,

This one's easy: Stop being friends with them. Maybe when they realize that their attitude is lame, they'll stop their jerky ways. Also, the company we keep is a reflection of ourselves. If I choose to hang out with Habbos that are mean, jerky, obnoxious, refuse to share, etc, what does that say about me?

- UA
