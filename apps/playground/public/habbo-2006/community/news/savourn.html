The Little Boy Who Cried Scam [8/24/06]

Savourn

Read this cautionary tale by Savourn...

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Bob. Bob loved his Furni, and would do anything to get more Furni. That's what the problem was. One day Bob had just got himself a Pura minibar, what he had been trying to get forever. Then he had got an idea. He thought if he said he had got scammed that his friends would donate some Furni. So he picked up all his Furni in his room and sent everyone a message saying, "Help! I've just been scammed! Please donate :'(" After he clicked the send button he waited for 2 minutes. His room filled with his good friends. He had tricked his friends into giving him over 100 coins worth of Furni. Then he said, "Fooled you! I wasn't scammed HAH!"

About a week later Bob had just lost a bet in a casino. So he tried his idea again. He sent the exact same message exclaiming, "HELP! I've just been scammed! Please donate :'(" Again, he clicked the send button and in less than 3 minutes all his friends were in his room. They had donated almost double what they had last time. Bob then chuckled, "Haha! I got you again! I wasn't scammed!"

About 5 days later Bob was in a casino and wanted to bet the dealer. The dealer told him to trade all his Furni for 3 HC Sofas then he could bet him. So Bob went to at least 20 Furni shops trying to get 3 HC Sofas for all his Furni. About 1 hour after leaving the casino Bob finally had 3 HC Sofas. So Bob decided to go back to the Casino. Right as he walked in the trade box popped up. So Bob put the 3 HC Sofas in the trade box and clicked agree. All of a sudden a guy in the door had said, "HAH! IT WORKED! I SCAMMED YOU!" and he dissappeared. It turned out a person had the same name as the dealer but with a dot at the end and Bob never noticed. He had been scammed. So Bob thought, "Oh well, my good friends will donate" So Bob sent everyone on his list a message saying, "Help! I've been scammed! Please donate :'( "

After he sent it Bob waited for about 3 minutes, no one had came. Bob decided to wait for another 5 minutes, but still nobody had come. So Bob sent out another message saying, "Why aren't you guys coming to donate?" After about 30 seconds everyone had replied, "Nice try, I'm not falling for that trick 3 times in a row. You're not a real friend. I'm removing you" Bob checked his list and not a single person was on it. Bob was left with no friends and no Furni. Bob had finally learned his lesson. His lesson was to never lie to people about something so serious because when something does actually happen the people you lied to won't believe you. Unfortunately for Bob, he had to learn the hard way.
