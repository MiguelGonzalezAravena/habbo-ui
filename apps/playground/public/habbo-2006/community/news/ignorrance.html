Vanished Underworld? [9/15/06]

Ignorrance.

Ignorrance. solves the mystery behind a missing Guest Room floor...

Those of us old Habbos who have been on Habbo practictally since it began, may remember a certain Guest Room Floor. Yes, I am talking about the Underworld. The glorious retreat for goths and emos alike.I myself was quite fond of it, and I, dare I say it, miss it?

I was out in the Picnic Garden with my friend one day when we started discussing this very topic, when a strange man came up to us. "I used to have a room on the Underworld floor," he said. "Really? Me too." I replied. "I know what happened to it." I stared at him in disbelief. I'd asked everyone I knew, and even random strangers in the Hallway, and no one I asked knew the answer. "What?" me and my friend said in unison. "Come with me," he replied.

He took us to his guest room and invited us to sit. It was glorious. His room had every rare in existance, and some I'd never seen before. I found it intruiging, really, and I thought about writing an article about it. But anyways, we sat, and he began his story. "One day, I was in my room, which was located on the Underground floor, when all of a sudden I felt the ground start shaking. It was an earthquake. I did what I'd been told a million times in school. I hid under a table and covered my head, and, of course, I had my stuffed bunny Mr. Noodles with me." Me and my friend giggled at the thought of him under a table with a bunny named Mr. Noodles. He seemed like the type that could handle everything, I guess not. So he continued. "After it was all done I got up to inspect the damage. EVERYTHING was ruined. The windows were all shattered, and it was raining heavily that day so everything was wet. I went out into the hall to see every single door was open and I saw just about as many mouths open in shock. The whole floor just crumbled. There was absolutely nothing left of it. So I and some other Habbos went through the hotel to see if any floors had been damaged. To our surprise, we were the only ones. We tried to get everything back in order, but we were unable to. And so the management took it off the list."

Me and my friend stared at him in shock. We were dumbfounded by the fact that they were the only floor affected. So that is the story of the vanished Underworld. The magnificent retreat was wiped out by a simple earthquake. The end.
