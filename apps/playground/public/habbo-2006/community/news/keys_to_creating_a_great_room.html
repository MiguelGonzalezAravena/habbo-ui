The Keys to Creating a Great Room [8/7/06]

poopkid21

I often roam the hotel's guest rooms in search of really well designed rooms...

Most of the time I see a lot of the same old room designs I see everywhere. Same layout, Same colors, and sometimes even the same furni. Now this can get very boring. Then every once in a while, I find a room that leaves me speechless. They’re so original, so colorful, and everything in it flows together as if the furni was made for this room. That’s when I start to think about how he/she created this room. The conclusion that I have come to is that they used some specific steps to create such a magnificent room, and I believe I know what they are.

Key One: To start, come up with a theme for your room. Some of the best rooms I have seen have been based off of themes. Even one of my rooms is themed after a Rainforest, and it’s been in a special group of rooms called the Habbo stars that was once in its own special category before version 9 came. The reason Theme rooms usually come out pretty well is because you take things from your theme and recreate them into a Habbo version. You can also come up with things of your own that fit that theme to place into your room. Also when building a theme room, you can start to build the room in your head before you ever start buying or trading for the furni that you need. Which brings me to my next point.

Key Two: Planning your room before you start the final project helps a lot. This helps you to decide what should go where, how big it should be, what furni to use and how to use it. Make sure you go through several different versions of your plans and pick the one that you think looks the best, and never lay down paint and floor until you’re finished with the whole room.

Key Three: Eye catching is something you want your whole room to be. Now I know what you’re thinking. "Why is this part of this article, I already know this?" Stop right there, because if your not careful you will mess-up the flow of your room. You want your Whole room to be eye catching not just a single spot of it. Here’s what I mean. Many Habbos will have the idea to build a big flashy part of their room and stick it in the center or at one wall. This is a big no no. Even if the flashy part looks good it will distract the human eye and cause them to look right at that part the second they walk into the room, thus leaving the rest of the room to be ignored. Now would you want that to happen to a room you worked so hard to build no. The only time this is usually a good thing is if you have a room dedicated to that specific thing.

Key Four: Originality is a big help when creating a room. Making new ways to use furni to suit your theme is always a bonus. Now even though originality is always good, sometimes some old things or things someone else has made are helpful too. If you think something that another Habbo has in their room looks cool and would work well in your room, then use it. Their is nothing wrong with that, but if you want to be nice you could always put a thank you or a shout out to the person you got it from on a sticky or in your room description.

Key Five: Your friends are a valuable asset. Not only are they the people you hang out with and share interests and ideas. They also are people that have the ability to help you with your room. They may have certain skills that you may lack. Such as a stacking ability. They also can give you more ideas on what to do. Whether it’s to add or remove something, or a better way to build something. Remember to as them what they think you should do. A little bit of constructive criticism never hurt anyone. They may have some ideas you like and some you don’t. if you like them use them, id you don’t then don’t. No one can force you to make the change. And if they are really your friends they won’t be upset if you don’t take their advice. Just remember, if you do end up using some of their ideas or skills put up a sticky thanking them for their help or put them in the room description. They will really appreciate it.

Key Six: Never say your room is perfect. Their is always room for improvement in any room. Whether if it’s to improve the room for the theme or just to change a few things around because you decide you like it better.

Key Seven: have fun designing your room. Never be too serious about it. If you don’t have fun with it, it will show and will hurt the flow of the room.

So that’s it. Those are the Seven keys to building a great room. If you follow these I guarantee you will be satisfied with the out come of your room. So what are you waiting for? Get building.
