Habbo Horoscopes [11/10/06]

WideRuledPaper

WideRuledPaper forecasts Habbo-futures!

Hello Habbos: I know that a lot of you have been asking me to bring back Habbo Horoscopes so I am. Of course, I am going to do this edition even better than any other one before so this one will include: Habbo Horoscopes, Pet Horoscopes, and STAFF Horoscopes!
I hope you enjoy this new jam packed edition of Habbo Horoscopes.

Habbo Aries: [March 21st thru April 20th]
This week is a very busy week for you. You are trying to do so much at once that you don’t know where to start. Just relax, calm down and take a breather. Maybe fit in a game of Battle Ball to take your mind away from the stress!
Habbo Taurus: [April 21st thru May 21st]
Taurus’ love the Lodge furniture line this week. The warm wholesome feeling of the furniture that attracts you to it.
Habbo Gemini: [May 22nd thru June 21st]
Wow, Geminis you are having the best week this week! You are doing well at school or work and you are even making people see such a hard worker you are!
Habbo Cancer: [June 22nd thru July 23rd]
Cancers are having a safety issue this week! Make sure you keep that personal information secret!
Habbo Leo: [July 24th thru August 23rd]
Leos love the Candy Furni line this week! It looks sleeker than the Mode and even more comfy!
Habbo Virgo: [August 24th thru September 23rd]
Virgos are competition masters this week. So participate in any competition you can find and you have a good chance of winning!
Habbo Libra: [September 24th thru October 23rd]
With your birthday just passing you want to make a new name for yourself. Start over and become an even better Libra!
Habbo Scorpio: [October 24th thru November 22nd] HAPPY BIRTHDAY!
Your favorite thing this week is the Pura fridge! You love how you can get three items from it for only 6 coins!
Habbo Sagittarius: [November 23rd thru December 21st]
The only thing on your mind this week is Wobble Squabble and being on the top five. Well, don’t go to far and good luck with your aspirations!
Habbo Capricorn: [December 22nd thru January 20th]
Capricorns are the innovators this week! They can make such a great room out of a little bit of furniture. Have fun and make that great room!
Habbo Aquarius: [January 21st thru February 19th]
Aquarius’ are being more like Pisces this week with taking over the plant Aloe Vera. This week more and more Aquarians want the Aloe Vera added to their room!
Habbo Pisces: [February 20th thru March 20th]
Your favorite piece of furniture this week is the HoloDice. So get one and make an awesome casino out of it!

Habbo Pet Horoscopes:
Habbo Cat: Your cat is very happy this week. It will love to play with you and be more active!
Habbo Dog: Your dog is a little grumpy this week. So get it another toy and give it much more attention!

THE SPECIAL ADD OF STAFF HOROSCOPES!
Kedo: Your week this week is an OK week. Lots of work but you balance it off with lots of fun!
Iomegadrive: Your Emo Oranges are starting to take back the hotel. So get that HQ set back up and let those Oranges run free!
Luna: Your costume for Habboween showed your more creative side so don’t be scared if all of a sudden you find yourself doodling or just thinking about art of some kind.
Caedma: Your blue hair shows your individuality. That is what Habbos LOVE ABOUT YOU!
Hiro-P: This week is a very stressful one for you; lots of work and not a lot of time for play. So just buckled down and get the stuff done that is needed.
Brown-Pants: You are becoming more and more jealous of the sudden Emo Oranges come back. Maybe another kidnapping?
Sandee: This week is a wonderful week for you. Yes, it has some work in it, but you will find yourself just humming or skipping.
Brena: You don’t want Habboween to end! That was the most fun you’ve had in Habbo yet!
Whimsy: This week you are a little bit grumpy. Just drink a couple cups of Mocha Master Regular and sit in your Tubmaster and chill.
Becs: You are overloaded this week. Just remember to have some fun in all this excitement going around.
Chickabiddy: Work is the main thing on your mind this week. Remember, like Whimsy, to just chill out and have some fun.

Well Habbos and Staff that’s this weeks edition of Habbo Horoscopes! Let the Stars Guide You
Happy Habboing!
