WideRuledPaper & Loderse, polishing the crystal ball

What Does Your Future Hold?

Heya Habbos! WideRuledPaper & Loderse here! Well we just wanted to come together and and re-vamp Habbo Horsocopes, but we're calling them HabboScopes!

 

Welcome to our very FIRST EDITION of HabboScopes! In this edition you'll find Habbo Horoscopes and Habbo Pet Horoscopes! We hope you enjoy!


Habbo Horoscopes

Habbo Aries:
[Mar 21st - Apr 20th]
This week will be an okay week, but you must not trust everyone. There will be someone who tries to get in your way this week; just brush it off and hop down to the pool deck and relax!

 Habbo Libra:
[Sep 24th - Oct 23rd]
This week may bring stress to you, Libras. Just remember to relax and everything will get better! Go play some Battle Ball to work off some of that stress!
Habbo Taurus:
[Apr 21st - May 21st]
You may be confronted with a problem at home. Just take it one step at a time and everything will be okay! Taurus' are also very luck this week, so head on down to the Trading floor and play some games of Defend your Pod!		 Habbo Scorpio:
[Oct 24th - Nov 22nd]
Scorpios are loving the new RARE Sand Patch Furni item! Be sure to pick one up, Scorpios for a better week!

Habbo Gemini:
[May 22nd - Jun 21st]
While the moon rests in its 12th house this week, the Gemini's creativity is going crazy! Be sure to focus your imagination on productive situations and you'll find yourself a happier Gemini!

 Habbo Sagittarius:
[Nov 23rd - Dec 21st]
Sagittarians need constant conversation, so be sure not to mess anything up with your friends this week! You may be heading for some trouble, so watch your actions because they can have some consquences.

Habbo Cancer:
[Jun 22nd - Jul 23rd]
Cancers are extremely talkative this week. Just remember, watch what you say - you don't want to start a rumor about Loderse!

 Habbo Capricorn:
[Dec 22nd - Jan 20th]

[Jul 24th - Aug 23rd]
HAPPY BIRTHDAY, LEO!
Leos find comfort this week with the love of friends and family. Be sure to return the favor in making sure they know how much you appreciate them! Maybe sending them some roses will do the trick!		 Habbo Aquarius:
[Jan 21st - Feb 19th]

Habbo Virgo:
