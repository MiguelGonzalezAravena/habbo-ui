Habbo Stock Market [8/1/06]

poopkid21

A quick guide to get the most of your Habbo Trading

Have you ever had a rare that you got for 25 coins out of the catalogue and sold it for two or three times the amount? The feeling is great when you are able to make a profit off of something like this. But then the opposite of this can happen to you as well. You may have traded for a rare that was worth two or three hc sofas, and then when you feel like trading it you end up having to trade it for less. That’s how Habbo trading plays out like the stock market.

To start, there is the catalogue effect. The best way to make a profit off of this is like I explained earlier. Buy the rare for 25 coins and sell it during the peak of the hype. When the rare is first released everyone is so excited and anxious to get it that it usually sky rockets in the trading rooms. Usually by the habbos that are unable to buy it from the catalogue. But if you're looking to trade for the rare you should seriously wait a few days until the excitement dies down. Like most rares from the catalogue they are usually about 1.5-2 hcs work a few days after they are released, then after awhile they go down to 1-.5 hcs. That is the time to trade. But be warned some rares are more popular and won’t go down past two hcs, such as the petal patch. Seasonal rares are usually easy to predict prices. When out of season they usually double in price. Since they come out at a specific time their prices don’t change until they are out, in which they usually go for the same price in the catalogue or foe a few coins more.

Super rares and Unreleased Catalogue rares are about the trickiest to determine. Super rares are as the name states super rare and harder to come by. That’s why they usually go for very high prices. Some of them can be pretty consistent, while others can have very large ranges in prices in which it would have to very from owner to owner. The riskiest items to have are the unreleased catalogue rares. These are rares that can be found in the catalogue under the rare section but haven’t appeared their yet. They are risky because you never know when they might be released. So if you pay 50 hc sofas for one and then a month later it is released in the catalogue then the value plunges and you are left with sad because you lost so many hcs.

To end, Trading in habbo hotel is in fact a skill. Just like trading in the stock market is. You have to be able to know when the best times to buy and sell each item in order to make a profit and keep your self from losing a lot of furni. So I hope this little article helps you with that task. Happy trading, or should I say Happy investing.
