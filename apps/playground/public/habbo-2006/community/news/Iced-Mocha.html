How to Build A Room That Fits You [12/6/06]

Iced-Mocha

Show off your Habbo in style!

Building sometimes can be a pain, especially when you don’t know what to make! You might be thinking “Trade room? But I don’t even trade!” Well, I have some great tips that would help you be able to think about what to make that will help fit YOUR personality!

Personality: Random/Perky - Think of building a party lounge. Fill it with all of the furniture that will make your room fit your personality like numerous amounts of mocha masters or the furni you adore the most!

Personality: Trades often - Think about building a trade room or a furniture shop. Let other Habbos trade with you if you need anything that they have! Make sure they say, “Trading” & “Offering” Major no-no: “Buying” or “Selling”.

Personality: Party Person - Think about building a party room for your Habbos to chill in and party. Maybe even have some music going if any of your friends work for a Habbo Fansite that has radio on it! (clubhabbo.net Bluebobble.com etc.)

Personality: Likes to help Habbos out - Think about making a help center with stickers that will help them understand more about the hotel. Don’t forget the helpers!

Personality: Food (mm.. Cake!) - Go for making a restraunt! Who knows you might be Habbos best chef & restraunt owner on this hotel!

Personality: Betting - Think about making a casino (scam free ofcourse!) Just make sure that you don’t scam any Habbos or give rights out to everyone that asks you. Make sure go by the Habbo Way, if not find a ban that’s comming very soon towards ‘ya!

Personality: Gaming - Not good at wobble squable? Try to make a room version of this one! You could also try to make other games like DYP (Defend Your Pod), Deal or No Deal!, Falling Furni (scam free ofcourse), Don’t Hit The Wall, Name That Artist/Song, etc.

Personality: Friendly & Loved - Try to make a memories room with all your presents, trophies and pictures that you have taken on Habbo to show your friends how much you care!

Hopefully this give you guys a pretty good idea about making a room that would suit you best! Enjoy and keep creating!
