Habbo Horoscopes has returned with a wonderful edition! This week it gets even better! Habbo Pets and THE CHINESE ASTROLOGY! So I hope you enjoyed this jam-packed Horoscopes!

Habbo Aries: [Created From: March 21st-April 20th]

As a taurus you are very determined. So join this new habbo competition and do awesome on it! Remember your determination will let you prevail!

Habbo Aquarius: [Created From: January 21st- Febuary 19th]
Aquarians are visionaries. They are always looking into the future. But remember the future is still far away deal with present issues and friends now!
Habbo Pisces: [Created From: Febuary 20th-March 20th]
Pisces are very easy going. You as a pisces can be very laid back but at the same time very focused. So stick with whatever mood makes you happier!

Habbo Dogs for all signs: Your dog wants a friend! If it already has another pet in the same room then put it in another room with a new friend. It thrives to make more friends!

Habbo Cats for all signs: Your habbo cat is very lazy and bored this week. Buy it an extra toy or have a party in your room!
