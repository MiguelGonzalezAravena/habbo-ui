Missions Galore [10/13/06]

Vixenx

Vixenx takes a closer look at console missions...

An interesting way of telling one Habbo from another is through their "mission". You can read a lot into their personality, likes and current mood just through this little sentence or two. It's really awesome [Honestly!] and led me to write up this little list of the main types of missions being clicked on around the Hotel. Maybe you can spot yours!

Quotes: Many Habbos have missions with famous quotes, or lines from songs and poems that mean something to them. This could show they are outspoken and if it's a song then they may be interested in finding friends with similar musical interests.

Helpful Habbo: You will often see a Habbo's mission as something along the lines of "Just Ask" or "Help is at hand" meaning that they feel they are an experienced player and wish to help other Habbos.

Randomness: Yep what I like to call "randomness", every now and then you'll come across a mission which is just well...WEIRD. Something like "Cheese", "Bob rocks" or "HULLABALOO". Usually this is the case if one can't think of anything else to write or perhaps they are just looking for a reaction among their fellow Habbos!

The Love Mission: One of the common missions expresses love, "Laura loves Jake", "James and Daisy 4ever" etc. Usually found in fancy accented letters or with love hearts, this habbo wants everyone to know they are in loove!

The Mission Mission: I think that most Habbos when they first started playing had a mission like this, "To get a job" or "To make friends and have fun", after all it is a MISSION! The Habbos who have these missions are usually new to the hotel, and you should be nice to them :)

Jobs/Armies: Newer players and a few experienced often have missions showing their "Habbo Job", for example "M@DM" or "[U.S.T] Rank 5". This is the often the case if they have yet to use their mission for expressing themselves, but usually it can be found along side something else.

Carryin: Something like "Carrying: Lightsaber" or "Carrying: Make-up" is common to see at the end of a lot of Habbos missions. Possibly it is something they really carry around with them in the real world, something that shows who they are.

Blank: Self-explanatory, a blank mission. A lot of Habbos go for this option if they can't think of a suitable thing to write.

Trading: Simply "Trading" as the mission is another common one and often used to trick other Habbos into thinking they are engaged in a trade, or maybe to limit the number of trades they are offered.

Name: This is the Habbo's real life name or a nickname they like to be known by, usually with decorative alt symbols around it or a TM sign at the end. This is most likely so they can be known by their actual name, and not as "hotbabex848" or "coolguy4285".

Offer me: "Offer me a lodge bar for my candy bed" Need I explain? This Habbo is putting their mission to use!

Advertising: Another practical way of using the mission, this is just a good method of advertising rooms or websites. (Be very careful if you visit websites advertised in a Habbo's mission, it's probably best if you don't go to them at all).

Phew, I think that just about covers them all! There are so many different ways you can express yourself through missions, but remember whatever one you choose to have try to be yourself and stay unique :]

Thanks for reading!
