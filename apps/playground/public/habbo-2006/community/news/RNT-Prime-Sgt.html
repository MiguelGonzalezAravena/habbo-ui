10 Easy Steps to Winning a Room Competition! [12/14/06]

RNT-Prime-Sgt.

Sharpen your decorating skills with this guide!

Have you ever wondered, “Man, I wish I could somehow get my Habbo name out there for the world to see?” Well here is your chance!

Every so often, Habbo Hotel will tell all the Habbos about some great, exciting, event that is happening in the Hotel. These events are called “Quests” and some of them can be won by making a radical room. Sometimes if you make a room and submit it to Habbo, Kedo and Luna and all those great Staff Members will choose your room, and put it on the homepage!

So now your thinking, “Wow, that is AMAZING, but how can I do it?” Well, here’s your chance my friends!

How to Make Your Room Competition Room:

  1. Find out what kind of theme the Quest is, and think of all kinds of Furni that you could use for that Theme.
  2. Go to Habbo.com/credits and get the necessary amount of coins for the Theme Furni. (Make sure you have your parents’ permission to get Habbo Coins!!!)
  3. Buy your Theme Furni from the Catalogue, and put it in the room you want to use for the Competition.
  4. Re-name your room, to something that has to do with the Theme that the Quest is all about. (Be sure to be CREATIVE and ORIGINAL!)
  5. Organize the Furni you bought for the room, and set it up so that it is a marvelous piece of your imagination. HINT: You can use Habbo Rollers and Mode Z-Shelfs to stack some amazing tower Themes!
  6. Get some buddies into your room and have them say stuff that has to do with the Theme. (Like if it’s Habboween, get them to say stuff like “RAWR, I’m coming for your BRAINS!”)
  7. Press the “Prnt Screen\SysRq” button.
  8. Open up “Paint” on your computer and press Ctrl+v to paste the “Screenie” that you just took of your room on Paint.
  9. “Save As” the Screenie, onto your computer.
  10. Click the Link that Habbo has willfully provided, and paste your Screenie in the e-mail, along with any thing else that Habbo wants you to put in with the e-mail. Click “SEND.”

Guess what Habbos? YOU’RE DONE! That’s it, that’s all. Now all you have to do is wait, and see if you won the competition.

Also, if you win the Room Competition, you could also win a couple months of HabboClub and a special piece or pack of Furni that has to do with the Theme. All winnings are FREE, as long as you win.

I hope you guys have fun with making these rooms, and I’m sure you’ll be CREATIVE and ORIGINAL. Right? Well, good luck to all of you Habbos out there! Peace.
