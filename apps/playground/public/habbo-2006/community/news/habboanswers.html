The Habbo Life Answers [6/9/06]

Retromatic

An answer to ,,corey,, 's Habbo Quirks

As a direct result to ,,corey,,'s weird findings in Habbo Hotel, I decided to figure out why some of those things were so weird. I guess you could say that I'm a Myth buster!
(For your information: ,,corey,, I am not trying to rain on your parade or anyone else's, just trying to answer the secrets!)

1) A Square of Grass Costs a Fortune.
Answer: Well, first you have to grow the grass and the foliage, and then you have to transport it high up in the hotel.

2) There's a weird key shape in the door.
Answer: Well, if you have noticed, you can put a lock on the door of your room. Well don't you need a key to unlock it? (Also if you were wondering: The Habbos you enter your room stand in what you might call a mudroom, where people take off their coats, and shoes in real life. Not many houses do have a mudroom, but putting on in your room keeps it spotless.)

3) Cats and dogs don't fight.
Answer: Well, the only logical explanation is - THE PETS ARE FROM MARS! No! Just Kidding! If you look at it, the cats must come from breeders that put them together with dogs, and visa versa. Sulake only "packages' the pet, sort of like a store where you can buy a dog or cat.

4) A fridge contains ice-cream's.
Answer: This is a great question - it took me a lot of researching to do. If you notice when you open a fridge, there is a little box at the top of the inside. That is an icebox, where anything frozen can be stored!

5) You don't have to refill a fridge.
Answer: Because it is always full! When you purchased any of the wide variety of fridges from the Habbo Catalogue, you bought a lifetime supply of beverages (and food in the case of ice cream).

6) All sticky pad corners are bent.
Answer: That is a very hard question to answer! There are a few possible answers to why sticky pad corners are bent. Well, one logical explanation is that they are like an easy pull off. That all the stickies are sticky on the whole back side and the corners are just a quick way to pull them off if they stay (A Bit confusing, but read it over and you might get it). Another logical reason would be that without knowing it, we Habbos just press down the corner when we stick the sticky down. It's like second nature to us!

7) There are shadows under furni but no sun.
Answer: Although it may seem like we don't have lights without buying the ones from the catalogue, we do. If we didn't our rooms would be a distorted color of what we see now. So we do have lights in our rooms, maybe just planted into the ceiling.

8) You don't have to move to turn the lights on and off.
Answer: The only logical reason is that the lamps (such as the Pura lamp) come with remotes. Or they have a "click light' system, somewhat like the "clap system".

9) Every room has a window except the box room.
Answer: The box room is an interior hotel room.

10) Fire places don't produce smoke.
Answer: The Fire in all of the fireplaces in like a television screen, as it is not a good idea to have a fireplace going in a hotel. The chimney and covering for the screen is purely decoration.

11) There are only 75 windows on the hotel but way more rooms.
Correction: There are 120 windows on the front and left side of the hotel.
Answer: Well, most of the stuff like public rooms and some private hotel rooms must happen on the rear side and right side of the Hotel, as we can not see it. Rooftop Rumble must have be placed on the highest point in the Hotel (That was quite redundant, but the hotel we see on the Hotel View is only a portion of the whole hotel). Most of the private hotel rooms are on the inside, and must have a window illusion built in.

12) There are no doorways to public rooms, how do we get in?
Answer: They all do! Although you do not stand in a "Mudroom" in most of the rooms, you do start out very close to the entrance area. In Club Mammoth, the entrance area is the stairs.

13) Habbo's don't need water or food and don't die.
Answer: This was the easiest question to answer. The answer (which could be the answer for all of the questions [I decided to make it fun and not use this answer for all of them]) is that it is a game. That's all there is to it.

14) The dead ducks blood doesn't dry out or fade.
Answer:The only logical answer is that the ducks are immobile robots that pump lifelike blood and green ectoplasm constantly, and suck it back up. Sort of like one of those small pebble fountains used for decoration in homes.

15) You can get in the bath with clothes on and you come out dry.
Answer: Well, you might not even get wet at all! If you really got wet, then you would have to buy a Habbo Clothes dryer, which would make people want to buy less baths and not hope for their new Tubmaster from Habbo Club.

16) The candles don't melt.
Answer: The only logical explanation is that the candles are electric, but have a lifelike flame made out of a flickering light bulb.

17) There are no hot or cold taps on a bath.
Answer: Habbo just knows the perfect bath temperature for a Habbo. Unfortunately, they do not know what it is in real life.

18) The plants don't die or need water.
Answer: One answer is that they are plastic! Another is that they have the energy to feed themselves and sustain life.

19) You can get a tan in an instance.
Answer: True, but you can do that in real life, even if it does cost you money at a tanning salon.

20) You can change from boy to girl straight away!
Answer: Well you could, but at heart you would be just the same person you always are. Well I've busted every Myth that ,,corey,, had to offer, but after doing that I had questions of my own that I was willing to solve...
