The Petal Patch Crisis [5/25/06]

Duhon

Duhon enlightens us on the state of Petal Patches...

All around the hotel and on my console, I have been witnessing how petals are being talked about everywhere. So how do I “wheel and deal” with this? Well petal patches are a nice kind of furni for your room. It is really cool to have them cover your whole floor. I have 35 petal patches, which I have been trading for them and not trading them away.

Why aren’t I? I could make a cool room. Yet, this is all part of the business in trying to get furni for your room. I’ve also noticed how smart some other people have been. People buy for say 1 Club Sofa then sell for higher OR wait for them to go out of the catalogue and let the price raise. Which is what I will do because this is exactly what happened with Dragon Eggs. So that is how I think you should deal with petals. Now you see why MANY and I mean MANY people are trading for those cool petal patches. I hope you find your way to get more furniture for your room!
