The Key to Making Popular Rooms [5/25/06]

Rure

Rure shows how to get your room to the top of the list...

So you’re sitting at your computer desk browsing through the rooms that never seem to be unoccupied and are always found at the top of the popular rooms list. You’ve just bought a batch of coins, or maybe you have extra furniture lying around, and you plan on making that one room that will have many Habbos coming in all throughout the day; however, you have no idea where to start or what to do! Many Habbos, like yourself, stress themselves out with the lack of ideas of making that one room; consequently, leading them to make rooms that seem to be more desolated than occupied throughout the day. I’ve had my share of owning some fairly popular rooms, ranging from apartments to wedding chapels. Here are some quick pointers that may help you into making that one room that will never find itself deserted?

1. Keep it ORIGINAL.
Not everyone wants the same old room. For example, making a new trade room or adoption center is a definite no-no. Rooms like these have been created millions of times by numerous Habbos, most of them seem to fail because of the lack of originality. The approach to having a populated room is to have it become noticed by people, and only a fresh or unique idea for a room will catch their attention. Instead of making another restaurant, make a sushi bar, or instead of making another falling furni room, create your own game, do whatever you have to do that will be able to catch the eyes of various Habbos. Catching the attention of Habbos is the key to a successful room, which leads me to my next pointer.

2. Make your room name CATCHY AND NOTICEABLE
In many cases, the name to your room may be the most important thing into luring Habbos into your room. Say that you were creating a romantic restaurant, now you wouldn’t just name it “Restaurant”, would you? Of course not. The fundamental to making your room stand out from the rest is by putting life into your room name, come up with a catchy name for your restaurant, for example “The Amour Café”, following with simple decorations like alt codes such as hearts on each side. Just by looking at that room name, many Habbos can conclude that is a romantic restaurant and many will want to come inside just because of that unique, well thought-out name.

3. Have a certain THEME
One thing a successful room cannot go without with is a theme. Without a theme, the room may seem all distorted and ugly. Iced sofas with area sofas, blue floors with red walls, you name it. Have a certain theme for your room, have the room’s wallpaper and flooring hold a certain color scheme that will match with the furniture. Have all the furniture have something in common with each other, keep the furniture iced or lodge, but whatever you do, never have two themes for one room, because most of the time it will only make your room look horrid and disgusting. The key to holding Habbos in your room is having them not be chased off by the hideous layout of your room.

4. Keep it ALIVE
Sometimes, having an original room with a catchy title and an attractive theme may not always give you that one room that will have Habbos coming in all throughout that day. On many occasions, you may have to come in to play just to keep your room energetic. When Habbos are in your room, they don’t want to come just to sit down. Mingle with your visitors, hold games and competitions, dance like a monkey if you have to, do whatever you have to do to have your visitors stay a while. Rooms are sometimes remembered for the person who owns them, having a likeable reputation will only bring you more visitors to come!

And that pretty much sums up all the pointers! Follow these four pointers, and there is a high chance that your room will have its spot at the top of the list, having Habbos from every hour coming in. Originality, creativity, theme, and ambition pretty much sums up the pointers to making a successful and popular room. All I can say now is get your creativity juices flowing, and good luck with your room!
