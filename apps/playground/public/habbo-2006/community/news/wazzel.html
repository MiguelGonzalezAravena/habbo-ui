Counting Down Until The Habbo Holidays! [11/28/06]

-::WazzeL::-

A little help to get you into the spirit of the season...

The Habbo Holidays will be here before you know it, and with that said, let me give you some tips on getting into the holiday spirit!

Firstly: Make a Christmas themed room. Load it up with lots and lots of Christmas Furni and design it for parties. Christmas Parties are always fun to throw and go to. You can have some games with doorprizes, a visit from Santa, a SECRET gift exchange (make sure everyone gets a gift), and, Of course, some mistletoe kissing!

Secondly: Give Some Gifts To All Your Closest Friends! Here's some ideas in helping you get the perfect gift:

  • Ask your friends, casually, what their favorite piece of Furni is. If it is impossible to get, then keep asking until they state something easy and casual to get. The nicer the gift, the more it can say, "Thanks for being a good friend." Rares would be the best for showing that you care about someone. Or maybe even a Lovely trophy with a great message on it. If you have no luck, Christmas Furni is always a great substitute.
  • If you have lots of friends and want less expensive gifts, you can always go with a duck, cause hey, it's the thought that counts.
  • Don't Forget To Sign The Prezzie with a nice message and, of course, your Habbo name!

Thirdly: Be cheery all of December. It's the month of giving and loving and caring for each other. So do so. Tell people nice compliments like, " Nice Outfit," or even "Hi. How Are You?" It could turn a Habbos crappy day the other way. So be cheery and be counting down the days until Christmas.

Remember, Turn A Crappy Day the Other Way!
