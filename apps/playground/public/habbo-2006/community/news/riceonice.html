Habbo I.Q. Test [5/2/07]

RiceOnIce

Can you beat RocketSkates at this mind game?

See how smart your brain is at these Habbo questions!

  1. What does HC mean?
  2. What is a Habbo eXpert?
  3. What does FF stand for?
  4. How many tickets do you need to play one game of Battle Ball?
  5. What is the 6th month gift of HC?
  6. How many types of pets are there?
  7. How many credits come from one voucher?
  8. What does 1 sack + 1 gold bar + 3 gold coins equal?
  9. What type of drink comes from the Candy Cauldron?
  10. When a room is “Orange”, what does this mean?

Answers:

  1. Habbo Club
  2. Someone who helps new players by telling them about the game and what to watch out for. Habbo eXperts normally hang out in The Welcome Lounge.
  3. Falling Furni.
  4. Two.
  5. Imperial Teleports.
  6. Three.
  7. Fifty.
  8. 100 Coins.
  9. Love Potion.
  10. This means the room is crowded.

If You Scored:
1-2, You Are Not Bright.
3-4, Getting Up There!!
5-6, Above Average.
7-8, Smarty Pants.
9-10, Intelligent!!
