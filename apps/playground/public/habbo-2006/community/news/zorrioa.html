A Witchy Poem [11/2/06]

Zorrioa

Zorrioa shares a scary poem for Habboween...

You asked for it! You made an article to promote the poem,
So I wrote this one just to show 'em.
You just may think it’s weird for me to do this,
So I’m writing a poem 'bout a lil' old witchy miss.
It was old Habbo's eve and all through the hotel,
People were shrieking because of a smell.
A little old lady had just brewed a stew
That would make you know just what to do.

 
She smelt it once and threw back her head,
She screamed to the world," Now I will learn how to fill Habbo with dread"
She sniffled that smell right into her nose,
Around old Habbo’s Eve just 'bout anything goes.
"It needs a few touches, to make it awesome,
Just to make this old potion really blossom."
She took from her shelf an old blue sandal,
It was taken from a little boy named Randal.

Just about when she would dip it in,
The Tele glowed and started to spin.
Through the doors looking awful surprised,
It was a little Habbo with shining blue eyes.
The witch screamed, “ No it can’t be you!"
Randal had just stepped through the door without his left shoe.
He ran at the witch and jumped over the cauldron.
It’s a good thing he had his other shoe on.

He grabbed the sandal right from the witch’s hand.
The witch let out a piercing scream that could be heard all through the land.
The witch then muttered a few words under her breath.
Then spiders came down and hit him right in the chest.
Randal dropped the shoe and ran away screaming.
Then with a jolt he realized he had been dreaming.
He got out of bed and looked for his shoe.
He found one but not number two.

For on Old Habbo’s Eve just about anything goes,
Even a witch with a wart on her nose.
