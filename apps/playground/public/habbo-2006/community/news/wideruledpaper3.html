Habbo Horoscopes [Habboween Edition] [10/20/06]

WideRuledPaper

WideRuledPaper tells you what is in store for Habboween...

Hello Habbos! Sorry that I took last week off with the Habbo Horoscopes but I had to have enough time to do this Jam-Packed Edition of Habbo Horoscopes HABBOWEEN EDITION! This Edition Includes: Normal Horoscopes, Normal Horoscopes With Habboween Twist, Habbo Pet Horoscopes, And Habbo Pet Horoscopes with Habboween Twist.
Normal Horoscopes:
Habbo Aries: [March 21st Thru April 20th]
The Aries favorite color is red or orange. So make your Habbo room red or orange and maybe buy some red candles or red teles. Having your room red or orange will make you a happier Habbo Aries.
Habbo Taurus: [April 21st thru May 21st]
Your magic number is six. Whether it be six year or the 6th day, something good will happen with a six.
Habbo Gemini: [May 22nd thru June 21st]
The Gemini’s day is a Wednesday. So on a Wednesday go out and play some Battle Ball or Falling Furni and you might just win.
Habbo Cancer: [June 22nd thru July 23rd]
Your favorite piece of Habbo Furni this week is Rubber Ducks. You find them the best thing in the world and you might find yourself wanting a large number of them.
Habbo Leo: [July 24th thru August 23rd]
Leos tend to like to move around a lot so Teleporters might do you some good this week. Any kind as long as you get to explore many rooms quickly.
Habbo Virgo: [August 24th thru September 23rd]
Your favorite thing is public service. Either helping at a help center or donating as long as you do something to benefit someone else either then yourself you will be a happier Habbo Virgo.
Habbo Libra: [September 24th thru October 23rd]
HAPPY BIRTHDAY! Habbo Libras are in love with Habbo Furni Deals. You like to get more Furni for the price of one. So buy a couple deals and you will find them coming into very good use.
Habbo Scorpio: [October 24th thru November 22nd]
Scorpios tend to like plants. So either trade for a couple of plants or buy some, plants will make you feel better and more at home.
Habbo Sagittaurius: [November 23rd thru December 21st]
Sagittaurians need change. So maybe change one of your shirts or even as far as your whole outfit. As long as you change something you will feel better.
Habbo Capricorn: [December 22nd thru January 20th]
Battle Ball is your thing this week. Either it be playing or watching there is just something about Battle Ball that makes you happy.
Habbo Aquarius: [January 21st thru February 19th]
Aquarians have the safety issue this week. You will either have trouble with scammers or go a little to far. Remember not to give out any personal information.
Habbo Pisces: [February 20th thru March 20th]
A new opportunity will come to you this week. Either to run a new Falling Furni or HQ or even a new job, some opportunity will come to you.

Normal Horoscopes Habboween Style:
Habbo Aries:
Your favorite costume this Habboween is a vampire. So dress in black and red and whip out those fangs!
Habbo Taurus:
Your favorite Habboween Furni piece is the Spider web. So get some spider webs or even just go in a room with them but adding spider webs will make your Habboween room ten times better.
Habbo Gemini:
Your favorite costume this Habboween is a Zombie. So become a Zombie and go to some costume parties.
Habbo Cancer:
Your favorite Habboween event this year is Habboween Costume Changes. So head to the game floor and find a couple Habboween Style Costume Changes :-).
Habbo Leo:
Your favorite Habboween color is red. So why don’t you try making a completely red room with Habboween style furniture in it.
Habbo Virgo:
Since you love public service why don’t you try making a Habboween Style Help Center. It will help you get in the Habboween Spirit while enjoying your favorite thing to do: Help.
Habbo Libra:
Since Libras love deals, try trading for the best deal on your favorite Habboween furniture.
Habbo Scorpio:
Since Scorpios love plants this week, try dressing up as a tree or a rose on Habbo and make that your Habboween costume.
Habbo Sagittarius:
Sagittarians need change so try changing around your Habboween or Halloween costume to make it even better. Remember change is a good thing.
Habbo Capricorn:
Since Battle Ball is your favorite thing to do this week, dress up in your Habboween costume and hang around the Battle Ball Open Lounge. Other Habbos will have fun by seeing your cool costume.
Habbo Aquarius:
Safety is a big issue with you this week so worry about staying safe and not so much having so much fun to give away your personal information.
Habbo Pisces:
Pisces love the costume of BOB THE BUILDER! So either dress up as Bob The Builder or Bobette The Builder you find it so funny to be dressed up as Bob.
Normal Habbo Pet Horoscopes:
Habbo Cat
Your Habbo cat might be a little grumpy this week. Just keep it happy by being around it and playing with it.
Habbo Dog:
Your dog is really thirsty this week. So make sure you have a water bowl down for them so they can get all the water they want.
Habboween Style Pet Horoscopes:
Habbo Cat:
Your cat is a little vampire this week. They want to be in a vampire related room or have their owner dress up like a vampire.
Habbo Dog:
Your dog is not going to like the Habboween style rooms so if you see it getting sadder and sadder in your Habboween room, just pick them up and set them in a non-Habboween related room. I hope you enjoyed my Habboween Edition of Habbo Horoscopes :-)
