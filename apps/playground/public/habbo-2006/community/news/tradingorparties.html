To Make Profit or Parties? [5/18/06]

BirdBoy30

Find out the pros and cons of both with BirdBoy30...

Whenever someone signs on to the Hotel, they will typically go to one of the following two rooms: The Party and Chill floor or the Trade floor. When I first got hooked on the second reality that is Habbo, I became a Party and Chill kind of guy. That is basically the only guest floor that I would go to. I knew nothing of this “trading” business, and to tell the truth, I really didn’t care too much about it either. I didn’t waste my time going to furni shops only to be kicked as soon as I entered my offer. I just took it easy and got my groove on!

But at the same time, as time passed, I became more and more interested in the art of trading and creating my own trade shops. Now all that I do is trade! I almost never go onto the Party floor. My whole Habbo attitude has changed, and I feel most others feel the same way. Traders and, if I may call them, “Partiers,” are almost completely different species. If I do go to the Party floor and join a room, I feel as though the people in the room have no idea what “HC Value” is or what a “.5” is. If you haven’t understood me so far, think of it like this.

Imagine the Hotel as a planet with two separate continents.

The Trade continent is on one side of the planet and the Party continent is on the opposite. But it shouldn’t be like this. Learn to do a little of both. There is so much to Habbo. Don’t limit yourself to only one part of the planet when you can explore the entire thing! If you have some furni, go try trading every once in a while. You don’t have to trade just for profit. If you really want something you may have to sacrifice a little. And don’t just create large furni warehouses but instead create cool rooms where you and your bro’s can crash and just hang out. Become a well rounded Habbo through and through!
