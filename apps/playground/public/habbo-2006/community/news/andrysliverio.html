Is DJ-Bling a Good Choice? [12/14/06]

AndrySliverio

Some unkind words for the Winter Holiday Boss...

Have you ever thought about why you picked DJ-Bling as the Winter Holiday Supreme Whatever? Did you really take time to think about it? Did you make a good choice? Well maybe this should clear your mind a little bit…

What is that thing that a lot of Habbos lack? That thing that is not the most important one but by some strange reason we people believe it is. I’ll give you a clue: It’s green, it’s square-shaped and attracts everyone... Duh! MONEY! And DJ-Bling has a lot of it. That’s why most Habbos are attracted to his greedy side. Shame on those that let DJ-Bling buy them like toys with his lousy big checkbook and his gold credit cards!

What happened to family dinners and getting together with friends just to sing holiday tunes and have a good time... POOF gone. Since DJ-BLING came it’s just party, party and more partying and forget all the holiday values. There’s nothing wrong with having a good family dinner and then party. DJ-Bling, just comes thinking he’s the royalty and starts throwing money and prezzies to keep making everyone forget the holidays…the real holidays.

Next to DJ-Bling and all his money and candies we have Rasta.Claus. He might have become a little crazy with the years and everything but not even that will make him forget what the holidays are really about... keeping the spirit and love alive in ourselves in a healthy and funky way. Sometimes the good things may look boring and stupid but in the end those that rather take the good path end up having twice the fun than those that chose to just party all night long and forget their family and relatives.

You should think again about who you think should be the Winter Holidays Supreme Leader Thingy. Happy Holidays!
