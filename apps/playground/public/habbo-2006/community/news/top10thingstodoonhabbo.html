Top Ten Things to do While on Habbo [7/14/06]

Clockspider128

TTTtdWoH, a list :)

10. Visit the Habbosphere pool deck. There is so much to do at the pool deck. While you're there you can go for a swim, take a seat and meet some new friends, or you can try some new stunts off the diving board.

9. Iomegadrive's MANIC FALLING FURNI. If you're bored on a Friday afternoon then go try your skill at Iomegadrive's Manic Falling Furni. But you've got to get there early to get a good spot in line. Once you're in the game though, you'll have to be quick on you're feet to win because the stakes are high and the game is fast and if you win the prize is great but if you lose then its back to the line.

8. Get a job. Getting a job on habbo can be a rewarding experience. Just be careful because there are scammers out there trying to get you're furni. If you do happen to find a good job then you can make some good friends while getting some furni in the process.

7. Go to a party. On habbo there is never a lack of a party. There is constantly a crowded room on the party and chill floor no matter what time I'm on, whither it be 3:00 PM or 3:00 AM. There are plenty of people in a party that make great friends.

6. Open up a game room. Wither it be a falling furni game or a maze there is a wide range of game rooms you can make, you can even come up with your own, new game. Once you have the room open you'll enjoy operating it, trust me.

5. Trade. There are endless amounts of trade rooms on habbo and in those trade rooms you can get plenty of furni and rares that you can, in turn, trade again to make a profit. You can open up a trade room and make some profit there too.

4. Battle Ball. There is always a good game of battle ball to be had. You can go to the battle ball lounge and pick up a voucher to start off. If you're just beginning, don't be overconfident, start out in the beginner's lounge.

3. Do a maze. There are so many mazes on habbo that I have tried, and almost every single one of them were fun. When you just start out doing mazes you'll discover how much fun they really are. If you're just a beginner I would recommend to do a maze that is empty or not very crowded because if you hold up the line people get very pushy. Also, I would recommend Tamay's maze, it was the first maze I tried and the first one I beat.

2. Wobble Squabble with staff. Every Friday you have a chance to compete with the habbo staff in wobble squabble. It is fun seeing how well you compare to the people who keep habbo up and running. It is one of the few chances available for habbos to compete with the people that without habbo wouldn't be operating.

1. Habbo Competitions. Things like Habbo Soccer and Habbowood give habbos the chance to win great prizes while having fun. Many people compete and while playing you can make great friends. Even habbo staff participate in the competitions in which staff and habbos can become good friends.
