Habbo Wrestling: [7/21/06]

eric1991

The True Story

Hey people have you heard about habbo wrestling? If you havent you should it's a fun game thats been around for a while and if you're a habbo wrestling fan YOU WILL ENJOY IT I BET YOU! Now you only need three things to wrestle on habbo.

  1. Learn how to fight.
  2. Yourself.
  3. Find a wrestling fed use the lower class feds there are ones for really good people and there are some for newer people.

Now people you got to learn to rp. Rp means role play, and most wrestling rooms use it now. Some examples are "Kick to mid", "grabs head", "tucks ddt", "left hook", "right hook", "trips and climbs tb". You will learn as you go trust me. I really stunk at fighting when I first started but there are alot of realy good people out there like Cod2,::melo:: and others. There are groups of people that are called hall of fame which habbo wrestlers vote you into and it means you're really known and there is another class called Legends that means you been fighting for a really good amount of time. Now you're asking, "how the heck do you make a wrestling room?". Well I'll give you the steps right here.

1.) You need a ring. All you need is about 4 by 4 ring or 5 by 5, any works. It cant be too small or big.
2.) You need a ramp and the ramp you just got to have black mats and some bars to make a straight line with it.
3.) You must have an area were your fans and wrestlers sit use just normal hcs or mode sofas will work. you MUST! have stickys it's a MUST! Now you use the stickys for your ppvs, wrestlers, titles, and stables.
4.) And at last you need a habbo wrestling name. Don't steal some ones, use something good that you never seen.

NOW! At last people you have your federation. Now open it and invite your friends to learn how to wrestle and remember to have fun. Now I'll tell you there is a lot of good feds after you learn how to fight like cod2,eric1991. I can't remember the others sorry guys lol. Now you know what does that sound boring well it does. But think of the matchs hardcore,ladder, TLC matchs, AND MORE! They're really fun. Sometimes if you get good you get a match for a title! So good luck and fight well maybe one day you will end up in the ring with me or melo or alim you never know people thanks.
