Top 5 Party and Chill Rooms [5/18/06]

Mishveh

Mishveh lets us know where the parties happen...

Have you ever wanted to just go chill in a great room with a great group of Habbos, but you were overcome by the vast number of choices that never seem to quite satisfy your urge. Well, I, Mishveh, have solved your problem. I went through Habbo's large party/chill room scene and chose the top five rooms.

5) Club Sassy
Owner: LlamaPhreak
This is an excellent room with a great atmosphere and live dancers. It's a great place to come and meet Habbos and relieve the stresses of the everyday Habbo, but don't forget to leave the kids at home for this one!

4) The Suite of Darkness
Owner: -RazorFangs-
For all those who want to be accepted and appreciate that dark gothic design, this is the place for you! -RazorFangs- has created a great smaller room where Habbos can let loose and have fun.

3) Allegra's NY Style Pizzeria
Owner: Shobbz
If you like pink and have a craving for some delicious pizza and a great time, then head on over to this amazing chill zone. For those who may not be party animals, but still want to have a good time, Shobbz has created your heaven.

2) Little Italy Cafe
Owner: :-TheGodfather-:
This is the perfect room for those seeking good food and the perfect place to chill and meet other Habbos. Upon entering this room, one gets a relaxed feeling and immediately feels welcome.

...And the number one party/chill room is...

1) NYC All Nite Bar and Club
Owner: -nYc-
Here's the best example of a great party room I have ever seen. This partier's delight contains a bar, a large number of seats, a great atmosphere, and a dance floor with a live DJ. What else could a Habbo looking for good time ask for?

Well, there are Habbo's Top 5 Greatest Party/Chill rooms (editors note: according to Mishveh, not Habbo Staff!). So why are you still here? Get out there and have some fun, and if you find other great rooms, don't keep them a secret!
