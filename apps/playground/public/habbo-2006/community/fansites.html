The Official Fansites of Habbo USA!

Welcome to our Official Habbo Fansites list!

Listed in no particular order, these Fansites are the source to go for the dedicated Habbo. Or even the new Habbo player. You can find out anything you ever wanted to know about anything Habbo related from these sites. Ranging from music to forums to everything in between! Check them out!


Habbo Discussion
Habbo Discussion is one of the most active forums in the habbo hotel community. It continues to expand with a profile of over 2,000 activated users, 12,000 topics, and 150,000 posts. Daily activity consists of nearly 40 visitors on at any time during the day.
Blue Bobble
Blue Bobble, which opened in March 2005, is a Habbo radio fansite that's dedicated to playing the latest tunes and hiring only the best of the best DJs. Our site used to be an official fansite for the USA hotel until the list was changed. Blue Bobble should remain an official site because we're very unique and dedicated to giving visitors some excitement!
Your Habbo
YourHabbo has been a UK official fansite for over a year now and is a very popular site, containing Habbo and real life news, a user system, guides, arcade games, great competitions to win top prizes, a forum, regular Habbo events and so much more!
Habbo Boards
Habboboards is a fully equipped, fully modded Habbo website that provides Habbos from all over the world with great content including news, goodies, hints, tricks and more fun stuff. We have a forum and a full blown user system (which is on BETA) that allows Habbos to create accounts on the site, get an avatar, get stickers and post comments, shouts and content on the forum. With its user system, and full list of features, HabboBoards.org is very unique and would welcome itself to the fansites list and holding the badge without a problem!
Habbo Fan World
Welcome to Habbo Fan World, another Habbo Hotel fansite. Our main focus is everything Habbo Hotel related. We are slowing growing into a big website. We have Flash animations, news, upcoming events, fun stuff, cool pictures, and much much more. A lot of the things on our website were ideas from visitors and friends. We even have a forum and radio!
Habbolot.us
Habbolot.us has been open now for about 6 months in this time we have had many problems but always got over them and now have some good staff and a lovely radio and news department keeping habbos updated and amused as long as we can!
Habtips
Habtips is an international Habbo Hotel fansite and we aim to bring all the best News reports from around the hotel aswell as around the world in our news section. Habtips Radio Provides great Music & Entertainment all week and is sutable for the whole family because of our no swearing/racism policy.
Rare Habbos
It contains up to date news and rare trade values on all English speaking hotels, Has 2 habbo eXperts working hard answering questions about Habbo! It has a great radio with Djs from all over the world! This is truly a fantastic and dedicated international site! It also has fun competitions on the radio and also around the hotel! This site is for Rare Habbos!
Habbos.net
Habbos.net is an international Habbo-haven; a place for Habbos from around the globe to discuss both Habbo and the outside world in a safe and friendly environment away from the hotel itself! Dedicated to promoting worthwhile discussion and creating the best competitions for all of you, Habbos.net is a forum you'll return to time and time again whether you want to chat about your favourite Habbo staff member or just blow off some steam and let your opinions be heard.
Hobtech
Hobtech was formed to help Habbo's with all things techy. It is the the site of the UK Tech Team run by Ex-UK Hobba Floyd. We advise on technical issues relating to Habbo Hotel and on internet security and safety, for example: keeping your anti-virus software up to date, installing a firewall, safe practices using instant messaging and password safety. Hobtech is used by Habbos from all English-speaking Hotels.
My Rares
MyRares is a unique site as there is not many like it, We make the site as user friendly as possible. We supply the most honest values with a staff of great players. We always try to be one step a head of the competition. That means we work 24/7 making MyRares better everyday. MuRares is a highly trusted HabboHotel fansite.
Club Habbo
ClubHabbo is a Fun filled fansite, owned by SkaterChu and SteveBrown03, focussing on Habbo USA, UK and CA. We offer news, games, guides, a forum and we also have a very popular radio station for you to tune into while playing habbo. ClubHabbo holds regular events and competitions so everyone has a chance to win, what are you waiting for, join the club!
Pure Habbo
www.Pure-Habbo.com is truly unique. We provide Habbo's with the latest Habbo news, and serve our guests the finest Habbo help that we can. We have areas of community relations, news, goodies, forums, and much more. We refer people to our website when they have common problems or need questions answered. We always have very nice layouts and our services are run very professionally.
Habnoxious
One of the latest and greatest Habbo Fansites, Habnoxious has just joined the fray but don't mistake that for poor quality! It's a great fansite so check it out!
Smile You're a Habbo!
Our DJs work hard to give the listeners what they want! We try to keep our radio as clean as can be and our site as safe as can be (the habbo way). The staff over at Smile You're A Habbo are working on getting rare values. At the moment we have a competition going on, It is called Smile Idol!
Habbo Valley
Habbo Valley includes news, games, articles etc. But is based on its fantastic guides and safety information. The staff provides great services, and make sure that the fansite is always updated!
Elite Habboz
We have a radio and post news for Habbo UK,CA,AU and now US and we have a forum that I hope users will be very active with. To be honest with you, We are still hiring staff but we have enough to post all the news what we really need is some more Djs!
HabbCrazy
Habbcrazy is a very unique Habbo Hotel Fansite that offers a diverse views and opinions from other Fansites. Habbcrazy keeps the people that come visit up to date with the current news around Habbo Hotel. We have competitions for members of Habbo Hotel to win furniture. We have a diverse staff that is here to give different point of view of Habbo Hotel.
