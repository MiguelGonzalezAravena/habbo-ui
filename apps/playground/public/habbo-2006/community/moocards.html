Make cool contact cards to share

What are Habbo MiniCards?

Habbo MiniCards are little contact cards with your Habbo on one side, and your details on the other.

Select different backgrounds, position your Habbo, and enter your own text in Habbo speech bubbles, to make a unique set of wonderful cards.

On the back you can put your Habbo name or Habbo Home URL, use them as bookmarks or party invitations – in fact, the options are endless!

The cards come in a cute little box, delivered straight to your door. The only question is, will you share them with your friends, or keep them all to yourself? Some people find them very hard to give away!

How do I make my cards?

It's very easy to make Habbo MiniCards just follow these steps:

  • Go to www.moo.com/habbo and tell us the name of your Habbo and the Hotel you live in
  • Design your cards by choosing a colourful background and your Habbo's mood, pose or speech bubble
  • Personalise the backs of the cards with text, coloured backgrounds and special Habbo icons
  • Finally, enter your address and payment details. That's it! Simple!
  • If you don't have a Habbo, we have plenty to share, head over to: www.moo.com/habbo/choose.php and you can use one of ours.

Who are Moo?

MOO is a different kind of printing company – a company that only prints fun stuff!

They team up with special friends like Habbo to help you share your online life with your friends in the real world. Based in London, they print and ship the amazing things people have made all over the planet. So far they've sent MiniCards to over 120 countries!
