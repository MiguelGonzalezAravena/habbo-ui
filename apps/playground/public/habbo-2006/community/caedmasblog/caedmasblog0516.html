Caedma's Blog

Hey Habbos!

How’s it going? Man, the past few weeks here have been busy! With the end of Habbowood, then we got the new site, and on top of ALL of that, I was thrown on to the radio!!!

So, first things first; my thoughts on Habbowood. Wasn’t it amazing this year!?!?!?! I saw so many great movies, it was hard to choose the best! So, here’s a few that I remember:

Habbo Musical – This movie is based on the musical “Rent”, which, as some of you know, is like, the best play/movie ever, and defiantly one of my all time favorites.

Milk and Cream to the rescue – It was classic… Watch them battle Dr. Lactose Intolerant….

And… *drum roll plz*

This one was made one of the very last days, so I never got a chance to vote for it, but, it was created by my new friend Andyism, and you just have to watch it. It’s awesome…. The name of the Habbowood movie was Sulake Agents by Andyism.

I loved Habbowood… I watched so many movies, I was singing the HabboVision Song in my sleep! My cats thought I was loosing my mind! Not to mention, every time I was in a guest room, I kept looking behind me for a Habbo lying on the floor with a carrot in his chest! AHH!

So anyways, Kedo, Iomegadrive, and Luna have been working night and day to get the new site to you. I think it looks awesome! I’ve gotten so many console messages from you guys saying that you like it too! Since I make the coffee, I’ve been here at the offices day and night, keeping the coffee hot so they could stay awake. I’ve also restricted Iomegadrive from having any caffeine. Him and his fro were bouncing off the walls – and you should have seen what fell out of it! **shudders**

I want to take a minute to thank Kedo, Iomegadrive, and Luna for all their hard work on the new site. I think it’s awesome! If you see them in the hotel, you should give them a “high-five” – They did a GREAT job.

In between brewing the coffee and serving it, I also answered the phones. Kedo’s mom called saying he was out past his curfew. LOL. Can you believe he has to be home by 9pm??? Anyways, Luna is trying to change that – She’s having a Mother’s Day Room Competition. I have a feeling this is going to be awesome! I have faith in all of my Habbo friends to make the BEST rooms for their moms. If you guys haven’t seen it yet, Luna posted all the details here: Mother's Day Room Competition!

So, last but defiantly not least – Have you guys all checked out the radio station?!?! JackietheDJ is awesome! He has a face for radio too – hehehe – JUST KIDDING!

If you haven’t checked it out yet, you can find it here: http://www.live365.com/stations/sulake1?site=pro

If you’re lucky, you may catch some of us on the air! Okay, okay, if you can tell time, you’ll be able to catch some of us on the air! Let me tell you this – You never know what is going to happen!

Yesterday, Jyve tied up JackietheDJ, and tossed him in the basement. He turned BobbaMusic into Habbo Hood. I heard the commotion, and found Jyve groovin’ with the Habbos in JackietheDJ’s VIP Room. So, not knowing what else to do, I pick up a microphone, and help him out a bit. I know that some of you may find this hard to believe, but I got shy! I couldn’t help it! There were so many Habbos listening, and all talking to me in the room – It was a little intimidating! Thank goodness that I had so many Habbos send me messages, letting me know I was doing a great job! Thanks guys! I appreciate it!

Anyways, so much to do today, and Iomegadrive is giving me the “hurry and post your blog” look, so I think it’s time for me to say farewell. I hope you all have a GREAT week, and I can’t wait to see all these Mother’s Day rooms!!

HH USA 4 EVER!!!!!

**Habbo Hugs**

Caedma!
