Caedma's Blog

How has your week been? I hope it was most excellent! I know that it’s been an exciting week in Habbo Hotel, SO much has been going on – it’s been hard to keep up! I tried my hardest to keep up with all the hotels events, so hard that I began to neglect the most important job I do here (which is making the coffee). Luckily, Jyve holds the same passion for the Java as I do – and he’s been picking up the slack for me.

On to the important stuff! I was lucky enough to get a look at some of the Mother’s Day rooms that you all made. They were awesome! Kedo, Iomegadrive, and Luna’s moms LOVED them. In fact, I think Kedo’s mom is loosening up on his curfew! We can only hope. Luna is posting the winners to the competition today – I can’t wait to see who wins! You all did a great job!

I’m starting to think that Luna loves these room competitions – either that, or she just loves American Idol. It’s probably a little of both! So, she’s put the Iced furni into the catalog, and is holding a room competition in honor of her favorite show! There’s only 2 people left, so make sure you tune in on Tuesday. Don’t worry if you miss it, Luna will make sure you keep you filled in with her “Eye on Idol” article - http://www.habbo.com/community/eyeonidol

Have you guys heard JackietheDJ on Bobba Music? He’s GREAT isn’t he?!?!? He’s also let us staff members have our “5 minutes of fame” (or an hour a day in this case) and he’s given us each our own radio show! I had my first show on Monday, It was a lot of fun! I decided to take the party to my “New and Improved Dragon Room” – since it’s my only big room – and we all hung out, listened to some music, and gave some “shout outs”. I didn’t know what kind of music all the Habbos like to listen to, so I decided that I would play songs from my ipod. I like a lot of different types of music, so you never know what I’ll play! I’m a big fan of rock music from all different time periods – so don’t be surprised if you hear some Pink Floyd, David Bowie, or Stone Temple Pilots mixed in with some newer stuff! Make sure you check out all the other staff shows too!

1-2pm PST – Music (and sometimes me or ScribeX)
2-3pm PST – Luna’s “un-named” Pop show
3-4pm PST – Jyve’s Habbo Hood
4-6pm PST – Hangin’ with Jackie the DJ

Okay Habbos – I must keep my blog short this week, There’s still a lot of work to be done! I’m preparing for summer – Should I change the color of my bathing suit? I just think the black matches my hair SO well! Only 5 more weeks until the official start of summer! I don’t think anyone in the office is more excited than I am!

Have a GREAT week Habbos – I’ll see ya in the hotel!

**Habbo Hugs**
