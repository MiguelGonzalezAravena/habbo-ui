Caedma's Blog

Hey Habbos!

How’s your week been going? It’s been great here, as usual… just getting the final preparations for summer all put into place. Is everyone excited? I know I am. I was thinking about changing my hair from blue to green, or maybe orange? The few Habbos I’ve asked so far have said to keep it blue. So I guess blue it is – Just hope it doesn’t fade in the pool. =)

A few Habbos have asked me what my favorite summer activities are… So, I made a short list…

  1. Sleeping in
  2. Swimming
  3. Sunshine!!!
  4. The Beach
  5. No School!!!

I’m really excited. I’ve heard that Kedo, Iomegadrive, and Luna have some great events planned – but they’re keeping it all on the low down… I’m excited to see what they have in store for us! In the mean time, Luna has a Design and Destroy room competition going on – to commemorate everyone’s favorite day of the year – the last day of school!

Also, this weekend, Jeffoo and her posse of Habbo X-perts have put together an event to get this summer started off right. Yep, you guessed it – Carnival X is back! Jeffoo has asked me on behalf of herself and the Habbo X-perts, to invite each and every Habbo to stop by and say Hello! There are TONS of events, and if you’re new to the hotel, you’re in luck since all the games are at NO CHARGE!!!!! You can learn to play some of the HOTTEST games in Habbo in a fun and safe environment! Who knows, you may get to meet the Queen Scammer Slammer or catch a glimpse of some other staff members that may happen to stop by! I dunno when I’ll stop by, but I am making sure not to miss it!

This week, I had decided that it was time to have another party. So I did! Wednesday, I popped into the hotel and got a few people together for “School’s out” Party. We hung out in someone’s “School’s Out” room – but I forgot whose room it was! I’m so excited about summer that my note taking skills have suffered! So, here’s the shout outs to my new Habbo friends, and also the “readers digest version” of all that happened…. Cress had a proposal for me - which was a little scary – but he just wanted to invite to the opening of his theme park (more on this later). Amanda gave me a new nickname of “c-ma” and is excited because there’s only 2 more weeks of school left. Lowell had a few more days of exams – so we all wished him luck! Blogging, Feble, 13iggie, and Banddiva all have big Habbo plans for the summer that you should all ask them about… and then, JudgeJudi (complete with her shiny Habbo X badge) came in to say Hello! We all got together for a screenshot, and then headed over to Rooftop Rumble for some Wobble Squabble.

Now, It’s rumored around the hotel that Kedo and Iomegadrive are the reigning Kings of the Wobble, however… I think after my turn on the Wobble on Wednesday, I may just be able to nab the crown. I had a pretty good run of 5-6 wins… I think I shocked everyone – Including myself! I can’t wait until TGIF so I can go up against Kedo and Iomega and claim my new title.

Thursday, I went to Cress and Humid’s theme park opening. To my surprise, I was not the only staff there! Hiro-P, Jeffoo, Kedo, and ScribeX had all popped in to say hello and hang out for a while. If you get a minute, you should stop by Habbo Towers, and take the tele to the Hallway. There are SO many rooms! My favorite was the pyramid of Petals. So awesome!!!

Anyways, I must be going – I have tons of things to do before TGIF today, and little time to do it. I hope you all have a great week, and remember only 12 days until the official beginning of summer! Stay safe and obey the Habbo Way!!!!

**Habbo Hugs**

Caedma
