Get Noticed!

Each week we receive hundreds of articles... so here's how to stand out from the crowd:

Write Interesting Articles - If your article's similar to another we've published, it's unlikely we'll include it. Think of something new and fresh.

Never Plagiarise - We won't publish articles that have been stolen. Taking someone else's work is stealing and against the Habbo Way.

Follow The Habbo Way - Articles that don't follow the Habbo Way, will never be published.

Spelling and Grammar - It's easy to spell check your articles before you submit them. You should also, observe proper British English grammatical rules, such as "realise" instead of "realize", and use proper English such as "your" and not "ur".
