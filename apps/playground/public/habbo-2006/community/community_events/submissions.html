Habbo Classifieds!

This is a NEW feature to the newsletter! Have you ever hosted a party, quest or event in Habbo and no one showed? Well this is how you get some BUZZ for it! Habbo Classifieds is a place where you can ADVERTISE your room, party, quest, event, giveaway or ANYTHING, and make sure thousands of Habbos know about it! So if you’re hosting some AWESOME party and want to make sure Habbos KNOW about it, here’s your chance! Let us know and we’ll put it in the newsletter!

Your Habbo Classified needs to have the following:
A title for the event
A time for the event
A short blurb (15-20 words) describing your event!

Remember! Since you're submitting for NEXT week's Habbo Classified, you have to plan at LEAST a week in advance! So we're looking for events that happen NEXT WEEKEND and on! That way, when we publish the classifieds, your event isn't already over!
