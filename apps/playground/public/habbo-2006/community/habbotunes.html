Artist: Audioslave
Album: Revelations
Audioslave is a little more funk and little less rock these days. On the band's latest release Revelations, there does seem to be a turn more towards a rhythm and blues genre, which is an interesting move given the musicians' collective histories in harder-edged bands like Rage Against The Machine and Soundgarden. Of course, RATM had plenty of funkified moments, but it always maintained a very power-driven base. Revelations is not necessarily a disappointment, but an intensity is missing that fueled Audioslave's earlier hits like 'Cochise.' The newer sound should not scare off steadfast Audioslave fans, and when the CD is at it's funkiest, it actually hits it's peak. But while the musical talents of vocalist Chris Cornell, guitarist Tom Morello, bassist Tim Commerford, and drummer Brad Wilk are very much present on the latest CD, the songwriting on many of the tracks still lack the same diversity of their collective former bands. The best song on Revelations is 'One And The Same,' a funky track that is highlighted by Morello's wah pedal and recalls some of the most memorable riffs from back in the RATM days. What cannot be denied is the mesmerizing ability of Morello -- as usual. While Revelations does have it's imperfections, it still is an amazing listen for Cornell's stronger-than-ever vocals and those instances of genius that Morello makes look so easy.

Artist:Flyleaf
Album:Flyleaf
This is an awesome band, and in fact, my current musical obsession. The metal head Habbos out there will definitely appreciate Flyleaf's style, although the female vocalist brings a certain quality that might remind you of Evanescence. Lacey Mosley is edgy without being overly scream-o, and is able to be soft and tender without falling into the category of pop. Truly, the powerful and incredibly flexible vocals are what make this band stand out from the rest of the hard rock/metal scene. Flyleaf is a great addition to any music fan's library. But caution: I guarantee you'll be humming 'Fully Alive' out loud by the end of the day!

Artist:Dave Mathew's Band
Album:Crash
Dave Matthews Band is a mixture of acoustic, rock, bluegrass, classical and jazz music. The songs that I recommend are 'Crash Into Me,' 'Two Step,' and 'American Baby.' The band mixes up their sound by using instruments such as the saxophone, violin, keyboard and, of course, the bass and acoustic guitar. Dave Matthews, the lead singer, uses the guitar much like a person would play a percussion instrument which makes the sound unique. The band members come from all over, making the band a giant melting pot, full of diverse and unique sound which is what caught my interest in the first place. They started in 1991 and have become world wide. The band is down to earth and has a calming sound. I enjoy their music just like many others! Go ahead take a listen!

Artist:Gnarls Barkley
Album:St. Elsewhere
CRAZY! That's the simple way to describe this Album fully in motion- Or it's just the name of one of these chaotic songs.
But either way, this album is the product of good beat, style and pretty much excellent work of pro artists. The interesting and quality part about St. Elsewhere is that it contains songs both dancing, wild and hip but strange, sci-fi-ish and alien-like.
Overall, this album is the perfect pick of the week for moody people, or just for all of you Habbo who want a bit of Gnarls Barkley Spice in their soup.
