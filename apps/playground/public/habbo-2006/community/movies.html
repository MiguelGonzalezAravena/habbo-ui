Halloween [2007]

Reviewed by YoYojam, The Critic

The remake of John Carpenter's 1978 Horror movie Halloween. I believe Rob Zombie had courage to try an remake this classic. When I watched the movie the storyline seemed a little different but not to much. I really like how he went into detail about Myers childhood life an how abuse it was, But I did think they spent to much time on the build up of Mike Myers crazy Psycho rampage. As you watch the film you see that the murder scenes are more Graphic than in the original. Myer's is apparently locked up in a Looney House but then escaped to head back home for his sister Laurie. Which leads to the showdown between Myer's an Laurie which I thought was a let down even thought most people would think it was exciting an thrilling. She spends most of the time screaming rather than at least trying to fight back, I understand he is a killer an your scared but this is a matter of life an death. Rob Zombie is no doubt a skilled director, But while his Halloween is a technically well-crafted film, it lacks any of the subtlety and spookiness of Carpenter's original film.

I rate Halloween 3 out of 5:
