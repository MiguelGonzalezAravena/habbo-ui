Habbo Tunes

Artist: Bjork
Album: Bjork: Greatest Hits

I was first introduced to Bjork when listening to the radio, they were playing “I Miss You” and I fell in love with it. I decided to buy her greatest hits album, and ever since Day 1, I instantly fell in love with many of the songs on this album. I love how each line in the lyrics don’t really connect, but they still sound like they should go together. Not only do I love the lyrics, but I really love the music itself. You know, behind the lyrics. Especially in “Pagan Poetry” and “Play Dead”. Bjork has a gorgeous voice. A very good example of this is in the songs like “Venus is a Boy” and “Hidden Place”. Her music is Alternative. I really don’t know who to recommend it to, because she’s very different to the music I normally listen to (The Cure, The Smiths, Morrissey, Elvis, etc.) but I fell in love with it! So give her music a try, you might like her just as I do.

Artist: Static-X
Album: Start A War

Static-X is a band that any metal head or rocker would love to listen to 24/7! I learned about this band on Yahoo music from their 1999 song, "Push It". Static-X is above one of the greatest bands I’ve ever heard, and I have heard a lot of bands. A great thing about how Static-X plays their music is the way the guitarist plays and how the lead singer fits the words in with it, it’s like they are connected. Good songs from the newest album they’ve made are: "Start A War", "DirtHouse", "I’m The One" and "The Enemy". Those songs will have you addicted to Static-X and have you listening to them for hours! Static-X is truly, one of the best bands in my opinion. So if any of you Habbos are into Heavy Metal/Techno, then Static-X is the band for you!

Artist: Dave Brubeck
Album: The Essential Dave Brubeck

Now I know that Jazz music might not be as big as it used to be, but a lot of modern music owes some influence to its smooth rhythms and versatility. For those of you who enjoy Jazz music, I highly suggest this album as it is a compilation of the works of Dave Brubeck, one of the greatest influences on modern Jazz music, consisting of songs picked by Brubeck himself, including the popular “Take Five”. If you haven’t gotten into Jazz, but would like to listen to some to get an idea about what music was like before Punk Rock and Boy Bands, I would highly suggest listening to this to get a good idea of what it’s all about.

Artist: Norma Jean
Album: O’ God the Aftermath

Norma Jean is a metal core rock band, but don’t let that steer you away from them. Their music is very fast paced, and very good to listen to if someone happens to be annoying you. Just switch ‘em on and forget about your problems. I found out about them from my friend Jordan and from Myspace.com, and thus far I’ve really enjoyed them. If you check ‘em out, I would recommend two songs to listen to: "Vertebraille" and "Baynetwork". So if you have any spare time or are looking for some good rock, I would listen to them.

Artist: The Costellos
Album: Outside of Paris

I heard about this band from my dad. So of course, my first thought, “Oh geez, another lame band”. But the truth is, they’re not. The Costellos are purely original in their lyrics and music. They’re rock n’ roll with a twist that makes them perfect. Lynn Costello, the lead, has the most amazing voice I’ve ever heard. If you want their CD, Amazon them. I would suggest "Outside of Paris" to anyone who has ever enjoyed rock n’ roll, no matter what kind.

Artist: Waking Ashland
Album: Composure

They are a good, soft rock band. They are most famous for their song, “I Am For You”. It’s like one of those bands that you can fall asleep to. Their CD Composure is well-made. They make their songs sound unique, like no other band could sing them as good. Listen to them, maybe you will like them. They are like a pop/rock/electronic band. I enjoy them, you might too.
