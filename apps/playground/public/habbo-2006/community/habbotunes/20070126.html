Habbo Tunes

Artist: Bowling for Soup
Album: The Great Burrito Extortion Case
Bowling for Soup has a quality that a lot of bands don’t have: humor. With songs like “High School Never Ends,” BFS is back and ready to rock. They provide catchy melodies and awesome guitar riffs to grab your interest and comedic lyrics to keep it. Remember great songs such as “1985” and “Ohio Come Back to Texas,” Bowling for Soup Has really pumped it up a notch this year. Sarcasm to the extreme!


Artist: Gym Class Heroes
Album: The Papercut Chronicles
Gym Class Heroes is a group of 4 guys. Their music usually tells a story and has a really catchy sound! Most of the songs are usually talking about girlfriends. This Album has 17 songs on it. My favorite is “Cupid’s Chokehold”. It was released in the year 2005. Gym Class Heroes is rock, modern rock, and indie rock. By far, the best band ever.


Artist: Nightcore
Album: Sensaciòn
Nightcore is a techno band that is made up of two boys from Alta, Norway, named Thomas S. Nilsen and Steffen Ojala Søderholm. They mix techno/dance music into faster and happier sounds. This upbeat band has great songs like, “Will My Heart Survive,” “Look at Me Now,” and “Don’t Say Goodbye.” All these songs are included in their “Sensacion” album. Sensacion means sensation in Spanish. It’s the perfect album name for this band, because this band is in fact a sensation. If you’re looking for happy, fast, upbeat, GOOD techno then Nightcore is the band for you (plus doesn’t the band name sound awesome enough on it’s own?).

Artist: Gwen Stefani
Album: The Sweet Escape
I am currently listening to “The Sweet Escape.” Its Gwen Stefani’s second solo album. Her music is so original and so crazy delicious! The songs on this album don’t all fall in the same genre. Like “U Started It” has a slow graceful kinda feel to it. “Now That You Got It” is fast paced, and it seems like a song they’d be playing in a downtown club. “Orange County Girl” has an urban hip hop beat to it. Gwen’s songs are based on different things. Like “Wind It Up” is a modern, trippy version of the movie “Sound of Music” which is Gwen’s favorite movie ever! “Don’t Get It Twisted” is about her thinking she is sick but finds out that she is pregnant with baby boy Kingston! I love this album because she collaborated with top chart performers. Akon has small parts in the song “Sweet Escape,” while Pharrell sings in “Yummy.” Her music is so great it puts me into a dancing mood. Her and her Harajuku girls have wicked style and I think this album might receive lots of awards.

Artist: Foo Fighters
Album: In Your Honor
Well, what can I say is that this is the greatest album I ever listened to. The Foo Fighters includes members Dave Grohl, Nate Mendal, Taylor Hawkins, and last but not least Chris Shiflett. “Mine is yours and yours is mine / There is no divide / In your honor I would die tonight.” Foo Fighters fifth and definitive album opens with a statement of purpose universal in its passion. This album is the greatest, “In Your Honor” would be their chance to commit that statement to music: The days of near-disintegration with every record had long since come to an end. Lots of songs including “The Last Song,” “Best Of You,” and also one of my favorites, “Over And Out.” The Foo Fighters are a great band and they still have a long journey to go. In probably about 10-11 years, when some kid asks his dad, ‘You ever hear of Foo Fighters? Which record should I get?’ They should say In Your Honor. Like if you wanna hear some Led Zeppelin? Get Physical Graffiti. That’s exactly what I want to happen with this record. I want Habbos and real life families to be like “Wow, that’s the album they’ll be remembered for.” This album and these songs will change your mind forever.
