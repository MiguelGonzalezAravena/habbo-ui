Habbo Tunes

Artist: +44
Album: When Your Heart Stops Beating
This is the newly-formed group that came out of blink-182’s breakup. If you loved blink-182, you’ll definitely enjoy +44. It’s a much darker side of Travis and Mark’s music style, and some of the songs are actually about the breakup. These songs are for anyone who is in the mood for high octane punk rock! If you were disappointed with Angels and Airwaves, [which I was] go grab +44’s album! It has some really good songs on it, including “Cliffdiving” and “When Your Heart Stops Beating.” I hope you like their music!


Artist: Muse
Album: Absolution
Muse is an amazing band. There is no other word to describe them. This album and the previous album ‘Black Holes and Revelations’ surpassed all my expectations. From this particular album however, it has my favorite song of all time on it - the amazing ‘Sing for Absolution.’ This is grouped under the rock genre; however is not hard, classical rock, or heavy metal. It’s a great combination throughout the whole album. After all, I love all songs by Muse, but this album just stunned. Even though it is relatively old, it is a real must have. Go out and buy it, today!


Artist: The Police
Album: Every Breath You Take: The Classics
In celebration of their reuniting tour, I’ve been listening to The Police. The Police originated in the early 80s and have written hit songs such as “Roxanne,” “Message in a Bottle,” and “Don’t Stand So Close to Me” which are all included on this album. This album gives you a great range of their greatest songs. The Police are a mix of ska and reggae and bring a unique and relaxing sound. With only three band members, they pack one of the best punches, and this album sure is packed. A couple lines of lyrics from “Message in a Bottle” that are simple but brilliant are “Love can mend your life but love can mend your heart.” Watching them perform at the Grammies only made me love them more and it showed their talent has stayed with them for the past 25 or so years. If you miss this album, you’re missing out on a whole sect of music history.

Artist: Hilary Duff
Album: Hilary Duff Most Wanted
Even though this album “Hilary Duff Most Wanted” isn’t a new album, I’m still very into it. It has some of her new songs like "Wake Up," "Beat of My Heart," "Mr.James Dean" and "Supergirl" (like my name? Ha-ha). This album I think shows more of herself in it and it tells more about her life. Like the song “Metamorphosis” is telling us that she is growing and learning how to change and start to think like a BIG KID (hehe) I hope this paragraph helped you learn about this album and even though A LOT of people don’t like her.. So what? Deal with it – H. Duff is here to stay.
