Artist: Cheyenne Kimbell
Album: The Day Has Come

This artist, most of you may know from her new reality show on MTV, called what else, Cheyenne. I first heard of her on that. Her music is great! She has brought back new flair to the pop genre. Her music is sort of a mix between Hilary Duff’s first and second album. This artist has added her own flair and pizzazz to this genre of music! Buy her CD before December 31, and get a free membership to her fan club!!

Artist: Johnny Cash
Album: Unchained (American Recordings 1996)

This Week I’m listening to Johnny Cash’s 1996 American recording - Unchained. Okay well I listen to a new Johnny Cash CD every week. First of all Johnny Cash has been making music for more then 50 years. He died in 2003. His music is really sentimental. He has the power to make you feel what he is feeling in his songs. Not many artists know how to do that. This kind of music can be really emotion filled. I really laughed a lot during the first 10 minutes that I listened to it. Then cried. Johnny Cash is a great artist I would really recommend his music. This is one of my favorite CDs! Johnny Cash may be dead but his talent and music will live on for ever!

Artist: Nelly Furtado
Album: Loose

This new Nelly Furtado CD is a very good one in my opinion. The all famous song “Promiscuous” is the only famous song on the album. “Promiscuous” is a cool and fast song that you will know all the words to and it is a good song for Habbo DJs to play. The song “Afraid” on the album gives you a techno and Spanish accent to the whole song. The song is a very good dance mix but not a good sing-a-long song. Another song on the album is “ShowTime”. “ShowTime” is a slow song with a faster paced beat. In this song you really can’t understand Ms. Furtado but you will grow to love the beat and the strange but very good mix of style and instruments in the song. I give the album a B+. It provides great range in different types and styles of music and is an album everyone should buy!

Artist: Sandi Thom
Album: Smile... It Confuses People

“I wish I was a punk-rocker with flowers in my hair, in ‘77 and ‘69, revolution was in the air...” Are these lyrics familiar? Have you heard them before somewhere, perhaps in a vague, distant dream that came to you the other night? If you have, don’t worry, you haven’t gone insane. But you HAVE fallen under the enchanting spell of the newest pop-rock singer on the block - Sandi Thom. This is the artist that brought you the ultimate acoustic song, “I Wish I Was A Punk Rocker With Flowers in my Hair”, which is so catchy that it will stay with you for at least three weeks after you listen to it. As a result, you’ll probably tear your hair out from it being jammed in your noggin for so long. Much to the world’s dismay, Ms. Thom decided to release an album, no doubt full of annoyingly upbeat songs similar to Sandi’s debut. But the world got a (pleasant) surprise. The comically named album Smile... It Confuses People has a vast range of genres all squashed into ten tracks. From that electric power ballad that makes you feels so much you get knocked down by your own wave of emotions (“Lonely Girl”) to that finger-snapping, toe-tapping country song that would be perfect for your mom’s line-dancing class (“When Horse-Power Meant What It Said”), Little Miss Sandi Thom has managed to win the hearts of millions as her album sells out across the world.

Artist: Blind Guardian
Album: Somewhere Far Beyond

Blind Guardian has always taken us all by surprise with there hard metal antics to old style medieval fantasy tales. Somewhere Far Beyond is an oldie but a goodie, including classics like the soothing “Bard’s Song” and the story of the mystic land of Tanelorn in “The Quest for Tanelorn”. This new brand of “Epic Metal” is something new and original in the blandness of everyday punk rock, rap, or whatever you’re listening too. Dawn your cloak, raise your fist, cast a spell, and bang your hand to the power of Blind Guardian.
