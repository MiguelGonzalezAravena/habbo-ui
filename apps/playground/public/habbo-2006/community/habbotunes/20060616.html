Habbo Tunes

Artist: Sydney
Album: Black Box

Sydney is another one of those Fresh-Out-Of-The-Oven bands. I discovered them on MySpace. My current favorite from them is “Mationaire”. Also another reason I like them is because of their name. Sydney for some reason appeals to me. It sounds like a girl band right? WRONG! Its an all boy band (Woot!) Expect to see them on shelves in a year or two. Ya never know, they could become a big hit!

Artist: Atreyu
Album: A Deathgrip on Yesterday

Atreyu is an Emo, hXc (Hardcore), metal band from OC who sing about bad relationships and the depression that follows a loss of true love. Every song on “A Deathgrip on Yesterday” is catchy and good enough to be thought of as a single. Some other albums they have produced are “The Curse”, “Suicide Notes and Butterfly Kisses” and “Fractures in the Façade”. If you like their type of music you will definitely love them. And if you don’t like their music try them. You might just like them anyways.

Artist: The Doors
Album: Morrison Hotel

Wow. What to say about their music other then it being amazing? You gotta give this band some credit. The drums, the guitar, the keyboards, the bass, and the singing are all done well. The low voice of Jim Morrison is the best voice I’ve ever heard! Perhaps he is the best vocalist in the world. The riffs to “Peace Frog” played on guitar sound amazing. This band has the best well-written songs in history. They deserve a lot more credit for the work they did. P.S. Rock On! Just like Jim Morrison did!

Artist: Deerhoof
Album: The Runners Four

As one of the better known bands that have emerged from the San Francisco Indie scene, Deerhoof has already found a large fan base with the release of their numerous former albums. With the release of their latest album, the art-punk quartet has reached newer audiences through numerous promotions. Though the band’s sound has progressed, the music still retains the lullaby-like quality that Satomi Matsuzaki (lead vocals) brings to all the songs. In Runners Four, new songs that are heavy influenced by the newly emerging artistic, dance-punk genre are played with familiar avant-rock style. My personal favourite track of the album is “Twin Killers”, which features one of the best guitar sequences following Satomi’s signature child-like singing.
