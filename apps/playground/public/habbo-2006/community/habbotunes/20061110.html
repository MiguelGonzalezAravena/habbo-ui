Artist: Professor Murder
Album: Professor Murder EP

Straight from the streets of New York are a band that is on the verge of going big! Professor Murder has already had a semi-national tour throughout the northern United States, and they are already talking about going on an international tour. They don’t work with any ‘major’ record labels because they are an indie rock band who doesn’t like doing contract agreements. The style of their music is a very unique sense of rock. It is definitely not emo, and definitely not heavy metal, but somewhere in between. They are what I would call, hardcore punk. The members include Andy Craven, who’s the drummer. Mike does the vocals, percussion, bass, AND keyboard. Tony does bass and vocals. And the last member is Jesse who does the keyboarding and sampling. They have already had a few celebrities come to their shows, including NATALIE PORTMAN who went to one of their local ones. You can explore the uniqueness if this band’s music on www.professormurder.com.

Artist:Toy-Box
Album:Fantastic

Toy-Box is a Danish group that was formed in the 1990’s. Their peppy, techno, sound is similar to the Scandinavian “Aqua”. This band isn’t very popular in the U.S., just do to lack of proper advertising. With songs such as Super Duper Man, Best Friends, and Tarzan and Jane, this band is a load of laughs, and good on the ears. Some of their songs such as Teddybear, are much quieter, like love songs. The band is split up now, with the lead singer Aneela trying to start up a solo career. The lyrics to all of these songs are witty, but some have a few sexual innuendos. Even though the band is split up, it doesn’t mean you can’t listen to their music, and if you ever see a CD, pick it up, or you could order it online.

Artist:Lil Wayne
Album:Lil Wayne

Lil Wayne is a new group onto the scene, relatively unknown but tipped by the critics to be a hit on a similar scale to Franz Ferdinand! Their first album contains a wide variety of musical moods and styles, from softly synthesized songs to more slammin’ punk songs! They should appeal to anyone with an open mind to music, as they have a song to fill every genre (except RAP and R& B). The UK band recently topped the NME charts with their single The Boy Who Ran Away, beating groups such as Placebo and Panic! At the Disco. This doesn’t surprise me, because with a variety of guitar sounds, brilliant drum rhythms and with main vocals that fit the mood of each song, this should be the music for Habbos EVERYWHERE.

Artist:Fergie
Album:Duchess (Parental Advisory)

This album is great, with singles like ‘London Bridge’ and ‘Fergalicious’. These singles may make this album look like it’s an all up beat, but don’t be fooled. Fergie also made some slow mellow songs like ‘Velvet’ and “Big Girls Don’t Cry”. In this album you might see a more mature Fergie. On some of her tracks like ‘Mary Jane Shoes’ Fergie uses a classic reggae beat. Some songs that I recommend as party songs are ‘Fergalicious,’ ‘London Bridge,’ and ‘Here I Come’. Overall, this album is worth the money and time. So go check it out!
