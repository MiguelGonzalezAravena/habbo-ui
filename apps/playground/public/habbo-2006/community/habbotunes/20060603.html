Habbo Tunes

Artist: Radiohead
Album: OK Computer

Quite possibly the most critically acclaimed album from Radiohead, OK Computer has been considered the album of the ’90s. Imagine walking down a dark street, late at night. You see strange-looking people, you pass by foreign strangers. But at the end of the street, you find home, an old familiar place. This is OK Computer in a nutshell. The album starts out with a traditional type of Radiohead song, an alt-rock indie sort of song. But as the album continues, the listener witnesses the “Let Down” of society, slaves of technology. “Karma Police” the most powerful song in the album, is the climax of the album, and at the end of the album, the listener feels un-easy, almost uncomfortable, especially after the jazzy “The Tourist” a very mellow song that makes one feel very depressed. The album itself is one huge roller-coaster, with plenty of ups and downs. This is definitely an album to look into if you are interested in getting into Radiohead.

Artist: Queen
Album: Greatest Hits 1 & 2

Wow, Queen has been one of the longest living legacies in music history. I think that sums it up quite nicely in one sentence. But I definitely have more to say about ‘em ;). The first song that I heard by them was “Bohimian Rhapsody”, and man, I couldn’t get ENOUGH of it! I went online and bought every song by them that I could find! Now my MP3 player is FILLED TO THE BRIM WITH QUEEN (well, not really, it has a lot of memory). But anyway, I KNOW there’s a lot of skeptics out there that would say “Bah! Queen is one of those hippie bands from the ’70s that wouldn’t last a minute in the modern times.” Well, to them I say, “THEY ARE LASTING IN THE MODERN TIMES!” After Freddie Mercury died, they rounded up Paul Rodgers and are on tour AS WE SPEAK! “The Show Must Go On”, “Bohemian Rhapsody”, “Don’t Stop Me Now” & “Dragon Attack”, are classics that can live on if given the chance! I’ll be willing to bet that thousands of people will love Queen, and the only thing stopping them from knowing that now, is that they haven’t had the pleasure of ever hearing them before.

Artist: Ashley Parker Angel
Album: Soundtrack To Your Life

His music is amazing. Really Amazing! The songs will hit you one way or another. The ballads (like “Where Did You Go” and “Apology”) are heartbreaking at times, but the upbeat songs (like “I’m Better” and “Let U Go”) will make you jump and dance around. Who knew that guys from boy bands could sing so awesome (not saying Justin Timberlake doesn’t sing good, but yea.) All I can say is that if you wanna listen to some pop-rock then Ashley Parker Angel is the one to listen to!

Artist: Procaos
Album: Never Wake Up

Procaos, the melodic death metal band hailing from Colima in Mexico have been around for a few years now but just last year released their debut album. With “Never Wake Up” we are served with eight tracks of a heavy caliber, each song more melodic than the last but all of reasonably high quality. Both the English and Spanish language is used in the lyrics to express feelings of anger and frustration. As for so many of us, the early “In Flames” albums have certainly had an impact on these guys. The result is that some songs sound somewhat Swedish but in combination with the Spanish language it is like nothing I’ve heard before. Besides that, there are also trash and old death metal influences to be found but don’t get fooled…no copycats at work here! The whole album sounds very raw and hard due to the dominating throbbing bass line and the not so clear-sounding final production, but never mind that. The music probably sounds better this way without all the extra polishing and artificial techniques. (Note: the recording of this album was done in only two days time). Procaos has proven that not only Scandinavian countries produce great melodic death metal acts and that Latin America indeed has lots of good and worthwhile bands out there still waiting to be discovered.
