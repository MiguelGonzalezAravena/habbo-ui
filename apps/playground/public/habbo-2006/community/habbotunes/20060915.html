Artist: Rush
Album: All the World’s a Stage

Rush is one of the best Rock Bands in the world to me. They have their famous Drummer who has a 5 minute 30 second Drum Solo on the song: “Working Man/Finding My Way”. He Knows How to a 1 handed drum roll and can play anything else. They have a Bass Player that also plays the keyboards. And they have a singer that plays the guitar too. They have a 3 man band and have been playing for over 17 years!!!!

Artist: Rise Against.
Album: The Sufferer and the Witness

You may just think that Rise Against is just another screaming punk band that’s played out of their basements. But they have serious messages. In their newest single “Ready to Fall” they take on some serious issues like animal slaughter and global warming. All the members of the band are PETA supporters and activists and they care deeply about the care of animals. What’s not to love about them?

Artist: Diddy
Album: Press Play

Diddy (aka P. Diddy) has a new album coming out soon and I’ve already heard some of the tracks off it and I’m telling you guys, his music is just pure craziness :P. He just has this certain flow to him that no one else has or can even attempt to copy and he does what he does very well. I recommend going and picking up his new album when it hits the stores October 17th. Enjoy your styles of music and don’t let people judge you cause of what you like. Well I’m out so just peace.

Artist: Patent Pending
Album: Save Each Other The Whales are Doing Fine

Well Patent Pending is a Punk Rock/Ska Band known for the unique stuff they do. Their new CD Save Each Other The Whales are Doing Fine is an awesome CD and contains a new song “This Can’t Be Happening Again”, which I say rocks major socks off. They have the coolest mascot ever, Benny The Whale. Patent Pending is originally from Long Island and they aren’t big. If you listen to some of their stuff on PureVolume.com or YouTube.com you will be amazed. They’re not even overrated like other bands are. The band members are cooler than coolio because they write the greatest songs about things. There is this thing they do on YouTube that’s called band world that’s really funny and many people would like it. It stars The P-unit and Benny the whale. Some of it is weird but people who like stupid little things would fall in love with the band. Remember Purevolume.com or YouTube.com to check them out. They rock!
