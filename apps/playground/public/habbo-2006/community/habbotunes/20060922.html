Habbo Tunes


Artist: Jesse McCartney
Album: Right Where You Want Me

Jesse’s new album (Right Where You Want Me) was released on September 19th 2006. I was anxious to get it in hopes that it would be just as satisfying as his last album (Beautiful soul.) I was quite pleased with my purchase as the songs have a great melody. They speak about relationships and love. “Right Back in the Water” would have to be my favorite song from his album. The lyrics tell of someone who is blinded by love and can’t end a relationship. The music is just deep and inspiring.

Artist: Lemon Demon
Album: Damn Skippy

Despite its name, Damn Skippy is a very kid-friendly album. Lemon Demon is basically a guy from Massachusetts who has WAY too much time on his hands. At first, the song “Dead Sea Monkeys” sets the tone for this zany and random little piece of work, going through songs with obvious names; “When Robots Attack”, “Geeks in Love”, “Kitten is Angry”, “Ode to Crayola” and ends on a high note with “Word Disassociation”. Videos have been made for some of the songs, so search around. Now, I command you, go online to LemonDemon.com and get yourself a Damn Skippy today!

Artist: Dave Matthews Band
Album: Crash

Dave Matthews Band is a mixture of acoustic, rock, bluegrass, classical and jazz music. The songs that I recommend are “Crash Into Me”, “Two Step”, and “American Baby”. The band mixes up their sound by using instruments such as the saxophone, violin, keyboard and of course the bass and acoustic guitar. Dave Matthews, the lead singer, uses the guitar much like a person would play a percussion instrument which makes the sound unique. The band members come from all over, making the band a giant melting pot, full of diverse and unique sound which is what caught my interest in the first place. They started in 1991 and have become world wide. The band is down to earth and has a calming sound. I enjoy their music just like many others! Go ahead take a listen!

Artist: Tupac
Album: All Eyez On Me

Tupac’s music is an instant classic. September is the month that he passed and I decided to play his music in respect to the profession he loved the best. All Eyez On Me includes so many songs that became a hit, not only on the billboards but also in the way we view life and how we should change it for the better for us and the others around us. Tupac changed so many people through his music. He was a great poet, writer, and actor and of course artist and we all miss him dearly. He lives on in you, if you simply respect the true lyrics of rap that are behind the violent, inappropriate and misleading rap that is presented to us these days.

Artist: Kisschasy
Album: United Paper People

Kisschasy is a great Australian band that plays alternative music with undertones of rock/pop. "United Paper People" is their debut album, though they have 2 EP's to their name. Their music has great lyrics that can really get you thinking. They have released 4 singles from this album their most recent being "This Bed" which has scored no standing on the charts as of yet (this song is also my top pick from the album.) Their first single "Do do's and Woah-oh's" got up to #25 on the Australian charts. So they're a great band with a fresh new sound and lyrics that can really make you feel better.

Artist: Plus 44
Album: When Your Heart Stops Beating [Being Released November 14,2006]

Plus 44 is a new band consisting of Mark Hoppus and Travis Barker of Blink-182, Shane Gallagher of The Nervous Return and Craig Fairbaugh of The Mercy Killers. Their songs are a bit darker then what Blink 182 used to sing about. For example their song “No It Isn't”, talks about suicide. Even Travis Barker himself admitted it to MTV, "If Blink was the daytime, Plus 44 is the nighttime. It's such a departure from Blink, but it's amazing."
