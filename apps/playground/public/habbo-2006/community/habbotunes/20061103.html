Habbo Tunes


Artist: Strange Daze
Album: Redefined

I’d like to talk about a local band of Minnesota; they’re called Strange Daze. I’ve had the pleasure of listening to them live in a bar near my hometown. It was a total head-banging dance revolution! They can take a nursery rhyme and rock it hard! And listening to their CD was just as good. It basically was a mix between Pearl Jam and Creed, so if you’re a fan of either band or both, I suggest picking up this CD. Like I said they’re a local band, based out of St. Cloud and Minneapolis, MN. I support local music as much as the big artists. And music like Strange Daze I listen to quite often. So you most likely will find me talking about locals or artists rarely heard of. Toodles!

Artist: The Mystery
Album: Making Dens

The Mystery Jets are a new group onto the scene, relatively unknown but tipped by the critics to be a hit on a similar scale to Franz Ferdinand! Their first album contains a wide variety of musical moods and styles, from softly synthesized songs to more slammin’ punk songs! They should appeal to anyone with an open mind to music, as they have a song to fill every genre (except RAP and R&B). The UK band recently topped the NME charts with their single The Boy Who Ran Away, beating groups such as Placebo and Panic! At the Disco. This doesn’t surprise me, because with a variety of guitar sounds, brilliant drum rhythms and with main vocals that fit the mood of each song, this should be the music for Habbos EVERYWHERE.

Artist: Rufus Wainwright
Album: Poses

Rufus Wainwright and his interesting take on his style of folk music make him a unique artist. My first thought of folk music was a banjo and a tambourine. I soon found out I was wrong. Rufus has a very melancholy feel to his music. You don’t get the heart pumping, rush of emotion, total excitement that we get with most artists today. For the music lover who enjoys soft music and the beauty that can be found on a gloom filled rainy day will love Rufus’s style. His song “Poses” hits that kind of feeling right on the head of the nail. I highly recommend his CD to others who go for that same melancholy feel.

Artist: Picasso Of Mess
Album: BloOd cELLs

Picasso Of Mess is a unsigned band out of London, Ontario. They were formed in 1998 and within the first year released there first album “LEVEL SEVEN”. The album was a big hit and sold over 5000 copies. In 2001 they released a second album called “SURFACE TO AIR”. “SURFACE TO AIR” went straight to number one on numerous college and university radio stations across North America. The band has been influenced by bands like Deftones, Sepultura, and System Of a Down, but listener may also hear influences such as The Clash, Ramones and Nirvana, and even a bit of Reggae. You can’t download there songs, But you can find them on www.myspace.com/picassoofmess.
