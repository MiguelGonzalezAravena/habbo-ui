Artist: The Starting Line
Album: Based On a True Story

I recently have been listing to The Starting Line a band from Churchville, Pennsylvania. They’re awesome! The Starting Line is great at making music. I got hooked when I first saw them on tour with Fall Out Boy in September of 2005(it was an awesome show). The Starting Line’s album, Based on a True Story, is a great album to listen to if you like an Alternative-Pop-Punk type of music. They’re whole Album is great! They have a lot of awesome songs like “Suprise, Surprise” and “The B-list”. They have five different albums. I only have one but it’s one of the best albums I’ve ever listened to. If you like bands like Fall Out Boy, Panic! At The Disco and Motion City Sound Track, The Starting Line would be a great new band to listen to. So go check them out.

Artist: Hinder
Album: Extreme Behavior

About three days before school ended last year I bought tickets to a Nickelback concert not knowing the opening bands. Once I heard the name of them (Hinder) I bought their album and loved it. This is really hard rock made for a head banger. Before you listen to this I have to warn you the lyrics and meanings of the songs aren’t always family friendly :D! Their song “Homecoming Queen” is actually an inspirational song that is still hard rock and it talks about seeing friends you haven’t seen in a long time, which in the long run (later in the song) means you shouldn’t take for granted what you have now because eventually its going to go away.

Artist: Carbon Leaf
Album: Indian Summer

Nobody was submitting any music I listen to, so I decided to submit some of my own! :) I’m kinda new to Carbon Leaf so I don’t know much about them…I know that they are uber though. Their songs are so different from one another; I guess that’s why I like them. Their music is so catchy and right when I heard it I was amazed. Hope you enjoy, fellow Habbos.

Artist: John Martyn
Album: Solid Air

John Martyn is an English born singer-songwriter who plays a mix of jazz and folk, at times incorporating a blend of folk-blues-reggae-ambient and rock. One of his most “known” tracks is named, “May You Never”, which is a song that I would say MUST be heard by all at least once.
