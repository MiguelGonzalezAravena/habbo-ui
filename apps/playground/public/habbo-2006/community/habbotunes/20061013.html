Artist: Shiny Toy Guns
Album: We are Pilots

Shiny Toy Guns is in fact not about guns, but about Indie / Rock / Electronica! I found them while checking out music and I fell in love with what they had. They are newly signed and have everything from songs about love to songs about the weather! I suggest if you like techno you may just love Shiny Toy Guns. “Now I’;m glowing like a sunset in the window Of my dreams...” Check them out. =)

Artist: Muse
Album: Black Holes & Revelations

This is by far one of the best albums that Muse has made so far. Muse is a Rock band all the way from the UK. The band formed back in 1999 when band members vocalist/guitarist Matthew Bellamy, bassist Chris Wolstenhome, and the drummer Dominic Howard, came out with their first CD, Showbiz. Their newest album has some pretty good songs like, "Knights of Cydonia", one of their popular songs at the moment. Others are "Starlight", "Take a Bow", and "Map Of The Problematique". So if you’re looking for a great band to rock out to, then Muse is it!

Artist: The Killers
Album: Sam's Town

Alright guys, well...I went to the mall a little while ago and I was looking around aimlessly for a new CD when I noticed that, The Killers were coming out with a new CD. When I saw that I almost freaked (seeing as how I loved their first album!) I went home and for the next little while I couldn't wait till it came out, so I counted down the days till and..."Boom" it was the day of its release! After picking it up, I popped it into my CD player and listened to it. I found that not only have The Killers adapted a new way of playing their music, they also have written better songs this time as well. Instead of the typical, "I know a girlfriend who looked like a boyfriend that I had in February of last year" repeated over and over again, we have deeper, much more poetic lyrics. I find that every song on this CD somehow relates to my life, and I'm sure it'll do the same for other people as well. I seriously love the song, "Bones" by The Killers on this album, it's such an awesome song, even though it's a little corny...but, whatever. I also find that listening to this CD while hanging out with friends, relaxes you and I love to hang out and be mellow so I can't stop listening to this CD. So, go buy this CD and hopefully you will enjoy it as much as I have.
