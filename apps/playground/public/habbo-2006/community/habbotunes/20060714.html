Habbo Tunes

Artist: Britney Spears
Artist: B In The Mix

The pop princess released an album solely dedicated to remixes of her top hits, and some new ones! I’m not really a huge fan of remixes, but some are really good and the organization is fantastic. My favourites are by far the racy “Slave 4 U” and “Breathe On Me”, as well as the new “And Then We Kiss” – Britney’s greatest hits - remixed!

Artist: Cute Is What We Aim For
Album: The Same Old Blood Rush With A New Touch

I first discovered Cute Is What We Aim For when I started listening to Panic! at the Disco and I was looking at other bands on their label on myspace. I saw CIWWAF and I decided to listen to some of their songs and I fell in love with them! Recently their debut album The Same Old Blood Rush With a New Touch was released and I bought it the day it came out! The songs on the album are absolutely amazing! I haven’t taken the CD out of my walkman yet! The CD is also on sale at Best Buy for only $9.99! If you like Panic! at the Disco or Fall Out Boy then you will definitely like this up and coming pop punk/power pop band with a great new sound!

Artist: Fleetwood Mac
Album: Rumours

Fleetwood Mac was one of the very first rock bands. They started in the early ’60s, And in 1977 they released there biggest album yet called Rumours, which includes such hits as “Don’t Stop”, “Dreams”, “Go your Own Way”, “Second Hand News”, “Never Going Back Again” and “You Make Loving Fun”. The album has sold 18 million copies to date making it the 5th best selling album of all time. If you like classic rock, I strongly suggest you listen to this album. Some of Fleetwood Mac’s other hits are “Landslide” (which was remade by the Dixie Chicks), “Little Lies”, “Gypsy”, “Rihanna”, “Everywhere”, “Say You Love Me” and “Tusk”. They truly are a classic band and are definitely worth listening to, so give them a try.

Artist: Gnarls Barkley
Album: St. Elsewhere

Two Words: GOOD STUFF! Anyone who has heard Gnarls Barkley’s leadoff single “Crazy” from the insanely progressive first album which arrived this year, knows why the single has been at the number one spot in the UK for weeks and is now arriving to the USA. Trying to describe the sound and style of Gnarls Barkley is impossible – it’s a bit of everything, from Soul to Pop to Hip-Hop and Funk vibes, but in the end it defies description. If there is any justice in this world, Gnarls Barkley will receive beaucoup accolades for their amazing and at the same time unique work!

Artist: The Dresden Dolls
Album: Yes, Virginia

Call The Dresden Dolls punk cabaret, theatrical rock, manic-musical, whatever. Amanda Palmer’s writing, piano playing and incredible voice mixes with drummer Brian Viglione’s talents in percussion to create an unexplainable sound that in the end you have to give up trying to classify and say, “You just have to hear it yourself.”
