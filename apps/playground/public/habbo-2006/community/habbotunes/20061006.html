Artist: Maxïmo Park
Album: A Certain Trigger

A few months ago, when I went out on a quest to find new music, I never fathomed I would find a band this amazing. (Move over Chili Peppers, there’s a new group of men in Wombat’s life.) The very first song, “Signal And Sign” immediately drags you in with its infectious beat and leaves you craving for more. And yes, you do get more. Song number deux, “Apply Some Pressure”, easily stands out as one of the many songs on this album that makes you shake and twist your body in strange ways. People may think you’re having a seizure, but no, you’re only dancing to Maxïmo Park. There are a few darker songs such as “Going Missing”, “Limassol” and “Once, A Glimpse” but by no means do they leave you feeling depressed. Some of the many things that give you that, “OMG, I LOVE THIS SO MUCH!” feeling are the lyrics, which are bittersweet, angry, thoughtful, romantic, and funny (sometimes all in the same song), and the untamed Geordie accent of lead singer, Paul Smith, which makes the songs all that more endearing. I can say without a doubt that I love every song on this CD, even the mixed-reviewed “Acrobat”, a song that is mostly spoken-word, but it has some of the most thought-provoking lyrics I’ve ever heard. Maxïmo Park are usually categorized as post-punk/new wave/alternative/pop, but don’t think of that new wannabe punk stereotype. They have their own, magical, original, upbeat sound and most important of all, actual talent. Even if you hate this genre (heck, even if you hate music), it would be almost impossible to completely hate this band. The End. P.S. CHECK THEM OUT!

Artist: Keith Urban
Album: Be Here

Keith Urban is such a fantastic guy because he is amazing at the guitar, can play bass, electric or acoustic, he can play piano, and he can play the banjo! But, boy, what a mean guitar he does play! He has a new song called “Once in a Lifetime Love”. Just like all his music, it is leaning towards rock music, so I’m sure you’d like it. To listen to his music, you can go to his website, or buy his CDs! But trust me, he is worth checking out, plus he’s got quite the looks!

Artist: Scissor Sisters
Album: Ta-Dah

Scissor Sisters is back again with their newest album, “Ta-Dah”. After their smash album, Scissor Sisters jumped quickly up the charts. The band knew they’d have a lot to live up to for their sophomore album. However, they managed to do it with incredible energy. The band maintains their quirky, signature sound while adding funky new beats that make you want to groove! From the disco-based, “I Don’t Feel Like Dancin’” to the Billy Joel sounding, “She’s My Man” every song on this album brings a different flavor to the album. The eccentric video for “I Don’t Feel Like Dancin’” will bring a smile to audiences of all ages. In addition to the poppy, melodic songs, there is also a selection of deeper songs that will touch the heart. I guarantee that everyone will find something to love about this album. So, go out and buy their newest CD, “Ta-Dah” at a music store near you today.

Artist: The Cat Empire
Album: Two Shoes

The Cat Empire is an Alternative band from Melbourne, Australia. With a group of horns that play with them and their own group of dancers, you know you’ll be getting something completely different when you listen to The Cat Empire. Two Shoes may not be their latest album, but it’s definitely their best. This album reached #1 on the Australian music charts and is host to three of their hit singles “Sly”, “The Car Song” and the album’s namesake “Two Shoes”. All three of these songs got into the top 50 on the Australian Charts. So, go get your greasy little hands on The Cat Empire’s album, Two Shoes, today (Or their newest album Cities but I’ll let you find out that one for yourself…)!

Artist: Stephin Merritt
Album: Showtunes

I just can’t get enough of Stephin Merritt’s most recent album, Showtunes. It consists of a collection of songs for various Chinese plays he has composed. The songs range in tone from dark, morbid ditties having to do with emperors being assassinated and very expensive rings being fought over, to light playful tunes about mermaids, centipedes and ugly ducks! Strings, percussion, and a collection of vocalists bring life to clever lyrics that illustrate the stories of the plays these songs are used in. It’s a great collection that never leaves me bored. My favorite songs include: “In China, Said the Moon...”, “The World Is Not Made of Flowers”, “Behold the Lowly Centipede” and “Ukulele Me!”.If you’re into music that’s just a little out of the ordinary, then I think you’ll enjoy this album!
